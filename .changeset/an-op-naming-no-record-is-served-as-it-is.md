An op that names an id which is no record is served as it is, and a seat from whom nothing is kept is served the log as it is (FR-67). Graview Cloud's repair removes a dangling link — `fills: vendor:bloom → category:ghost`, whose far end was never a record — and `redact(ops, seatLens(store, seat))` served it to the app's own owner as "A change you cannot see", though no policy declared a sight, `hidesFrom(store, seat)` was false and `seesId(store, seat)("category:ghost")` was true. The seat view judged a link by whether both its ends were records served at its moment, and an end that is no record was never served. Now a seat lens says whether anything is kept from its seat, and `redact` hands a seat from whom nothing is kept its ops unchanged. With sights, a link's end that is not there at its moment is judged as `seesId` judges the id — nothing that names no record is kept from anybody, and a record the log knows is judged by the kind it was — so the repair is served as it is, its `writes` still naming the end its primitive names, unless it names a record the seat may not see. A link to a record that was hidden from the seat is still withheld, whether the record was removed before the link, after it or in the same op, in either order, and its id is served nowhere (FR-55). Tests hold Cloud's reproduction, the same repair landed through `applyPrimitives` on a graph holding the dangling link, each of those removals, and every seat from whom nothing is kept across 1,000 random worlds; the FR-55 worlds now point fields at ids that are no record too, and still serve no unseen id and still fold. `capabilities().shipped` names FR-67.

Compatibility: ops and primitives — an op is served to a seat differently in two cases only: to a seat from whom nothing is kept, every op as it is; and to any seat, a link whose missing end names no record, or a record it may see, as it is rather than withheld. `SeatLens` gains an optional `hides`. Stored formats, check codes and tool schemas are unchanged. The wire — additive: `capabilities().shipped` gains `FR-67`.
