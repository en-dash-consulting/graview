A worker view is a place, with a manifest the host enforces (FR-91). A guest view was handed one kind's members and the edges among them, so LifeLogics' package lens could not see a package's offers. `GuestNode.label` was declared and never filled, no theme came with the props, and a guest view could not be a named place. A worker view now says in its manifest what it is and what it may touch: `{ name, title?, attach: kind | "home", cardinality, reads?: { kinds, edges }, acts? }`. `mountWorkerView` takes the manifest, and `checkManifest` names every kind, edge and act the app does not declare, and a name that is not lower-case letters, digits and hyphens. The host refuses to start a view whose manifest says any of these, and `onFailure` hears `manifest`. What the view is handed, `workerViewProps`, is the viewer's sight cut to the kind it attaches to (the members the face hands it, or every one the viewer sees) and the kinds and edges it reads. A record the viewer may not see is in none of it, and a kind or an edge the view did not ask to read is not handed to it however visible. The acts it is told of are only those its manifest names. Every record's `label` is filled as the host labels it, for a frame guest too.

`registerWorkerView` in `@graview/guest/host` registers a view for the kind its manifest attaches to, in the cell its cardinality says, and given a title, as a named place on the Graview face and the pages face. Whatever the registry drew there before is drawn if the view fails. `workerHome` makes a view whose manifest attaches to `"home"` a component for the routed face's `home` surface. The scene face has no home body to replace yet: the declared home slot that FR-81 asks for is its place, and `workerHome`'s component is ready for it. A view's props carry `theme`, a `GuestTheme` with the scheme and the accent, ground, panel, ink, muted ink and edge colours and the body and mono fonts. It is read off the region the view is drawn in: the scheme of the nearest `data-graview-scheme` (the embed's), else its `color-scheme`. The host pushes again when the app's own toggle changes the scheme, whatever the system prefers. `mountWorkerView` takes `author`, said under the region ("Made by Claude for Nick").

A unit test reads the props of the package lens over an offers fixture in the shape of LifeLogics Offers. Lin, of the client, is handed the packages, the three offers made to her firm and the `includes` edges among them, with no trace of the internal margin review: not its id, label, cost or edges. Erin, staff, is handed it. Neither is handed parties or notes, and a frame guest's records carry their labels. `guest-sandbox` gains `--transport=place`, which mounts the embed over the fixture with the lens registered by its manifest. On the pages face the lens is listed among the places and drawn at `/places/the-packages`, titled "The packages" and with its author said. On the Graview face pressing the "The packages" pill draws it. On both faces it lists each package's offers, and the offer Lin may not see is nowhere. In light the packages take the app's light panel. After the app's toggle to dark, with the system still preferring light, they take the dark panel and the view is pushed the dark theme. All twelve claims hold in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox.

Compatibility: the wire — additive: `GuestProps` gains an optional `theme`, and a guest's records now carry `label` as `GuestNode` always promised. Ops, stored formats, check codes and tool schemas are unchanged.
