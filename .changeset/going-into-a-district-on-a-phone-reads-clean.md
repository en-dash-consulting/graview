Going into a district on a phone reads clean (Roadmap feature:f-phone-drive-in-reads-clean). Inside Things' rules on a phone the bar said "The whole thing"; Up and the Key stood over the card's right edge; the card read "Acts…", a block of problems with a link under it, Filter, Arrange and Find, an author's "A list of rules, derived from the declaration · Keep as a lens", and then two of its three rules, with heavy boxed "…" buttons; and "+3 MORE" under it said nothing of what it was.

The bar says where you are. The scene's first place is called "Everything" — every district, from above, which is what pressing it shows — on the bar, in the seat's "where you are" and on Up's hover ("Up — everything, from above"). Not "Home": that is the routed face's front page, and an app's scene may open on a district of its own. Inside a district no picture draws, or on a record, the one control names it ("Rules", "Pay the deposit") as a place offered nowhere, its key `scene:district:<focus>`; a district a picture draws is named by the picture, as before.

Content first. Where a district's stop is narrower than 560 pixels it is its head — the name, the count and one line of what they are, the creating act, and one "⋯" — then one line of problems ("⚠ 2 problems · Nothing done before what it waits for, Nothing overdue · Show") whose press shows only the records with a problem and then "Show all", then the records. Find, Filter, Arrange, the shape's switch and "Acts…" are behind the "⋯". Where rows stand below the card's foot it says so ("3 more eras below ↓"), a press that scrolls to them. Rows are light there: names not underlined, and a quiet "⋯" with no box, in a 44-pixel target.

No author's words to a reader. "… derived from the declaration" and "Keep as a lens" show only to a seat that may change the app (`mayChangeDeclaration`, the studio's and the seat's own judgment, now on `@graview/tools/draft` too), and never on a phone. Things' rule kind says "Something every list and task is held to." where it said "this list".

The width is the card's. On a phone's ground Up and the Key share a row of their own above the picture, the Key to Up's left, and a district gone into takes the width under it with even gutters; a chosen record's "Acts…" (`chosen-acts`) stands in that row too, where it covers no card's "Open". From altitude the Key stays under Up and the city keeps the corner it always kept. The card for the districts the row has no room for says "3 more districts". A district's heading taking the keyboard as it opens no longer pans the camera while the card is still on its way in (the eras landed 26 pixels left of their gutter, with the pan in the address): the landing is judged once the ground has settled.

verify-drive-in holds `aDistrictGoneIntoOnAPhoneNamesWhereYouAre`, `itsRecordsComeBeforeItsTools`, `everyRecordItCountsIsReachable`, `noAuthorWordsReachAReader` and `nothingOverlapsTheCard` on every district of the todo example, the discography and the rota, on a Pixel 7 and an iPhone 13, and runs in the Nightly. verify-kind-stop's `aRowsActsAreVisible` asks a phone's "⋯" for its glyph's contrast, not an edge. A hosted page carries 0.6 KB more up front (579 bytes minified), and one handed a compiled app 0.5 KB more (554 bytes): the bar's district, the Key's row and the phone's layout are drawn before anything is turned on. The scene face carries 1.7 KB more before it draws; every face 2.0 KB more gzipped, the studio's embed 0.6 KB and the pages face alone 0.2 KB, Cloud's worker 688 bytes; raised in `scripts/budget-raises/going-into-a-district-on-a-phone-reads-clean-*.json`.

Compatibility: ops and primitives, stored formats, the wire, the declaration and check finding codes, and derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged.
