Datetimes are said in the app's time zone, and a template can say a time of day. An app declares its zone once, `brand.timeZone` (an IANA name, "America/New_York") on `defineApp`'s brand or a document's `brand`, and every instant kept with a zone (`2026-10-16T14:47:00Z`, `…-04:00`) is said on that clock: in lists, cards, record pages, a rule's sentence, an act's sentence and its echo to an agent, Activity's digest, the static page and the date editor, which reads and keeps an instant on the same clock. A person's zone wins over it where a host passes one — `timeZone` on `GraviewProvider`, the embed and the pages face, beside `locale` — and each reader's own clock is said when neither says, as before. A moment kept without a zone is still the wall time written. Describe templates take `{at | time}` ("10:47 AM") and `{at | datetime}` ("Oct 16, 2026, 10:47 AM EDT"), and a field block takes `as: "time"` and `as: "datetime"`; `{at | date}` and `relative` count an instant's day on the app's clock. An agent's act given `2026-10-16T10:47:00-04:00` echoed "2:47 PM", the server's UTC; a tool runtime handed the app now says it in the app's zone, and a host answering away from any page says its own words in it with `sayingIn({ appTimeZone }, run)`. A view is handed the zone rather than formatted strings: every props push carries `locale` and `timeZone`, for `Intl.DateTimeFormat(props.locale, { timeZone: props.timeZone, … })`, so no view hard-codes a zone again. `graview check` and the document's checker refuse a zone the runtime does not know, or an offset, by name (`brand-time-zone`); `set-brand` sets one (`timeZone`, null clears it), and a diff says "Times are said in America/New_York." `describeApp` says the zone. The `graview-brand`, `graview-node-kind` and `graview-worker-view` skills say so. A hosted page carries 2.0 KB more up front for it (the compiled page too), and the guest host about 0.5 KB; their budgets are raised by that.

Compatibility: ops and stored records are unchanged; the declaration takes an optional `brand.timeZone`, and a declaration without it says every time as before. The check finding `brand-time-zone` is new. The guest wire's props carry `locale` and `timeZone`. `Money` (a block's and a template's `money`) takes `timeZone`; `readableFields` takes `timeZone`; `momentAsRead`, `timeAsRead` and `whenAsRead` take a zone after the locale. `sayIn` takes `readerTimeZone` and `appTimeZone` beside `reader` and `app`. A derived edit's sentence and Activity's digest say an instant's day and time on the page's zone, where they said them on the runtime's clock.
