What FR-170's edits left the older ones doing to a policy, and what the studio and a creating act's line said, reviewed before 0.1.28. `remove-kind` took the kind out of every sight and dropped a sight left showing nothing, so removing the kind the last sights showed took the sights away, and every other kind came to be seen by everyone, which the edit never said: it is now refused, saying "every sight shows only matches, so with them gone everyone would see everything", and `remove-sight` first, in the same change, says it in words. It also took a grant on the kind's derived edit or remove with it no more, so removing the people kind of the Secret Santa left a grant naming "edit-participant" and a change the check refused (`grant-unknown-mutation`): those acts now go from every grant, and a grant left naming no act, or no kind where it named some, goes, as a grant whose last act is removed does. A grant on no kind (`kinds: []`) gives nothing, since the policy matches no kind against it, and was said as a grant on every kind: `add-grant` refuses one, and a document that holds one is said "on no kind, so on nothing". `diffDocuments` says a role taken away ("A participant is no longer a role."), which was unsaid beside any other change to the policy, and a new policy with no grant says nobody but the system may do anything. The studio could not revoke a grant on a kind's derived edit: it has no act for one, so the grant read as naming no act, and revoking it was no edit at all; a grant the document had reads those acts from the document now. And a removal the studio says names what the grant leaves out — `kinds: "*"`, `self: false`, `ownEnd: false`, a sight's `own: false` — so revoking a grant to every kind beside one held to a person's own record no longer means both and is refused. What goes with a removed kind or field is named as a person reads it: "The match kind is removed, and every match with it; so is the act "Draw a match"", where it said "so are act draw", and a rule by its title ("the rule "A booked vendor has a quote"", and a repair of it), where it said the rule's id. A creating act's line said "Acme Holdings Inc." as a sentence and left it out: a stop after an abbreviation ("Inc.", "U.S.") is not a sentence's.

Compatibility: ops, stored formats, wire messages, the document format, the compiled format and check finding codes are unchanged. `editDocument` refuses `remove-kind` while every sight shows only that kind, and `add-grant` with `kinds: []`, each as `edit`; `remove-kind` takes the kind's `edit-<kind>` and `remove-<kind>` out of every grant, and a grant left with no act or no kind goes. What `remove-kind` and `remove-field` say names an act or a rule that goes with them by its title, where it named its id. `DocumentDiff`'s sentences may say a role taken away, and "on no kind, so on nothing". The studio's `documentEdits` says a removal with `kinds`, `self` and `ownEnd` (a sight's `own`) given. A creating act's line keeps a value ending in an abbreviation's stop. What a host does: a chat removing a kind its last sights show sends `remove-sight` first, in the same change.
