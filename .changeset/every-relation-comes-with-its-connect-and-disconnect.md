Every relation comes with its connect and disconnect, as every kind comes with its edit, remove and archive. After an `add-relation` — a moment taken on a day, a moment that features a traveler — nothing could link two records until somebody also wrote an act that `connects`. Now each kind's relation is given `connect-<kind>-<relation>` and `disconnect-<kind>-<relation>` (the relation in kebab-case, `connect-moment-taken-on`), on the record it leaves as `id` and the record it reaches as `to`. A cardinality-one relation is set and cleared ("Set the day", "Clear the day") and connecting another replaces the link it had; a many one is linked and unlinked ("Link a traveler", "Unlink a traveler"); each reads from the other end too ("Link a moment"), its description carries the relation's own, and a kind with two relations to the same kind tells them apart by the relation's words ("Set the person (reviewer)"). Any act the app declares that `connects` a relation replaces the derived connect, any that `severs` it the derived disconnect; an `appendOnly` relation is given no disconnect, and a relation to another app's kind is given neither, because it is a field the derived edit already sets. They are permitted as a derived edit is — to whoever may make or change the kind the relation leaves, or a grant naming them — and refuse a record the seat may not see. Because they declare what they connect and sever, a line's acts, the far record's page, the inline AI's fills, an import's links and an agent's tools offer them with nothing more; `graview describe` says, per relation, which acts make and break it. A grant or a repair in a document may name one, and it follows a renamed kind or relation. `edge-without-severer` is gone: a relation some act makes and none breaks is now given its disconnect, so the asymmetry it warned of cannot be declared.

Compatibility: ops, stored formats and the wire are unchanged. Derived tool names and input schemas gain `connect-<kind>-<relation>` and `disconnect-<kind>-<relation>` ({ id, to }, strict) for every relation no act connects or severs — in this repository's apps, only the desk's `uses` relation (`connect-app-uses`, `disconnect-app-uses`); the conformance kit announces `connect-vendor-fills` and `disconnect-vendor-fills` on the 0.1.10 shortlist. The check no longer gives `edge-without-severer`.
