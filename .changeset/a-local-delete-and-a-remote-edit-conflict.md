A record deleted here that the remote edited is a conflict, not a delete that wins. Under `conflicts: "report"` the sync engine held a field both sides changed, but a record deleted here was sent as a delete whatever the remote had done to it since they last agreed, so somebody's edit there was thrown away without a word. Now an inbound change to a record deleted here that changes what both sides agreed is a conflict like a field's, and goes where the policy says. `report` holds it — `{ localId, deleted: true, theirs, base }`, with `theirs` the remote's record in our field names — and the delete is not sent until `resolve` chooses: `"ours"` sends the delete on the next run; `"theirs"` makes the record again with the remote's values through `createInbound`, authored by the system, and links what it made to the remote's record at the version of that edit. `theirs` does the same in the pass that hears the edit, and `ours` sends the delete in it; the report's `resolved` says each. Without `createInbound`, taking theirs lets the remote keep its record and leaves this one deleted and unlinked, sending nothing. A record already held in conflict and then deleted here is held as a deleted record; one whose delete is taken back is held field by field against the remote's record, never sent over it; one the remote then deletes too is nobody's to choose and is not said `gone`. A record projected with a version and deleted here before the engine was made is linked as it was projected, so its delete is sent (or held), where it was never sent.

Compatibility: ops, stored formats, wire messages, the document format, the compiled format, check finding codes and tool names and schemas are unchanged; the hosted page's first load is the same to the byte. Changed in `@graview/core/sync`: `SyncConflict` is `FieldConflict | DeletedConflict` (both new exports), so `field` and `ours` are absent on a deleted record's conflict; `SettledConflict` is that union with `took`; `SyncConflictOptions.argsFor` is handed a `FieldConflict`, and `syncConflictInvariant` passes over a deleted record's conflict; `SyncState.conflicts` may carry a deleted record's; `createInbound` is also called to make a record again.
