The edge says what is past it. A city wider than the window keeps its shape and is reached by panning, and on a phone the gauntlet's Topics and Staff stood wholly past the left edge with nothing on the screen saying they were there: a pointer had nothing to press. From altitude, every district whose middle is past an edge now has a sign on that edge, in its own name and pointing at it, and pressing the sign pans the ground until the district is in view. `districtsPastTheEdge(layout)` names them, with the side and the pan.
