What a selection agrees on is read among records of one kind, and an act offered whole is one its input accepts (the eleventh walk). A shelter's "Room in the kennel" chose a kennel and the animals in it; the structure provider read the three as one set agreeing on `status`, said "2 share the status in-care; "Run E05" does not" with the animals' stored value raw, and offered "Align the status of "Run E05" with the other 2" — the kennel's edit told a status only an animal can have, refused on press. A field of one name on two kinds is now two facts: agreement and the odd one out are read within each kind, an alignment is offered only where the act's input accepts its arguments as they stand, and a neighbor that is itself one of the chosen is no longer "shared by all but" itself.

Compatibility: ops, stored formats, the wire, the declaration and derived tools are unchanged.
