A card says its kind's glance and no more, the bar names a place once, and a drawn home stays Home beside `pages.first` (the same review app). A finding card read "Critical · Security · High" — the third its confidence, though a severity can be high too — and still did after `confidence` was taken out of the kind's `glance`: the glance chose the order and the rest of the kind followed it. A glance a kind declares is now said exactly, and a choice another field shown beside it shares says its field ("Confidence: high", "Likelihood: low"), on a card, a row, a describe line and a table alike. A default card no longer draws a long or Markdown text as a chip cut after a couple of dozen characters: it says the text's start on a line of its own under the chips. With a home declared and `pages.first: "This week"`, the app opened on This week, and a press on Home from the scene drew the pages at their first entry and went to This week again, so the home the author drew was nowhere: `pages.first` now picks where a new visit lands, once, and after that `/` is the home on both faces. `graview check` says so when both are set (`pages-first-beside-home`, a note). And a kind whose plural is "root causes" beside a blocks place titled "Root causes" put two "Root causes" on the bar: the place keeps the name and stands, as `pages.first` and `pages.primary` already read the word, and the kind's list folds into More as "All root causes", still at `/root-causes`; `graview check` warns of it (`pages-place-named-as-a-list`). The graview-pages and graview-lists skills teach each. With the template fixes beside them, a hosted page carries 1.2 KB more up front (and 0.6 KB more up front handed a compiled app).

Compatibility: ops, stored records and wire messages are unchanged. New check finding codes `pages-first-beside-home` (a note) and `pages-place-named-as-a-list` (a warning). `readableFields` with `glance: true` says only a kind's declared `display.glance` when it has one, and a choice's `alone` may carry its field's label. `barPlaces` labels a kind's list "All <plural>" and folds it when a place has its name (`allLabel` from `@graview/core`). `@graview/react/provider` exports `hasLanded(views)` and `markLanded(views)`, which the pages and the embed read `pages.first` once by. A default card's prose is `p[data-graview-excerpt="<field>"]`.
