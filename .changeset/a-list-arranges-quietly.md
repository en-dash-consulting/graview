A list page leads with its records. On Graview Cloud's "Purchase scenarios", about 400 pixels came before the first record. There was an eyebrow that counted ("◆ 16 PURCHASE SCENARIOS"), then the title, then "Related:" and "See purchase scenarios as:" on lines of their own. Then came "Sort by [as they come ▾]" and "Group by [nothing ▾]" as form-sized selects, and a full-width "Only… ▾". Now the title has the kind's mark beside it. Under it come the declared description and one quiet line: "Related: …" with the kinds as plain links, and "Also as: …" with the kind's pictures. Last is the arranging line, which says the count once: "16 purchase scenarios · Sort ▾ · Group ▾ · Filter ▾". Each word opens a short list in the top layer. A default says nothing; a choice says itself ("Sorted by due date ↓", "Grouped by the list it is on, a month each"). The sort's direction is in the Sort list, in the words its values read in ("Earliest first", "Highest first", "A to Z"). A date group's width is in the Group list. Each condition is shown as words with a × ("Due before 15 Sep 2026 ×"). A condition that is on shows as pressed in the Filter list, and pressing it again takes it off. A date asks for its day inside the list. The keyboard goes into a list at the choice already made, the arrow keys, Home and End move through it, and Escape gives the keyboard back to the word that opened it. Where the line is narrow (a phone) it shows the count and one "Arrange", which holds all three. A list of fewer than eight records is read, not arranged: the line is only its count, unless its address arranged it. "N of M" is said only when something narrowed the list. The first record is now 160–200 px below the bar at 1440, where it was 240–380, and 140–215 px on a phone, where it was 290–440. No select is left for arranging.

The bar's Find narrows the list you are on, an app's own list as well as the derived one ("Narrow tasks…"). A list page no longer draws a second Find box under it.

A declared row keeps the glance. Where a document declared a kind's row as only its title, the list showed bare names. The facts the kind's glance names that the row does not already say now follow it, quietly, on its line. Adding a record is one quiet press at the list's end ("+ Add a deliverable"). It opens the act's form in place with the keyboard in its first field, and Escape puts it away. The form stands open while the list is empty and when the words found nothing and the name is already in it. The end of every page now scrolls clear of the ask field, which used to cover a phone's last row. The field tells the page how much room it takes (`--graview-foot-room`), and the page pads its end by that much.

The bar ranks its places. On the todo app's pages, ten words of equal weight stood on the bar: Home, Lists, Tasks, Rules, Reasons, People, Invitations and three pictures. They gave no sign of what the app is about. Now the primary places stand, in their order and never more than six with Home, and the rest fold into "More". A declaration names the primary places with `pages.primary`: kinds by name or plural, pictures by title. When it names none, every kind stands with its pictures except the ones that support the others: a kind `pages.hide` leaves off the home, a kind of a module drawn only for its keepers (the installation's people and invitations), and a note that points at several kinds and that nothing points at (a reason "about a task or a list"). Connections folds into "More". `graview check` warns on a word that names nothing (`pages-primary-unknown`) and on more names than can stand (`pages-primary-many`). `arrange-pages` takes `primary`. The todo app and the rota declare theirs.

A large list has a way through it. Discography's 479 albums were a bare column of names under an eyebrow ("THE DISCOGRAPHY"), "479 of 479 shown." and a page Find. A list of 40 records or more (`LARGE_LIST`) now opens in an order that means something, unless its address says another. It opens newest first where the kind says when it starts (`fieldRoles.start`), and otherwise by name, and its line says so ("Sorted by released ↓"). Each record is one line with its glance at the line's end. An index of its years, or its letters, runs along one line above the rows, and each entry jumps to its section.

A design draws the same list. `ListPage` (`@graview/pages`) is the derived list page as a building block. A design hands it a kind and what is its own: how the list opens (`opening`), its row (`row`, which gets the record's label, address, problem and glance), whether its rows are cards, flush lines or a grid (`layout`), the sentence under the title (`description`), and `bare` to draw inside the design's own main. The head, the arranging line, the index and adding one come from the framework. The todo app, the rota, Discography and Seedbed's almanac now draw their lists this way. None of them has its own eyebrow, its own "N of M shown." or its own Find any more. Their rows are their own: a task with its tick; a shift with its day, its hour and who has it; an album with whose it is, its year and what it is; a plot as a card. Their pages are on neutral paper, and their names wrap where they used to be cut off. Under the one bar, the almanac draws no rail, since the rail repeated the bar's places and standing.

The bar's Find says "Narrow albums…" where that fits, and "Narrow…" where it would be cut.

Compatibility: ops, stored formats and the wire are unchanged. The document format and `arrange-pages` gain an optional `pages.primary`, and check gains the warning codes `pages-primary-unknown` and `pages-primary-many`. Which places stand on the bar changed. A harness never counted on how many stand, and docs/stability.md's recipe still reaches every place: press `app-place-<key>` if it is visible, else `app-places-open` and the place in `app-places`. The arranging controls changed from selects to buttons:
- `<prefix>-sort` and `<prefix>-group` are now buttons. Their `value` is still the chosen sort or group, so reading `.value` works as before. `selectOption` no longer does: press the word, then the entry `[data-testid="arrange-list"] [data-value="<key>"]`.
- `<prefix>-add` ("Filter", which was the "Only…" select) is a button. Its entries are `[data-testid="arrange-list"] [data-value="<key>:<value>"]` (`holds:today`, `price:at-most:25000`, `due:before`), with `aria-pressed` on the ones that are on.
- New: `<prefix>-count`, `<prefix>-all` (the one "Arrange" on a narrow line), `<prefix>-sort-default`, `<prefix>-group-none`, `<prefix>-direction-asc` and `-desc`, and `<prefix>-bucket-<day|week|month|year|decade>`. The last six are inside the open list.
- Removed: the `<prefix>-direction` button, which is now a group in the Sort list. `<prefix>-bucket` is now a group in the Group list, not a select. `list-filter-note` is gone: a narrowing by a relation is a `<prefix>-condition` like any other. The eyebrow count is gone.
- `kind-pictures` reads "Also as:" where it read "See … as:". It sits beside `kind-relations` on one line (`kind-related`), and the relations are plain links without their line marks.
- `<prefix>-kept` is still "N of M". `<prefix>-condition`, `<prefix>-query`, `<prefix>-ask-day`, `<prefix>-day` and `<prefix>-day-add` keep their names.
- On a list page the act forms are inside `act-<act>`. Press `act-open-<act>` (or the button named for the act) when `form-<act>` is hidden.
- The derived face's list draws no query box under a bar. The bar's `nav-find` narrows the list on an app's own list page too.
- `POPOVERS` gains `arrange` (trigger `arrange-add`, pane `arrange-list`).
- A large list (40 or more records) opens sorted when its address says nothing: newest first by the kind's `fieldRoles.start`, else by name. It is sectioned under `[data-testid="list-index"]`, with one `list-section` per year or letter, and `data-sectioned` (`year` or `letter`) on `records`. A harness that read a large list's rows in the order they were stored should now pass `?sort=` (empty) to keep that order.
- The examples' own list pages use `ListPage`, so their `list-*` ids (`list-controls`, `list-query`, `list-sort`, `list-group`, `list-show`, `list-filter`) are now the shared line's `arrange-*` ids. Adding a record there is `act-open-<act>`. The rota's old words (`show=bare`, `group=place|day|none`, `sort=when|name`) are rewritten in place into the shared ones.
- New in `@graview/pages`: `ListPage`, `ListPageProps`, `ListRowFacts` and `LARGE_LIST`.
- `ArrangeBarProps` gains `noun`. New: `directionWords`, `ARRANGE_NARROW` and `preloadArrangeLists` (`@graview/primitives`), `ARRANGE_FROM` (`@graview/pages`), and `MOST_STANDING`, `primaryOf` and `supportingKinds` (`@graview/core`). `BarPlace` gains `primary`, and `placesThatStand` takes `primary` and `most`. Removed from `@graview/primitives`' main entry: `roundSteps`. It now lives with the arranging lists, which are fetched the first time one opens.
- Budgets: the hosted page goes up 1 251 bytes to 579.0 KB (the bar's ranking), and its budget is 579.2 KB. The pages face goes up 5.5 KB (the line, a large list's index and `ListPage`). Every face goes up 14.9 KB, about 8.4 KB of it the arranging lists' own chunk, which is fetched only when a list opens.

For Graview Cloud's harnesses: read the chosen sort or group as before. To choose one, press `arrange-sort`, `arrange-group` or `arrange-add`, then the `data-value` entry in `arrange-list`. At a phone's width, press `arrange-all` first. To add a record from a list, press `act-open-<act>` before filling `form-<act>`.
