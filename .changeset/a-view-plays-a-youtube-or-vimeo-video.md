A view plays a YouTube or Vimeo video where it stands, in a player the host draws. A worker view never draws a frame and played only an uploaded video, so a talk or a trailer was a link that left the app. It now names the video, `<graview-video data-provider="youtube" data-video="dQw4w9WgXcQ" title="The keynote">` or by its watch address (`data-watch="${talk.link}"`), with `data-start`, `data-shape` and a `poster` from the pictures it may draw already. The providers are a closed list: the host reads what the view says down to a checked id and a start in whole seconds, and builds the only address the player may have, on YouTube's no-cookie player or Vimeo's. A poster and a play button stand first, and nothing is asked of the provider until a person presses play. The player is a sandboxed frame in the box the poster stood in, told the page's origin alone (YouTube's needs it), with a link out below it throughout. A playing video plays on as the view draws again, since the view's runtime now keeps a video by the one it names and moves what is around it instead, and goes back to its poster when another plays, or when it is hidden or taken off the page. In the Scene's card it is the poster and the link; printed, the poster and the address. A provider not on the list or an id that is none draws nothing and is said to `onProblem` as `embed-refused`, and by a headless run among its problems with that `code`; a page whose policy allows no frame from the provider says so when a video is pressed, keeps the link, and tells its host `embed-not-allowed`. `embeds: { providers: [...] }` on `mountWorkerView`, a registered view's definition (a draft's too) and `runWorkerViewHeadless` plays from those alone, an empty list from none: a provider turned off is drawn as its link. A view's place also says it has drawn only once its videos' player has arrived. A frame guest is unchanged: what its document frames is its sandbox's and its own policy's. Held by a unit test of the guest (`a-view-plays-a-video-from-a-closed-list-of-providers`, with a fuzz of 40,000 hostile addresses and ids) and the new harness `video-embeds`, in three engines against stand-in providers. The player is in the chunk a view that places a video fetches. The open kit, the view's runtime and registering a worker view load a little more; the batch writes the raises.

Compatibility: ops and primitives, stored formats, the wire and derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged. The declaration and check finding codes — additive: a described problem may carry `code`, a described video `provider` and `link`, and `VIEW_PROBLEM_CODES` has `embed-refused` and `embed-not-allowed`. The JavaScript API is additive (`embeds`, `EMBED_PROVIDERS`, `EMBED_ORIGINS`). What a host with a content security policy does: add `https://www.youtube-nocookie.com https://player.vimeo.com` to `frame-src`, or pass `embeds: { providers: [] }` so a video is its link.
