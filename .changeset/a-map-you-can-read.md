The map from altitude reads, and says where things stand (Roadmap f-map-readable, f-map-shows-state). `cityMap` lays the districts out by how the kinds relate: each kind one column past the furthest kind it points at, so the map reads upstream to downstream, left to right — People, Sessions, Questions, Findings, Opportunities, Actions — with the kinds of a column one above the other near what they point at, a cycle broken where the walk meets it, and a kind tied to nothing in a margin under the rest. Turned upright (`upright`), the same map reads top to bottom, which is how a window taller than it is wide draws it. A plot is two cells a side, or three past four records, so the biggest kind no longer takes the map; blocks are four cells apart (`BLOCK`, was five) and the largest side is three (`MAX_SIDE`, was four).

On open the whole city fits the window — what is left of it beside a panel docked by the map — clear of the bar's corner controls and the zoom: the cell is the largest at which every plot and the name under it stand inside the room, and the names keep a fixed size on the screen whatever the zoom. Under each district is its name, its count and how many have a problem, on a solid backing — and, for a kind with a status, a bar of where its records stand ("9 open · 8 doing · 14 done", the words where a segment has room and always on hover). Where two names would collide the less pressing one says less (`LayoutNode.nameplate`: whole, short, over or beside its plot, or nothing until the camera comes closer); the most pressing is always said whole, over the edge of a neighbor's ground if it must, and a chosen record's link labels are placed clear of every name. Counts are said in the app's locale (`numberAsRead`). The lens names left the district: they are in the bar, and come up under the name when the district is hovered or focused. The marks nobody could explain are gone or said: the ◆ after a count and the open chevron's dot are gone from the map, "1 of 17 tied" is not said there, "moved ×" says "Reset view", the altitude control says "Close the map" (its name says where it lands), and the Key opens with what each mark on a block means. "From above", beside the zoom, sees the same ground from straight overhead (`tilt`, `TOP_DOWN`), kept in the browser.

A district's blocks stand in rows by its kind's main choice field — its lifecycle's, else one called status, stage, state or phase — one row per value in declared order (`districtState`, `districtBlocks`): done lies flat and pale, waiting stands gray, active stands darker and taller, and a row cut for room says `+n` where it begins, never under the name. Color carries state, not kind: plots and blocks are the ground's own ink, and the strong colors are a problem or a record past its due date, lit; one somebody else changed since you last left the page, aglow; your own, rimmed. Coming down, the blocks say their records' names. The ground of the city — its plots, blocks, roads and their rules — is fetched the first time the scene rises (`plots.tsx`), with the bar of states and the Key's legend, so a page that never rises carries none of it.

The layout no longer stands a billboard on a plot for a focused kind with a named picture — that picture opens in the panel beside the map — so a focused kind's district keeps all its ground. `@graview/core/city` is a new entry: the map alone (`cityMap`, `districtState`, `toIso`, `sideFor`, `BLOCK`, `MAX_SIDE`, `TILTED`, `TOP_DOWN` and the rest of what lays a city out), which the scene lays out with before it draws; `@graview/core/scene` keeps everything it had and adds `districtState`, `districtBlocks`, `TIER_HEIGHT`, `TILTED` and `TOP_DOWN`. `toIso` takes a tilt. A fitted thumbnail's district fills its whole block.

Compatibility: the map moves. Every kind not hand-laid stands on a new plot, sides cap at three and blocks are four cells apart, so a thumbnail of an app (`sceneThumbnail`, `sceneDistricts`) draws a different picture and two plots declared five cells apart now leave a cell more street between them; a declared `plot` is still used verbatim. `@graview/core/city` is additive. `placeCity` takes the canvas and its insets, no longer a card base. `LayoutOptions.screenHeight` and `SCREEN_LEASH_CELLS` are removed with the billboard.
