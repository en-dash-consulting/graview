A worker view may be drawn edge to edge on a phone. Threat Watch, on Graview Cloud, put a hero at the head of its home and of each record page, and at 390 pixels the page's column stood 20px of gutter either side of it: 40 pixels of 390 the view could not reach, and its `margin: 0 -14px` was clipped at its box's edge, cutting the hero's rounded corners rather than widening it. A manifest now says `bleed: true` (`WorkerViewManifest.bleed`, which `checkManifest` takes as true or left out), and where the pages face draws that view — the home's body, a record's page — on a face 680 pixels wide or less, its box takes the gutter back, less the device's safe areas (`env(safe-area-inset-left)` and `-right`), so it is the phone's width; at a desk it is the column's width, as every view is. Nothing else moves: a view without it is drawn inside the gutter as before, and what any view draws past its box is still clipped there. `registerWorkerView` marks the view (`markBleeds`, `bleeds` and `BLEEDS` beside `markReplacesPage` on `@graview/react` and `/provider`), the pages draw it in `Bleed`, the column's sides are `var(--graview-page-gutter, 20px)` so the gutter and what a view takes back are one number, and the column says `data-graview-page-main`. `graview-view-drawing` says where a view's box is and what `bleed` changes, and `graview-worker-view` names the key. `guest`'s tests find a view that bleeds drawn in a box that takes the gutter back on a phone and any other view as it was, and the key refused as anything but true; `verify-address` mounts the tasks as Cloud does with a home view and a record view that bleed, and at 390 each spans the viewport less its safe areas with nothing scrolling sideways, at 1440 the column's width (aViewThatBleedsSpansAPhoneLessItsSafeAreasAndTheColumnAtADesk). The pages face carries 722 bytes more (191 gzipped), a hosted page 0.2 KB more up front (186 bytes, 49 and 37 gzipped, handed a compiled app or not), the guest host registering a worker view 288 (70) and drawing one on the open kit 122 (22), under raises of those budgets by that (`scripts/budget-raises/a-view-that-bleeds-*.json`).

Compatibility: additive in the manifest — `bleed`, optional; a manifest without it draws as it did, and a build that predates it passes it over and draws the view inside the gutter. Additive in the API: `markBleeds`, `bleeds`, `BLEEDS`, `Bleed`'s attribute `data-graview-bleed` and the column's `data-graview-page-main`. The declaration, the wire, the stored formats and the derived tools are unchanged.
