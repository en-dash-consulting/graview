A figure may be said large. In a home whose headline wears a display face, a stat tile's number was drawn at a figure's modest size with no way to say it was the point of the page. `{"figure": "count(all('finding'))", "label": "findings", "emphasis": "large"}` draws it at the size of the home's own heading, in the display face (smaller on a card or a row). The check refuses any other word at its path (`view-emphasis`) and refuses `emphasis` on `{"figure": true}`, the kind's picture. `FIGURE_EMPHASES` is exported beside `FIGURE_FORMATS`.

Compatibility: the declaration and check finding codes: a figure block takes a new key, `emphasis`, whose one value is `"large"`, and a new code, `view-emphasis`, refuses another. A document that does not say it is unchanged. Ops, stored formats, the wire and derived tool schemas are unchanged.
