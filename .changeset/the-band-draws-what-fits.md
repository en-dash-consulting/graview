Above it each relation (a run of one edge kind in one direction) stands whole if it is small, groups by what its members' own declaration offers — another edge's far end, a choice, a date by decade, year or month, never the relation's own edge, about five groups where it can — or keeps its most relevant members (the selection, the search's hits, the flagged, the recently written, the most connected) in its own order beside one "+N more" door.
