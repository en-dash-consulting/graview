Inside the panel a line crosses no one else's words. On Graview Cloud's Deep Time, a line from the second to the fifth pill in a row ran left across the earlier pills to the panel's edge, striking through Iguanodon, Mosasaurus, Elasmosaurus and Patagotitan, and the list's left third was a fan of strokes. Now each line leaves its own pill; where the straight way to the edge would cross another part, it drops into the gap above or below its row (toward where it is going first), runs along that gap to the edge, and goes on to the city from there. Lines along one gap stand a pixel and a half apart, and lines into one district meet at the edge as one line, as before.

Beside the wider panel, Nearby stands back as far as just short of the City to take in what a record is connected to, so it points under the page less often. A shared line's count is always said: where a board or a name stands at its district's middle, the count stands back along the line, clear of them. A record opened from the city beside the page moves no camera where it and everything it is connected to already stand in the city's side. On the home at Nearby, where the whole city cannot stand beside the page, the city stays as near the middle of its side as the districts the home's views show let it.

Tests: `verify-frame-lines` holds that inside the panel no line crosses another pill or row on a feature's page of eight pills in two rows, and that the shared line's count is shown. `verify-one-face` holds the same on Deep Time's formation page at every size, and that a record opened in the city keeps its place in its district and the camera stays where everything it frames is already in sight.

Compatibility: the look — changed on the one face only (`sceneUnderPages`): lines inside the panel run through the gaps between rows, a shared line's count may stand back along it, and Nearby may stand back further beside a wide panel. The JavaScript API — unchanged. Ops and primitives — unchanged. Stored formats — unchanged. The wire — unchanged. The declaration and check finding codes — unchanged. Derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged.
