Security and reliability fixes for bound people. The graview-sight skill says who binds a person. A hosted page carries 3.2 KB more up front, and one handed a compiled app 2.7 KB more.

Compatibility: a declaration may now be refused that was accepted before, and a call may now be refused that was taken before; what a seat is served of who did something may read differently. Nothing a host calls changes shape, and the JavaScript API is additive.
