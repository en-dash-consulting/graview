Ask AI in an app proposes a change to the app itself (Roadmap f-seat-reshape). The seat's tools read, and the right-click menu's suggestions — learned and tidying — cover records, so "we also need to track who's driving" or "split this into before and after the trip" had no path inside the app: it went to a chat somewhere else. Now the person's words are matched to structure through the same judgments as the new-app starter: `@graview/tools/reshape`, a new entry, asks a Noul per trait the app lacks (`need:<trait>`: "does what they asked for need people who own things, which this app has no part for yet?") and a Noul per part it has (`drop:kind:<name>`, `drop:trait:<name>`: "did they ask to stop keeping areas?") over one state — what they said and the app in words — through whatever `decide` the host passes (Jev, as `dream` takes one; a stub in a test); `readReshape(answers, thresholds)` reads the answers (a trait added at 0.7, a kind or trait dropped at 0.7 — dropping loses records, so the bar is never lower) with every raw Noul kept; `reshapeCard({ said, document, add, drop })` says the card in words — `said` in one sentence ("This adds people who own things and keeps categories and vendors."), `adds` (each trait with what its module brings, from a new `TRAIT_BRINGS` beside `TRAIT_WORDS`: "a person kind (or the people the app has), an owner link, Give it to someone, …"), `keeps`, `drops`, and `breaking` as far as the seat can say ("All categories records go, with their links.") — and `proposeReshape({ said, document, decide })` runs the turn, its proposal null when the reading changes nothing. The conversation draws the card beneath the seat's answer (`ChatPanel`): added, kept and dropped as chips the person turns off and on, each toggle said again by the same function so what they accept is always the reading the card shows, breaking changes above the press; Accept hands the proposal — `{ said, add, drop, card }`, the reading as the person left it, never edits — to the host's `onProposeChange(proposal) → "applied" | "declined"`, on `GraviewProvider`, `FrameOptions` (so `mount`, `<Embed>` and the pages alone take it) and the pages' context, beside `onKeepLens`. The host composes the edits from that reading: `composeReshape({ document, add, drop })` on `@graview/core/compose` runs `startingDocument` on the app's own document and answers its `edits` (which `startingDocument` now carries beside `took`, `left` and `said`: the ones that took, in order, replaying onto the base in one `editDocument` call to the document it composed) with `breaking` said from each removal (`breakingOf`) and `notDone`; the host previews them with its data and applies them as the person, as one undoable change, under its own policy, or declines. Code writes every edit, the model never a kind, a field or a rule — and the composer never rides on a page: a page that fetched `@graview/core/compose` when asked made the editor and the critic live up front, which no page carries, so the vocabulary and a document's reading alone stand on a new entry, `@graview/core/traits` (`TRAITS`, `TRAIT_WORDS`, `TRAIT_BRINGS`, `traitsOfDocument`, `hasTrait`, `traitWords`), `traitsOf` of a TypeScript app stays on `/compose` with the composer, and the document barrel says `documentOf` and `rememberDocument` from where they live rather than through the writer. A host's model with tools is offered `propose_change` on its turn — the Roadmap's `propose-change`, spelled as the surface spells its tools — told the card and never an edit, and the card it proposed travels on the reply. The tool exists only for a seat whose role may change the declaration (`mayChangeDeclaration` on `@graview/tools`, the judgment the studio's door now shares: an owner or editor, never a viewer) with a host that handed the hook and a model that decides, over an app with a document to change — the one it was compiled from (`documentOfApp`); an app declared in code remembers itself first (`rememberDocument(app, toDocument(app).document)`, as Things does for its harness) or is offered none. For any other seat there is no door and no card, nothing refused. The panel reads the words for a change to the app conservatively ("we also need", "keep track of who", "add a field", "no longer need … any more"), and a reading that adds and drops nothing goes on to the usual answer. `graview serve`'s own seat passes no hook: the served page has no path that writes its document. A hosted page carries 0.1 KB more up front (145 bytes minified, 70 gzipped: the provider and the frame carry the hook, and the barrel exports `documentOf`); handed a compiled app, 0.2 KB (197 and 197); the pages face alone 334 / 218 more, the embed with the studio handed in 702 / 950, every face 23_150 / 9_033 across the doors a face can fetch — the reading, the card and the seat's tool riding the seat's model, never up front — and the guest host registering a worker view 0 / 1, its own bytes unchanged and the chunk names its imports carry gzipping a byte larger beside the new doors, each under a raise in `scripts/budget-raises/the-seat-proposes-a-change-to-the-app-*.json` naming this changeset. `the-seat-proposes-a-change-to-the-app-from-words.test.ts`, `the-seat-offers-a-change-to-the-app-only-with-a-host-to-apply-it.test.tsx` and the starting-document test hold it, and the seat harness drives the keeper's "we also need who's driving" on Things to the card and through Accept, with a member drawing none. The agent-seat and studio skills say it.

Compatibility: ops and primitives — unchanged. Stored formats — unchanged. The wire — unchanged. The declaration and check finding codes — unchanged; `startingDocument` answers one more key, `edits`, and `composeReshape`, `breakingOf` and `TRAIT_BRINGS` are additions on `/compose`, `@graview/core/traits` a new entry carrying what `/compose` already named. Derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged on `graview mcp` and `toolDefinitions`: `propose_change` is listed only on a seat's turn whose context carries `reshape`, which only a host with `onProposeChange` hands it. What Graview Cloud does: pass `onProposeChange` to `mount` for an owner's or editor's seat; on its server, `composeReshape({ document, add, drop })` from the proposal, preview the edits through its own propose_change and apply them through apply_change as the person; decline for a plan without structure changes.
