An archive is served as the seat may see it (FR-198). Roadmap f-archive-seat-view, from Graview Cloud: to answer `HistoryHost.archived(from, to)` for a seat, Cloud judged each slice with `logSeenBy` over a store rebuilt from the whole history, because a compacted store's own seat view does not know a record made and removed behind its horizon and would serve that record's id, minted from its label, to a seat that may not see it. It had no way to hand such a seat an epoch judged for it, so in an app with sights every seat, the owner's included, folded the past from seq 0, and on a plan with limited retention the chunks older than the cutoff were refused for them. Now `archivedSeenBy(store, archive, principal, { from, to })` on `@graview/core/seat` answers with both. `store` is the app as it stands, compacted or not; `archive` is what `store.compact` returned, every compaction's ops and epochs together, reaching back to seq 0. It answers `{ ops, epochs }` for seqs `from` up to but not including `to`. The ops are every op of the range in its place, redacted exactly as `logSeenBy` redacts the log of the store never compacted. The epochs are those in the range, each graph holding only the records the seat is served, as its sight shows them: its kinds, `own`, `via` and the fields a sight hides, a field that names a record it may not see cleared or the record withheld, the links between those records, and who made each one, named only for them. The answer is a valid `HistoryHost.archived` answer, and each epoch is the graph the seat's ops fold to at its seq, so playback can fold from the nearest epoch and draws only what the seat may see. An archive with a gap behind the horizon is refused, since what the gap held could not be judged. `core`'s test ports the 1,000-world property to compacted histories: records made and removed behind the horizon, links to hidden records, required fields naming them. No unseen id appears in any range's ops or epochs, the ops equal what the never-compacted store serves, and each epoch equals the fold of those ops at its seq. A second test lets a seat that may not see one kind scrub a compacted app to seq 1 from the seed's epoch it is served, and `capabilities().shipped` gains FR-198. Nothing is added to the main entry or to what a hosted page loads up front.

Compatibility: ops, stored formats, the wire, the declaration and derived tools are unchanged; `capabilities().shipped` gains FR-198. Additive on `@graview/core/seat` (`archivedSeenBy`, and its `ArchivedSeen` answer type).
