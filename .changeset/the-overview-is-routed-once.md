Returning to the overview no longer freezes the scene while its ribbons are routed (Roadmap request:gv-scene-ribbon-routing-blocks). An app of twenty-five kinds held the main thread for one and a half to two seconds each time it came back to the whole map, and out of a district, because every ribbon was routed again on every frame of the way in (the cell changes as the camera moves), again once the name cards were measured, and even while the map was being left. Now a picture is routed once: when the city has landed and its name cards are measured, and never again for the same ribbons, plots, cards, cell and tilt (the last sixteen pictures are kept). On the way in, the streets last routed for the same ground are drawn as the camera moves over them, or none are. A picture not seen before is routed between frames, a ribbon at a time in slices of 8 ms, and its ribbons are drawn when it lands. The router itself is about seven times faster with the same routes: the ground (every grid point and every step between two, with and without the name cards, and each point's distance from the nearest district) is worked out once per routing instead of at every step of every search, the stretches already run are kept in a typed array instead of string keys, the search buffers are reused, and the way under the cards is found first, so the way round them is searched only as far as it could still be chosen. A page drawn to a string still routes at once. On apps/discography's new districts.html (twenty-five kinds, 800 records, 75 relations), `verify-scale` measured tasks of 4,698 ms leaving the overview, 207 ms back to it and 1,699 ms out of a district before, and none over 50 ms after; its new claim `returningToTheOverviewNeverHoldsTheMainThreadOver100ms` holds the way back to 100 ms with every ribbon drawn. The ribbons chunk, fetched when the city is first drawn, is 0.9 KB larger gzipped. React's tests hold that seventy-five ribbons route within a budget scaled by a reference measurement, that a picture already routed is answered without routing after another between, that anything a route follows from routes again, that a picture routed between frames lands as the routes it would have had at once and can be stopped, and that the camera's way in draws only routes already made.

Compatibility: the five surfaces — unchanged. The JavaScript API — unchanged; the ribbons are drawn a few frames after a city not seen before lands, where they were drawn with it.
