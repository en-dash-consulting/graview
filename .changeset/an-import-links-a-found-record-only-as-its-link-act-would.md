A sheet's row that finds a record (`importRows` with `match`) links it only as pressing the link act on that record would. The import asked the act that makes the link with the far end alone and never named the record the row found. So where only an everybody grant rides the act that makes a job, and its derived `connect-job-owner` reaches only the jobs a seat made ("only on what you made"), a sheet could link somebody else's job, and for a relation of one take the owner it had, while pressing `connect-job-owner` on that job was refused. Now the record a row finds is the act's subject when its link is asked, so the made-by rule, a `self` grant and a grant's kinds answer as they do for a press, and the row is refused in the act's own words, with nothing written. A record the row makes is its importer's own, and is asked as before. Core's tests hold it (`an-import-links-a-found-record-only-as-its-link-act-would.test.ts`).

Compatibility: ops and primitives, stored formats, the wire, the declaration and check finding codes, and derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged.
