Declared acts can do what the org app's mutations do (FR-115). org-graview's `assign-ownership`, `propose-owner` and `share-ownership` were TypeScript because a document's act could not say them; each is now one declared act, proved by a parity test that runs the TypeScript mutation and the act on the same seed and compares the graphs and the changes they made. Three things arrived in the act language. `connects` and `severs` link from whichever end the declaration puts the subject: `owns` is declared on person, so an act `on: "component"` that `connects: "owns"` makes the link from the person it is given (`to`) to the component, as a TypeScript mutation's `connects` reads. `"replaces": true` on an act that connects severs the subject's existing links of that relation first, or `"replaces": ["owns", "proposedOwner"]` those of each relation named, at the subject's own end, keeping the link being made: `assign-ownership` replaces whoever owned the component and whoever was proposed for it. `"setsOther": { "ownership": "shared" }` sets fields on the record at the other end of what the act connects or severs, with values as `sets` takes them and expressions read standing on that record. A value in `sets` may choose with the rule language's `if` — `{ "expr": "if(to == 'person-nick', 'nick_owns', 'owned_elsewhere')" }` — which judges only the branch it takes, under the rule's budget. Each is previewed with `previewAll`, logged and taken back by one undo like any act; a replaced relation is in the act's `severs` and makes it destructive, and what it sets on the other record is not in its `writes`. A seat may not set a record it cannot see: the other record is the act's `to`, and one hidden from the seat is refused as missing, changing nothing. `graview check`'s `act-reads-hidden-kind` follows the new reads — a value set on the other record, worked out from what it reaches, and the links an act replaces. The document refuses `act-sets-other` (a `setsOther` with nothing connected), `act-replaces` (a `replaces` with nothing connected, or naming a relation that does not join the subject to what it connects), and a field the other end has not got (`act-field`). `editDocument` keeps them in step: a renamed relation is renamed in `replaces`, a removed one leaves it (and an act that only replaced it keeps its other relations), and a renamed or removed field is renamed or removed in `setsOther`.

Compatibility: unchanged for stored data, ops and the wire. Additive within `graview-document@1` (docs/stability.md): an act without `setsOther` or `replaces` does what it did. A `connects` or `severs` shorthand on a subject that is only the far end of its relation now links from the record it is given, where it used to try the link the other way round, which the graph refused. `effectsOf` returns `ActEffect`s, which may include a `ReplaceEffect`. `capabilities().shipped` gains `FR-115`.
