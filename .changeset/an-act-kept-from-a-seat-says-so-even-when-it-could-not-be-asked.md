An act kept from a seat says so even when it could not be asked (the ninth walk, W-192). The faces derive a seat's acts over what it sees, and an act whose picker had no candidate was dropped before the policy was asked — so somebody browsing a beekeeping co-op, who sees no beekeepers, was shown no "Add a yard" and no reason, where a member was told "a coordinator can", and the district said the act "cannot begin until there is a beekeeper". An act the policy refuses the seat is now withheld with the policy's sentence whatever its pickers would hold; only an act the seat may take waits for a candidate. `DeriveContext` carries the `principal` the acts are judged for.

Compatibility: derived tools are unchanged. `deriveAffordances` may now list in `withheld` an act it used to leave out, for a seat the policy refuses it, and leaves out the "cannot begin until there is …" observation for that act; `DeriveContext` gains the optional `principal`.
