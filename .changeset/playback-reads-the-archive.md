Playback reads the changes a compaction archived (FR-196). Roadmap f-playback-reads-archive, from Graview Cloud: its rooms compact behind the undo horizon, the ops before it archived to R2, and an open carries the checkpoint and a short tail; after a compaction `pastOf` refuses any seq behind the horizon and `HistoryHost` had no way to hand the archive back, so the timeline stopped where the kept tail starts. Now `HistoryHost.archived(from, to)` answers the ops of a range behind the horizon, oldest first and each with its `seq`, and the epochs among them (`ArchivedOps`: `{ ops, epochs? }`, which a `LogArchive` from `store.compact` already is; or the ops alone). It is asked only when the timeline is moved to a moment behind the horizon, in chunks aligned to 256 seqs, newest first until an epoch — a checkpoint an earlier compaction made, a seed's — or seq 0 is reached, and each chunk once while it is the same function; scrubbing through what came back asks nothing more. `pastAt` folds the moment read-only from the nearest epoch (or an empty seq 0, or a point it kept on the way), under the declaration the host says it was made under, and `askArchived` on `@graview/core/past` says whether it can be drawn yet. With `archived` the timeline's scrubber reaches back to the start, a step per seq behind the horizon, captioned with the change that ended each; without it the scrubber begins at the horizon and says "The changes before the timeline's start were archived." While a moment is read the face holds the picture it had, and the playback's `archive` says which moment it is reading; a refusal, a failure or a gap in the answer is said on the timeline ("Could not read the older changes from the archive: …") and the present is untouched. Undo still stops at the horizon: no "Restore to here" is offered behind it. `core`'s test compacts a store twice, hands the host the archives and scrubs to seq 1, every moment behind the horizon drawn as the uncompacted store's; the playback harness does the same in a browser, and `capabilities().shipped` gains FR-196. A hosted page carries 0.1 KB more up front (64 bytes, 40 gzipped; the compiled page the same), the moment held in the provider's playback, under a raise of each budget by that (`scripts/budget-raises/playback-reads-the-archive-*.json`).

Compatibility: ops, stored formats, the wire, the declaration and derived tools are unchanged; `capabilities().shipped` gains FR-196. Additive on the mount options' and the provider's `history` (`HistoryHost.archived`, and the `ArchivedOps` type on `@graview/core/history`), on `@graview/core/past` (`askArchived`, and `pastAt` reading behind the horizon when the host keeps the archive), and on `Playback` (`archive`, `null` unless a moment behind the horizon is being read or was refused), which a host that builds a `Playback` of its own now sets.
