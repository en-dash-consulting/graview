A suggestion in the inbox reads as one line with its buttons, and a placement is no longer suggested on one shared word. A book app's card said one placement three times: "Graview, tidying suggests Place in chapter: What I steer by (my values, as one example)", then "Jev: 0.95 that the in chapter of … is “09 · Without a Map” (they share the word “what”) · 95% sure · jev-latest", then two chips naming both records, then Accept, Decline and Not now. It now says "Put “What I steer by (my values, as one example)” in 09 · Without a Map?" with both records as links in the sentence, a long name cut where it stands and whole in its title, then Yes, No and Later. Why it was suggested waits behind "Why?", in the maker's words alone, never a probability or a model's name; a basis written before 0.1.66 is read for the reason in its brackets. Several suggestions of the same act stand under one line, "3 notes to put in chapters", a row each. Yes, No and Later are Accept, Decline and Not now as they were, from the keyboard too, with the same test ids. A run of several changes says its headline as before.

The sentence is read off the call and the declaration: a relation that starts with "in", "under" or "on" puts the record there, one that is a verb ("waits for") asks "Should … wait for …?", a one-link noun ("venue") is "Make … the venue of …?", a merge, a setting aside and a choice filled in each a sentence of their own, and anything else its act's own words. It is drawn in the inbox's lazy chunk, so a page carries none of it before the inbox opens; every face spends 4,035 bytes more minified, 1,788 gzipped, and nothing up front.

The regression: f-tidying-link-evidence-not-one-word held the far record's name to its telling words, but an empty one-link relation, such as a note's chapter, still put a far record forward on any one telling word the two records' texts shared, "opening", and a model judged it at 0.95. Blocking now asks for two shared telling words there, and a run withdraws its own waiting link suggestion whose basis rests on one shared word, as it withdraws one below its bar; nobody is recorded as declining it.

The unit tests `a-suggestion-is-said-as-one-question-with-its-records-in-it`, `a-suggestion-reads-as-one-line-with-its-buttons` and `a-placement-is-not-suggested-on-one-shared-word` hold it; verify-chrome's `whatTidyingDidAndSuggestsIsSaidOnTheTodoExample` now reads the one sentence, Why? hidden until pressed, and Why?, Yes, No, Later.

Compatibility: the look — a suggestion is one sentence with its records as links (`suggestion-record` now in the sentence, not chips below it), its buttons say Yes, No and Later, its basis is behind "Why?" (new `suggestion-why`; `[data-graview-lineage]` is hidden until pressed and holds the reason alone), several alike stand under one line (new `suggestion-group`), and "Not made yet: each waits for you." is gone. The JavaScript API — unchanged, but for `TargetBlock.evidence`, which for an empty one-link relation says "they share the words “…” and “…”". Ops and primitives — unchanged; a run may withdraw its own waiting one-word link suggestions. Stored formats — unchanged. The wire — unchanged. The declaration and check finding codes — unchanged. Derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged.

What Graview Cloud does: nothing to change; the next night's run withdraws the book app's waiting one-word placements.
