A lens double-clicked from Up opens it. Nick: "fix the double-clicking of a lens when in elevated Scene view so that it zooms in on that lens, going from up to down." From Up, each district says its pictures under it ("The board", "The year", "The season"), and pressing one shows it on the district's billboard and flies the camera to it. The flight moved the name out from under the pointer, so the second click of a double-click landed on the district's own card, whose double-click jacked into the district: zoomed, still Up, the district selected, and the picture never opened. The pointer did not move; the city did. So the first press of a picture now takes the next press made where it was made — within 12 px, by the same kind of pointer, for as long as a double-click lasts (500 ms) — as the second half of its own gesture, keeps it and the dblclick that follows from whatever is under it now, and goes down into that picture: the kind's district in focus, on the ground, the picture `in.view`, the same stop the bar's place list, Find and a host's `go.place` make, which now all say it through one function, `withPicture` (`@graview/layout` and `/view`). A double-tap is the same two presses. A press anywhere else in that half second — another district, the bar, another picture — goes through untouched. From the keyboard, Enter on a picture shows it, and Enter again on the picture already showing goes down into it, as does a second press of it however late; a single press keeps its meaning. The descent is the scene's own, so reduced motion cuts it as it cuts every other. The picture's title says so: "Show The board on the billboard — double-click to open it", and "The board is showing — press again to open it". `pnpm verify address` holds, in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox at a desk, that from Up a double-click on a district's picture puts the scene Down, focused on that kind with that picture `in.view`, at the scene's address with the stop saying so, and that Back returns Up; on main the scene stayed Up, zoomed. The pages face alone carries 1,039 bytes more for it (518,775), every face 985 (1,562,787) and the scene before it draws 983 (898,680); their budgets rise by that.

Compatibility: behavior a host's page reaches, and a new export. New: `withPicture(state, kind, as)` in `@graview/layout` and `@graview/layout/view`. Changed: on the scene from Up, the second press on a district's picture within 500 ms and 12 px of the first, by the same kind of pointer — whatever is now under it — and a press of the picture already showing go down into it, where the second press of a double-click landed on the district under it, which zoomed in still Up, and a press of the picture already showing did nothing; the picture's press says so in its `title`.
