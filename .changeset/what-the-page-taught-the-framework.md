What the page taught the framework, folded back in. A board slot holds several occupants, each name its own target, and the bench lists only what is in no slot. The coverage lens reads its rows and columns from the graph, as the board already did, so no app wraps it to hand over the other kind. A titled registration names the picture. `focus=agg:plot` is a stop's short form for `aggregate:plot`. An embed infers its face from its stop and its scheme from the host page, and `mountWhenNear` mounts many embeds as a reader comes near them. The scaffold's views file says how a lens becomes a place.
