A home's Recently leaves out the changes the reader cannot see, Roadmap f-recently-hides-unseen. A log is served to a seat with the ops it may not see withheld in place, as FR-16 has it, each saying only "A change you cannot see — Someone"; the derived home listed the last five ops as they came, so a stranger on a Secret Santa's public page — an app that keeps every match to its giver — read five such lines: a list that looked broken, and said activity existed that was never theirs to know of. Recently now lists the last five changes the reader may see, each as before, and folds what was withheld across the span those rows cover (the whole log, when fewer than five are visible) into one quiet line at its foot, "and 2 changes kept from you" — plain words, no author, no time, never a row per withheld op; a reader from whom nothing was kept sees no such line, and a reader who sees nothing recent is told once how many changes were kept. The Activity panel already folded them and is unchanged. `a-homes-recently-leaves-out-what-the-reader-cannot-see.test.tsx` holds it on the gift-exchange document, as a participant, a stranger and the organizer. The pages face alone carries 0.3 KB more (337 bytes minified, 140 gzipped) for the walk back over the served log and the one line, under the raise in `scripts/budget-raises/a-homes-recently-leaves-out-what-the-reader-cannot-see-pages-face.json`; a hosted page's weight up front is unchanged.

Compatibility: ops and primitives, stored formats, the wire, the declaration and check finding codes, and derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged. What Graview Cloud does: nothing; the public demo's Secret Santa reads right once it takes the version.
