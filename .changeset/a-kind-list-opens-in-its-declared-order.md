A kind's list opens in the order and groups its declaration says (Nick, prioritizing the Graview Roadmap in a Graview app). Only a blocks lens could sort and group; a kind's own list, `/requests`, could not be told to, so done and declined requests read among the new ones. `pages.lists.<kind>` now takes, beside its `filters`, a `sort` — a field, a computed field (a rank the kind works out) or `label`, lowest first unless `"desc"`, with a `then` for ties — and a `group`: a choice or yes-or-no field, an `order` its groups come in, and the `closed` ones, drawn last and shut. A closed group is a disclosure: its heading is a button saying "done · 12 requests", expanded or not, the rows it controls drawn once it opens; it stands open when the list is narrowed by words or by the very field it groups, and `?open=done` keeps it open, so an opened group is a link too. A person still sorts or groups the list another way from its arranging line, and the address says so; a declared order keeps its tiebreak whenever the list is in it. `graview check` refuses a list sorted by what the kind does not have, grouped by a field that is not a choice or a yes or no, or ordering or closing a value that is not one of its choices, each in a sentence at its path (`pages-list-arrangement`, an error). `arrange-pages` sets them and says what it did (the list of requests opens grouped by "status", "done" and "declined" closed at the end), as `diffDocuments` does; a field renamed is followed, and a field removed, a computed field no longer worked out or a choice dropped lets go of what named it, said. `describeApp` says how each declared list opens. The graview-lists skill teaches it.

Compatibility: ops, stored records and wire messages are unchanged. The document format takes `pages.lists.<kind>.sort` (`"<key>"` or `{ "by", "direction"?: "asc" | "desc", "then"?: "<key>" | { "by", "direction"? } }`) and `pages.lists.<kind>.group` (`"<field>"` or `{ "by", "order"?: [...], "closed"?: [...] }`), optional within `graview-document@1`; a build that predates them refuses a document that says them. `arrange-pages` takes `sort: { "<kind>": … | null }` and `group: { "<kind>": … | null }`, and setting a kind's `filters` no longer drops what else its list says. New check finding code `pages-list-arrangement` (an error). `DocumentDiff`'s sentences may say "The feature list opens sorted by "rank", highest first, then "name", A to Z." `PagesArrangement.lists` is `Record<string, KindList>` (`KindList`, `KindListSort`, `KindListGroup` from `@graview/core`); `Sort` may carry `then`, and `ArrangeContext` takes `read` for a computed field's value. `@graview/core/arrange` exports `listOpening`, `listSort`, `listGroup`, `orderListGroups`, `kindListFindings`, `listFieldsOf` and `withWorkedOut`. A kind's list page reads `?open=<value>,…`; a closed group's section is `[data-testid="list-group"][data-closed="shut" | "open"]` and its button `[data-testid="list-group-toggle"]`.
