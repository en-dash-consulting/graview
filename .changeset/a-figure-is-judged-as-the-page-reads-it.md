A kind's figure is judged as the page reads it, and judged again where it is drawn. `figureFaults` found attributes by looking for a space or a quote before each name, but the HTML parser also takes a `/` as the gap between two attributes, so `<path d="…"/onclick="…"/>` passed `graview check`, the checked `compileDocument` and a model's `drawFigure` as a plain path, and `KindFigure` drew it with a live click handler (a smuggled `style` could stretch it over the page). Now `figureFaults` first reads the art with `svgProblem`, the reader a logo is judged by, which walks tags as the parser does and refuses a handler, a style sheet or anything that reaches outside the picture ("it cannot be drawn as it is: it has an event handler (an on… attribute)"), and its own scan of attribute names counts a `/` as a gap. `figureSvg` hands back inline art only when `svgProblem` finds nothing in it that could run or reach outside it, so `KindFigure` and the studio's proposed drawing draw nothing that could run even from a figure nothing checked — a brand's own drawing, a document compiled without the check. A figure that only breaks the house style (a literal color) still draws. Every shipped figure passes. `a-figure-is-judged-as-the-page-reads-it.test.ts` holds three slashed payloads faulted, undrawn and refused by `compileDocument`; `a-figure-that-could-act-is-not-drawn.test.tsx` holds that `KindFigure` draws no handler from a kind's figure or a brand's.
