Carrying a search's words into a district is never the way back out. `withJackIn(…, { carry })` on the district you are already zoomed into narrows it where you stand; before, the jack-in's toggle ran first, so pressing a lit district's count or a kind hit in the Find box from inside that district zoomed you out and dropped the words. A bare jack-in on the zoomed district is still the toggle out.
