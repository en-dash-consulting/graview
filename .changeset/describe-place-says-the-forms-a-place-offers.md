describe_place says the forms a place offers. An agent that declared "Add a threat" could not see what the form asks: which fields, in what order, which must be answered, a box or a choice and of what. A kind's list (and a lens's place over one kind, unless it says `create: false`) now carries `forms`: each act this seat may run that makes the kind's records, its title, its button and its fields in the order asked — each field's label in the form's own words, whether it must be answered, what it takes ("text", "long text", "date", "date and time", "number", "amount", "choice", "yes or no", "record", "picture", "list of …", "group"), a choice's choices as the form offers them, a number's range and a picker's kinds — less what the seat's sight keeps from it. A visitor's form has a place of its own, `forms/<name>` (or `/forms/<name>`): its title, its intro and what it asks the visitor (`drawnBy: "form"`), and a place not found lists the forms among the places. The text says each form under the place, a star on a field that must be answered. It is read from the walk every face's form is drawn from (`formFields`, `argumentWords`, `keptArguments`), so what is said is what is asked. `graview describe --place forms/<name>` says the same, the `describe_place` tool's description says forms and `forms/<name>`, and `graview-visitor-forms` says how to read one. Pressing a view's `data-act` headless was already there (`runWorkerViewHeadless(…, { press })`, `graview-view-forms`) and is unchanged. Core's tests find a list's creating form said field by field in order with labels, stars, inputs, choices and a range, no form offered to a seat that may not run it, a visitor form said at its own address, and the forms named among the places.

Compatibility: additive — `PlaceDescription.forms`, `DescribedForm` and `DescribedFormField` on `@graview/core/describe`, `drawnBy: "form"`, and `forms/<name>` as a place. Places described before are said as they were, with their forms after them. Stored formats, the wire, the declaration and check findings are unchanged. What Graview Cloud does: nothing — its describe_place carries the forms once it takes this version.
