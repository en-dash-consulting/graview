A workflow labels each record it is handed, Roadmap f-feedback-triage-agent (the framework's half). Feedback that arrives from anybody, signed in or not, wants one judgment each — spam, NSFW, rude, sensitive, needs another look, good to show — said with how sure and why, spam set aside without anyone deleting it, and nothing shown to the public without a person's yes. That is now a step of its own kind: `{ "classify": "feedback", "field": "category", "options"?: [...], "instruction"?: "...", "floor"?: { "spam": { "links": 3, "words": ["casino"] }, "rude": { "expr": "..." } }, "setAside"?: { "spam": 0.9 }, "confirm"?: ["good-to-show"], "relabel"?: true, "budget"?: { "calls", "tokens" }, "approve"? }`, started by `{ "made": "feedback" }` like any workflow. Each record the step's `where` picks — those a change started the run for, when one did — is one request to the host's System One model (its Fast slot, or its `decide`) through dreaming's asking, within the step's budget and answered from its cache where nothing changed: one Choice, which option fits, or none. The label is set through the act that writes the field, and the op's lineage says why — "Jev: 95% sure “Buy cheap watches” is spam", its confidence and its model — so provenance reads "based on … · 95% sure · jev-latest". Where no model answers — none lent, it failed, the budget ran out — the floor decides, in the order the step says its options: at least `links` links in the record's text, one of `words` as a whole word in any case, or a rule that holds; its lineage says which ("Sure: spam, from 3 links"). A record neither labels is left for a person and the run says how many. An option sure enough for its `setAside` threshold archives the record too (`archive-<kind>`, never `remove-`). The step's changes wait for a person, gathered into the run's one suggestion, unless the step says its own `approve`; an option in `confirm`, and a choice the model barely made over the next, wait whatever it says — so a host holds "nothing public without a human yes" by naming its public label in `confirm`. A record already labeled, by a person or a run before, is passed by unless the step says `relabel`. A step's change may now say `ask: true` to wait for a person whatever `approve` says (`StepChange.ask`, `@graview/tools/workflows`). `graview check` says, in the person's voice: a field that is no choice of options (`workflow-classify-field-not-choice`), an option the field lacks in `options`, `floor`, `setAside` or `confirm` (`workflow-classify-option-unknown`), a floor rule that tests nothing (`workflow-classify-floor-empty`) and a threshold outside 0 to 1 (`workflow-classify-threshold-invalid`). The step reads "Label each feedback by its category as spam, rude or good-to-show; archive spam at 90% sure (asks before good-to-show)", and the Scene wires it to the kind it labels. `classifyStep`, `classifyWords`, `classifyAsks`, `floorLabel` and `linksIn` are on `@graview/core/workflows`, `runClassifyStep` on `@graview/tools/workflows`.

Compatibility: ops and primitives — unchanged; a label is an ordinary edit and a set-aside an ordinary archive, by the workflow's automated author, with `basis`, `confidence`, `model` and `run` on the lineage it already had. Stored formats — unchanged; the document's `workflows` may hold a step with `classify`. The wire — unchanged. The declaration and check finding codes — one new step kind in `workflows.<name>.steps`, refused by a build that predates it as a step of no kind it runs; four new error codes, all `workflow-classify-*`, said only of such a step; no declaration that passed is refused. Derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged. What Graview Cloud does: runs the step on every submission (a `made` trigger) with its metered Fast model lent as the host's AI, shows the run's one suggestion in its confirm queue so a person confirms or changes each label in one step, names its public label in `confirm`, and shows only records a person confirmed as good to show on the public face.
