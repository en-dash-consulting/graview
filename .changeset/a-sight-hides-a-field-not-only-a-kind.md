A sight hides a field, not only a kind (Roadmap f-field-sight). A role saw a whole record or none of it, so a roster where parents see every player's name and not their phone number could not be said. A sight may now say `hide: ["phone", "medical"]`, or `fields: ["name"]` as an allow list, and sights add up: a field is shown when any sight that shows the seat the record shows it, so `{ roles: ["parent"], kinds: ["player"], own: true }` beside the hiding one gives each parent their own child whole. A field kept from a seat is judged in the seat view, so it never leaves the store for that seat: not on the record it is served (a required one withholds the record, as a hidden required reference does), not in the log — an op that adds or removes such a record, writes such a field or whose sentence read one is withheld, whatever the field held, its primitives served without it — nor in an act's answer, a conflict, a search hit, a name a call resolves, a form, an edit offered in place, a static page or a tool's answer. A computed field and a rule are worked out over what the seat is served, so a phone it may not see reads as none. The seat may not write such a field: an act that would is refused as one given an argument it does not take (`invalid`), the same whatever it held and whatever was written, and an import refuses a column that goes to one as it refuses a column that goes to no field. `graview check` names a field a sight misspells (`sight-names-an-unknown-field`), one a record cannot do without (`sight-hides-a-required-field`) and a label or description that reads a kept field (`sight-hides-a-labeled-field`, a warning). `add-sight` and `remove-sight` take the keys, a field the sight's kinds lack is refused (`sight-field`), and the studio carries, adds, changes and writes them back. Judging a field is `@graview/core/field-sight`, which `@graview/ship`'s `openStore` (on a server and in a page), and its server routes load; a host or a page that makes a `Store` of its own imports it once, and a page `openRemote` opened needs none, its host having judged what it sent. Without it, a sight with `hide` or `fields` shows nothing: the store keeps the record rather than serve a field it cannot judge. The new graview-sight skill teaches it; graview-permissions points there.

Compatibility: the declaration gains two optional keys on a sight, `policy.sees[].hide` and `policy.sees[].fields` (field names), and the document schema with them; a declaration without them compiles and serves as before. Four finding codes are new (`sight-names-an-unknown-field`, `sight-hides-a-required-field`, `sight-hides-a-labeled-field`, a document edit's `sight-field`). Ops and the wire are unchanged in shape: a seat with a kept field is served fewer fields and more withheld ops, and an op's `described` may name `<id>#<field>` — a field its sentence read — beside the record ids it named; a build that predates it reads those as ids that name no record. `WIRE_PROTOCOL` does not move. The risk is an older build: a host or a page on a build before this one ignores `hide` and `fields` and SHOWS the field to the role it was hidden from — so a host serves a declaration that says either only on a build that has `@graview/core/field-sight` (`FIELD_SIGHT` on `@graview/core`), and refuses such a declaration otherwise; `capabilities().shipped` names no FR id for it. Stored data is unchanged. docs/stability.md says it under the seat's sight.
