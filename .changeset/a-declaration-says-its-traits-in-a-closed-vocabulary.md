A declaration says what it is made of, in a closed vocabulary (Roadmap f-document-traits, the starter's framework half). Cloud's starter answers a sentence with the templates that fit and, on "kinda", the parts to keep and the parts to add; to do that it needs each template deconstructed into structure rather than prose, and the same reading of what a person asked for. `traitsOf(document | app)` on `@graview/core/compose` reads a declaration's traits off the declaration — never off hand-written tags — in the closed vocabulary `TRAITS`: `owners` (people who own things), `status` (a status that moves), `schedule` (dates it is due by, or a schedule), `budget` (money against a budget), `places`, `sources` (sources behind claims), `stock` (counts in stock), `votes` (votes or picks), `private` (private to its owner), `history` (a history, a record book), `publishing` (content to publish), `roster` (a roster of people), `routines` (repeating routines) and `comparison` (a comparison to pick one from); `TRAIT_WORDS` says each in a person's words, `hasTrait` asks for one. Each trait found names the kinds, fields (`<kind>.<field>`), relations (`<kind>.<relation>`), rules, lenses (by title) and acts that carry it, and says it in one sentence a person reads: "Vendors' quote against each category's budget.", "Each chore has one of the people on it (assigned).", "Vendors compared within each category, one booked." The reading is conservative, as `inferJobs` is: a status needs the named step acts that move it (a live album's `type` is not one), ownership a relation of one to a kind that is people (declared `people: true`, or named so), a budget a field called one beside a money field, a schedule a date a record is due by or kept on a calendar and never the day it was made, a comparison the rule that holds one chosen per group. Over Cloud's catalog of 22 templates, copied as a fixture, every template says its traits and its headline rule stands under the trait it serves (`within-budget` under budget, `one-booked-per-category` under comparison, `chore-not-overdue` under routines, `stock-above-reorder-point` under stock). The vocabulary is on the stability surface (`docs/stability.md`, `scripts/lib/surfaces.mjs`): a name in `TRAITS` is never renamed or taken away within the major. On no page: the compose entry is a host's.

Compatibility: ops and primitives — unchanged. Stored formats — unchanged. The wire — unchanged. The declaration and check finding codes — unchanged; `TRAITS` arrives on this surface as a vocabulary a host may keep answers by, and which traits a declaration is read to carry is a judgment a version may sharpen, not a break. Derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged.
