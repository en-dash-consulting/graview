The one face is what ships. 0.2.0 drew Pages and the Scene as one face — the page over the app's own city, one control at the bottom left moving it from Page to Nearby to Map — only where a host turned it on (`sceneUnderPages`), and the docs, the skills, `graview create` and the example went on teaching two faces: the Scene at `/` and the pages at `/pages`. Now a routed face that owns its page is the one face unless its host says otherwise: a `PagesApp` handed `views` draws the page over the city with no option given, and `sceneUnderPages: false` keeps the routed face alone, with its Scene/Pages switch and its "In the scene" link. `onTheOneFace(context)` on `@graview/pages` says which a context draws. An embedded face (`embedded`, which `@graview/embed` sets) is still the routed face in its box unless its host says `sceneUnderPages: true`, as before: the one face stands against the window, and drawing it inside a host's box is not built yet. A page drawn to a string where a document is shimmed draws the page as on a server, rather than suspending.

`graview create` writes one `PagesApp` at `/` on the one face: no `/pages` address, no `src/ui/app.tsx` (the Scene's own face, its Studio button and its starter seat), and the record page written in your words has no "See it in the scene". An empty app's way in is the home's (`Begin`), as it was on the pages. Things (`apps/todo`), the example the docs teach from, serves the one face at `/`. The skills, the package READMEs, the walkthrough and the docs teach the one face, and a test holds that none of them teaches two faces, a pages face or a Scene face where the one face is meant (`no-page-teaches-two-faces`). The routed face's unit tests that held the page before the city's part arrived wait for the one face and read the page alone; the claims about the record's way to the Scene hold on the routed face alone, where it is drawn. Held by `the-one-face-is-what-a-routed-face-draws-unless-told-otherwise` and the scaffold's `the face a project opens on`, and by `pnpm smoke:create` on the one face.

Compatibility: what breaks for a host — a `PagesApp` it mounts with `views` and without `embedded` now draws the one face: the bar has no Scene/Pages switch, no "In the scene" link and no Up, and is portaled to a fixed slot at the window's top; a host that relied on the routed face alone passes `sceneUnderPages: false`. A project made by `graview create` has no `/pages` address and no `src/ui/app.tsx`; one made before keeps its own files. `@graview/embed` is unchanged: `mount` and `<Embed>` draw the one face only with `sceneUnderPages: true`. The JavaScript API is additive (`onTheOneFace` on `@graview/pages`). Ops and primitives, stored formats, the wire, the declaration and check finding codes, and derived tool names and input schemas are unchanged. What Graview Cloud does: for every app it mounts with `PagesApp` itself and has not moved, pass `sceneUnderPages: false`; through the embed, nothing.
