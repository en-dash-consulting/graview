A repair says what it still asks for, whether or not its rule said so. A festival's "Every selected film is screened" named `schedule` with the film and left the venue and the time to choose, as graview-invariant teaches, without writing `missing`; the problems page offered "Schedule Under Weather" as one press and answered it "Not yet: Venue id — invalid input: expected string, received undefined", so the repair could not be taken on either face. The store's violations now carry, on every repair whose rule did not say, `missing`: the arguments its act's input requires and the repair does not give. Every surface that asks before it applies asks for them. And a press that reaches the store and is refused by the act's own input is told to a watching harness (`refusedInput`), as a refusal by the policy is, so the harnesses hear an act offered whole that its input refuses.

Compatibility: no surface changes; a violation's repairs may now carry `missing` where their rule left it out.
