A connect that replaces replaces the link it should (FR-156). Graview Cloud, on a private pricing-model app on 0.1.18: an act declared `{ "on": "feature", "connects": "tier", "replaces": true }`, `tier` a relation of one, ran on a feature with no tier and was refused on a feature that had one, "Cannot remove missing edge tier feature:apps plan:free", although `get_node` showed exactly that link; alone and inside a batch, for every relation tried, and the whole batch with it. So `replaces` only ever worked on a record with nothing to replace, the one case it is not for, and it had been so since `replaces` shipped (FR-115). Every effect of a declared act reads the graph as it stood before the act, and a relation of one is kept to one by the connect itself: `replaces` severed the record's old link, the connect found the same link and severed it again, and the store refused the second removal. A declared act now severs a link once however many of its effects ask it to, so the old link is severed and the new one made, as one act, one batch and one undo, by `apply`, `applyAll`, `preview`, `previewAll`, an act's tool and `preview_mutation` alike. On a relation of many, `replaces` severs every link of that relation the record has but the one the act makes, as FR-115 said; on a relation declared from the far kind, the record's links at its own end; two acts on the same relation in one batch leave the last one's link. A record that already has the link it is given changes nothing. Refusals, their reasons and who may run what are as they were. `capabilities().shipped` gains FR-156.

Compatibility: ops, stored formats, the document format, the compiled format, wire messages, check finding codes and tool schemas are unchanged. Changed: a declared act with `replaces` on a relation of one, run on a record that already has a link of it, severs that link and makes the new one, where it was refused "Cannot remove missing edge". What Graview Cloud changes: once it takes this release it can delete its workaround, a `severs` act per relation run before the connect in one batch, and the note in its guide that tells a chat to do so; an act with `replaces: true` is the one act it means.
