The switch's Pages goes back to the page Back last landed on. Under address routing a page reached by Back or Forward is the routed face's arrival, which its router reports to nobody, so after a reload on the scene the embed knew no page: from the board, the scene, a reload, Back, Forward and Pages went to the home ("2 tasks and 1 person.") instead of the board. The address bar now hands the embed the page each Back or Forward lands on. With the other fixes of this round, a hosted page carries 0.2 KB more up front, the compiled page too.

Compatibility: no surface changes.
