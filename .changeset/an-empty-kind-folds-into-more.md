A kind with no records folds into More until it has one. n-dx's projection of a v1 tree has no constraints, and the bar still said "Constraints" beside the work, whether `pages.primary` named it or the bar ranked it so. Now the bar does not stand an empty kind's list, or its pictures, on the row, declared and derived alike; they are in More, and the kind's first record brings them back. A graph with nothing in any kind folds none, since nothing yet says which kinds matter. The place you are on stands whatever it holds, and a list that is open keeps the row it opened over (FR-158). The embed's bar is drawn again as the graph changes, so the first record shows on the row without waiting for some other change. A kind's list on the bar is named the way every other surface names it (`pluralLabel`), so a kind with no declared plural reads "Shelf items", not "ShelfItems". The bar costs nothing more up front: the hosted page's first load is 24 bytes lighter than before.

Compatibility: unchanged.
