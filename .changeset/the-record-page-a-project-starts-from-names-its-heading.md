The record page `graview create` writes names the record in its heading as the derived page does (the eleventh walk). The name in the heading was a button called "Name: Daisy #0071" where the derived record page's is called "Daisy #0071" — its heading already says what it is — so a person looking for the name to press found "Change the animal" instead, a press more on the pages at either width; the discography's song page, copied from it, did the same. The scaffold's `src/ui/pages.tsx` passes `said={facts.label}` to the heading's `EditableValue`, and a test holds it to the derived page's.

Compatibility: ops, stored formats, the wire, the declaration and derived tools are unchanged; a project made before keeps its own page until it changes the line.
