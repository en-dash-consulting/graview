The pages alone say when a page has drawn, and open on a draft view, as the whole embed does (FR-188, FR-187). `@graview/embed/pages` — a chat's widget, a phone's page — took neither `onDrawn` nor `draftView`, so a host picturing a proposal there guessed when it had drawn and could not draw the proposal at all. Now its `mount` takes `onDrawn(place)` and its root wears `data-graview-drawn` (the page's path) once the page has drawn and settled, off in the commit that moves; and it takes `draftView`, drawn where the manifest attaches and marked "Draft — <title> · Not kept", kept nowhere, the worker's host fetched before the pages draw and only when a draft is handed. Both come from the same code as the whole embed's: what watches the place is one hook (`drawn.ts`), and the draft's registration one module (`draft.ts`). `DRAWN_ATTRIBUTE` and the `EmbedDraftView` and `EmbedWhere` types are on `@graview/embed/pages` too. And the pages alone go where `handle.setPath` sends them when they were mounted without a `path`: the routed face took the first path a host handed after it opened for the one it began at, and stayed. The pages face alone measures 3.5 KB more (1.7 KB gzipped) for them, and its budget is raised by that; nothing of it is in a hosted page.

Compatibility: ops, stored formats, the wire, the declaration and derived tools are unchanged. New on `@graview/embed/pages`: `onDrawn` and `draftView` on `mount` and `<PagesEmbed>`, `setDraftView` on its handle, `DRAWN_ATTRIBUTE`, and the types `EmbedDraftView` and `EmbedWhere`. `PagesApp` follows its `path` prop from any value, not only from the one it was first drawn with. What Graview Cloud does: a widget on `@graview/embed/pages` waits for `[data-graview-drawn]` as the hosted page does, and passes `draftView` there too.
