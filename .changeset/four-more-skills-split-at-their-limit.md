Three more skills that had reached the 11,000 characters a skill is held under are split along their seams, and a fourth that was close is split where it held a job of its own. `graview-node-kind` had 1 character free and had shaved sentences to fit; it keeps declaring a kind — fields, edges, label, the ids a caller chooses, verbs, edits, `creates`, `fromTheOtherEnd`, `display.page`, `lifecycle`, a figure and a hue — and has them back: what a kind gets "without another line", the bootstrap ids never to hardcode (`"today"`, `"u-nick"`), the typecheck that sees an edge to an undeclared kind, the picker with nothing in it, the one way in on a blank installation, the brief `graview figure` prints and the chip's twenty pixels; `graview-view-specs` is new: a kind's card, row and page as data (`viewSpecs`, a document's `views` and `set-view`), headlines, figures and lists of related records, block keys, `computed` values and the template words for counts and times, with `view-*` and `computed-*` findings beside them. `graview-lists` had 2 characters free; it keeps filters, order and groups, cards at a glance, a kind's stop, several records at once, `renderPlace` and a date that repeats, and has back the right-click menu's whole paragraph (Shift+right-click, `data-graview-editable`), while `graview-tidying` is new: archiving and bringing back, merging a duplicate, and the app's own actor suggesting what it is not sure of and held to "Leave this one alone" and `tidying`. `graview-ship` had 18 characters free; it keeps opening a store, the seed, migrations, export, health, files, `serve`, a host that compiles once and `graview preview`, with its shaved sentences back (the readable adapter's stored version, the demo with no exit, the content steps, the tenant who cannot leave), and `graview-sync` is new: a snapshot projected from another system, `SyncEngine` and its conflict policies, an MCP remote behind `mcpDoor` (`hosts` included), and a Google calendar declared under `connections`, with the `connection-*` findings `graview check` says. The dev server's door to a coding agent (`localIntelligence`) moves from `graview-ship` to `graview-desk`, beside the door it is. `graview-brand` keeps the name, mark, typeface, palette, kit and capsules, and `graview-locale` is new: a currency, a locale and a time zone declared once, a field kept as money and the template words for each, with "3:30 PM", "en-GB keeps the day first" and "without one, sums are numbers" back. Each half points at the other in one line, and every one is under 9,100 characters. `graview skills install` installs twenty-six skills.

Compatibility: ops, stored formats, the wire, the declaration and derived tools are unchanged. A product's `.claude/skills` and `.agents/skills` gain `graview-view-specs`, `graview-tidying`, `graview-sync` and `graview-locale` on its next `graview skills install`, and the AGENTS.md list names them.
