A subscription is judged as its subscriber is now, where the host says who that is. A subscription keeps the person who asked for it as they were then, roles and all, and every later commit was judged with those roles: a person who subscribed to the notes as staff and was then made a guest stopped seeing the notes in their own reads, but a change to one still matched, and its label, its fields' values and the author's sentence were handed to `onSubscriptionMatch` — the hook a host turns into an email — although it promises never to tell of a record the subscriber cannot see. Now the handler takes `subscriberNow(who)`, the host's word for who the person is today (their principal, or undefined for one it no longer knows, who is told nothing; it may answer a promise), and judges each commit as that person; `matchSubscriptions` and `watchSubscriptions` take the same as `subscriber` (`subscribersNow` on `@graview/core/subscriptions` applies it to a list). A host that says nothing has each subscription judged with the person as they subscribed, as before, and the ship README says such a host removes a person's subscriptions when it takes a role away; `graview serve`, which trusts the seat headers and keeps no directory of people, is one. `a-subscriber-is-judged-as-they-are-now` holds it in a served app: a person demoted after subscribing is not told of a change to a note, is told as a guest of a change to a task they still see, and is told nothing once the host no longer knows them; and `a-subscription-is-told-what-its-subscriber-may-see` holds `subscriber` on `matchSubscriptions`.

Compatibility: ops and primitives, stored formats, the declaration and check finding codes, derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged. The wire — additive: the handler's options (`HandlerOptions`, so `createStoreHandler` and `serveStore`) gain `subscriberNow`; a stored `Subscription` is the same. What Graview Cloud does: passes `subscriberNow`, reading each person's roles from its own directory, so a person whose role it takes away hears only of what they may see now.
