A kind's list offers adding one beside its heading, not at its end. Since 0.1.30 following a place puts the keyboard on the new page's heading, so a screen reader says where it arrived; "Add a task" stood after the last row, and from the heading the keyboard went back through the whole bar to reach it, so making a record on the pages from the keyboard cost seven to nine presses more (a task 21 then 28, a talk 21 then 30, a song 17 then 26). Each creating act's press now stands beside the list's title, the next stop after the heading, and its form opens under the list's head, above the rows: making a task takes 13 presses, a talk 14, a song 10. A closed form takes no room, so the first record stands where it stood; the form still stands open where there is nothing yet, Escape still puts it away and gives the keyboard back to its press, and an empty list says the first one "starts above".
