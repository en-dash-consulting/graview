A record does not offer to sort and filter a relation that holds one thing by declaration. A deal's record read "Sort and filter who is buying →" under its one buyer, and a vehicle's "Sort and filter the lot it is parked on →" under its one lot — a link to a list that can only ever hold the name above it. An edge declared `cardinality: "one"` offers the pile only from its far end, where it may be many.
