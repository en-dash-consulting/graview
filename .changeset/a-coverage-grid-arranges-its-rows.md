A coverage grid arranges its rows before it draws, as the board, the timeline and the calendar do. A kind whose stop is a coverage grid — the discography's songs, a rescue team's callouts — had no Filter on the scene at all, so narrowing them by a number or a choice could be done only on the pages. The grid draws the arranging line over its rows at full size, filters the rows (its columns stay whole), and offers no sort or grouping, which a grid has no place for.

Compatibility: no surface changes.
