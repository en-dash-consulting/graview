A view is handed the app's whole brand, its logo included (FR-127). A worker view or a frame guest got the colors and the body and mono fonts. It drew headings and the app's name its own way, because nothing said what the app's wordmark is drawn in, how round its panels are, or what its logo looks like. And a view may load nothing, so a logo Graview Cloud keeps at `/graview/assets/<sha256>.<ext>` on the app's own host could not reach one. `GuestTheme` now carries `fontDisplay` (`--graview-font-display`) and `radius` (`--graview-radius`) beside the rest, the app's `name` as its wordmark says it, and its `logo` as a URL the host made. The host makes a Blob from an inline SVG logo. A logo at an address on the page's own origin is fetched by the host, with the page's credentials, and must answer with an image type. A logo on another origin is never asked for and never handed. A `data:` image is handed as it is. A worker view is drawn in the host's own page, so it is handed a `blob:` URL of that page, which the open kit already draws (FR-90). Where the page's policy refuses `blob:` images, it is handed a `data:` image of the same bytes instead. ChatGPT's widget policy is one such page: its `img-src` is `'self' data:` and its hosts, with no `blob:`. The host finds out by loading the `blob:` once itself before handing it. A frame guest is always handed `data:`, since a frame is another origin and the host page's `blob:` does not resolve there. When the brand changes, the view is pushed the new name, face, radius and logo. Until the new logo is ready it keeps the last one, and the last `blob:` URL is revoked once the next is handed and when the view goes. Both React bindings (`guestView` and `registerWorkerView`/`workerHome`) hand the provider's brand, so `handle.setBrand` on the embed reaches every view, which is pushed and not redrawn. `mountGuestView` and `mountWorkerView` take `brand: () => ({ name, logo })` for a host that mounts views itself. The CSS custom properties `--graview-font-display` and `--graview-radius` already reached a worker view's stylesheet by inheritance, and the graview-worker-view skill now says to draw headings with them. `guest-sandbox --transport=brand` mounts the embed with a branded app and a worker view that draws `<img src="${props.theme.logo}">` and the app's name in `var(--graview-font-display)`. It runs on the app's own page and inside Claude's and ChatGPT's widget policies, in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox: nine runs, all held. In each run the view's name and heading face match the app's wordmark and page heading by computed `font-family`, its radius is the app's, and its logo is the brand's byte for byte at its natural size. The logo is `blob:` on the page and under Claude and `data:` under ChatGPT. After `setBrand` with another name, face, radius and logo, the view follows in the same region and the same image element. On the page and under ChatGPT the new logo is a PNG on the page's own origin, fetched once and by the host alone. Under Claude, whose frame is an opaque origin, it is another inline SVG. The last `blob:` is revoked and nothing else is requested. `--transport=client` holds the same for a frame guest through the inlined client, under Graview Cloud's view policy with `img-src data:` added. The logo's maker rides with the frame host, so the hosted page carries it: 1 425 bytes more up front, 572 488 compiling (559.1 KB of 563) and 527 642 handed a compiled app (515.3 KB of 520). The bundle budgets for the guest host before a worker is drawn and for drawing a worker view rise to 12 100 / 5 700 and 122 600 / 43 800 bytes (minified / gzipped), measured at 11 931 / 5 544 and 122 122 / 43 558.

Compatibility: `GuestTheme` gains `fontDisplay` and `radius`, which are required, and `name` and `logo`, which are optional. A host that builds a `GuestTheme` itself (`mountGuestView`'s or `mountWorkerView`'s `theme`, `runWorkerViewHeadless`'s `theme`) now supplies the two required ones, and `HEADLESS_THEME` has them. `MountGuestViewOptions` and `MountWorkerViewOptions` gain an optional `brand`, and `@graview/guest/host` exports the `GuestBrand` type. Cloud's view policy, `default-src 'none'`, refuses every image, so an uploaded frame view that shows the logo needs `img-src data:`. Ops, stored formats, the document format, wire messages, check codes and tool schemas are unchanged. `capabilities().shipped` gains FR-127.
