A document may name an act in another spelling in a grant or a rule's repair, and a name that says no act is refused with the nearest one. 0.1.48 let a call name `connect-threat-partOf` for `connect-threat-part-of`; a grant (`policy.grants[].mutations`) or a repair (`rules.<name>.repairs[].act`) that named it so was still refused by `graview check` and the compile, which wanted act names in kebab-case. Now a document is read with every act a grant or a repair names written as the act it spells — case, hyphens and underscores aside, exactly one of its declared acts, its kinds' derived acts and its relations' acts — before its shape is judged, so the compiled policy and rules hold the act's own name and the policy compares real names when it runs. A name that spells no act is refused as before, and `grant-act` and `repair-act` now say "did you mean "connect-threat-part-of"?" when one is near. A grant or a repair written in TypeScript is matched as it is written, so it is not rewritten: `graview check`'s `grant-unknown-mutation` and `repair-unknown-mutation` name the act a near or other spelling says ("Did you mean "edit-budgetLine"? Name it as it is spelled."). `core`'s tests compile a grant and a repair that say `connect-threat-partOf` as `connect-threat-part-of`, let the granted role run it, pass `graview check`, and refuse a name near one act with the nearest, in a document and in TypeScript. A hosted page that compiles its document carries 0.9 KB more up front — the compiler reads the spellings — under a raise of that budget (`scripts/budget-raises/an-act-in-another-spelling-hosted-page.json`); one handed a compiled app carries none of it.

Compatibility: a behavior change in the compile: a document whose grant or repair names an act in another spelling of its name compiles, with the act's own name in its compiled policy and rules, where it was refused. A document that compiled before compiles to the same app. Check findings: `grant-act`, `repair-act`, `grant-unknown-mutation` and `repair-unknown-mutation` carry a "did you mean" fix where an act is near; their codes are unchanged. The wire and the stored formats are unchanged.
