Relationships are structure on the routed face. `kindMap(store)` derives every declared relation between the kinds with its own words and its live count, and the face draws it as "How it fits together" on the home page and at `/map`, each line with the same mark the scene's key draws (`RelationMark`, now exported from primitives and usable without a scene), each count opening the far kind's list narrowed to the ones that have the relation. A kind's page says what it relates to, groups by a relation from the address (`?by=<edge>`) and narrows by one (`?<edge>=<id>`, `?with=<edge>`), so a list you arranged is a link you can send. A record links the other way round — the far kind's list narrowed to itself — and says which pictures it is seen in, each a page and a stop in the scene.
