A policy says who may see what, as well as who may do it. `Policy.sees` keeps a kind to the roles a sight names — with `own`, to the principal's own record and what an edge joins to it — and a kind no sight names stays everybody's. `store.seenBy(principal)` is the store as that principal may see it: its graph, log, history and problems hold only what they may see, and every act still goes to the store itself; with no `sees` it is the store, unchanged. The scene's provider and the routed face hand every surface that view, a kind a seat sees none of and may not begin is kept from it like an administered module, and the way in leaves it out. `graview check` refuses a sight naming an undeclared kind (`sight-unknown-kind`). A watching harness is told what the seat may not see (`tellTheWatchWhatIsUnseen`, `useTheWatchKnowsWhatIsUnseen`).
