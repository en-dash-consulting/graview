A field typed together stays open while a tour, a dialog or a popover holds the keyboard. Graview Cloud's typed-together harness found the live editor closed, and its caret lost, when a first-look tour opened over it: the tour took the keyboard, and the editor read any keyboard leaving its text area for anywhere but its own buttons as the person leaving the field. Now a keyboard that goes to a layer over the page — Graview's own overlays (`data-graview-overlay`, the first-look tour among them), a `dialog`, `role="dialog"` or `"alertdialog"`, a popover — leaves the editor open, the caret where it was in the text area; given back, the person types on. Where the layer closes and leaves the keyboard on nothing, the text area takes it back, its selection as it was; where the keyboard goes from the layer to somewhere else on the page, the editor closes as before. `primitives`' test opens a tour over the editor and gives the keyboard back, closes a dialog without giving it back, and moves on from a dialog to the page, and the editor stays open with its caret, takes the keyboard back with its caret, and closes. `pnpm verify typed-together` puts a tour's hint over Ada's editor in a served preview, its button focused, and takes it away: the editor stayed open, the text area has the keyboard back with her caret where it was, and what she types next lands there (`aTourThatTakesTheKeyboardLeavesTheEditorOpenAndGivesItsCaretBack`).

Compatibility: ops and primitives, stored formats, the wire, the declaration and check finding codes, derived tool names and input schemas, the JavaScript API, the theme and the embed — unchanged. What Graview Cloud does: nothing in its code; its typed-together harness's first-look case holds once it builds on this.
