A record is brought "in line with the others" only through the relation the others share (the gauntlet's two kinds sharing a field, carried from walks XI and XII). A workshop whose leader presents both of the day's keynotes breaks a rule, and its problem chooses the workshop and the two talks together: the talks are presented by Zoë and the workshop is led by her, so the structure provider read "all but the workshop share Zoë (presented by)" and offered "Add a leader: bring the workshop in line with the others" — an act that ties another relation, to somebody who already leads it — and "Unlink a speaker", which unties one. Now a record whose kind cannot hold the relation is not out of step with it, and an act that says what it ties (`connects` or `severs`) is offered only where it ties that relation; an act that says neither is still read by its arguments, as before.

Compatibility: ops, stored formats, the wire, the declaration and derived tool names and schemas are unchanged; the structure provider offers fewer "in line with the others" acts.
