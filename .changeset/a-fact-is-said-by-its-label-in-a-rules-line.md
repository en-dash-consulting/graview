A fact in a rule's line is said by the words it is shown by. A rule that read `fact('proposed')` said it as "fact (proposed)" — "Nothing proposed — fact (proposed) 3 ≥ 1". Now a fact is said by its `label`, or, with none, by its name in words, as a field is: "proposals in play 3 ≥ 1", "open work 4 ≥ 2".

Compatibility: ops, stored formats, wire messages, the document format, the compiled format, check finding codes and tool names and schemas are unchanged; the hosted page's first load is the same (the words are `@graview/core/lines`'). A rule's line, and the message of a rule with no sentence of its own, read differently where they say a fact.
