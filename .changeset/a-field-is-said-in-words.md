What a person reads names a field, a relation and a group in the declaration's words, never by id. `fieldWords(definition, key)` is the one place a field becomes words (its `display.labels`, else the key spoken); an editable value's tooltip no longer says "changes "plannedAt" through a mutation", the inspector no longer says a line's edge is "rides-in", the calendar's refusals and the structure suggestions ("all 3 share the day "mon"", "(assigned to)") read in words, and `bandAggregateWords` names a band's group — the seat had been calling one "album|released-by|in|type=album".
