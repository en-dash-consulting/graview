A sender with no role sees their own ticket without what a sight hides, Roadmap f-sight-own-with-hide (request:sight-own-with-hide-drops-the-record). Graview Public's ticket sight left as `{ roles: "*", kinds: ["ticket"], own: true }` lets a sender read their ticket's triage label; the reported shape, `{ roles: "*", kinds: ["ticket"], own: true, hide: ["triage"] }`, was said to take the sender's ticket away altogether, and the same with `fields`. In the framework it does not: with `@graview/core/field-sight` loaded, a principal with no role who ran `file-ticket` is served their own ticket whole but for `triage`, after Graview's triage labeled it, and never another sender's — in `store.seenBy`, the log a seat is served (`redact` over `seatLens`), an act's answer (`answerSeenBy`), a search, the routes' `/graview/state`, a page `openRemote` opened and an agent's `get_node` and `get_graph`, with `hide` and with `fields` alike, over a declaration shaped as Graview Public's with its triage label (and, by hand, over Graview Public's own document with Cloud's triage edits and the sight pair its proposal names, through the room's shape: the routes and `openRemote`). The one place the record drops is where field sight is not loaded at all: a sight with `hide` or `fields` then shows nothing, the maker's own record included, by design (it fails closed rather than serve a field it cannot judge). `a-sender-sees-their-own-ticket-minus-what-is-hidden.test.ts` (core) and `a-sender-sees-their-own-ticket-without-what-a-sight-hides.test.ts` (tools) hold every surface, and the graview-sight skill says that a maker shown nothing by an `own` sight with `hide` means the import is missing.

Compatibility: ops and primitives — unchanged. Stored formats — unchanged. The wire — unchanged. The declaration and check finding codes — unchanged. Derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged.

What Graview Cloud does: it can apply the sight pair its triage proposal names (`remove-sight` of the plain `own` sight, `add-sight` of the one with `hide: ["triage"]`); its room and worker already load field sight. Its test of a sender's own ticket after triage is the check — if the ticket still drops there, the store that served it is one where `@graview/core/field-sight` was never loaded, and that path is the one to bring back as a new request.
