An act you may run only on yourself is still offered to you. `permittedMutations` asked the policy with no subject, and a `self` grant is only satisfied by a subject that is the principal, so a shopper granted "ask, on yours" was offered nothing: no tool for their agent, no act in a guest frame. The offer now reads the principal (or the person an agent acts for) as the subject. The store still refuses the same act on anybody else.

Compatibility: tool names and schemas — a principal with a `self` grant is now offered that act where before it was left out; nothing else changes.
