Cards you can read at a glance (the Roadmap's f-glanceable-cards). On the Graview Roadmap's "Up next", each card printed its whole thirty-line summary, so a row of cards was a wall of text, every card stretched to the tallest beside it, in a box of its own to scroll under the page's scroll. A long text on a card is now three lines at most and on a row one, cut with an ellipsis; the record's page says it whole. A template says a long text's start on purpose: `{summary|excerpt}` is its first sentence, or about 140 characters cut at a word, and `{summary|excerpt: 80}` says at most 80. Cards side by side keep their own heights. A place drawn from blocks flows with the page, never a box that scrolls inside itself; a list cut by its `limit` says "See all 120 features" on the routed face and goes to the kind's list (or "24 more · all features" when it listed some of them), where the scene still says "and 24 more" of a list without a limit. `graview check` warns where a card or a row puts a `text` field whole in a text block (`view-whole-text`: "feature card shows summary whole; it will be clamped. Use {summary|excerpt} or move it to the page."). `excerpt` is in `@graview/core` too, and the stylesheet the blocks are drawn with is written without the spaces it does not need, to make room for this under the hosted page's budget.

Compatibility: ops, stored formats and wire messages are unchanged. The document format's templates take the formatter `excerpt` (`excerpt: <20–1000>`); a template that names it is refused by an older build. New check finding code `view-whole-text`, a warning. `ResolvedList` carries `total`, how many its source named before the limit. `SpecLinkTo.list` is `SpecLinkTo.more`, a face's way to draw the rest of a list cut short. `SPEC_VIEW_CSS` says the same rules without spaces (`.graview-spec [data-graview-tone="good"]{--graview-spec-tone:var(--graview-good)}`), with `.graview-spec-card .graview-spec-text` clamped to three lines and `.graview-spec-row .graview-spec-text` to one. On the routed face a place drawn from blocks has no fixed height (`[data-testid="place-lens"][data-flows]`). What a host does: a card that should say more than three lines of a text moves it to the page.
