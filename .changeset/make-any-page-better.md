Right-click any page to make it better, and a menu that opens on what you want, Roadmap feature:f-make-any-page-better. Changing how a page looks was offered only inside a worker view, under three rows of its own; on a page the app draws by itself — the Tasks list, a task's page, the home — a right-click never offered it, and "make this page look better" typed in the menu went to the record quick ask, which answered about a task.

The menu now opens with its field first, "Tell Graview what you want…", focused where there is a mouse. Under it are two buttons, Chat (the quick ask and its plan) and Better UI; as words are typed the Fast slot lights the one they belong to (`aboutTheLook`), Enter goes to the lit one, a click, Tab or an arrow chooses the other, and pressed with the field empty each opens its own card. Both are real buttons with names, and the lit one says so (`aria-pressed`). Below them, Suggested: two or three things the Fast slot picks in one call from what was rated well here before, the obvious acts on the record and what the page lacks, each marked 💬 or ✨ for the way it goes and worded as the act alone; it fills in without moving the menu's top, and a pick runs through its way's card. Then the first four acts and More… for the rest; typing filters all of them. The heading names only the thing ("Someday · list"). On a phone the same column is a sheet from the bottom. Worker views and the scene's menu are laid out the same way. `MenuSection.mode` (`MenuMode`: its id, label, mark, what words it claims, how it opens and its nudges) replaces `takesWords`.

Better UI on a page the app draws — a row, a card, the heading, the page's ground — is offered to a seat that may change the app and named as a person would name the part: a kind's list by its plural, a group by its heading's words, a record's page as every page of its kind ("Task page"), the home "Home". Its card offers Just do it, Describe changes… and Highlight area…. The page's settings first: the Fast slot says whether they can say what was asked (`specCanSay`, sure at 0.6 or more for a new design), the light model drafts the least edits over the view spec (`draftPageSpec` on `@graview/tools/section`: `set-view`, `set-glance`, `set-page-fields`, `arrange-pages`' `sort`, `group` and `filters`, `set-label`, `set-format`), the host's composer judges them with the checker (`composeChange`), and the page is drawn with them on this page only through the host's new `onDraftChange` (`DraftChangeHost`). Where the settings cannot say it, the card says "Its settings can't do that. Graview can draw a new design for “Tasks” and change it. Draw one?", and Draw it drafts a worker view seeded from the page as it stands (`draftPageView`, `forkManifest`).

A draft is never shown broken. While Graview works the part is covered by a shimmer, "Graview is redesigning “Tasks”…", with Graview's presence. Each draft is drawn and judged before anyone sees it: the host's checks of a view's code and of what it drew, `compareViewRevision` against the page it replaces, a style rule the host would strip (`cssFindings`, from the sanitizer itself), and what the page showed before against what the draft shows (`drawnFindings` on `@graview/tools/section`: no record and no heading lost, no heading twice, no markup, field name, raw date or `undefined` shown as words). What fails is handed back to the light model twice and then the full one (`repairTries`); if all three fail the card says "I couldn't make a version that looks right." and offers to try again with words. The view prompt now writes CSS the host keeps (rules on its own class, never `:host`), names regions by `aria-labelledby`, says dates in words and builds markup with `graview.html` rather than strings. A good draft is drawn in place, full width and uncovered, and the card becomes a slim bar along the bottom (a sheet's peek on a phone), never over the part: one line of what changed measured from the page itself (`describeChange`: "Tasks now show side by side, three to a line and in bigger type."), never the model's own account; Before and After; "Tell it what to change…" to go another step, with Step back; Keep and Discard. Keep makes it the page, as one change to the app with one Undo; a new design replaces the kind's list, a record's page or the home's body, and the page the app draws stays reached by "Original" under its heading (`?drawn=built-in`). The words say "This changes how Tasks looks for everyone in this app." and never "view", "spec", "host" or "stylesheet".

`KeptViews` gains the optional `fork(fork)`, `draftFork(fork | null)` and `forked()` (`ViewForkKept`, `ForkedPage`); `withMemoryRevisions` keeps them in memory; `checkViewForkHost` on `@graview/core/conformance` runs a host's door as the page does. The embed draws what `forked()` lists over the app's views, and a page's card outlives a host's `setApp`. On `pnpm dev` the todo example lays a kept change over its code app, shows a draft on the page alone and draws a page's new design in place of Things' own. Tests: `a-built-in-page-is-made-better-spec-first`, `a-built-in-page-s-part-is-named-as-a-person-would`, `a-page-made-its-own-view-is-drafted-kept-and-undone`, `a-section-is-revised-alone-and-the-rest-kept-byte-for-byte`, `typing-in-the-menu-filters-its-rows-and-asks-about-what-it-is-on`, the opt-in `a-page-made-better-is-routed-on-real-jev-live`, and `verify-page-better`, `verify-section` and `verify-menu` in Chromium and WebKit; `graview-view-specs` says how a person makes a page better.

What a hosted page loads up front is 0.6 KB more up front (566 B minified, 210 B gzipped; 566 / 164 handed a compiled app): the host's `onDraftChange` handed through, and the frame drawing what the host's views list as forked. Before a face draws, the scene face measured 1_112_586 B, 741 more than main (the limit is 1087 KB, from 1086 KB), and the budget raise files ask the pages face alone for 1.6 KB minified and 0.5 KB gzipped more, every face for 20.1 KB gzipped (the menu's Suggested, the page's parts, the card, its bar, the judging of a draft and the drafts as chunks of their own, fetched with the first right-click on a page and the first card), and the embed with the studio handed in for 0.4 KB gzipped.

Compatibility: ops and primitives — unchanged. Stored formats — unchanged; a host that keeps its views may now keep views made of a built-in page (`KeptViews.fork`), optional. The wire — unchanged. The declaration and check finding codes — unchanged; a page's draft is only ever the existing edit ops, compiled by the checker. Derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged. The JavaScript API — `KeptViews` gains the optional `fork`, `draftFork` and `forked`, with `ViewForkKept` and `ForkedPage`; `checkViewForkHost` is new on `@graview/core/conformance`; the provider, the pages' context and the embed take `onDraftChange` (`DraftChangeHost` from `@graview/tools/reshape`); `MenuTarget` and `PointerMenu` take an optional `page` (`MenuPage`); `MenuSection.takesWords` is replaced by `MenuSection.mode` (`MenuMode`); `partsWanted` is new on `@graview/react/drawing`; `@graview/tools/section` adds `aboutTheLook`, `specCanSay`, `draftPageSpec`, `draftPageView`, `forkManifest`, `notTheSpec`, `PAGE_SPEC_OPS`, `OWN_VIEW_WHEN_SURE`, `mayChangeDeclaration`, `repairTries`, `COULD_NOT`, `readPageViewReply`, `cssFindings`, `drawnFindings` and `describeChange`, and `reviseSection` repairs before it refuses and never answers with a note of what the host left out. The look — the menu opens with its field, Chat and Better UI under it, Suggested, the first four acts and More…, a sheet on a phone; Better UI covers the part while Graview redesigns it and then shows the draft in place with a bar along the bottom; a page given a new design shows "Original" under its heading. What Graview Cloud does — nothing until it lends `onDraftChange` (its `setApp` with the changed app, and back) and `KeptViews.fork`/`draftFork`/`forked` (its `propose_view` and `apply_view` for a view that replaces a kind's list or page); with `composeChange` and `onProposeChange` alone it already offers the settings path, its steps said and not drawn.
