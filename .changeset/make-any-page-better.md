Right-click any page to make it better, and a menu that opens on what you want, Roadmap feature:f-make-any-page-better. Changing how a page looks was offered only inside a worker view, under three rows of its own; on a page the app draws by itself — the Tasks list, a task's page, the home — a right-click never offered it, and "make this page look better" typed in the menu went to the record quick ask, which answered about a task.

The menu now opens with its field first, "Tell Graview what you want…", focused where there is a mouse. Under it are two buttons, Chat (the quick ask and its plan) and Better UI; as words are typed the Fast slot lights the one they belong to (`aboutTheLook`), Enter goes to the lit one, a click, Tab or an arrow chooses the other, and pressed with the field empty each opens its own card. Both are real buttons with names, and the lit one says so (`aria-pressed`). Below them, Suggested: two or three things the Fast slot picks in one call from what was rated well here before, the obvious acts on the record filled in and what the page lacks, each marked 💬 or ✨ for the way it goes and worded as the act alone; it fills in without moving the menu's top, and a pick runs through its way's card. A suggestion never says a row again: an act is offered with its answer in it ("Set the status to Doing", the next of the record's choice; "Finish “Pay the deposit”" for an act that asks nothing), one whose answer cannot be guessed is left to its row, and anything a row below says as it is is dropped (`filledActs`, `notARow`). What is true about the record ("Is connected to nothing yet") is read after More… with the rest, not between Suggested and the acts. Then the first four acts and More… for the rest, which hands the keyboard to the first of them; what the seat may not do is said at once where the acts leave room, and behind More… where they fill it; typing filters all of them, and the up arrow from the field goes round to the last row. The heading names only the thing ("Someday · list"). On a phone the same column is a sheet from the bottom. Worker views and the scene's menu are laid out the same way. `MenuSection.mode` (`MenuMode`: its id, label, mark, what words it claims, how it opens and its nudges) replaces `takesWords`.

Better UI on a page the app draws — a row, a card, the heading, the page's ground — is offered to a seat that may change the app and named as a person would name the part: a kind's list by its plural, a group by its heading's words, a record's page as every page of its kind ("Task page"), the home "Home". Its card offers Just do it, Describe changes… and Highlight area…. The page's settings first: the Fast slot says whether they can say what was asked (`specCanSay`, sure at 0.6 or more for a new design), the light model drafts the least edits over the view spec (`draftPageSpec` on `@graview/tools/section`: `set-view`, `set-glance`, `set-page-fields`, `arrange-pages`' `sort`, `group` and `filters`, `set-label`, `set-format`), the host's composer judges them with the checker (`composeChange`), and the page is drawn with them on this page only through the host's new `onDraftChange` (`DraftChangeHost`). Where the settings cannot say it, the card says "Its settings can't do that. Graview can draw a new design for “Tasks” and change it. Draw one?", and Draw it drafts a worker view seeded from the page as it stands (`draftPageView`, `forkManifest`).

A draft is never shown broken. While Graview works the part is covered by a shimmer, "Graview is redesigning “Tasks”…", with Graview's presence. Each draft is drawn and judged before anyone sees it: the host's checks of a view's code and of what it drew, `compareViewRevision` against the page it replaces, a style rule the host would strip (`cssFindings`, from the sanitizer itself), and what the page showed before against what the draft shows (`drawnFindings` on `@graview/tools/section`: no record and no heading lost; each record still saying what its row said — its status, its dates, its counts, in its own words or the same day said another way (`rowSays`) — unless the person asked them away ("hide the dates", "just the names"); each record under the heading it was under unless a regrouping was asked; no heading twice; no markup, field name, raw date or `undefined` shown as words). The page is read for it record by record (`DrawnRead`'s `facts`, `row` and `under`), and the prompt tells the model what each record showed and where it sat. What fails is handed back to the light model at most twice, with exactly what it lost, and all of it within 30 seconds (`repairTries`, `DRAFT_WITHIN_MS`, `inTime`): the full model is never in a loop a person waits on. Where none passes, the card says plainly what it could not keep ("I couldn't keep the dates and statuses in a new design.", `couldNotKeep`) and offers Try harder — the full model once, from the light model's last try and what broke in it, the part covered with "Graview is thinking harder about “Tasks”… this can take a minute or two" and the time it has taken, and a Cancel that puts the page back at once — and Say it differently, which puts the keyboard in the words field. The light model is handed, per record, a checklist of what it must show and the heading it sits under (`checklistOf`), which the code then checks line by line, and the page's own markup to start from, so what it shows survives by default. The settings path, too, repairs with the light model and never the full one. The view prompt now writes CSS the host keeps (rules on its own class, never `:host`), names regions by `aria-labelledby`, says dates in words and builds markup with `graview.html` rather than strings. A good draft is drawn in place, full width and uncovered, and the card becomes a slim bar along the bottom (a sheet's peek on a phone), never over the part: one line of what changed measured from the page itself (`describeChange`: "Tasks now show side by side, three to a line and in bigger type."), never the model's own account; Before and After; "Tell it what to change…" to go another step, with Step back; Keep and Discard. Keep makes it the page, as one change to the app with one Undo; a new design replaces the kind's list, a record's page or the home's body, and the page the app draws stays reached by "Original" under its heading (`?drawn=built-in`). The words say "This changes how Tasks looks for everyone in this app." and never "view", "spec", "host" or "stylesheet".

`KeptViews` gains the optional `fork(fork)`, `draftFork(fork | null)` and `forked()` (`ViewForkKept`, `ForkedPage`); `withMemoryRevisions` keeps them in memory; `checkViewForkHost` on `@graview/core/conformance` runs a host's door as the page does. The embed draws what `forked()` lists over the app's views, and a page's card outlives a host's `setApp`. On `pnpm dev` the dev server's model door (`aiDevProxy`) now waits as long as the model thinks rather than dropping the request at fetch's five-minute headers timeout, which a reasoning model drawing a whole page can reach; the todo example lays a kept change over its code app, shows a draft on the page alone and draws a page's new design in place of Things' own. Tests: `a-built-in-page-is-made-better-spec-first`, `a-built-in-page-s-part-is-named-as-a-person-would`, `a-page-made-its-own-view-is-drafted-kept-and-undone`, `a-section-is-revised-alone-and-the-rest-kept-byte-for-byte`, `typing-in-the-menu-filters-its-rows-and-asks-about-what-it-is-on`, the opt-in `a-page-made-better-is-routed-on-real-jev-live`, and `verify-page-better`, `verify-section` and `verify-menu` in Chromium and WebKit; `graview-view-specs` says how a person makes a page better.

What a hosted page loads up front is 0.6 KB more up front (566 B minified, 210 B gzipped; 566 / 164 handed a compiled app): the host's `onDraftChange` handed through, and the frame drawing what the host's views list as forked.
