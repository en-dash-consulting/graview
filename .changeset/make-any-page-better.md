Right-click any page to make it better, Roadmap feature:f-make-any-page-better. "Make this better" was offered only inside a worker view; on a page the app draws by itself — the Tasks list, a task's page, the home — a right-click never offered it, and "make this page look better" typed in the menu went to the record quick ask, which answered about a task. Now a right-click anywhere on such a page, on a row, a card, the heading or the page's ground, offers "Make “Tasks” better" with the same three ways in (Just do it, Describe changes…, Highlight area…) to a seat that may change the app. The part is named as a person would name it: a kind's list by its plural, wherever on it the press landed (never the record a row is); a group of it by its heading's own words; a record's page as every page of its kind ("Task page"); the home "Home"; a place by its title. Words typed in the menu are read by the Fast slot first (`aboutTheLook`): about how the part under the cursor looks or is laid out ("make the cards bigger", "show the due date on each row"), they open that card already asked; about the records ("close these", "assign to sam"), they go to the quick ask as before. Inside a worker view the same words open the section's card.

The view spec first. The Fast slot says whether the spec can say what was asked (`specCanSay`; a view of its own only where it is sure, at 0.6 or more). Where it can, the light model drafts the least edits over the spec (`draftPageSpec` on `@graview/tools/section`: `set-view`, `set-glance`, `set-page-fields`, `arrange-pages`'s `sort`, `group` and `filters`, `set-label`, `set-format`, and nothing else of the app), the host's composer judges them with the checker (`composeChange`), and what it refuses is handed back once to the full model and refused in words if it fails again; a draft that does not compile is never offered. The page is drawn with the edits as a draft on this page only, through the host's new `onDraftChange` (`DraftChangeHost`: the changed app laid over this page alone, answered with the way to put it back), and a draft that changes nothing drawn there (a page a design draws without reading the spec) is not offered as one. Keep hands the same edits to `onProposeChange` as one change to the app, with one Undo. Without `onDraftChange` the card says what would change, "Not shown on the page until it is kept."

Then its own view. Where the spec cannot say it, the card says so in its own words — "The page's settings have no word for that. That needs its own view. Make “Tasks” its own view and change it?" — and Yes drafts a worker view seeded from the page as it stands (its headings and what it shows, a line each, and the data it reads), revised as asked — by the light model, the full one where its answer cannot be read and to repair (`draftPageView`, `forkManifest`), drawn as that page's view on this page only and judged there: the host's checks of a view's code, and `compareViewRevision` against the page it replaces, so a heading the page drew and the view drops is handed back once, then refused. Keep makes it that page's view: a view of many that replaces the kind's list, of one that replaces a record's page, or the home's body. The page the app draws stays reached under "Also as" (`?drawn=built-in`: "Built-in list", "Built-in page", "Built-in home"). `KeptViews` gains the optional `fork(fork)`, `draftFork(fork | null)` and `forked()` (`ViewForkKept`, `ForkedPage`); `withMemoryRevisions` keeps them in memory, "Kept until this page reloads"; `checkViewForkHost` on `@graview/core/conformance` runs a host's door as the page does. The embed draws what `forked()` lists over the app's views. A page's card outlives a host's `setApp`, which draws the embed again: its draft keeps its Keep and a kept change its Undo.

On `pnpm dev` the todo example lays a kept change to the spec over its code app (its view spec, glances and how its lists open), shows a draft on the page alone (`onDraftChange`), and draws a page made its own view in place of Things' own, which stays under "Also as". Tests: `a-built-in-page-is-made-better-spec-first`, `a-built-in-page-s-part-is-named-as-a-person-would`, `a-page-made-its-own-view-is-drafted-kept-and-undone`, the opt-in `a-page-made-better-is-routed-on-real-jev-live`, and `verify-page-better` in Chromium and WebKit; `graview-view-specs` says how a person makes a page better.

What a hosted page loads up front is 0.6 KB more up front (566 B minified, 203 B gzipped; 566 / 154 handed a compiled app): the host's `onDraftChange` handed through, and the frame drawing what the host's views list as forked, with the worker's host fetched only once it lists one. Before a face draws, the scene face measured 1_112_578 B, 733 more than main (the limit is 1087 KB, from 1086 KB), and the budget raise files ask the pages face alone for 1.5 KB minified and 0.5 KB gzipped more, every face for 12.1 KB gzipped (the page's parts, its card and the drafts as chunks of their own, fetched with the first right-click on a page and the first card), and the embed with the studio handed in for 0.4 KB gzipped.

Compatibility: ops and primitives — unchanged. Stored formats — unchanged; a host that keeps its views may now keep views made of a built-in page (`KeptViews.fork`), optional. The wire — unchanged. The declaration and check finding codes — unchanged; a page's draft is only ever the existing edit ops, compiled by the checker. Derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged. The JavaScript API — `KeptViews` gains the optional `fork`, `draftFork` and `forked`, with `ViewForkKept` and `ForkedPage`; `checkViewForkHost` is new on `@graview/core/conformance`; the provider, the pages' context and the embed take `onDraftChange` (`DraftChangeHost` from `@graview/tools/reshape`); `MenuTarget` and `PointerMenu` take an optional `page` (`MenuPage`), `MenuSection` an optional `takesWords`; `partsWanted` is new on `@graview/react/drawing`; `@graview/tools/section` adds `aboutTheLook`, `specCanSay`, `draftPageSpec`, `draftPageView`, `forkManifest`, `notTheSpec`, `PAGE_SPEC_OPS`, `OWN_VIEW_WHEN_SURE` and `mayChangeDeclaration`. The look — a right-click on a built-in page shows "Make “…” better" first in the menu for a seat that may change the app, its card beside the page, and a page made its own view shows "Also as: Built-in list" (or page, or home) under its heading. What Graview Cloud does — nothing until it lends `onDraftChange` (its `setApp` with the changed app, and back) and `KeptViews.fork`/`draftFork`/`forked` (its `propose_view` and `apply_view` for a view that replaces a kind's list or page); with `composeChange` and `onProposeChange` alone it already offers the spec path, with the steps said and not drawn.
