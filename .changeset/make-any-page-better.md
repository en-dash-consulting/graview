Right-click any page to make it better, and a menu that opens on what you want, Roadmap feature:f-make-any-page-better. Changing how a page looks was offered only inside a worker view, under three rows of its own; on a page the app draws by itself — the Tasks list, a task's page, the home — a right-click never offered it, and "make this page look better" typed in the menu went to the record quick ask, which answered about a task.

The menu now opens with its field first, "Tell Graview what you want…", focused where there is a mouse. Under it are two buttons, Chat (the quick ask and its plan) and Better UI; as words are typed the Fast slot lights the one they belong to (`aboutTheLook`), Enter goes to the lit one, a click, Tab or an arrow chooses the other, and pressed with the field empty each opens its own card. Both are real buttons with names, and the lit one says so (`aria-pressed`). Below them, Suggested: two or three things the Fast slot picks in one call from what was rated well here before, the obvious acts on the record filled in and what the page lacks, each marked 💬 or ✨ for the way it goes and worded as the act alone; it fills in without moving the menu's top, and a pick runs through its way's card. A suggestion never says a row again: an act is offered with its answer in it ("Set the status to Doing", the next of the record's choice; "Finish “Pay the deposit”" for an act that asks nothing), one whose answer cannot be guessed is left to its row, and anything a row below says as it is is dropped (`filledActs`, `notARow`). What is true about the record ("Is connected to nothing yet") is read after More… with the rest, not between Suggested and the acts. Then the first four acts and More… for the rest, which hands the keyboard to the first of them; what the seat may not do is said at once where the acts leave room, and behind More… where they fill it; typing filters all of them, and the up arrow from the field goes round to the last row. The heading names only the thing ("Someday · list"). On a phone the same column is a sheet from the bottom. Worker views and the scene's menu are laid out the same way. `MenuSection.mode` (`MenuMode`: its id, label, mark, what words it claims, how it opens and its nudges) replaces `takesWords`.

Better UI is a conversation beside the page. On a page the app draws — a row, a card, the heading, the page's ground — or a part of a worker view, it is offered to a seat that may change the app, names the part as a person would (a kind's list by its plural, a group by its heading's words, a record's page as every page of its kind, "Task page"; the home "Home"), and opens a panel docked flush under the app's bar at the right, down to the window's foot, on the shell's own surface with one line between it and the page — the bar spans the window as always and the page gives up the width beside it, so the panel never stands over what it changes; below 1100 pixels it is a drawer over the page's right side, and on a phone a sheet from the bottom with room left below the page. A chip says what the conversation is about ("About: Tasks", or the part pointed at by its own heading, "About: Someday"), handed to the agent beside the person's words, which are kept exactly as typed. The person says what should change (or presses "Make it easier to read", or "Point at a part…" and drags over it), and Graview answers in short plain sentences and acts, its steps shown as it takes them ("Looking at Tasks", "Drafting: cards side by side, each with its status and date", "Check: dates lost on 3 tasks — fixing", "Check: nothing lost — it's on the page"), with the time it has taken and Stop, which puts the page back at once. Where the page's settings can say it, the quick way still lands in seconds with no conversation needed: the Fast slot says whether they can (`specCanSay`), the light model drafts the least edits over the view spec (`draftPageSpec` on `@graview/tools/section`: `set-view`, `set-glance`, `set-page-fields`, `arrange-pages`' `sort`, `group` and `filters`, `set-label`, `set-format`), the host's composer judges them (`composeChange`), and the page is drawn with them on this page only through the host's new `onDraftChange` (`DraftChangeHost`).

Otherwise Graview's agent makes a new design the way a connected chat does (`runDesignAgent`, `DESIGN_AGENT_SYSTEM`), on the host's new design slot (`HostAi.design`: quick enough to talk with, good enough to keep what a page shows; unsaid, the Thinking slot serves it), and where the slot streams (`ThinkingProvider.stream`) the panel shows the agent's sentence as the model writes it, then how far into the design it is ("Tasks are cards side by side… — writing it, 109 lines so far", `writingSaid`), never its code: one plain request a step, each answered as one JSON object — what it says and what it does — so any model a host lends works. It looks at what the page draws, record by record (`lookOf`: headings, each record's words and the heading it sits under, how they stand), reads the records it is handed, and drafts a whole script; the page draws the draft under a cover where nobody sees it and judges it — the host's checks of a view's code, a style rule the host would strip (`cssFindings`), `compareViewRevision` for a part of a view, and what the page showed against what the draft shows (`drawnFindings`: no record and no heading lost, nor a record shown more often than the page showed it; each record still saying what its row said — its status, its dates, its counts, in its own words or the same day said another way (`rowSays`) — unless the person asked them away; each record under the heading it was under unless a regrouping was asked; no heading twice; no markup, field name, raw date or `undefined` shown as words). What the checks found and what the draft drew go back to the agent as the result of its step, never to the person as a refusal; it fixes them and drafts again until one passes, says it is done, or asks the person. A draft that passed is drawn on the page itself: the panel holds Before and After, Keep and Discard, and says what changed in the code's words, measured from the page (`describeChange`), never the model's account of itself. Keep makes it the page, as one change with one Undo; a new design replaces the kind's list, a record's page or the home's body, and the page the app draws stays reached by "Original" under its heading (`?drawn=built-in`). The conversation goes on after a draft and after it is kept ("make the dates smaller" changes the draft or the kept design). The agent is handed, per record, what it must show and where it sits (`checklistOf`), how the page grouped its records, read from the graph (`groupingRule`), the page's own markup to start from, and a date rule that keeps a stored day from becoming the day before. The words say "This changes how Tasks looks for everyone in this app." and never "view", "spec", "host" or "stylesheet". The 30-second light-model loop, Try harder and "I couldn't keep…" stop of the version before are gone.

The embed draws what `forked()` lists over the app's views, and a page's card outlives a host's `setApp`. On `pnpm dev` the dev server's model door (`aiDevProxy`) now waits as long as the model thinks rather than dropping the request at fetch's five-minute headers timeout, which a reasoning model drawing a whole page can reach; it answers `tier: "design"` with the design model (`GRAVIEW_AI_DESIGN_MODEL`; on xAI `grok-4.20-0309-reasoning`, which drew Tasks, Home, a task page and Lists in 31–62 seconds an answer where `grok-4.7` took two to eight minutes; Claude Sonnet and GPT-5.4 on the others) and streams an answer asked with `stream: true` as lines of JSON (`{ "delta" }`, then `{ "text", "usage" }`) from chat completions' and the Messages API's events, the probe saying `design` and `streams`; `dev-ai`'s door lends the page a design slot that streams; the todo example lays a kept change over its code app, shows a draft on the page alone and draws a page's new design in place of Things' own. Tests: `a-new-design-is-made-in-a-conversation`, `a-built-in-page-is-made-better-spec-first`, `a-built-in-page-s-part-is-named-as-a-person-would`, `a-page-made-its-own-view-is-drafted-kept-and-undone`, `a-section-is-revised-alone-and-the-rest-kept-byte-for-byte`, `typing-in-the-menu-filters-its-rows-and-asks-about-what-it-is-on`, the opt-in `a-page-made-better-is-routed-on-real-jev-live`, and `verify-page-better`, `verify-section` and `verify-menu` in Chromium and WebKit; `graview-view-specs` says how a person makes a page better.

A new design keeps what a person could do on the page, and Graview says what its controls do only as pressing them found. Every draft, a worker view's or a page's new design, has each of its controls pressed before anybody sees it — run headless for the seat in a worker of the page's own, each bound act rehearsed with nothing applied, each link followed (`checkPresses`, from the press check of `@graview/guest/headless/presses`) — and a control that does nothing, throws or is refused goes back to the agent as a finding. What the page let a person do on each record is a check of its own: a record whose row had a control (a circle that finishes it) must still have a press bound to the same act (`data-act` with `data-record` its id, accepted when pressed), and a record that opened by its name must still open (`pressFindings`); a checkbox or a button bound to nothing fails, and so a draft with one is never shown. The agent is handed the acts a press may ask for on the kind (`acts_a_press_may_ask_for`, each with its title, the argument the record goes in and the yes-or-no field it marks) and taught the bound presses (`data-act`, `data-record`, `data-prefill`, `data-save`), and a new design's manifest lists those acts (`forkManifest`'s `acts`). What a passing draft's controls do is said from what pressing found ("Pressing each control checked it: Mark it done works on 3 tasks and 3 open their pages.", `pressSaid`); a sentence of the model's about what a control does is not said for it (`CONTROL_WORDS`). Told "it doesn't work" or "I can't mark them complete" (`IT_DOES_NOT_WORK`), Graview presses every control of the design on the page first, says what it found, and hands that to the agent (`the_controls_as_pressed`) before it says anything of its own; those words never go to the settings path. A view's place lends `presses` where the embed lent it the headless run (`lendViewPressing` on `@graview/guest/host/pressing`, `pressedHere` on `@graview/guest/headless/pressed-here`), so a guest host alone carries none of it; a page that draws `PagesApp` itself lends it the same way, as the todo example does. A press must show that it landed, and that is a check, not a request: each act a draft binds is applied in a copy of the store nobody sees, once for each state its records are in, and the draft is drawn again from the changed records — the record it was pressed on must be drawn differently after (its words or its control), or the agent is told "Pressed on “Write the brief”, it lands, and the design draws “Write the brief” just as before it"; and a press that changes nothing where it stands (finishing a finished task) is told with the act that would, one that writes the same field ("Bind its press there to “reopen” instead, as the page did"). On the todo example's dev server, an act done on a page drawn with a change — finishing a task from a kept design — reaches the store the browser remembers as the same op, and is still done after a reload; only the change to the app is kept until the page reloads (`forwardActs` in `apps/todo/src/dev-change.ts`). The conversation scrolls under the panel's fixed header with the newest message at its foot, and its first message is never cut off behind the header or the About chip. A record's row that has a control is read as such (`DrawnRead`'s `presses` and `opens`). The scene's menu offers ✨ Better UI too, and says true things when nothing applies. A right-click on a place or a record in the scene offered Chat alone, and below the columns of the todo example's "The lists" it said "Nothing can be done with a list yet — nothing this app declares acts on it" of an app that renames a list and adds a task to it: the menu was about every list at once. Now a right-click on a place in the scene ("The lists") or on a record (a task: "Tasks") offers Better UI for that part of the pages; pressed, it switches to the pages, goes to the part's page and opens the panel there, which says "From the scene: Better UI changes how “The lists” looks on its page here, for everyone in this app." — carried across the switch in the tab's session, so a host that draws the pages at their own address works the same. A right-click in a list's column, between its tasks, is about that list and offers its acts (`holderFrom` on `@graview/react`'s card host: the nearest part around the pointer that holds one record and only what is linked to it). And where nothing is offered, the menu says why in plain words — "Nothing here is done to all the lists at once. Right-click one list to see what you can do with it.", or "There is nothing to do with a list in this app yet." only where that is so — and a read-only field's hint says "nothing in this app changes" it. On main the empty-menu sentence and the menu about every list were the same; Better UI in the scene is new here. Tests: `a-right-click-in-a-records-column-is-about-that-record`, and `verify-scene-menu`'s `aRightClickInAListsColumnInTheSceneOffersItsActs` and `aRightClickOnAPlaceInTheSceneOffersBetterUiAndOpensThePanel`, in Chromium and WebKit.
