A skill for lists that stay usable as an app grows, `graview-lists`: what a kind's list offers to filter by (`pages.lists`, the chips and the Filter headings), cards read at a glance with the `excerpt` formatter, records set aside with archive, merge and "Bring it back" instead of deleted, and a place printed by `renderPlace` — each verified with `graview check` (`pages-filter-unknown`, `pages-kind-unknown`, `view-whole-text`) and `graview describe`. The pages skill was at its length, so the lists are a job of their own. `graview-agent-seat` says how a host passes what an agent's guess was based on (`lineage`), and that a link made less than sure stays provisional until a person keeps it. The skills test reads a view's finding codes where the view's validator says them.

Compatibility: unchanged.
