A scene's lines are measured again once its cards stand. On the nightly's runner a page opened on todo's `rule-order` with its relation shown drew its waits-for line ending above and below the two cards it joins (`pnpm verify lines`, `everyLineLandsOnSomething`): the box the layout gives a view keeps its size, the panel a line lands on stands in the middle of it, and a panel whose words settled a frame after the lines were measured moved inside a box that never changed — the lines watched the boxes, not the panels. They watch the panels too now (`drawingIn`, what `measureVisible` measures), and are measured again when one changes size. And skipping the flight from a layout to itself, which a hand letting go had set off, had also skipped the one render on the next frame that a scene's first layout used to end with: `useAnimatedLayout` still flies nowhere when the layout is already there, and still draws it once more on the next frame unless asked not to settle (`settle: false`, a hand's pan, painted where it lands before it was let go). React's test holds that a scene's first layout is drawn once more on the next frame, and that turning motion back on over a hand's pan still draws once. `pnpm verify lines` says where a line on nothing ends, what is under each end and the cards nearest (`astray` in its verdict).

Compatibility: ops and primitives, stored formats, the wire, the declaration and check finding codes, derived tool names and input schemas, the JavaScript API, the theme and the embed — unchanged.
