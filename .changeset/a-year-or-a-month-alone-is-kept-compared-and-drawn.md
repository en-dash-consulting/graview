The four follow-ups to 0.1.56 (Roadmap f-partial-dates-in-conversation, f-partial-dates-arithmetic, f-blocks-home-flagged, f-logo-on-dark-light-ground). A date has known only its year or its month since 0.1.56, and four surfaces had not caught up with it or with the dark page beside it.

A rule compares a date that knows only its year or its month by the span it covers (`daySpanOf` on `@graview/core`: `2006` is January 1 to December 31, `2016-05` the first of May to the thirty-first). It is before another date only when the whole span is, after it only when the whole span is, and equal only when the spans are — so a month the day is in is neither before nor after `today()`, where by their letters alone "2006" < "2006-03-04" had been true. `days()` and `hours()` between such dates are measured from the first day each covers, and `date()` of one is that day. The calendar lens draws a month alone as an all-day bar across its month, and a span that ends in a month runs to that month's last day; a year alone is still not placed — a bar across a whole year says nothing a grid can place, and a day picked for it would be a false exact date — and the lens says once how many it left off ("2 know only their year, so they are not placed."; `daysCovered` and `knowsOnlyItsYear` on `@graview/primitives`). `a-rule-compares-a-partial-date-by-the-span-it-covers.test.ts` and `a-calendar-draws-a-month-alone-across-its-month.test.tsx` hold each case.

The seat's own reading of a sentence keeps a year or a month as said: "Acme was founded in 2006" proposes `founded: "2006"` and "founded in May 2016" (or "sept. 2016", "2016-05", "5/2016") `2016-05`, for a date that may know only that much (`partial` on its form field), where every date had been filled with the day it was; a date that must be a day is still the day it is, and a whole day, an id or a number in the sentence is not read as a year. A record's facts say such a date back as "May 2016" or "2006", never "2016-05". `the-conversation-keeps-a-year-or-a-month-as-said.test.ts` holds it.

A home written as blocks marks each record it draws that a broken rule names, as the derived home's chips do: the kit's flag beside its name, said as implicated in a problem, its own card in the warning tone, and `data-graview-flagged` on the listed item; `SpecPlace` takes `flagged`, and the home's view passes what it is handed. `a-blocks-home-marks-a-record-that-breaks-a-rule.test.tsx` holds it.

A light scheme whose ground is dark has been drawn as a dark page since 0.1.56, but the light logo was still the one drawn there, so a dark mark made for paper vanished on it. `logoScheme(brand, scheme)` on `@graview/core` says which scheme's logo a scheme draws — the dark one in the dark scheme and on a dark light-ground — and `logoFor`, the theme's sheet and a static page's sheet hide the other by it, inverting a light logo that asked to be (`darkFallback`) there as in the dark scheme. `a-light-scheme-drawn-dark-draws-the-dark-logo.test.ts`, the logo case in `the-logo-switches-with-the-scheme.test.tsx` and `a-static-page-on-a-dark-light-ground-draws-the-dark-logo.test.ts` hold it.

The span a date covers, a rule comparing by it, and which scheme's logo a scheme draws cost a hosted page 0.8 KB more up front (857 bytes minified, 316 gzipped), and a page handed a compiled app 0.8 KB (835 / 313), under raises naming this changeset; the pages face alone 2_829 / 1_139 more, a visitor's form alone 1_997 / 827, a visitor's form handed a compiled app 840 / 290, every face 1_063 gzipped, the studio handed in 975 gzipped, the guest host 424 / 162 before a worker is drawn, 424 / 156 registering a view, 409 / 150 drawing a worker and 794 / 329 on the open kit, and Cloud's worker 1_214 bytes, each under a raise of its own; the scene face before it draws measured 1_062_625 B (1038 KB).

Compatibility: ops and primitives, stored formats, the wire, derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged. The declaration and check finding codes — no shape or code changes; a rule that compares a date holding a year or a month alone (which a document's date has accepted since 0.1.56) is now judged by the span the value covers rather than by its letters, so such a rule may hold or break where it did the opposite before, and `days()` over one is measured from the span's first day. The JavaScript API — additive: `daySpanOf` and `logoScheme` on `@graview/core`, `daysCovered` and `knowsOnlyItsYear` on `@graview/primitives`, `flagged` on `SpecPlace`.
