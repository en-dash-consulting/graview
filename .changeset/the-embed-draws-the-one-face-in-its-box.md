An embed draws the one face, held to its box. `mount` and `<Embed>` drew the routed face with its Scene/Pages switch, "Close the map", "Reset view", "From above" and the zoom unless the host said `sceneUnderPages: true`, because the one face stood against the window: its bar fixed at the window's top, the window scrolling its page. So every chapter on graview.dev's docs still showed two faces. Now an embed is the one face unless its host says `sceneUnderPages: false`, as `PagesApp` is, and where it stands is said by `routing`. Under `routing: "address"` the host's page is the app (Graview Cloud, a hosted app, `graview preview`), and the one face stands against the window as before. Under memory routing, an embed in a box of somebody else's page, it is held to the embed's element, `height` tall (`"auto"` holds it at `AUTO_SCENE_HEIGHT`). The root is the containing block of every layer (`contain: layout paint`, `data-graview-one-face-box`), so the bar stands at the box's top and Page, Nearby, Map and the panel are the box's rect. The page scrolls inside the box, nothing is drawn over the host's page, the host's page scrolls on when the pointer is outside it, and the level keys move the face only while the keyboard is in it. With no `path`, a `stop` names the page it opens at: `#overview=1` the Map, a record's focus its page, a kind's group its list, a place's view that place. `PageContext.inBox` on `@graview/pages` holds the one face to the element marked `data-graview-one-face-box` that holds it, and `onTheOneFace` no longer makes an embedded face the routed one. A face held to a box never scrolls the host's window when it moves to another page. `@graview/embed/pages` is still the routed face alone unless told `true`, and held to its box there.

graview.dev mounts every chapter, recipe and demo as the one face, and its docs no longer teach the switch: chapter 9 is "An ordinary web app", and "This site, as an app" says to rise to the map. `graview create`'s `src/embed.tsx` opens on the home with no stop. `graview preview` says `sceneUnderPages: false` unless it is opened with `?graview-one-face=1`, as before. Held by `the-one-face-is-mounted-whole-with-its-bar-in-the-slot`, and by `verify-embed-one-face` on graview.dev's docs page at a desk and on a phone: the bar is inside the box, no point of the window outside the box lands on the face, Map, Nearby and Page each rest, the box scrolls its own page, the host's page scrolls outside it, and there is no Scene/Pages switch.

Compatibility: what breaks for a host — `mount` and `<Embed>` now draw the one face unless they are told `sceneUnderPages: false`. There is no Scene/Pages switch, no scene or Graview face, and a `face` other than `"picture"` is drawn as the pages. Under memory routing the face is held to the element: give the element a height (or pass `height`). `sceneUnderPages: false` keeps the routed face, its switch and its faces exactly as before. `PageContext` takes `inBox`, and `onTheOneFace` ignores `embedded`. Both are additive for a `PagesApp` that owns its page. Ops and primitives, stored formats, the wire, the declaration and check finding codes, and derived tool names and input schemas are unchanged. What Graview Cloud does: pass `sceneUnderPages: false` to `mount` for every app it has not moved. An app it has moved needs nothing.
