A status board arranges its cards before it draws (the eleventh walk). A shelter whose animals stop at a status board, "The floor", had no Filter on the scene at all: narrowing the animals by weight could not be done there at either width, by pointer or keyboard, since the `columns` lens read its cards from the graph and took no arrangement, as the coverage grid did until the tenth walk. The board now draws the arranging line over its columns at full fidelity, filters and sorts the first bound kind's cards from the stop (`in.filter`, `in.sort`, `in.q`), counts what it keeps, and offers no grouping: its columns are the groups.

Compatibility: ops, stored formats, the wire, the declaration and derived tools are unchanged.
