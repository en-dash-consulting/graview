What people looked for and could not do is recorded, in the graph's own words (Roadmap f-friction-log). The op log keeps every change, an AI interaction its outcome and a declined suggestion its decline, but nothing kept what happened before a change or instead of one. Now `@graview/core/friction` holds four events — `search-miss` (the words, and the kind they were looked for in), `unanswered` (a question, and the place), `refused` (an act and its refusal code) and `tool-invalid` (a tool, its act, the sentence, and `then`: the names and types of the next call of that tool that worked) — each with `{ id, at, by, kind? }`, never a click or a selector. `frictionEvent` bounds a draft's text, checks its names and refuses a visitor; `frictionOf` counts across distinct people, never who, and leaves out words fewer than `minPeople` (2) people used; `ownFriction` gives a person their own; `frictionDigest` says to a model the words searched for that no kind or field is called, the questions left unanswered and the tool form that worked, with no refusal's sentence; `withinRetention` lets the old go; `toolMistakes` watches an agent's calls; `memoryFriction` keeps it in memory. A page records through one door on the main entry, `recordFriction(store, draft, by)` over whatever `attachFriction` gave the store, and a store nobody attached a sink to records nothing. `aiContextOf` takes `friction` events from its host and says their digest; core reads no storage of its own.

`serveStore` and `createStoreHandler` keep it at `/graview/friction`: `POST { event }` with the author the session's, as an act's is, never the body's — 201, 202 and nothing kept for a visitor, 400 for what is not one of the four, 413 over 2 KB, 429 with `Retry-After` past 30 a minute from one person (`frictionPerMinute`) — and `GET` the asker's own events and the counts (403 to a visitor). `friction` takes the host's `FrictionStore`, in memory unsaid; `false` keeps none and the route answers 410. `graview serve` keeps it in `friction.json` beside the store for 90 days (`fileFriction`, `frictionRetentionDays`; `--no-friction` keeps none), `browserFriction` keeps it in a page's storage for `openStore({ friction })`, and `remoteFriction` is the door's client. `openRemote` posts each draft (`friction: false` sends none, and a visitor's remote never does) and records an act the server refused where it takes it back. The routed face records words on `/search` that found nothing once they stand for 1.5 s, and a form's refused act with its code and kind; the quick ask records a question it had no answer to (not one AI was off or failed for); `graview mcp` records a tool call refused as `invalid` with the form of the next call of that tool that worked (`--no-friction`). The ship README says what is recorded, who sees it, how long it is kept and that a visitor never is.

Core's test bounds the text, refuses a visitor, counts one person searching ten times as one, gives a person their own, lets the old go and says the digest; ship's posts as one seat claiming another, reads as Ada only her own and the counts, refuses a visitor, holds the size and the rate, answers 410 where the host keeps none, records a refusal the server made where the client takes it back, and keeps it in a file and a browser's storage; pages' records a miss once it settles and not a visitor's, and a form's refusal; tools' records the quick ask's unanswered question and an MCP call's mistake with the form that worked. A hosted page carries 0.8 KB more up front (808 bytes, 403 gzipped; handed a compiled app 808 and 416), under a raise of each budget by that (`scripts/budget-raises/the-friction-log-hosted-page*.json`), and the scene face's limit before it draws is 1004 KB. The pages face alone carries 727 bytes more (294 gzipped) and a visitor's form handed a compiled app 960 (412), each under a raise of its own (`the-friction-log-pages-face-alone.json`, `the-friction-log-visitor-form-compiled.json`).

Compatibility: ops and primitives, the declaration and check finding codes, derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged. The wire gains `/graview/friction` (GET and POST); every other route answers as before. Stored formats: `graview serve` writes `friction.json` beside the store, outside the op log and every export; `openStore` takes an optional `friction`. Additive on `@graview/core` (`recordFriction`, `attachFriction`, `isVisitorAuthor`, `FrictionDraft`, `FrictionSink`), the new `@graview/core/friction`, `@graview/core/catalog` (`AiContextOptions.friction`, `AiContextState.friction`), `@graview/ship` (`frictionDoor`, `fileFriction`, `browserFriction`, `remoteFriction`, `FRICTION_PATH`, `frictionFileOf`, `RemoteOptions.friction`, the handler's `friction`, `frictionRetentionDays`, `frictionPerMinute`) and `@graview/tools/cli` (`settledAdapter`). What Graview Cloud does: hands its handler a `FrictionStore` over its own storage (or `false` for a workspace that turned it off), shows "What people couldn't find" from `frictionOf`, and passes `aiContextOf` the events for every connected chat's digest.
