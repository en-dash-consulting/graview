A draft view takes no title a titled view the host keeps already has, and keeping a lens under a title another lens holds says which lens holds it. A draft numbered its title after the app's places and lenses but not the host's views, so a draft could be named "Live threats" beside Threat Watch's own Live threats view and land on the same `/places/live-threats`. `DraftOptions` and the agent's `DraftTools` take `views` — the manifests the host keeps, as `placesOf` and `describePlace` take them — and a titled view's place is a title a draft does not take. And keeping a lens on an app with no document refused a taken title with a bare "there is already a lens called…"; it now names the lens that holds it, what it is over, and whether it is kept off the lists, as the document's own `add-lens` refusal does. A plural said mid-sentence there is lowered by its first letter only, so "CVEs" is never "cves".

Compatibility: additive in the API — `views` on `DraftOptions` and `DraftTools`. A refusal's words change; ops, stored formats, the wire and derived tools are unchanged.
