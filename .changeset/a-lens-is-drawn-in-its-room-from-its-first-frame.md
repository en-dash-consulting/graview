A lens is drawn in the room it has from its first frame. The width a lens chooses its shape by was read after the paint, of whatever box it held at mount, and a resize was rendered after the paint too: on graview.dev at 360 wide the coverage matrix in a 225-pixel card on the ground painted one frame laid out for a desk, its rotated column names 83 pixels past the screen, before it stacked. The width is now read before the first paint, of the box the lens draws now, and a resize the browser reports is drawn in the same paint.

Compatibility: no surface changes.
