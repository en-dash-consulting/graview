Every record says when it was made and last touched, and by whom, kept by the framework. A view's nodes carried no created or updated time and no last editor, no expression could read a record's last change, and an app on Graview Cloud kept a `lastTouched` date of its own, set by fifteen acts, which missed files, links, imports and tidying and read in UTC. Now every record keeps `createdAt`, `createdBy`, `updatedAt` and `updatedBy`. Every op that touches a record stamps `updatedAt` and `updatedBy` on it as it lands — a field, a file, a link at either end, an import, a host's own ops, the app's own tidying, an undo — and the op that adds one stamps all four; in the store, an undo of a removal puts the record back with when it was first made, and a document migration that changes nothing of a record touches nothing (a kind renamed keeps when each was made). `…At` is the op's time; `…By` is who acted, kept as an op's author is kept — the seat: the account's id (an agent acting for a person is that person; a profile is the account's and the profile's, `~` between; a seat with no id is its kind) — and said to a reader as the record of the people kind that seat is bound to, as an op's author is said, a binding since ended or moved included (a client that folded an op before its author was bound says the account until it is next served the state). No act or patch writes them, and a migration's op (a `system` author whose id ends in `migration`) leaves them as they were.

They are the record's own, so a snapshot, a checkpoint, the archive and an export of the installation carry them, and they are read wherever a field is: a view's nodes, `get_node` and `find`, a seat's view (kept as each seat may see them: a seat from whom nothing is kept is told them, said as the person a seat is now bound to; one from whom anything is kept is told none of them), a list (sorted, filtered and grouped by "Last changed" and "Added", by day on the app's clock: `?sort=updatedAt:desc`, `filter=updatedAt:on:2026-11-20`), a template (`{updatedAt | relative}`, `{updatedAt | date}`, in the app's `brand.timeZone`), and the rule language (rules, computed fields, a job's `where` and `sort`). A sight that keeps anything from a seat, a field or a kind, keeps all four from it, whatever the sight names. A record a visitor makes through a form says when, and not who. A record's own page and card say its fields and not the four, unless its glance or page asks for them. Nothing but the framework's own undo (which says none where the record it puts back said none), and a kind renamed by a migration, says when a record was made: an add from an act, a host, an import, a seed or a template is made by its op, and a seed or a template carries none of the four out of the installation it came from. A value a kind kept under one of the names is no stamp: a migration renames or clears it, and only an instant is read as a time. A store from before them opens with what its log holds: a record takes them from the ops that wrote it, its making only from the op that added it, and says none rather than a time made up. `graview check` refuses a kind that declares a field, relation or computed value of one of these names (`touched-field-taken`). On `@graview/core`: `CREATED_AT`, `CREATED_BY`, `UPDATED_AT`, `UPDATED_BY`, `TOUCHED_FIELDS`, `isTouchedField`, `touchedOf`, `toucherOf`, `untouched` (a record without the four), `Touched`, `toldTouches`, `offersTouches` and `markServed` (whether a seat, or a store as its reader holds it, is told them), `Graph.touch`, `Operation.stamps`, `Epoch.bound`, `arrangeable`'s `touched` and `unseatedSnapshot`'s `touched: "kept"`. On `@graview/core/check` as well as the main entry: `TOUCHED_FIELDS`, the four names, `CREATED_AT`, `CREATED_BY`, `UPDATED_AT` and `UPDATED_BY`, and `TOUCHED_FIELD_TAKEN`, the code a kind declaring one is refused with, so a host's release gate reads the framework's own list. A rule, a computed value or a job's `where` over the store's own graph reads `createdBy` and `updatedBy` as the record the seat is or was bound to, as a seat's view says them, and a template or a view spec's text says them as that record's label, or "someone", before any format; a kind's own label and describe line are worked out from the record alone, so there they say "someone". A store given no seat of its own, a host's, is offered them whatever its policy; a page's store over what its seat is served (`openRemote`, `markServed`) acts as that seat and is offered them as that seat is. An act through a seat's view (`store.seenBy(seat)`) that names no author is the seat's. For a seat told none of the four, a rule that reads them, in what it judges or what it says, itself or through a computed value of any kind, however reached, is not reported, in its violations or in an act's answer, and an act whose condition reads them says it cannot be judged for that seat; `graview check` warns of a rule's condition or words, an act's condition, a computed value or a job's `where` or `sort` that reads them in an app whose policy keeps anything from a seat, a role no sight names included (`touched-read-kept`). An export keeps who as the store keeps it, the seat that acted, as it keeps each op's author, and carries nothing of whom each seat was bound to; restored from its snapshot and log, its log alone or its checkpoint and log, a record says the same four. A list, a search and the agent's search offer and judge the four only to a seat told them. The `graview-view-specs` and `graview-lists` skills say how to read them. Held by unit tests of core (`every-record-says-when-it-was-made-and-last-touched-and-by-whom`, `a-records-times-are-kept-as-each-seat-may-see-them`, `a-records-four-are-said-as-the-person-and-found-as-served`, `a-records-four-are-judged-only-where-they-are-told`, `a-records-four-are-said-and-judged-alike-on-every-surface`) and of ship (`an-export-keeps-who-touched-a-record-as-the-store-does`, `a-page-store-is-offered-a-records-four-as-its-seat-is`).

Compatibility: stored formats — additive: a record holds the four as keys of its own, no format number moves, and a store stored before them opens with what its log still holds and no time made up; `snapshotHash` leaves them out, so a graph hashes as it did. Ops and primitives — additive: an op's own fields are unchanged and each fold stamps the four from its `at` and author, but what an op carries of a record does: a removal's payload, and an undo's that puts the record back, carry the four in stored logs, journals, archives and exports, and an op served to a seat may say `stamps: false`; a checkpoint may keep `bound`, whom each seat was bound to, never served to a seat. A build before them reads those payloads as keys no kind declares, and refuses one into a kind declared `.strict()`, so a client and its server take this version together. The wire — additive: records carry them as each seat may see them. The declaration and check finding codes — a new warning, `touched-read-kept`; breaking for a declaration with a kind that declares a field, relation or computed value named `createdAt`, `createdBy`, `updatedAt` or `updatedBy` (`touched-field-taken`); every other declaration checks as before, and a rule, template, view or job may name the four. Derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged. What Graview Cloud does: rename any app field of those names before it takes this version (an app's own `lastTouched` field and the acts that set it can go), read the four off a record wherever it showed its own, say `…By` through its directory (a seat's id or a person's record id), deploy its pages and its server together, and pass `touched: "kept"` to `unseatedSnapshot` only for an export it restores, which holds in `…By` the seat that acted, as its ops hold their authors.
