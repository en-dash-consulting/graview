What people tell Graview is kept, rated, seen in the Studio, and suggested back, Roadmap f-menu-learns. Nick: "let's also ensure that we're capturing what these things are, and the ability to thumbs up/down the response so build up that intel. that could also be stored in the studio like in each app so people could see what was training the system. and then we could use that stored prompt + action + optional result, then we could use jev to autosuggest based on similar stored actionresults." Each Enter or reply on the quick ask's card is now a telling (`Telling`, on a new entry, `@graview/core/told`): the words, what they were on (`target`: its kind and id, or the place), the records they were read as (`reading`: the record itself or the records it holds, by id), the people they named (`people`, resolved to record ids, pronouns included), what was decided (`decided`: an act over N records, a plan of N changes to the app, an open answer, or a refusal), which slot decided it (`slot`: `graph` for Jev over the graph, `light`, `full`) and its `stages` and total `ms`, how it ended (`outcome`: previewed, accepted — Yes, Take or Accept, on the card or on the tray once it is put away — declined, undone, or abandoned when the card closed with nothing done) and the person's `rating`, linked as one `conversation` with the telling it `follows`. Records are kept by id and kind, never their content; the person's own words are kept (300 characters at most). It is named for what the card asks — "Tell Graview…" — and for the Studio's own words, "What Graview has been told": a telling, not a "prompt" (a model's word for it) nor an "interaction" (`AiInteraction`, which keeps the model calls; a telling points at its one).

A host keeps tellings and hands the page its door as `HostAi.told` (a `ToldStore`: `add`, `change`, `remove`, `list`), mirroring the friction log: `memoryTold()` for a page alone, `toldOver(url)` for a served one, which posts to `/graview/told` and never makes the page wait. `graview serve` keeps them for a year in `told.json` beside the store (`fileTold`, `toldFileOf`; `--no-told` keeps none, and the route answers 410), and `createStoreHandler` takes `told` (`ToldStore | false`; unsaid, memory). Its author is the session's, never the body's; a visitor's is never kept (202) and a visitor reads nothing (403); its teller changes how it ended, its rating and what it should have done, whoever keeps the app (`keepsTheApp`) pins, excludes and deletes any (403 otherwise), and a telling deleted is gone. `GET` serves an owner every telling, anybody else their own and the others' that worked in a lesson (an act over a kind, or a plan on one) two other people's agree on (`toldFor`), and either only those about records the asker sees (`toldSeenBy`): a telling naming a record kept from the reader — its target, its reading or a person — is hidden from them whole. The dev server keeps them too: `devTold()` from `@graview/ship/dev` answers `/__graview/told` and writes `.generated/told.json`, the todo example draws it, and `aiThroughDevServer` hands the page `told` where it answers. `checkToldHost(host, { people, seen, hidden? })` on `@graview/core/conformance` runs the rules against a host's door as each seat and names what failed; `graview serve`'s door passes it.

👍 and 👎 sit quietly at the foot of every answered card where the host keeps tellings, reachable from the keyboard and named "This was right" and "This was wrong"; a rating changes while the card is open, and a 👎 offers one optional line, "What should it have done?", kept with the telling. Undoing an accepted change says `undone`, a quiet sign, never a 👎 (`AiInteraction.undone`, which the ghost's Undo now records). With no host keeping tellings no thumb is drawn: a press that keeps nothing would say something untrue.

The Studio lists them: "What Graview has been told", a button on its bar where the host keeps tellings, opens a panel of every telling its owner may see — the words, what it did, how it ended, its rating and what it should have done, who, when and how long it took — filtered by rating, outcome and kind; from one an owner marks it a good example (ranked first among like ones), excludes it from suggestions, or deletes it, and the line at its top says what is teaching the system: "Suggestions come from 23 rated tellings in this app."

And Jev suggests from what worked. As the menu is typed in, up to two kept tellings that were accepted or 👍 and not excluded are offered, ranked by how alike their words are, the kind they were on or over and the act (`similarTellings`: local and cheap, no model), each said as what it did re-aimed at what the menu is on — "Mark the 6 tasks in This week done, like last time" (`reaimed`) — and re-ranked with the menu's rows in the same Fast-slot Choice at the same 250 ms rest. Choosing one asks its words on the card, previewed and taken with Yes like any plan; nothing is applied from the menu, and the telling it makes names the one it came from. On Enter the top three like 👍 tellings are given to Jev's decision over the graph (`workedBefore`) and to the light model (`worked_before`) as examples, by name — `QuickAsk.examples`, `tellingExamples` — and the telling says which were used (`examples`). Another person's is suggested only once two others' agree, the rule `@graview/core/learn` holds, and never one about a record the viewer cannot see.

`what-people-tell-graview-is-kept.test.ts`, `what-people-tell-graview-is-kept-beside-the-store.test.ts`, `a-telling-hears-how-it-ended.test.ts` and `what-worked-before-is-re-aimed-at-what-the-menu-is-on.test.ts` hold it, and a new harness, `verify-told`, drives it with a fake host AI on the todo example and on a document mounted as Cloud mounts one, whose sight keeps the people from a member (aTellingIsKeptWithWhatItDidAndHowItEnded, aThumbsDownAsksWhatItShouldHaveDoneAndKeepsIt, theStudioShowsWhatGraviewHasBeenToldAndAnOwnerCanExcludeOne, aSimilarTellingThatWorkedIsSuggestedAndStillPreviewed, anotherPersonsTellingIsNotSuggestedUntilEnoughAgree, aViewerNeverSeesATellingAboutARecordTheyCannotSee). `docs/stability.md` says the shape, the wire and the check; the graview-desk skill says it.

A hosted page carries nothing more up front: the card's telling and its thumbs are the card's, the suggestions the menu's, the panel the Studio's, and `@graview/core/told` is fetched with whichever comes first. Every face carries 24_646 / 9_000 bytes more (`scripts/budget-raises/what-people-tell-graview-every-face.json`, 9 KB gzipped), and the embed with the studio handed in 5_107 gzipped bytes more (`what-people-tell-graview-studio-handed-in.json`, 5.1 KB), each under a raise of its own.

Compatibility: ops and primitives — unchanged. Stored formats — `told.json` beside the store is new (`{ tellings: Telling[] }`), and an `AiInteraction` may carry `undone`, an optional key a build before this one ignores. The wire — `/graview/told` is a new route (GET, POST; PATCH and DELETE at `/graview/told/<id>`), answering 410 where the host keeps none; nothing else changes. The declaration and check finding codes — unchanged. Derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged. The JavaScript API — `@graview/core/told` is a new entry; `HostAi.told`, `QuickAsk.examples` and `QuickAskOpening.suggested` are new optional keys; `checkToldHost` is new on `/conformance`; `toldDoor`, `fileTold`, `toldFileOf`, `TOLD_PATH` on `@graview/ship` and `devTold` on `/dev` are new. The look: the answer card draws `[data-testid="quick-ask-rating"]` with `quick-ask-right`, `quick-ask-wrong` and `quick-ask-should`, the menu `[data-told-suggestion]` rows in `[data-menu-section="told"]`, and the Studio `[data-testid="told-open"]` and `[data-testid="told-panel"]`. What Graview Cloud does: keeps tellings per app behind its own `/graview/told` (or hands each page a `ToldStore` of its own as `ai.told`), author from the session, read back under the reader's sight and the two-people rule, owners pinning, excluding and deleting, a deleted telling gone for good — running `checkToldHost` against it in its tests; shows the Studio's "What Graview has been told" by handing the Studio the same `ai.told`; and respects deletion by removing a telling everywhere it is kept.
