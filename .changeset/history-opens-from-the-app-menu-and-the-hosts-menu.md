The app's history opens from the app menu and from the host's own menu (Graview HQ f-host-opens-history). People could not find an app's previous versions: the timeline was reached only from a row of Activity, by "As it was". The app menu, the chevron beside the app's name on the bar, now has one plain row, History, wherever the face keeps a playback, whether or not anything has happened yet. It opens that same timeline at now — a tick per change, the player, "Restore to here", "Back to now" — on the scene and the routed face alike, puts the menu away and gives the keyboard to that app's own scrubber, unless the person has gone on elsewhere by the time it is drawn. An app with no changes yet opens a timeline that says "No changes yet". Under a host's own bar that says the app's name (`bar: { name: false }`) the app menu offers no History, and draws no chevron for it alone: the host offers it from its own menu. `bar: { history: false }` leaves it out of the app menu anywhere.

A host opens the same timeline from its own menu with `showHistory()` on the handle `mount` returns, from `@graview/embed` and from `@graview/embed/pages`. It is that mount's alone: another mount over the same store is not opened. It answers whether a timeline will draw: `false` on the picture, which draws none, and after `unmount`, when it opens nothing then or on a later mount; called before the face has drawn, it answers `false` and the face opens it as it arrives, unless it draws no timeline or another app is swapped in first. The keyboard goes on to the scrubber where nothing else holds it, as a host's menu put away leaves it. Underneath, each mount makes a door (`createHistoryDoor`, handed down as `HistoryDoorContext` from `@graview/react/provider`) that the face listens on where it draws the timeline (`PastBar`), so a studio's provider or a picture is never opened unseen, and `Playback.show` takes `{ focus: true }` for a timeline that should take the keyboard as it is drawn. No address opens it: the timeline is a layer over whichever page is shown, not a page, and a path of its own would take a name a kind may have. The graview-host-page skill names the method.

Tests: `the-history-opens-from-the-app-menu-and-from-the-hosts-menu` (embed) presses History in the app menu and finds the timeline at now with the keyboard on its scrubber, finds History before anything has happened with its timeline saying "No changes yet", gives the keyboard to its own app's scrubber with another app's timeline open and leaves it where the person took it meanwhile, and opens the timeline with `showHistory()` on the routed face, on the scene as it is mounted and on the pages alone, never after `unmount` on the mount over the same store and element that took its place, and on only the mount asked where two draw one store; says a picture draws none and opens none when the host goes on to the pages; and offers no History under a host's bar with `name: false` (no chevron) or `history: false`, where `showHistory()` still opens it and gives the keyboard to the scrubber. `the-history-opens-under-an-open-studio-as-the-apps-own` (embed) opens the app's timeline outside an open studio, whose map still offers its acts. `a-mounts-history-door-opens-only-its-own-faces` (react) holds the door: a request kept until a timeline arrives, dropped when the face drew none or another app was swapped in, and nothing after it is closed. verify-playback holds `historyInTheAppMenuOpensTheTimelineAtNowOnBothFaces` (the menu's control pressed with Enter, Tab walked to the row named "History", Enter, on both faces) and `historyIsInTheAppMenuBeforeAnythingHasHappenedAndSaysNoChangesYet`.

A hosted page's first load grows by 0.8 KB up front (0.8 KB handed a compiled app). Every face grows by 2.1 KB, the pages face alone by 0.9 KB, the embed with the studio handed in by 0.4 KB gzipped, and a visitor's form handed a compiled app and the guest host registering a worker view by a few bytes, under a raise of their budgets; the scene face before it draws is held to 1113 KB.

Compatibility: ops and primitives — unchanged. Stored formats — unchanged. The wire — unchanged. The declaration and check finding codes — unchanged. Derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged. The JavaScript API gains `showHistory()` on `EmbedHandle` and `PagesEmbedHandle`, `createHistoryDoor`, `HistoryDoorContext` and the `HistoryDoor` type on `@graview/react` and `@graview/react/provider`, `history?: false` on `EmbedBar`, and the optional `focusWanted` and `focused()` and a `{ focus }` option on `Playback.show`; nothing is taken away. The app menu's control is now drawn wherever the face keeps a playback and the bar says the app's name, also on a bar that had no faces, host rows or scheme to put in it.

What Graview Cloud does: a History row in its own menu, if it wants one, calls `handle.showHistory()`.
