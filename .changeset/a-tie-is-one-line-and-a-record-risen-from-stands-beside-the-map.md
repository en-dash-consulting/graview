A tie on the ground is one line, and a record risen from stands beside the map. With a record in focus, its card over a band of chips under captions ("their songs", "their releases"), each line from the card ran center to center and was clipped wherever it passed a chip or a caption: dashed stubs hanging off the card, stubs with a dot just short of each chip, gaps where it went behind the words. A tie from the card now leaves the card's edge facing the band, drops through the open ground, crosses any row through a gap between its chips and its caption's words, runs along a gutter and arrives on the chip's own edge, in its relation's manner from the Key: one path, crossing nothing. A caption hung over its run is of that run's row, so a road goes round its words, and comes in at the chip's side where the words cover its whole top. Where the card already names the record among its own chips, the card has said the relation, so the tie is drawn for the record a person points at or tabs to, on the card or in the band, or chooses, and not for all of them at rest. A road between two chips of the band, and a tie from a chosen chip to a district on the shelf, take the same gutters from the chip itself rather than from its mention in the card above. A chip is measured as the chip, not the slot it was allotted.

From altitude, a record in focus — risen from by Up or by ctrl+wheel (a trackpad's pinch), zoomed out further with −, reached by Back or by `#focus=<record>&overview=1` — is the record chosen: its card is no longer stamped over the city at a seventeenth of its size; it stands in the panel beside the map (a sheet on a phone) with its name, its acts, its fields (or the kind's own card where the app drew one) and its links, its building stands lit in its district and in sight, and its links are drawn from it whole, clipped only by the districts' names rather than by the plots they stand on. Closing the panel leaves the record, at altitude; "Close the map" returns to the ground with its card. Without a panel beside the map the record still stands in the city's middle. A chosen record is always drawn as a building of its own, ahead of the "+504" of its row. `verify-lines` asks both of the discography's artist at a desk and a phone: every line on the ground is one path whose ends lie on drawings and no stretch of which lies behind or across a chip, a card or a caption, at rest and pointed at; and after each way up the record is in `[data-graview-map-panel]`, nothing of it is drawn over the city and its building is on the map.
