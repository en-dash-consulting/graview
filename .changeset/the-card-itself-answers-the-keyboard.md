The card itself answers the keyboard. A view host is a tab stop, and its key handler returned unless the key had landed on one of the view's own pick targets — so Enter and Space on a focused card did nothing. On a blank app that is the whole interface: one district, whose selection opens the actions strip, where the only act lives. Enter on a host now does what a click does, and Enter again on a card already selected alone does what the second click does.
