The one face's lines at Nearby read as what they join, from Graview Cloud's test of the lines from framed views on its own Deep Time. A line left the page from its edge, level with the part it was for, so a page of pills had a comb of lines along its side and none on the pills; a period's page of twelve species drew twelve lines into one district; a line to a building behind a board was cut by the board; and the home at Nearby fitted the whole city beside the page, which on a phone it cannot, leaving the districts its own views show behind the sheet.

Now each line starts on its own pill, card or row, on the edge that faces the city, and runs from there to the page's edge and on into the city. Four or more lines into one district are one line: each part's own short line meets the others at the page's edge, level with the middle of them, and one line goes on into the district with how many it carries on its marker, which opens that district's list ("12 species here: open the Species list"). The boards stand under the lines, still over the page. At Nearby on the home, where the camera cannot stand back far enough to take in the whole city beside the page, the districts the home's views show are what it frames there.

Tests: `verify-frame-lines` holds that at Nearby each line starts on its own card and never on the page's edge, that a feature's page of eight requests draws one line into the requests' district with 8 on it, each pill's own line meeting it at one point, and that where the city's side cannot hold everything every line ends in that side or points toward its end from the page's edge. `verify-one-face` holds, on Deep Time at every size, that every district the home's views show stands on the city's side at Nearby and that the boards stand under the lines, and reads a phone's city side as the part above the sheet.

Compatibility: the look — changed on the one face only (`sceneUnderPages`): lines start on their parts, many into one district are one line with a count, the boards stand under the lines, and the home's Nearby on a small window frames its views' districts.
