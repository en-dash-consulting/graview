The city is said drawn when it has drawn, not at the ten seconds. The one face's city writes its own attributes every frame, most of them as they were, and the embed's `data-graview-drawn` took each such write for a move, so a place over the city was said drawn only at its ten-second cap: about 13.6 seconds from the page's start with the face held back three and a half, and a picture of the city waited all of it. `data-graview-painted` already took an attribute written again as it was for no change; `data-graview-drawn` now does too, and the city writes the count on a gathered line's marker only where it changed (text set again as it was is a node taken out and another put in), so the city is said drawn a moment after its last real change (about a second and a half after the held part came, in verify-drawn). Held by `the-page-says-when-a-place-has-drawn` and verify-drawn's `theCityIsSaidDrawnSoonAfterItHasComeNotAtTheTenSeconds`.
