A page opens on the snapshot and a short tail of the log. Measured on Graview Cloud on 2026-10-20, on a phone, the Beatles demo's `/graview/state` was 6.0 MB, 4.9 MB of it the op log (1,267 ops): every op ever made rode into every open, though the page draws the snapshot and reaches the log only for its recent history and its undo. `liveProtocol({ openTail })`, and `createStoreHandler`'s, `serveStore`'s and `servePreview`'s `openTail`, cut what an open carries, `GET /graview/state` and the welcome of a socket that has nothing, to at most that many of the latest ops, said with the `horizon` they begin at, while the host keeps its whole log for its own undo, export and history. The page's store opens as it does on a store compacted behind an undo horizon: it draws the snapshot, undoes back to the horizon and says so past it, and `/graview/since` and a socket's catching up are unchanged. A field written before the tail is no false conflict: `FieldRevisions.of(ops, { horizon })` puts a field no op of a tail wrote at the seq before the horizon, which is what the page claims to have seen, and a claim is stale now only when the field was written after the revision it names (it was any revision but the field's own), so a server holding the whole log takes the claim as seen, and a server reading its own compacted log holds no claim of a field behind its horizon stale. `ship`'s test opens a page on three ops of twelve, renames a record made long before them with no conflict, undoes its own rename and is refused one behind the horizon, and is still refused a rename over somebody else's since. A hosted page carries 0.2 KB more up front for it, handed a compiled app or not (`scripts/budget-raises/a-page-opens-on-a-short-tail-*.json`).

Compatibility: ops, stored formats and the declaration are unchanged. On the wire, a host that sets `openTail` serves a state whose `log` begins at its `horizon`, a shape the wire has carried since compaction came (FR-23 in the changelog); with none, every answer is as before. Additive on `liveProtocol`, `createStoreHandler`, `serveStore` and `servePreview` (`openTail`) and on `FieldRevisions.of` (`{ horizon }`). `FieldRevisions.stale` now calls a claim stale only when the field's revision is later than the claim, where before any other revision was.
