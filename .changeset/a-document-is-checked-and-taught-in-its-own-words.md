A document is checked, described and taught in its own words. `graview check` refuses a rule's repair that is an act on another kind than the rule judges — a rule over tasks offering an act on modules hands that act the task, and it could only refuse — as `repair-wrong-kind`, naming both kinds and the act, with the acts on the judged kind as the fix; it is asked of every rule written in the rule language (a document's rules and `expressionRule`), whose repairs are handed the record they judged, and not of a rule that is a function, which names its repairs' arguments itself. Every finding `graview check --document` hands back is located by its JSON path — `acts.file.connects`, `rules.no-orphan-task.repairs.1`, `kinds.task.glance` — and its sentence and fix name the document's places, never `defineMutation(...)` or `defineNode(...)`. Kinds whose hues sit within 12° on the color wheel are one answer for `graview describe` and `graview check`, measured round the wheel: describe says so as before, and check now asks it out loud as the note `brand-hues-crowded`. The `AGENTS.md` that `graview create` writes, and the `graview-new-app`, `graview-node-kind`, `graview-invariant` and `graview-seed` skills, teach the document path beside the TypeScript one: `app.json` as a graview-document, its schema at `node_modules/@graview/core/dist/document.schema.json`, `graview check --document`, `graview describe --document` and `graview preview app.json --seed seed.json`.

Compatibility: the declaration and check finding codes — `repair-wrong-kind` is a new error, so a document or an `expressionRule` that checked clean with a repair on another kind now fails `graview check` and a host's `compileDocument` refuses it (no example app or fixture relied on one); `brand-hues-crowded` is a new note, never a failure. A finding's `where` for a repair names its place in the list: `defineInvariant("x").repairs[0]` (was `.repairs`). A document finding from the checker carries a JSON path where it carried the TypeScript call, and its message and fix are reworded to match. Ops, stored formats, the wire, the document format and tool names and schemas are unchanged.
