A worker view's links are real links. An `<a data-record>` or `<a data-place>` was followed on a press but was no link to the browser: no address on hover, nothing in a new tab on Cmd- or Ctrl-click, nothing to copy — the host gave it a `role="link"` and a `tabindex` in place of an `href`. Now on the routed pages the host gives each the address the face goes to as its `href`, with the face's base, filter and section (`/a/<app>/threats/threat%3A7`, `/places/by-day?filter=day%3Afri`), opened in this tab: a plain click, a tap and Enter are followed in the app as before, and a click with Cmd, Ctrl, Shift or Alt, or a middle click, is the browser's — a new tab, a new window — and never also taken by the face around the view. With an address, the link needs no role or tab stop of the host's, and they are taken back; on the scene, which has no addresses, a link keeps them. The view still never writes an app's address itself: an `href` it gives is a link out, judged as before, and an `<a>` with one is left as the open kit drew it. A link out alone says it opens a new tab. `GoTo` (`@graview/react/provider`) takes an optional `href(to, also)`, the address going there arrives at, and the routed face's gives it (`/a/<app>` joined on, as a listed record's link does); a host that mounts a worker view itself gives `hrefOf` on `mountWorkerView`, or none.

`guest`'s tests give a record link and a place link their addresses with no role, tab stop or target, follow a plain click and leave Cmd, Ctrl, Shift and middle clicks unprevented, unfollowed and unheard by the face, give no address to a link that goes nowhere or on a face with none, and put the open kit's target back when a link stops naming a record. `pages`' tests read the routed face's `href` for a record, a list narrowed and to a section, and a lens under `basename="/a/hall"`, the address the face then arrives at, and none for what it does not have. In the taps transport, in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox, Threat Watch's `<a data-record>` carries `/threats/threat%3Aphishing`, a Cmd- or Ctrl-click opens a new tab there and leaves the app on its home, and a plain click opens the threat's page at that address. `graview-view-links` says so. The guest host drawing a worker view on the open kit carries 4.1 KB more for the three changes together (4_094 bytes, 1_381 gzipped), and the guest host before a worker is drawn 207 bytes more (36 gzipped), under raises of those budgets (`scripts/budget-raises/a-views-keyboard-and-real-links-open-kit.json`, `…-guest-host.json`); nothing a hosted page loads up front grew.

Compatibility: additive for a host — `GoTo.href` and `mountWorkerView`'s `hrefOf` are optional, and a host that gives neither draws links as before; on the routed pages a view's `<a data-record>` and `<a data-place>` now carry an `href` and no `role` or `tabindex`. The wire, the stored formats, the declaration and the derived tools are unchanged.
