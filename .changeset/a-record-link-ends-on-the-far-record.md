A chosen record's link line ends on the far record, or on its district, and nowhere else. A task opened on the map shows the list it is on as a chip inside its own card; the line took that chip for the far end and ran from the card to itself, bowing out of the card's lower edge to end on empty ground. The chips inside a chosen record's own card are no longer a far end, so the line reaches the list's building (or, if the list is not drawn on its own, runs dashed into its district with a count). `verify-lines` now asks it of a task chosen from altitude: every record-link line's end lies on a building or a district's ground outside the chosen card.

Compatibility: no surface changes.
