A store is judged on its host's day. A rule about a day reads `today` from its context and keeps no clock of its own; the browser apps handed one, and `graview mcp`, `graview apply` and `graview serve` opened their stores with none — an agent at a tool library's door saw nothing overdue where the pages listed nine loans. `openStore` now judges on the host's own day, read when asked, unless the host pins one (`invariantOptions.today` or `context.today`). (W-186, the eighth walk.)

Compatibility: ops, the wire, the document format, the compiled format, check finding codes and tool names and schemas are unchanged. Stored formats are unchanged; a store `openStore` opens without a day is judged on the host's clock, where it was judged on none. What a host does: nothing, unless it wants another day — then it pins one.
