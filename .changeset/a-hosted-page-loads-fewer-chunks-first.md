A hosted page loads thirteen chunks before it draws where it loaded twenty-three, and 7.4 KB less (4.8 KB gzipped), so the round's repeating dates, money formats and field-level sight have room. A bundler splits what a page loads first by which of its doors (each part fetched with `import()`) reach each file, so the frame's files were spread over twenty-three chunks, each with its own list of names in and out and each compressed alone. Every door in `@graview/core`, `@graview/react` and `@graview/primitives` now imports the barrel the page drew first (`@graview/core`, `@graview/react/provider`, `@graview/primitives/frame`) for nothing but that, which makes the files every door reaches one set and costs a door nothing at run time. What draws a notice is fetched with the first notice and prefetched once the page is idle (`notice-stacks.tsx`); the board and the two live regions that say each notice aloud stay up front, so a notice is said at once, and `fetchNotices()` fetches it at will (the embed's `preload` does). What only fetched faces say or do left the barrels a page imports: `edgeWords` and `valueWords` for `@graview/core/words`, and a face's moves (`withShown`, `withQuery`, `withoutSearch`, `withPast`, `withPan`, `withPin`, `edgeSelectionId`) for a new `@graview/layout/view-moves`. Built as Cloud builds it over the vendors document, the page that compiles its document goes from 543 734 bytes (189 877 gzipped) to 536 125 (184 911), and the page handed a compiled app from 501 895 (175 137) to 494 284 (170 165). Their budgets fall to keep about 8 KB of that as room (4 KB gzipped): 531.5 KB (184.5 gzipped) from 538.0 (188.0), and 490.5 KB (170.0 gzipped) from 497.5 (173.5). No face loses anything: the pages face alone is 3.4 KB less, and every face fetched together 3.7 KB less.

Compatibility: BREAKING imports. `edgeWords` and `valueWords` move from `@graview/core` to `@graview/core/words`; `withShown`, `withQuery`, `withoutSearch`, `withPast`, `withPan`, `withPin` and `edgeSelectionId` move from `@graview/layout` and `@graview/layout/view` to `@graview/layout/view-moves`; `FOOT_MOVED`, `FOOT_OBSTACLES`, `NARROW_PICTURE`, `placeAtTheFoot` and `placeAtTheTop` leave `@graview/primitives/frame` (still on `@graview/primitives` and `@graview/primitives/pages`). `withoutMoves` stays on `@graview/layout/view`. New: `@graview/layout/view-moves`, and `fetchNotices` from `@graview/primitives`, `/frame` and `/pages`. Ops, stored formats, the document format, the compiled format, check finding codes and derived tool names and input schemas are unchanged. What Graview Cloud does: import those names from their new entries; its shell built from these sources loads about 7 KB less before the app draws.
