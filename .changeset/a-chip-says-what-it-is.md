A fact on a chip says what it is. `readableFields` gives every field an `alone` reading — a word as itself, a number with its label ("Track 8", "Length 4:27"), a yes/no as "Explicit: yes" — and the default summary card, the pages' gallery and the list lines all use it. A song's card used to read "8 · 4:27 · Yes".
