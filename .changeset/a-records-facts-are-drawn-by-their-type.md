A record's facts are drawn and changed by their type, Roadmap feature:f-facts-by-type (from Nick, on a task's page in the todo example: "The facts" drew Finished as a text box reading "No", the list it is on as a full-width slab, the empty due date not at all, and What can be done as a wall of buttons that each repeated a fact). The record page's facts are now one sheet — labels in a column on the left, each value or its control on the right at a desk, each label over its value on a phone, nothing boxed but a chip — and each value is its type: a yes or no is a switch (`role="switch"`) that runs the act that changes it, whether that act takes the value or is the act that says yes ("Mark it done") or no ("Put it back"); a day or a moment is said with its weekday and how far it is ("Fri, Sep 4 · in 3 days"), in the warning color only where a problem with the record asks for that field again, and changed by the date editor on a press; a choice of up to four short options is those options side by side, the chosen one pressed, one press to change, and of more a menu that opens at the first press; a value worked out from others is read and quietly marked "worked out"; an empty field this seat may fill says how ("Pick a day", "Choose…", "Add notes"), a blank yes or no is a switch that is off, and a required one left blank says "Needed". A change the store refuses is said beside its value in the act's own words, and a number past its range is said so ("Length is at most 180; it keeps 25 min.") rather than stopped in silence by the box. The scene's record card draws the same values, through the same `EditableValue`.

Each relation is a row of the sheet: its records as chips that open them, one held alone (`cardinality: "one"`, or set by an act that also severs it, a move) changed from its chip's own picker ("Someday ▾" opens the other lists, with a search past seven), several added to by a quiet press in the act's own words ("This has to happen first", "Add a presenter") and taken from by each chip's ×; a relation it has none of yet that an act here could give it is drawn with that press. Each runs the act the declaration says ties or unties it. The heading is the name and renames in place whichever field holds it (`titleFieldOf`: a talk's `title` as well as a task's `label`), and is not said again among the facts. "In the scene" and "Notify me when this changes" are quiet presses beside each other under the name.

An act whose whole effect is one fact is done there and leaves What can be done: one that writes a single drawn field and asks nothing but its value (or nothing), or ties or unties a single drawn relation with the one record it asks for — decided from what each act declares it writes, connects, severs and asks, never by its name, and only where the face changes values in place. What can be done keeps the rest ("Block time for it", "Give it a slot") as a quiet list, one under another; what takes the record away still waits behind "Show N more" unless nothing else is left. New on `@graview/pages`: `FactSheet` (what the derived page, the todo example's own design and the page `graview create` writes all draw), `relationFacts`, `foldedActs`, `actsBeyondFacts` and `titleFieldOf`. Tests: `a-value-is-drawn-and-changed-by-its-type`, `an-act-a-fact-already-does-is-folded-into-it`, and `verify-facts` in three engines, light and dark, at a desk and a phone.

What a hosted page loads up front is unchanged (the relation picker is drawn with the sheet: a door of its own split a chunk a hosted page loads first). The scene face before it draws is 1_117_595 B, 5_750 more than main (the limit is 1092 KB, from 1086 KB), and the budget raise files ask the pages face alone for 17.6 KB minified and 5.1 KB gzipped more, every face for 5.2 KB gzipped, and the embed with the studio handed in for 5.2 KB gzipped.

Compatibility: ops and primitives — unchanged. Stored formats — unchanged. The wire — unchanged. The declaration and check finding codes — unchanged. Derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged. The look moves: a record page's facts are `section[data-testid="record-fields"][data-graview-fact-sheet]` holding `dl[data-graview-sheet]` rows, each `[data-graview-fact="<field>"]` (a blank one still `[data-graview-blank-fact]`), and its relations are rows of that sheet, `[data-graview-relation="<edge>"][data-graview-direction]` with `[data-graview-chip="<id>"]`, `[data-graview-relation-set]`, `[data-graview-relation-remove]` and the picker `[data-graview-relation-picker]` (`[data-graview-candidate]`) — the one section per relation with its own `h2` and eyebrow is gone, and `related-all` is in its row; a yes or no is `[role="switch"][data-graview-switch]`, a choice of a few `[role="group"][data-graview-choices]` with `button[data-graview-option][aria-pressed]` and of many a `select[data-graview-field]`, where each was a `button[data-graview-field]`; an empty slot's words say what it does ("Add allocation", "Pick a day") where it said "add" and was named "Add Allocation"; a refusal is `[data-graview-refused]`; `[data-graview-worked-out]` and `[data-graview-needed]` are new; What can be done is a vertical `role="toolbar"` of plain presses where it was a row of outline buttons, holding no act a fact's control does; "Notify me when this changes" is a quiet press in `[data-graview-record-asides]` beside "In the scene". The scaffold's record page draws `FactSheet` and `actsBeyondFacts`.

What Graview Cloud does: nothing, for an app drawn by the framework's record page — its pages and its card draw the sheet with the next version; a harness of its own that pressed a fact-shaped act under What can be done ("Mark it done", "Rename", "Move it to …") presses the fact's control instead, and one that read a relation's `h2` reads its row's `dt`.
