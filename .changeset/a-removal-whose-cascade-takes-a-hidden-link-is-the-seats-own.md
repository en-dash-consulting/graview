A removal whose cascade takes a link to a record its seat may not see is still the seat's own. Removing a room that a hidden hold pointed at took the hold's link with it, the link named the hold, and the seat was served its own op withheld — "A change you cannot see" — and could not undo it, which told it a hidden link had been there. Now a link the seat is not served is no part of an op that did anything else: it is left out of the op's primitives and of its inverse, and the op is judged without it. So the seat is served its removal whole, exactly as it would be with no such link; it may undo it, the undo puts the hidden link back and is served to it the same way, and so is the redo, or somebody else's undo of it. An op that did nothing but such links is withheld as before. `docs/stability.md` now says so, and names the three things a hidden record still changes for an act, as known limits: a create asking for an id a hidden record holds is refused "Id … is already taken", a minted id steps past a hidden record's (`room:green-2`), and an act that sets a single-source link replaces a hidden record's link and is served withheld. The hosted page loads 32 bytes less up front (589,069), and 26 less handed a compiled app (546,768).

Compatibility: ops, stored formats, wire messages, the document format, the compiled format, check finding codes and tool names and schemas are unchanged. What a seat is served changes: an op whose only hidden part is a link the seat is not served arrives whole, with that link left out of its primitives and inverse, where it arrived withheld. Its log still folds to exactly the snapshot it is served.
