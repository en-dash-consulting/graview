The check warns when an act reads a kind some role allowed to run it may not see (FR-105). The store hides what a seat may not see and refuses a call naming a hidden record as one naming nothing, but an act's own logic is not hidden: a condition that counts records its caller may not see, a refusal worded from one, an effect that copies from one can each tell a seat that such a record exists. `graview check` now names each such act, role and kind with `act-reads-hidden-kind`, a warning, and says where the act reads it: a document's act is read for its condition (`allowedWhen`), its refusal and the values its sets and effects work out, following sweeps (`all('memo')`), walks (`out('notes')`, `in(…)`, a walk from every member of a set) and relations by name to the kinds they reach. A TypeScript act's `apply` is not read; an act declares the kinds it reads with the new optional `reads` (`reads: ["memo"]`), and an act that asks for a record of a kind its runner may not see is told that runner can never give one. A role that sees a kind only where it is its own is not named. Nothing changes at run time. `docs/stability.md` says the limit and the warning, and records FR-99, FR-100 and FR-101's additions to `graview-document@1`. The `graview-permissions` skill says what the warning means and how to answer it. `capabilities().shipped` names FR-105.

Compatibility: additive to the check finding codes: `act-reads-hidden-kind` is a new warning, so a declaration that passed still passes. `MutationDefinitionSpec` gains an optional `reads`; a mutation without it is judged as before. The wire: `capabilities().shipped` gains `FR-105`. Ops, stored formats, the document format and tool schemas are unchanged.
