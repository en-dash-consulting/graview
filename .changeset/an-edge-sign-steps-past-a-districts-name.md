A sign at the edge of the ground steps past a district's name, as it steps past the corner Up and the key stand in. On graview.dev's landing page at 600 wide the sign for Decision records, past the right edge, stepped down below the corner and stood on "MODULES 16 ⚠ 10", so a press on the problem count landed on the sign. The city's frame now says where each district's name stands (`CityFrame.nameplates`, boxes from the top left of the district's card, beside `showings`), and a sign walks past each box it would stand on, one way along its edge and then the other. A hosted page carries 0.1 KB more up front for it, filed as a raise of its own.

Compatibility: no surface changes; `CityFrame` gains the optional `nameplates`.
