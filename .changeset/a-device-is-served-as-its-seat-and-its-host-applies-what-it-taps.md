A device's screen is drawn as the seat it is paired at, its host applies what it taps, and the screen can say how a tap went (Roadmap f-display-framework-follow-ups). `@graview/pages/device`'s `resolveTap` no longer applies an act: a tap on an act the screen still shows answers `{ ok: true, does: "act", act, args, label, said, place, page }`, the call the host applies under its own seat (`store.apply({ name: act, args }, { author, via: "device", intent: label })`), with `said` the words for once it is done, and a refusal of that apply is the host's to say. Nothing else about judging a tap changes: a press the screen no longer offers, or one tapped on a picture of a state that has moved on, is refused in words as before. `renderScreen` loads `@graview/core/publishing`, as `renderPlace` does, so a screen paired at a reader of an app that publishes is drawn the last publish rather than failing for want of it, and holds what that seat's sight shows; a device can be paired at a seat below the owner's. A new `notice` option says one line over the head of the screen, in the app name's place ("Marked done", a refusal's words): it moves no press, and it is part of the picture and its `renderId`, so a tap on a screen drawn with one resolves as on that screen.

Compatibility: the look — unchanged without `notice`; with one, a black band over the app's name at the top of the head. The JavaScript API — changed: `resolveTap` applies nothing, and its `does: "act"` answer carries `act`, `args` and `label` for the host to apply; a host that relied on `resolveTap` to apply the act must now apply it itself. `RenderScreenOptions` gains `notice`. Ops and primitives — unchanged; an act tapped on a device is an op the host writes, with the `via` and intent it gives. Stored formats — unchanged. The wire — unchanged. The declaration and check finding codes — unchanged. Derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged.
