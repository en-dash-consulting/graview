The inbox opens on what needs you (Roadmap f-inbox-needs-you-first). "Kinda confusing and hard to find what I need": the bell's badge read 4, "Inbox — 2 waiting for you, 4 changes", and the 4 was Activity, which asks nothing of anyone; it could open on Activity with suggestions waiting; For you was one undivided list; a broken rule was a separate orange dot by the avatar saying `quote = —`; and a person who kept saying No to a suggestion had no way to stop that kind of thing.

The bell counts only what needs this person: suggestions and proposals they may decide or vote on, the rules broken that they could fix, and every host notice marked as needing them, read or not, so the bell and the list agree. Its name says so, "Inbox — 3 need you: 1 rule broken". An unread notice that asks nothing draws the bell's dot, with no count, and its name says "Inbox — 1 new update". Activity's count is on its own tab. It opens on For you whenever something there needs them, wherever it was left, and holds the tab once it has opened.

For you is two parts, each with a one-line empty state: **Needs you** (suggestions grouped by record, proposals, broken rules, host notices marked as needing you) and **Updates** (the rest: other notices, what was done while you were away, a suggestion or a rule this seat cannot answer, and what you said not to suggest). Answering a row takes the keyboard to the next row, whichever buttons it has.

A broken rule stands under Needs you in words, "Crumbs Bakery is booked but has no quote", with its fix by its own label. A fix that needs nothing more is made at a press, and one refused is said in words; one that asks for something, and the sentence itself, open the acts on the record. The `Standing` dot says only that all is well, and is not drawn while a rule is broken: a custom shell that draws `Standing` alone, with no `Inbox` beside it, says nothing while a rule is broken, and should draw `Inbox`, as the launcher now does. A standalone `PagesApp` keeps its link to the problems, with no badge. A scene's skip link, a seat's "what is wrong" and a first look's `problems` anchor go to the bell, on For you.

Activity says what it means: "undo +1" reads "Undo 2 changes", the history icon reads "As it was", and a template's example batch, named `examples` or `examples:<template>:<stamp>`, reads "Example records from the template" with "Remove examples", which takes back that batch and nothing else. No client may name either id.

"Don't suggest this kind of thing" stands beside No, on what the app's own tidying actor suggests, for whoever may decide every suggestion of that kind now waiting. `stopSuggesting(store, id)` on `@graview/core/tidying` declines the suggestion and its kind (`suggestionKind`: a link by its relation, an edit by its act and the fields it writes, anything else by its act, whatever it is about). Whatever else the actor has waiting of that kind leaves every tray, a suggestion of several only when all its calls are stopped, and `suggest` and `dream` refuse the actor that kind from now on (`stoppedKind`). A declared workflow and a chat agent are told nothing of it: a person asked the actor to stop suggesting, and did not turn off their own workflow. Its words say the kind and name no record, "Stopped suggesting changes to the note of tasks". It is a row of Activity with its Undo, and `stoppedSuggestions(store, principal?)` lists what stands, which Updates offers with "Suggest again" to whoever may lift it. A checkpoint keeps its batch, its words and its kinds (`KeptStop`), so it can still be listed and lifted after its op went to the archive, by an ordinary undo of its batch. A person's proposal of that kind still waits.

The store judges a stop wherever it comes from: an undo saying `lineage.stopsKind` from a seat that may not decide is refused, on the socket as anywhere, and so is taking one back.

An undo whose target is withheld from a seat is now withheld from it too. Until now one was not when the target touched no record, as a suggestion does not, so a seat kept from a record could read "Undo: Changed the name of …" in the log of anyone's decline of a suggestion about it. That had been so since declines began, and this closes it for declines and stops alike.

The unit tests `a-person-stops-a-kind-of-suggestion`, `a-stop-is-held-to-who-may-say-it-and-what-it-says`, `stopping-a-kind-leaves-workflows-and-agents-alone`, `a-stop-is-judged-on-the-wire` and `the-inbox-opens-on-what-needs-you` hold each claim; the `chrome` harness holds them in a browser on both faces as `theBellCountsWhatNeedsYouAndNotWhatHasHappened`, `theInboxOpensOnForYouWhereverItWasLeft`, `forYouIsNeedsYouAndUpdates`, `aBrokenRuleIsSaidInWordsWithItsFixAndNoDotOfItsOwn` and `aKindOfSuggestionIsStoppedRememberedAndTakenBack`.

Compatibility: the look — the bell's count and name change as above, an unread host notice not marked `needs` is no longer counted on it and draws its dot, `[data-testid="standing"]` and its `problems` popover are gone while a rule is broken (the rows are `[data-testid="problems"]` under Needs you), and `Standing` no longer takes a press; a standalone `PagesApp`'s `standing-link` has no `standing-count`. The JavaScript API — new, optional: `HostNotification.needs`; new on `@graview/core/tidying`: `stopSuggesting`, `stoppedSuggestions`, `stoppedKind`, `stoppedIn`, `suggestionKind`, `suggestionKindWords`, `mayStopSuggesting`, `isTidyingActor`, the type `KeptStop`; `keptDeclines` takes the ops and meanings it needs to keep a stop; `inboxWords` on `@graview/primitives` now takes the count, the rules' words and the new updates; the `problems` popover name is gone from `POPOVERS`; a dream's outcome may be `stopped`. Ops and primitives — unchanged in shape: an undo that declines a suggestion may carry `lineage.stopsKind: true`, and one that lifts a stop behind a checkpoint `lineage.liftedStop: true` with an `undoes` naming the archived op, each of which an older build keeps as an unknown lineage key. A seat is now served an undo withheld where its target is withheld. Stored formats — `Epoch.declines` may hold a kind's key, `∀` and the kind, and its entries an optional `stop`, additive within format 1, ignored by an older build, which forgets the stop if it compacts again (`docs/stability.md`). The wire — unchanged: a stop is an undo with its intent and lineage, and lifting one is an undo of its batch; such an undo from a seat that may not decide is now refused. A page served by a host is not told what a checkpoint keeps, so "Suggest again" lists a stop there only while its op is in the log's tail. The declaration and check finding codes — unchanged. Derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged.

Up front, a hosted page's scene face before it draws grows 6.0 KB (1_131_562 to 1_137_663 B, minified), since Activity's digest brings `@graview/core/tidying` with it; the hosted page's own budget and the pages face hold. Cloud's worker grows 7.5 KB.

What Graview Cloud does: mark its notifications that ask something of a person, proposals to review and failed workflows, with `needs: true`, for they stand under Updates and are not counted until it does; its example batch, `examples`, already reads as such. To offer a stop behind a checkpoint to a person, list `stoppedSuggestions(store, principal)` on the server, where the checkpoint is, and lift one by an undo of its batch. Load `@graview/core/tidying` wherever a store takes undos from clients, as `@graview/ship` does, or a stop is refused.
