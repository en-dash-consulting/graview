The inbox opens on what needs you (Roadmap f-inbox-needs-you-first). "Kinda confusing and hard to find what I need": the bell's badge read 4, "Inbox — 2 waiting for you, 4 changes", and the 4 was Activity, which asks nothing of anyone; it could open on Activity with suggestions waiting; For you was one undivided list; a broken rule was a separate orange dot by the avatar saying `quote = —`; and a person who kept saying No to a suggestion had no way to stop that kind of thing.

The bell counts only what needs this person: suggestions and proposals they may decide or vote on, the rules broken that they could fix, and every host notice marked as needing them, read or not, so the bell and the list agree. Its name says so, "Inbox — 3 need you: 1 rule broken". An unread notice that asks nothing draws the bell's dot, with no count, and its name says "Inbox — 1 new update". Activity's count is on its own tab. It opens on For you whenever something there needs them, wherever it was left, and holds the tab once it has opened.

For you is two parts, each with a one-line empty state: **Needs you** (suggestions grouped by record, proposals, broken rules, host notices marked as needing you) and **Updates** (the rest: other notices, what was done while you were away, a suggestion or a rule this seat cannot answer, and what you said not to suggest). Answering a row takes the keyboard to the next row, whichever buttons it has.

A broken rule stands under Needs you in words, "Crumbs Bakery is booked but has no quote", with its fix by its own label. A fix that needs nothing more is made at a press, and one refused is said in words; one that asks for something, and the sentence itself, open the acts on the record. The `Standing` dot says only that all is well, and is not drawn while a rule is broken: a custom shell that draws `Standing` alone, with no `Inbox` beside it, says nothing while a rule is broken, and should draw `Inbox`, as the launcher now does. A standalone `PagesApp` keeps its link to the problems, with no badge. A scene's skip link, a seat's "what is wrong" and a first look's `problems` anchor go to the bell, on For you.

Activity says what it means: "undo +1" reads "Undo 2 changes", the history icon reads "As it was", and a template's example batch, named `examples` or `examples:<template>:<stamp>`, reads "Example records from the template" with "Remove examples", which takes back that batch and nothing else. No client may name either id.

"Don't suggest this kind of thing" stands beside No, on what the app's own tidying actor suggests (an automated author marked `tidying: true`, as `TIDYING_ACTOR` is), for a seat whose grants reach that kind on any record, and every suggestion of it now waiting. `stopSuggesting(store, id)` on `@graview/core/tidying` declines the suggestion and its kind (`suggestionKind`: a link by its relation, an edit by its act and the fields it writes, anything else by its act, whatever it is about). Whatever else the actor has waiting of that kind leaves every tray, a suggestion of several only when all its calls are stopped, and `suggest` and `dream` refuse the actor that kind from now on (`stoppedKind`). The store refuses the actor making that kind on its own too, in a change with any call of it. A declared workflow, an importer and a chat agent are told nothing of it: a person asked the actor to stop suggesting, and did not turn off their own workflow. A suggestion with a field the stop does not name is still asked about. Its words say the kind and name no record, "Stopped suggesting changes to the note of tasks". It is a row of Activity with its Undo, and `stoppedSuggestions(store, principal?)` lists what stands, which Updates offers with "Suggest again" to whoever may lift it. A checkpoint keeps its batch, its words and its kinds (`KeptStop`), so it can still be listed and lifted after its op went to the archive, by an ordinary undo of its batch. A person's proposal of that kind still waits.

The store judges a stop wherever it comes from: an undo saying `lineage.stopsKind` from a seat that may not decide is refused, on the socket as anywhere, and so is taking one back; `liftedStop` is the store's alone. A served page holds a tail of the log, so the state and the welcome say what it may no longer hold (`stops`, `stoppedKinds`), for its tray, its bell and its way back, and the way back stands behind a checkpoint on a local store too. The bell brings its own look, and Activity's digest now comes from `@graview/core/history`, so `@graview/core/tidying` loads with the tray.

The unit tests `a-person-stops-a-kind-of-suggestion`, `a-stop-is-held-to-who-may-say-it-and-what-it-says`, `stopping-a-kind-leaves-workflows-and-agents-alone`, `a-stop-is-judged-on-the-wire` and `the-inbox-opens-on-what-needs-you` hold each claim; the `desk` harness holds the launcher's bell as `theDeskDrawsTheBellWithItsOwnLook`, and the `chrome` harness holds the rest in a browser on both faces as `theBellCountsWhatNeedsYouAndNotWhatHasHappened`, `theInboxOpensOnForYouWhereverItWasLeft`, `forYouIsNeedsYouAndUpdates`, `aBrokenRuleIsSaidInWordsWithItsFixAndNoDotOfItsOwn` and `aKindOfSuggestionIsStoppedRememberedAndTakenBack`.

Compatibility: the look — the bell's count and name change as above, an unread host notice not marked `needs` is no longer counted on it and draws its dot, `[data-testid="standing"]` and its `problems` popover are gone while a rule is broken (the rows are `[data-testid="problems"]` under Needs you), and `Standing` no longer takes a press; a standalone `PagesApp`'s `standing-link` has no `standing-count`. The JavaScript API — new, optional: `HostNotification.needs`; new on `@graview/core/tidying`: `stopSuggesting`, `stoppedSuggestions`, `stoppedKind`, `stoppedIn`, `suggestionKind`, `suggestionKindWords`, `mayStopSuggesting`, `isTidyingActor`, `servedStops`, the types `KeptStop` and `ServedStops`; `Author.tidying`; `Store.stopsServed`; `suggestionsMayWait` on the main entry; `digestWords`, `foldDigests` and `typedRowWords` on `@graview/core/history` as well; `keptDeclines` takes the ops and meanings it needs to keep a stop; `inboxWords` on `@graview/primitives` now takes the count, the rules' words and the new updates; the `problems` popover name is gone from `POPOVERS`; a dream's outcome may be `stopped`. Ops and primitives — unchanged in shape: an undo that declines a suggestion may carry `lineage.stopsKind: true`, and one that lifts a stop behind a checkpoint `lineage.liftedStop: true` with an `undoes` naming the archived op, each of which an older build keeps as an unknown lineage key. A seat is now served an undo withheld where its target is withheld. Stored formats — `Epoch.declines` may hold a kind's key, `∀` and the kind, and its entries an optional `stop`, additive within format 1, ignored by an older build, which forgets the stop if it compacts again (`docs/stability.md`). The wire — additive: `stops` and `stoppedKinds` on the state, the welcome and a `/graview/since` answer that brings ops, absent where there are none; a stop is an undo with its intent and lineage, and lifting one is an undo of its batch, and either from a seat that may not decide is refused, as is `lineage.liftedStop` from anyone. The declaration and check finding codes — unchanged. Derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged.

A hosted page grows 0.2 KB up front, minified, for the few lines the store asks about a stop; it takes 2.6 KB off the scene face before it draws, since `@graview/core/tidying` now loads with the tray. With the rest of its batch the scene face measures 1_133_632 B, minified. Cloud's worker grows 17 KB.

What Graview Cloud does: mark its notifications that ask something of a person, proposals to review and failed workflows, with `needs: true`, for they stand under Updates and are not counted until it does; its example batch, `examples`, already reads as such. Mark the author Cloud dreams and suggests as with `tidying: true`, as `TIDYING_ACTOR` is, or a stop does not bind it and "Don't suggest this kind of thing" is not offered on what it suggests; a `cloud:triage` or an importer stays unmarked. A suggestion kept before this version by an unmarked actor stays unstoppable: it has no such button, and marking the actor reaches only what it suggests from then on. Load `@graview/core/tidying` wherever a store takes undos from clients, as `@graview/ship` does, or a stop is refused.
