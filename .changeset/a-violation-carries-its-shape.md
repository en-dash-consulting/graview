A broken rule says its values to a host that reads only its message (FR-159). 0.1.20's notes said a rule shows its shape everywhere. That was true of the framework's own surfaces and not of a host's: `Violation.line` was set only where a surface called `withLines` from `@graview/core/lines`, and a violation's `message` was still the rule's title. Graview Cloud's connector hands a chat the message, and authors had shortened their titles to let the shape speak, so since 0.1.20 a chat read "A wedding planner on Small: Margin above target" with no values. Now, once `@graview/core/lines` is loaded, the engine gives every judgment its line: `evaluate`, so `store.violations()`, `preview`'s `violationsAfter` and every surface over them carry `line` on each broken rule written in the rule language, and a rule that wrote no sentence of its own (`says`) says the line in its `message` after its title: "A wedding planner on Small: Margin above target — margin 31% < target 40%". A rule's own sentence is left as its author wrote it, its line beside it, and a rule that is a function is as it was. `@graview/ship`'s store handler (`createStoreHandler`, `serveStore`, `graview serve`) loads the words when it is made, and a seat's tools (`createToolRuntime`, so `graview mcp` and a connector's runtime) fetch them before `get_violations` or `get_node` answers, so those answer the values in the message, as an act's `introduces` and `resolves` do from then on, still without the parts a page draws the line with. A page still carries none of the words up front: it fetches them when it first draws a problem, as before, and its judgments carry lines from then on; the engine's part is 68 bytes, measured on the hosted page at 593 446 bytes up front (593 378 before, budget 593 613) and 547 843 handed a compiled app (547 775 before, budget 548 045), and the door the seat's tools fetch the words through 88 more before either face draws, with no budget raised. An act that changes a broken rule's values and leaves it broken neither resolves nor introduces it: a violation with a line is the rule and its record (`violationKey`). `capabilities().shipped` gains FR-159.

Compatibility: ops, stored formats, wire messages, the document format, the compiled format, check finding codes and tool names and schemas are unchanged; `capabilities().shipped` gains FR-159. Changed: the `message` of a broken rule written in the rule language with no `says` gains " — " and its line once `@graview/core/lines` is loaded, which a store served by `@graview/ship` and a seat's tools now always have; a host that matched such a message by its exact words matches its start. New: `judgeWithLines()` on `@graview/core/lines`. What a host with its own problems surface does: nothing, where it judges through a store `@graview/ship` serves, through a seat's tools, or on a page that has drawn a problem. A host that judges on a server with none of those, in a bundle that may drop an import nothing names, calls `judgeWithLines()` from `@graview/core/lines` once at start; `withLines(store, violations)` still adds the line, and the values in the message, to violations judged before.
