An embed does not say a landmark's name twice. It prefixes every landmark inside it with its own label, and a picture whose panel bore the same name — "The pipeline", in an embed labelled "The pipeline" — became a region called "The pipeline · The pipeline". A region of the embed's own name is the embed's region, so it is named once and becomes a group rather than a second region of that name.
