Every key typed into the pages' Find box arrives. The box wrote each keystroke to the address and took the address back, and an address that caught up after the next key was taken for a change and written over the box — typed at full speed, "pay the deposit now" became "pyte depoi now", and on a slow runner "digital" was searched as "dgtl". Words the box handed to the address are its own echo; it follows the address only when something else changed it.
