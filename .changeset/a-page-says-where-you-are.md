A page says where you are. Nick, from building a product face with its own shell over the pages: "people got caught in the derived pages and didn't know where they were". On a step's page nothing said "Plan › Steps › Consolidate", no tab lit, and the app rebuilt a trail by matching `pluralSlug` against the path and reading the record's label. Each derived list, record and picture now opens with a trail: a `nav` called "You are here", the section the kind's pages are in, the kind's list as a link, and the page itself last with `aria-current="page"`, said to a screen reader and kept out of sight because the heading under it says it. It stands on one line at a phone's width, a long step cut short rather than wrapped, and its hrefs carry the face's base. It takes the place of the record page's kind link above the name; a list with no section has nothing before its heading and draws none. `registry.section(kinds, label, { path })` names the section ("Plan" over change plans and their steps; without a `path` the trail says it and links nowhere) and `registry.sectionOf(kind)` reads it back. A design that draws its own shell asks `usePlaceHere()`: `{ as: "home" | "list" | "record" | "place" | "page", kind, id, label, section, trail: [{ label, href, path, current }] }`, to light its own tab and draw its own title. A bar outside the face's router, told the path by `steering` or `onNavigate`, reads `placeHere(context, path, { registry, base })`. A shell that places `<PageTrail />` holds THE trail, and the derived pages leave theirs to it; `surface("shell", Shell, { without: ["trail"] })` goes without. Seedbed's garden puts plots and plantings in "The garden", and its own plot page places `<PageTrail />` where its eyebrow was. The product-design rung of `graview-pages` is a skill of its own now, `graview-pages-design`, which teaches the section, the hook and the trail. A hosted page carries nothing more up front; the pages face before it draws is about 3 KB more, and the pages face alone, every face and the embed with the studio handed in are raised by what they measured (3_330 / 1_219, 3_288 / 1_195 and 3_299 / 1_207). `verify-pages` holds that a record's page says where it is, its trail's link goes to the kind's list under `/pages`, and a planting's page says "The garden › Plantings", at 390 and 1440, in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox.

Compatibility: ops, stored formats, the wire, the declaration and derived tools are unchanged. Additive on the pages' API (`PageTrail`, `usePlaceHere`, `placeHere`, `PlaceHere`, `PlaceStep`, `PageSection`, `registry.section`, `registry.sectionOf`) and on `FaceControl` (`"trail"`). On the markup, the record page's kind link above its name is now the list step of `nav[aria-label="You are here"]` (`[data-graview-trail]`), and a list or a picture in a named section opens with one.
