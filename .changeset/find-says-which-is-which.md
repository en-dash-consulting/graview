Find says which is which. A song and an album both called "Gone Digital" were two rows that read "Gone Digital" in the Find strip, the album first and nothing in either row saying which was the song: hits were told apart only within their own kind, on the grounds that the strip heads each kind — but a row is read alone, by the pointer that lands on it and by the screen reader that speaks it. Same-named hits of different kinds now say their noun ("Gone Digital · song", "· album"), in the declaration's own word (`nounOf`: a staff member, never "staff"), and same-named hits of one kind still say the fact that differs. `search` takes `inKind`, the kind the person is in, and its records lead the others of the same strength; the scene passes the district or record it is focused on, so "Gone Digital" typed inside the songs is the song.
