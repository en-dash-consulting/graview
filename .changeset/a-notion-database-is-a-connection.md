A Notion database can be kept in step with an app, declared as a connection the way a Google calendar is (Roadmap f-notion-connection). `connections.<name>` with `"system": "notion"` names one `database` (its 32-character id, or `{ "env": "TASKS_DATABASE" }`) and one `kind`, and `properties` maps a field to a property by its name in Notion (`"stage": "Stage"`), or as `{ "name", "type" }` so the checker judges the type; without `properties`, each field finds the property of its name, ignoring case and punctuation, and the kind's name field is the database's title. Auth is an internal integration's secret: `graview serve` and `graview preview` read it from `NOTION_TOKEN` (`<auth.env>_TOKEN`, or the `auth.file` the declaration names), and a host hands it through `runConnections`' `token` (the request carries the declaration, so a host can tell a Notion connection's from a Google one's) and the database's id through the new `database` seam. The secret is never written in the document, the op log, the sync state or an export, and `graview check` refuses one written anywhere in a connection (`connection-secret`).

The transport is `notion({ databaseId, token, … })` on `@graview/core/sync`. A pull asks for the pages edited since the last `last_edited_time` it saw, oldest first, following `next_cursor` to the end, and reads the whole database when it has no cursor and every ten minutes, when a page it had linked and no longer finds — archived, in the trash, moved out — is gone here through `remove-<kind>`. A push makes a page for a record made here, sets the properties of one edited here, and archives the page of one removed here. Title and rich text, number, select and status (an `enum` field, its option matched to Notion's ignoring case and punctuation, so `in-progress` is "In progress"), multi-select (a `list`), date (a `date` field holds the day, a `datetime` field the instant in UTC), checkbox, URL, email and phone go both ways; people are read as their names and never written; relations, formulas, rollups and files are left alone and said once (`left alone in Notion: Related (relation: …)`). Words are written as plain text in Notion's 2000-character pieces, a pair of surrogates never split. A version is the page's `last_edited_time` with a hash of its values, so our own write coming back is an echo and somebody's edit in the same minute (Notion keeps the time to the minute) is still news; a field both sides changed is held as the engine holds any. Notion's 429 is waited out as its `Retry-After` says, up to a minute a pass; a refused secret is said by the name it is kept under ("Notion refused NOTION_TOKEN (HTTP 401) …"), and a database the integration cannot see as one to share with it. A `SyncReport` now carries `problem`, what the remote said when it could not be reached, and a served store's line says it: `board: could not reach Notion (notion: …); will try again`.

`scriptedNotion()` is a Notion in memory — retrieve, make and archive a database, query one, make, read, change and archive a page, with 401, 404, 400 and `limit(n)`'s 429 as Notion answers them — which core's and ship's tests run against: every property type, a round trip both ways, every page of a 205-page query, our write not echoed, a conflict held, an archive each way, the rate limit, a refused secret, an unshared database, long words, and a whole read that loses and duplicates nothing. `scripts/verify-notion.mjs` runs the same claims against a real workspace (credentials in `~/.graview-notion-test.env`, never printed; not in `pnpm verify` or CI), making its own database inside a page shared with the integration and archiving it at the end, and `--stub` runs them against the scripted Notion. The graview-sync skill and the core and ship READMEs say how to declare one. Relations between two synced databases are not kept in step yet. The transport and the scripted Notion stay on `@graview/core/sync`, which no page reaches; the checker's judgment of a Notion connection rides with the studio, so every face carries 5,635 bytes more minified (1,686 gzipped), under a raise of its budget (`scripts/budget-raises/a-notion-database-is-a-connection-every-face.json`), and a hosted page's first load stays within its budget.

Compatibility: ops and primitives, the wire and derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged. Stored formats — unchanged: a `SyncState` is format 1 as before, and a Notion connection's `cursor` is its own JSON inside it. The declaration and check finding codes — additive: `connections.<name>` may say `"system": "notion"`, which a build that predates it refuses by path, and the checker gains `connection-properties` (error), `connection-read-only` (warning) and `connection-secret` (error, said only of a connection that holds a secret). `ConnectionRun.calendarId` is optional now, beside `databaseId`. What Graview Cloud does: keeps each workspace's Notion integration secret and the database's id with its other credentials and hands them to `runConnections` through `token` (answering by `request.declaration.system`) and `database`; says `report.problem` where it says a connection could not reach its calendar; and offers `system: "notion"` only on a build that has it.
