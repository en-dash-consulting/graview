A connection refused is said to the person filling the form, with the ways out (FR-229), Roadmap f-connection-refusal-in-plain-words. A calendar connected to a kind whose make-act needs a field the event cannot give was refused as `an event made in the calendar becomes a block through an act that makes one from the event's fields alone, and add-block asks for title, type…` — true, and the framework's own words to someone filling a form. Now `connection-creates` says "Blocks need a Type, and Google events don't have one." (the kind's plural and the field's label, several fields listed together; a kind only ever made on another record is said as such) and its `fix` the three ways in one sentence: "Map a calendar field to Type, give Type a default, or sync to a kind made for events." The finding carries `fixes`, the ways out a host offers as buttons, each `{ label, edits? }` with the edits that do it where one can be written — "Give Type a default" (`set-default`, an enum's first option or a yes-or-no's no), "Let Type be left blank" (`set-required`), "Map a calendar field to Notes" (`set-connection`, the event's description or location mapped to a words field), "Sync to event instead" for each kind already there with a title and two dates an act makes from alone, and "Add a kind made for events" (`add-kind` with a title, a start, an end and a place, then `set-connection` pointing at it) — and the framework's sentence as `details`, for an agent and the CLI. A Notion connection's refusal says the same of its pages, with the default, the blank and the property fixes. Every written fix, applied with `editDocument`, leaves a document the connection passes on (`a-connection-refused-says-why-in-plain-words.test.ts`). `set-connection` is a new edit (`{ name, connection }` sets or replaces `connections.<name>` whole, judged for its shape as it is written and for its kind, fields and acts by the checker; `null` takes the connection away), and `every-edit-says-what-it-did` holds it. `graview check` prints `details:` and each `fix: <label>` with its edits beneath the sentence, in both its voices, and `checkApp`'s findings carry the two keys through `compileDocument`. The `graview-sync` skill says what the refusal says and the fixes it offers. `capabilities().shipped` gains FR-229. A hosted page carries nothing more up front for the two new keys (`error` and `warning` share one builder now: 21 bytes fewer minified, 8 more gzipped); one handed a compiled app, which says its findings, carries 0.2 KB more up front for the lines beneath a sentence (194 bytes minified, 91 gzipped), under a raise naming this changeset; every face carries 4_119 / 1_399 more (the studio's editor now judges a connection's shape), under a raise of its own.
