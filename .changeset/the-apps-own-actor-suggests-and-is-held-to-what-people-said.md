The app's own actor suggests what it is not sure of, is held to what people said, and its run of changes is one row of Activity. A background actor that links and prunes records on its own ("Graview, tidying") could apply a change with its lineage (FR-172) and nothing else: what it was unsure of had nowhere to wait, a person could not say "leave this one alone", an app could not say "never auto-link Vendors", and a pass of thirty links buried a person's own changes in Activity. An author may now say `automated: true` (a `system` or `agent` author: the app's own actor; `isAutomated`).

A suggestion that waits is `suggest(store, calls, { author, lineage, via?, intent? })`, on `@graview/core/tidying`: the calls are judged as `applyAll` would judge them for that author, on a rehearsal, and none of it is applied — the log keeps one op that `suggests` them, with no primitives and its lineage, so it persists, travels and is withheld from a seat exactly as any op is. The same suggestion asked again answers the one that waits, and one that would change nothing is not suggested. `suggestions(store, principal?)` lists what waits, under the seat's sight (or the seat whose view `store` is); `acceptSuggestion(store, id, options)` applies the calls as the person, carrying the suggestion's lineage with `lineage.suggestion` naming it; `declineSuggestion(store, id, options)` is an undo of the suggestion, which keeps its lineage as `undoneLineage`. `declined(store, calls)` answers the op that declined these calls, or any one of them — a declined suggestion, or an actor's confident change a person took back — and the store refuses the app's own actor the same change or suggestion again, in a sentence ("Graview, tidying may not make this again: a person declined it on 2026-10-14."). The log is where suggestions live, rather than a seam a host fills, because accepting and declining are then a `call` and an `undo` like any other, and a host persists nothing new. Both faces show what waits in a quiet Suggested tray beside Activity on the bar — what it would do, who suggested it, its basis and how sure, Accept and Decline — fetched, with what reads which suggestions wait, only once the log holds one, so a hosted page carries up front only what holds the actor to what people said (what the 0.1.30 wave adds up front together is said in `.changeset/the-0-1-30-wave-leaves-the-past-and-the-suggestions-off-the-first-load.md`).

Leave this one alone: every kind is given `leave-alone-<kind>` ("Leave this one alone") and `allow-tidying-<kind>` ("Allow tidying again"), ordinary undoable acts that set and clear `$leftAlone` on the record as archive sets `$archived`, offered on the record's page and the scene's record card beside archive, and said at the head of the record's page and in its card. `isLeftAlone(store, id)` reads it. The store refuses the app's own actor any change that touches a record left alone — a patch, a removal, a link at either end — and a suggestion about one, naming the record. A declaration says what is never tidied: `tidying: "never" | "no-links"` on `defineNode` (a document's kind) and `tidying: "never"` on a relation; the actor is refused a change to a kind never tidied, a link to or from a kind never linked, and a link of a relation never tidied, each in a sentence, and may still suggest them for a person to decide. The document edit `set-tidying` (`{ kind, relation?, tidying: "never" | "no-links" | "allowed" }`) says it in a sentence, `diffDocuments` says the same sentence, and `graview check` refuses a grant naming a leave-alone act of a kind never tidied (`grant-act`) and warns of a relation that says what its kind already says (`tidying-redundant`).

A digest in Activity: consecutive batches by one automated author fold into one row, "Graview, tidying Tidied 6 things today", which opens to each change with its own lineage and its own undo; a person's change and a chat seat's never fold, and a suggestion and its declining are the tray's, not rows. The agent's tools gain `list_suggestions` (the actor's basis handed over as somebody else's words), `accept_suggestion` and `decline_suggestion`. `@graview/core/tidying` makes, settles and reads it all for a host and a face: `suggest`, `suggestions`, `acceptSuggestion`, `declineSuggestion`, `declined`, `suggestionsIn`, `declinesIn`, `declinedIn`, `suggestionKey`, `tidyingOf`, `tidyingRefusal`, `foldDigests`, `digestWords`, `isSuggestionBatch` and `LEFT_ALONE`. The embed's budgets for the pages face alone, every face and the studio handed in are raised by what they grew (`scripts/budget-raises/the-apps-own-actor-*.json`). The graview-lists skill teaches it.

Compatibility: ops gain two optional fields, `suggests` (on an op with no primitives, which folds to nothing) and, on a lineage, `suggestion`; authors gain the optional `automated`; a record may carry `$leftAlone: true`, written by an ordinary `patch-node` — additive within op format 1, and a log folds to the same graph on an earlier version. The wire is unchanged: accepting is a `call` whose lineage names the suggestion, declining an `undo`. The document format gains the optional `kinds.<kind>.tidying` and `kinds.<kind>.edges.<relation>.tidying`, the edit `set-tidying`, and the check finding `tidying-redundant` (a warning); `grant-act` also names a leave-alone act of a kind never tidied. Derived tools: every kind gains `leave-alone-<kind>` and `allow-tidying-<kind>` (`{ id }`) unless it is never tidied, and the agent's surface gains `list_suggestions`, `accept_suggestion` and `decline_suggestion` (`{ suggestion }`). New APIs: `isLeftAlone`, `isAutomated` and the type `Suggestion` on `@graview/core`, and `@graview/core/tidying` with `suggest(store, calls, options: SuggestOptions): Operation | undefined`, `suggestions(store, principal?): readonly Suggestion[]`, `acceptSuggestion(store, id, options?): ApplyResult`, `declineSuggestion(store, id, options?): ApplyResult`, `declined(store, calls): Operation | undefined` and `SuggestOptions`. Stored formats are unchanged.
