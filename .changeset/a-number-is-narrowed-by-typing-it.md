A number is narrowed by typing it. The Filter list offered a number only as the round steps its records happened to make, a heading opened and walked down, and nothing at all where every record held the same number. Typed into the list's own box, "35" offers "at most 35 min" and "at least 35 min" first, one key away, and a number with no steps is still found that way.

Compatibility: no surface changes.
