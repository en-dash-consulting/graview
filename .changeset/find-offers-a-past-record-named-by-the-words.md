Find offers a past record the words name when no current record is named by them, only mentions them (the eleventh walk). An animal shelter with 62 animals in its care and 3,000 gone home searched for "1539", the number in "Moose #1539": the adopter whose phone ends 1539 answered, and since a current record had answered, the horizon held and Moose, adopted in 2022, was offered nowhere — on the scene's Find or on `/search`, at either width. A current record that carries the words only in a field no longer keeps the past out: where no current record is named by the words and a past one is, the past ones named are in the answer, said as past, ranked by how they matched (the name before a field), and `searched.past` says it looked there. A current record named by the words keeps the horizon as before.

Compatibility: ops, stored formats, the wire, the declaration and derived tools are unchanged.
