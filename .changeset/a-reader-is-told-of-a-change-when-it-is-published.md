A reader of an app that publishes is told of a change when it is published, not when it is made (FR-206). `matchSubscriptions` judged every batch the moment it landed, so in an app declared `publishing: { mode: "on-publish" }` a reader — a seat holding no `working` role — subscribed to a record heard the instant a collaborator changed it, and heard nothing at the publish that brought the change to them. Now a reader's subscriptions are held from every batch until a `publish` brings it: at the publish, each batch it brings — from the publish before it that stands to the seq it publishes through, in the order they landed, a change taken back before it was published and the undo that took it back left out — is judged as its own batch, against the app as the reader is served it then. A collaborator is told at once, as before. `watchSubscriptions` and `@graview/ship`'s handler both match through it, and a run of typing the publish brings is held and joined by `holdTypingRuns` as one. Core's test holds a reader told nothing at an edit and of it at the publish, once; each batch a publish brings told in order by who made it; a change taken back before the publish told nobody; a condition reached as the published app says it; and typing held and told as one run.

Compatibility: ops and primitives, stored formats, the wire, the declaration and check finding codes, derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged. A match for a reader of an app that publishes now comes from `matchSubscriptions` called with the commit holding the publish, and carries the batch, seq and moment of the change it tells.
