One part of a worker view that fails falls back alone, and the rest of the view still draws (Roadmap f-view-part-fails-alone). A view marks a part of what it draws with `graview.part(name, () => markup)`, which hands on the part's markup in a `<div data-part="name">` (`{ as: "li" }` or `{ as: "article" }` for another element). Where the part's code throws, or takes more than its share of the push — a quarter of `pushMs`, as fitted to the device — the runtime hands the part on empty, marked `data-part-failed` with why in `data-part-why`, and the host draws its own note in the part's place: "This part could not be drawn.", small and muted, a note to a screen reader. Why is said once for each part and way it failed, in the view's console and to `onProblem`, naming the part and the error, quoted and cut to 300 characters, or the time it took; a mount says at most ten parts. A part's time is its own: a slow part inside another fails alone, and the outer part keeps what it drew. A part is known by its name and its turn among the parts of that name in one render, so rows drawn in a loop under one name are told apart. A part that threw draws again on the next push once it does not throw, and the note goes; a part that took too long is left as the note for the next three pushes without running, so it cannot go on to take the whole view's budget, and is then tried again. The runtime stamps a part it hands on failed with a mark the host hands it for the mount, and the host draws and says nothing for a failed mark the view wrote itself. A part that never returns is still the whole view's stop, and so is the page's own draw budget, both unchanged. A headless run, and so `graview view check`, says a part that failed as a problem. The `graview-worker-view` skill says how a part is written.

Compatibility: ops and primitives — unchanged. Stored formats — unchanged. The wire — a worker view's `props` push carries an optional `partMs`, the most one part may take, and `partMark`, the mark a failed part carries; a runtime before this one ignores both. The declaration and check finding codes — unchanged. Derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged. The JavaScript API — new, optional: `graview.part`, and the `PartDrawn` and `PartElement` types of `@graview/guest/worker/view`.
