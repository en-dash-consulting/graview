The right-click menu is ready before the click (FR-186). The menu derived its acts and sections as it opened, fetched its AI section behind Suspense, and asked for suggestions afresh every time, so it filled in while the reader watched: "Thinking…", then rows arriving. Now when the pointer rests on a record, a card, a row or a kind for 150 ms — in the scene and on the routed face; a pass-over across a list does nothing — or the keyboard lands on one (so Shift+F10 opens at once), or a finger goes down on one (so a long press does), the menu that would open there is got ready: the pane and its act forms fetched, its acts derived and kept, and each section's new `prepare` called, which fetches the inline AI's chunk and the sheet "Notify me…" opens. Nothing is fetched before that, so a hosted page's first load is unchanged. The acts derived for a target are kept per store, by the selection, the seat, the providers, the pins and the focus, until the graph changes (`prepareAffordances` and `AffordancesAsked` from `@graview/react`; `useAffordances` reads the same kept derivation). Suggestions are kept per target, by the seat, the declaration and the host's `ai`, until the record changes (for a list, a lens or a place, until the graph does): a menu opened again draws what was suggested in its first frame and asks nothing, and a failure is not kept. Hovering asks no model, ever. A host's own menu section may give `prepare` too (`MenuSection.prepare`); `useMenuPrepare()` from `@graview/react/drawing` calls every section's for a target, and the routed face's `Inspector` takes `aimAt`, what a menu opened at an element would be about. `capabilities().shipped` gains FR-186.

Compatibility: ops, stored formats, the wire, the declaration and derived tools are unchanged; `capabilities().shipped` lists FR-186. What Graview Cloud does: nothing for its menus to be ready before the click; a section it adds through `menuSections` that fetches what it draws can say so in `prepare`.
