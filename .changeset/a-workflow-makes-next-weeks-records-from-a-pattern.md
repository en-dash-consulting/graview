A workflow makes next week's records from a pattern, Roadmap f-job-generate-from-pattern. Homeflow made its week of 34 events with a script somebody had to remember to run; now a workflow says it, in a step of its own kind: `{ "generate": "event", "from": [{ "kind": "routine", "where": "active", "dates": "startsAt" }], "window": "next week", "set": { "name": { "expr": "name" }, "startsAt": "$date" }, "link": "pattern" }`. For each day of the window — "next week" (Monday to Sunday on the app's clock), "this week", "next 7 days", "today", "tomorrow", or `{ "from", "to" }` in the rule language — that a source record's date falls on, it wants one record: a date that falls in it, each occurrence of a date that repeats (a skipped day is not among them), or each day of a set of weekdays a drive is kept as. `set` is read on the source, with `date` the occurrence and `source` its record (`"$date"`, `"$source"`, `{ "expr": "datetime(date, at)" }`), and `link` names the relation back, on the made kind or on the source's. Each record is made through the act that creates the kind (`using` says which, where several do) under the id its key gives — `"{source}:{date}"` when the step says none, `event:wf.next-weeks-events.routine:swim:2026-11-03` — so a second run, or a retry after a run stopped half way, makes nothing twice and reads as nothing new. A rerun changes what the source changed (a plan item renamed renames its event) and leaves what a person changed (a drive moved half an hour stays moved) unless the step says `overwrite: true`; one a person set aside stays aside, and one they removed, or a run they undid, is not made again; the run says each in a note. What it wants is pure and on no page: `generateWants`, `windowDays`, `datesIn` and `keyedId` on `@graview/core/workflows`, run by `@graview/tools/workflows`' `runWorkflow` like any step. `graview check` says, in the person's voice and with the edit where one can be written: a date field the source lacks (`workflow-generate-dates-unknown`), a window it cannot read (`workflow-generate-window-invalid`), a field the made kind lacks (`workflow-generate-set-unknown`), a value that reads what the source lacks or a `$` name it does not give (`workflow-generate-value-invalid`), a relation neither end declares (`workflow-generate-link-unknown`), and an act: none that makes the kind (`workflow-generate-no-act`), several and none named (`workflow-generate-act-ambiguous`), or one named that makes another (`workflow-generate-act-makes-other`).

Compatibility: ops and primitives — unchanged; a generated record is made by the app's own create act, under an id asked for as a client asks for one, and changed by its edit. Stored formats — unchanged; the document's `workflows` may hold a step with `generate`. The wire — unchanged. The declaration and check finding codes — one new step kind in `workflows.<name>.steps`, refused by a build that predates it as a step of no kind it runs; eight new error codes, all `workflow-generate-*`, said only of such a step; no declaration that passed is refused. Derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged. What Graview Cloud does: nothing for a generate step to run on a host that already runs workflows; its workflow editor offers the step with a kind to make, its sources, a window and the fields to set, and shows the run's notes (what it kept of a person's changes, what it did not make again) beside the run.
