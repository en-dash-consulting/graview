The billboard is the point of flying closer. Chosen from altitude, a lens was drawn at half the span and under half the height — a window into the picture rather than the picture — and the caps were the window's, so zooming grew the city under the board and never the board. It may take most of the span now, and its caps grow with the zoom, so zooming in enlarges it the way it enlarges everything else. Its grey grip strip with a "⤢ Full screen" pill floating over it is a title bar now: the picture's name on the left, one "Open ↗" on the right, and the whole bar the handle that moves the board.
