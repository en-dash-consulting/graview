An act is offered only where it applies, and a link that is not there is refused in words (Roadmap f-me-offered-without-sight-of-people, f-several-bar-offers-only-what-applies).

An act done as yourself (`$me`, a code act's `ctx.me`) runs under the seat's sight, so a seat whose sight keeps the people kind from it has no record of its own to be there. It was offered the act anyway, `permits` said yes, and applying it refused with "is done as you, and you have no record here", with a profile and without one. Such a seat is now nobody here: the act is not in its permitted acts, the actions strip or its agent tools, and `permits` refuses it in the words `apply` does. The stateless tool surface a host lists for a seat (`toolDefinitions`) reads the same off the declaration. A seat that sees its own person is unchanged. The act is withheld rather than made to work, since making it work would mean an act reads a record its seat is not served.

The several bar offered "Take back a vote" over every chosen record, whoever held the link, and a seat with no vote on them was refused on each in the graph's own key for the link. `actsForSeveral` now rehearses each act over the chosen records as `actOnSeveral` will run it and offers it only where it would change at least one; each act carries `applies`, the records it applies to, which the bar counts ("— 1 of 3") and runs it over. An act still to be asked is offered over the records the seat may run it on. A declared act that severs a link that is not there is refused by the records' names ("has nothing to take away: A hike is not linked to Ann that way"), and a code act's, a kept link's or a host's own removal of one is refused as "That link is not there to take away.", the link's key kept on the error's `id`. `permittedMutations` still lists an act a seat may run at all, judged per record when it is called: with no record to judge against it cannot say more, and the refusal now arrives in words.

Tests: `a-seat-that-cannot-see-the-people-is-not-offered-an-act-done-as-it` and `an-edge-removal-on-a-record-lacking-the-edge-is-refused-in-words` (core), `a-seat-kept-from-every-person-is-offered-no-tool-done-as-it` and `the-several-bar-offers-only-what-applies` (tools). verify-kind-stop and verify-pages hold `noOfferedSeveralActIsThenRefused`.

A hosted page's first load grows by 0.4 KB up front (0.4 KB handed a compiled app). Every face grows by 1.2 KB, Cloud's worker by 2.2 KB, a visitor's form by 0.2 KB (0.4 KB handed a compiled app), and the guest host drawing a worker view on the open kit by 10 bytes gzipped, under a raise of their budgets.

Compatibility: ops and primitives — unchanged. Stored formats — unchanged. The wire — unchanged. The declaration and check finding codes — unchanged; a declared act that severs a link that is not there is refused in a sentence of its own. Derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged; a seat whose sight keeps the people kind from it is no longer handed the tool of an act done as itself.

What Graview Cloud does: a member whose sight leaves out the people kind is no longer offered an act done as them, on a page or through an agent, and a selection of several is offered only what applies to it.
