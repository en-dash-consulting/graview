A record a lens draws as a link answers the right-click menu on a page mounted under a base, and a new tab keeps the base (FR-189). On a page mounted with `basePath` (`/a/<app>`), a board's card and a blocks lens's card or row were `<a href="/features/live-documents">`, the base dropped, so the menu read them as a link somewhere else: "Open in a new tab" and nothing more, no Open, no acts, no "Move to ›" — and with the Move button gone in 0.1.38, a phone could not move a card at all — while the new tab opened outside the app. Now every record a list draws as a link (a board's card, a blocks lens's cards and rows, grouped or not, a home's lists) links to its address under the router's base (`/a/<app>/features/live-documents`), the menu reads the record an item is listed as (`data-graview-listed`) wherever in it the press lands, its own stretched link included — a link the card draws to somewhere else stays that link — and "Open in a new tab" opens the record's address under the base. On a status board a finger resting on a card opens its menu, with Move to ›, rather than choosing it; on a phone that menu offers "Choose several", which chooses the card and begins choosing as the long press did, and while several are chosen a tap and a long press choose as before (a card is `data-graview-choose` only then). `capabilities().shipped` gains FR-189. The pages face alone grows 218 / 81 bytes (minified / gzipped), every face 679 / 250 and the embed with the studio handed in 218 / 97, each raised in `scripts/budget-raises/`; a hosted page's first load holds to its budget unraised.

Compatibility: ops, stored formats, the wire, the declaration and derived tools are unchanged; `capabilities().shipped` lists FR-189. What Graview Cloud does: nothing — a page mounted with `basePath` draws its cards' links under the base and its menu answers them; a host that read a card's `href` as basename-relative now reads it with the base on.
