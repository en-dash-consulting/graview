A kind opens to something useful. Gone into, a district the app drew no picture of — "open", a double press, `#focus=aggregate:<kind>` — drew six chips and "+30 more" in a box floating in an empty field, under a "⚠ 36" that was the number of rooms in the warning tone: nothing said what the records were, what was wrong with them or what to do. It opens now to the kind's stop, in the box a lens fills, derived from the declaration and the records. Its head says the plural, how many and what they are, and offers the kind's creating act first ("Add a room"), asked in place, with the rest behind "Acts…". Under it, only where there is something to say: how many are overdue or due in the next seven days on its due date, each a press that narrows the list; its problems counted by the rule that found them ("1 problem in rooms: A workshop fits its room"), each opening the bar's own rows and their repairs, and "Show only the rooms with a problem"; and what was added or changed since this reader last looked. Then the picture its data chooses: a board by its main choice (the lifecycle's field, or one called status, stage, state or phase) up to 300 records; a calendar on its main date where today's month holds records a fortnight long or less; otherwise a table of the kind's glance and its relations, read as the far records' names, an empty value said as a quiet dash ("none" to a screen reader), 50 rows at a time with "Show 50 more". Each row is its record to the scene, as a record is anywhere: pressed, it is chosen; right-clicked, long-pressed or given the menu key, its acts open; its name goes to it and its "⋯", drawn with an edge at rest, opens the same acts. Opened from altitude — a double press on its district, or Enter on it chosen — a kind with no picture opens to its stop in the panel beside the map a lens opens in, taking the place of a lens or another kind's stop already open there (one panel at a time; with a lens open it used to open behind it), its district shut on the map; the address says so, the keyboard lands on its heading, and Back returns to what was open. The other shape is one press away, the stop says the picture is derived from the declaration, and "Keep as a lens" keeps it as the seat keeps a view it drew (`add-lens`). The stop is a region named by its heading, a real table on a desk and rows of words in a narrow box, with 44-pixel targets under a finger and nothing scrolling sideways; it is fetched when a district is first gone into, so a page that never opens one carries a door of about a hundred bytes. `@graview/tools/draft` gains `recipeDraft(recipe, options)`, a draft from a recipe with no ask, titled and judged as an asked-for one ("Every room" for a plain list). The graview-lists skill says what a kind opens to and what to declare first, and `pnpm verify kind-stop` walks every example's kinds with no picture at a desk and a phone, light and dark.

Compatibility: from altitude, opening a district with no picture now focuses it (`#overview=1&focus=aggregate:<kind>`) and draws its stop in `[data-testid="lens-panel"]`, where it used to choose it (`sel=kind:<kind>`, the district panel), which a single press still does; `LayoutOptions.besideTheMap` (set by the scene where a panel is drawn) keeps a focused group's district shut and stamps nothing in the city's middle. A view an app registers for a kind at many × full replaces the stop as before; the framework's default for that cell is now the stop, fetched lazily, drawing the old list until it arrives. New test ids are `kind-stop`, `kind-stop-primary`, `kind-stop-acts`, `kind-stop-problems`, `kind-stop-as-<shape>`, `kind-stop-keep`, `kind-stop-table`, `kind-stop-row-go`, `kind-stop-row-acts`, `kind-stop-more` and the arranging line `kind-arrange-*`, and the table's rows carry `data-graview-pick` as a record's mark does; `@graview/tools/draft` adds `recipeDraft`. The embed's every-face budget is raised by the stop's chunk (`scripts/budget-raises/a-kind-opens-to-something-useful-every-face.json`); the hosted page's up-front budget is unchanged.
