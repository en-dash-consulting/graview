A kind opens to something useful. Gone into, a district the app drew no picture of — "open", a double press, `#focus=aggregate:<kind>` — drew six chips and "+30 more" in a box floating in an empty field, under a "⚠ 36" that was the number of rooms in the warning tone: nothing said what the records were, what was wrong with them or what to do. It opens now to the kind's stop, in the box a lens fills, derived from the declaration and the records. Its head says the plural, how many and what they are, and offers the kind's creating act first ("Add a room"), asked in place, with the rest behind "Acts…". Under it, only where there is something to say: how many are overdue or due in the next seven days on its due date, each a press that narrows the list; its problems counted by the rule that found them ("1 problem in rooms: A workshop fits its room"), each opening the bar's own rows and their repairs, and "Show only the rooms with a problem"; and what was added or changed since this reader last looked. Then the picture its data chooses: a board by its main choice (the lifecycle's field, or one called status, stage, state or phase) up to 300 records; a calendar on its main date where today's month holds records a fortnight long or less; otherwise a table of the kind's glance and its relations, read as the far records' names, its rows going to their records and opening their acts, 50 at a time with "Show 50 more". The other shape is one press away, the stop says the picture is derived from the declaration, and "Keep as a lens" keeps it as the seat keeps a view it drew (`add-lens`). The stop is a region named by its heading, a real table on a desk and rows of words in a narrow box, with 44-pixel targets under a finger and nothing scrolling sideways; it is fetched when a district is first gone into, so a page that never opens one carries a door of about a hundred bytes. `@graview/tools/draft` gains `recipeDraft(recipe, options)`, a draft from a recipe with no ask, titled and judged as an asked-for one ("Every room" for a plain list). The graview-lists skill says what a kind opens to and what to declare first, and `pnpm verify kind-stop` walks every example's kinds with no picture at a desk and a phone, light and dark.

Compatibility: a view an app registers for a kind at many × full replaces the stop as before; the framework's default for that cell is now the stop, fetched lazily, drawing the old list until it arrives. New test ids are `kind-stop`, `kind-stop-primary`, `kind-stop-acts`, `kind-stop-problems`, `kind-stop-as-<shape>`, `kind-stop-keep`, `kind-stop-table`, `kind-stop-row-go`, `kind-stop-row-acts`, `kind-stop-more` and the arranging line `kind-arrange-*`; `@graview/tools/draft` adds `recipeDraft`. The embed's every-face budget is raised by the stop's chunk (`scripts/budget-raises/a-kind-opens-to-something-useful-every-face.json`); the hosted page's up-front budget is unchanged.
