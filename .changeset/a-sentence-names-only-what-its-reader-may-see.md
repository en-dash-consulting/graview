**Security.** Another author's sentence no longer names a record its reader may not see (FR-55). Two ways it did. The own-words exception excused too much: a seat that wrote a guessed id (`ref: "b:freya"`) into its own record was then served another author's `drop` of that record whole — its call `{ because: "b:freya" }` and its sentence "Drop d:barn in favour of Freya Davies" — because the removal carried the seat's own value; the seat learned the guess was real and the hidden record's name. Now a seat's own words excuse only what it wrote: values in the primitives and the inverse that puts them back, and its own calls; another author's call, sentence and name are judged by sight alone. And a sentence that read a hidden record through a link — "Tag Barn near Freya Davies" — named no hidden id for any check to catch: an act's op now records which records its `describe` read (`described`), and another author's op is served whole only when its reader may see every one of them, withheld otherwise. An act's own refusal worded from a record its caller may not see is said without its words: "“Claim” could not be done as asked."

Compatibility: ops — additive: `Operation.described` is a new optional field, the ids an act's `describe` read; ops without it read as before (and are judged by their ids alone). The wire — narrowing: a seat with sights is served withheld another author's op whose sentence read a record it may not see, or whose call names one it wrote itself; a refusal an act's body worded from such a record comes back `invalid` with "“<act>” could not be done as asked.". Stored formats, derived tools and check codes are unchanged.
