A worker view of one record is a card on the Scene, as a view of many already was, and a host with no Pages expands the card in place. The record in focus kept to its box and scrolled inside it (FR-141) — a page inside a picture, with the record's own fields under the view at the foot of a scroller. `registerWorkerView` now marks a view of one record previewed too, and the Scene draws it as the same card (`ScenePreview`): the record's label, as much of the view and the fields under it as the box holds — it measures all it holds, not only what the view drew — faded at the foot where there is more, and Open, which goes to the record's page on Pages (`/tasks/t1`, under the page's base where it has one). FR-141's rule changes with it: the record in focus is still held to its box and scrolls inside it when the framework's record, a declared page or a view the host draws in React stands there, but no longer a worker view's card, which the stylesheet's rule passes over (`[data-graview-record-focus] > :not(.graview-kind-tag):not([data-graview-preview])`); a panel inside the card is still drawn whole. Where there are no pages to open — a Shell drawn without the embed's page door — the card offered nothing; now it offers Expand, which grows it in place from its top to the height of all it holds, with nothing in it scrolling, laid over the cards below it until Collapse or Escape takes it back.

`react`'s tests find Expand grow the card past its box and its `scene.maxHeight`, unfaded and with nothing scrolling, Collapse and Escape take it back, no control where the card holds it all, and a view of one record drawn through `ResolvedView` as a card named for the record with Open to its page; `guest`'s find a view of one record marked previewed (it was asserted unmarked under FR-141, and that test now says the opposite on purpose); `primitives`' find the record's scroll passing over the card and a panel inside it still drawn whole. `verify-address`, at 1440 and 390, opens a view of one task eighty notes long on the Scene and finds a card no taller than its box and inside the window, faded, with nothing scrolling and Open landing on the task's page (aTallRecordViewOnTheSceneIsACardWhoseOpenLandsOnTheRecordsPage), and in the whole-page Shell finds Expand growing it from its top to all it holds and Collapse taking it back (withNoPageDoorExpandGrowsTheRecordsCardInPlace). `graview-worker-view` says so. The scene face carries 1.3 KB more before it draws (1_376 bytes), under the before-it-draws limit raised to 974 KB.

Compatibility: on the Scene a worker view of one record is drawn as a card held to its box with Open to the record's page, where it was a box that scrolled, and a Scene card where there are no pages offers Expand. The API is unchanged. The wire, the stored formats, the declaration and the derived tools are unchanged.
