A video plays as a hero background, Roadmap f-video-hero (the framework half). A record's video was a player with the browser's controls, poster first, and nothing else: an immersive home that wanted a clip running quietly behind its words had no way to ask for one, and a worker view may not draw `<video>` at all. `<graview-video autoplay>` is now a background — muted, looping, inline and without controls, since autoplay without muted never plays and so the one word means all of it — and `<graview-video cover>` fills the box the view sized (`object-fit: cover`; the view's own stylesheet rounds and clips the box). The poster stays the value's, and the source, the poster and the controls stay the host's: `src`, `poster`, `controls`, `loop` and `muted` on a `<graview-video>` are refused as before. Where the person asked for less motion (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`) a background stands still at its poster, fetching only its metadata, and is let go — or held again — as the setting changes while the page is open. The face's player (`Files` as a field or an image block, `VideoWay`: `autoplay`, `cover`) draws the same, with nothing said under a background. A view is handed `poster: true` among a video's facts where it has one, never the poster's path. A headless run lists each video the view places — `videos` on the place's description, each `{ record, field, text, name, source, duration, poster, autoplay, cover, playable, why }` — and says it in one line under "Videos:" in the place's words, `playable` being a source every browser plays (MP4, WebM or Ogg, what a host's upload transcodes to) with a poster to stand at, so an agent that wrote a hero confirms it plays without opening a browser; the part itself reads "The waves at dusk, a 45-second video, playing as a background, muted and looping, filling its box". The guest's, the primitives' and core's tests hold it (`a-video-plays-as-a-hero-background`, `a-place-description-lists-its-videos`), and the guest harness's pictures run places the porch's clip as a hero on every engine: played by nobody's press, muted and looping and covering its 120 px box, and still at its poster where the page asked for less motion. The guest README and the graview-view-drawing skill say it. The open kit's table and the host's player cost the guest host drawing a worker view on the open kit 1_226 / 377 more, a worker view's runtime 56 / 15 more, every face 2_721 / 939 more and Cloud's worker 3_446 bytes more, each under a raise naming this changeset; nothing a hosted page loads up front changed, and the guest host before a worker is drawn is as it was.

Compatibility: ops and primitives, stored formats and the declaration and check finding codes — unchanged. The wire — a worker view's file facts gain `poster: true` on a video that has one; a view that read none is unmoved. Derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged; a place's description (`describePlace`, a headless run) gains the optional `videos` list and its text a "Videos:" section only where a view places one. What Graview Cloud does: nothing for a view that writes `<graview-video autoplay cover>`; its `propose_view` answer carries `videos`, which its authoring guide may point an agent at to confirm a hero plays; an app-level reel not tied to a record stays Cloud's half.
