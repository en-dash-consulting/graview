"Keep this link" is permitted per relation (FR-172). A seat that could run any act that made or broke links, or only made records, could keep any provisional link it saw: a clerk who could add a property could accept a link a model drew between two people. `keep-link` is now permitted, as the act that connects would be, to a seat that may run a declared act whose `connects` or `severs` names the link's relation, or that a grant names `keep-link` to; a link of a relation no act makes or breaks is kept by nobody a policy names. A keep whose either end the seat may not see is still refused as `missing`.

Compatibility: derived tools and the wire are unchanged in shape. Under a policy, a `keep-link` from a seat that may make records or links of other relations but not this one is refused as `forbidden` where it landed. With no policy nothing changes.
