A move or a merge of a person carries every link they made as themselves, older than the log too, and a profile's own person can be merged. `rebindPerson` and `mergePerson` (`@graview/core/binding`) take `archived` beside the author (`PersonMoveOptions`): what the host's compaction archived behind the log's horizon, as `store.compact` returned it or its ops alone (`ArchivedHistory`). A link the person's acts made through `$me` in those ops moves with them as a link in the log does, and is not counted in `leftBehind`; without it a move reads the log alone, as before. `mergePerson` now merges a profile's seat (`account~profile`) whose person has an id of its own, tied to it by the seat's mark: the seat, what it did and that person's links go to the person entered for it, and that person is set aside, one change undo takes back whole. Which person is the profile's own is read from how it was first made, the store's own record carrying the seat's mark, in the log or in `archived`: a person entered for the profile and bound, moved, put back or restored there is moved, not merged, and is refused as before, and so is a profile's person made behind the horizon when no archive is handed. Two links of one relation between the same two records keep their own ids through a move, its undo and the moves after it, and a link from the person's record to itself moves at both ends, in a move and in a merge, where it was dropped before. Held by a unit test of core (`a-move-or-merge-carries-every-link-made-as-the-person`).

Compatibility: the host's binding entry — additive: `rebindPerson` and `mergePerson` take an optional `archived`, and the types `PersonMoveOptions` and `ArchivedHistory` are new; a call without it reads the log alone as before. `mergePerson` accepts a profile seat it refused before, and a merge keeps the own record's link to itself on the person merged into. The wire, stored formats, ops and primitives, derived tool names and input schemas, and the declaration and check finding codes — unchanged. What Graview Cloud does: hand `archived` (the adapter's `loadArchive`) to `rebindPerson` and `mergePerson` for a store whose log was compacted, and offer the merge for a profile's own person on the People page.
