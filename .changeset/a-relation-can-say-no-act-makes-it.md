A relation can say no act makes or breaks it (`acts: "none"` on an edge declaration), and is then given no derived connect or disconnect: it is read from what was declared. The studio's own meta-schema says it of what an act creates, connects and severs and of the kind a computed field is on, which its named acts and the opened declaration set; once every relation came with a derived pair, "Link a kind (creates)" had taken a kind's strip past "Add a field".

Compatibility: `EdgeDeclaration` takes `acts?: "none"`; the studio's acts are as they were.
