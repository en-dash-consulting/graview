What an act broke and mended is told to a seat as the seat judges it, over its own world. `answerSeenBy` took the whole graph's judgment of a change and, where a rule reads beyond its subject (a fact, a sweep, a relation), said what it mended, and a preview's problems, by the rule's title: a guest who previews proposing its draft was told "too many proposed", and one who withdraws its one proposal was told it mended that, though by what it sees there were never too many — so each was told about records it may not see. Now all three of a change's lists are judged over the seat's own world: what it is served now, and that world with the change as it is served taken back (a change made, or undone) or put on (a preview). What it broke and mended is the difference, each in the seat's own words and lines, and nothing in them moves for a record it may not see. The judging is `@graview/core/lines`', which a seat's tools load before they answer; without it an answer to a seat says no problems at all. `answeredTo` is gone from `@graview/core/lines` (`judgedForASeat` does this), and the engine's lines hook no longer takes a fourth argument.

Compatibility: ops, stored formats, wire messages, the document format, the compiled format, check finding codes and tool names and schemas are unchanged; the hosted page's first load is smaller (see the removal's changeset). What a seat is told changes: where a policy keeps records from a seat, a preview's problems and what a change mended are the seat's own judgment, with lines, never a rule's title over the whole graph.
