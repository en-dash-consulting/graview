The seat takes you where you ask. Nick: "I want it to help me navigate through the graview app." An ask is read against what the declaration and the graph already name, without a model and in order: a place by its title ("go to The week", "open Email to Todd", "go to the scene"), a record by its name ("go to Ongoing support"), a kind narrowed by a date or a status ("deliverables due this week", "overdue tasks", "open decisions", "plantings sown this week" — today, tomorrow, this week, next week, this month and overdue, read against the kind's date field and its choice and yes/no fields), what's wrong, and what is here or about a thing ("tell me about Pay the deposit", "what is here"). Each is answered by moves, data both faces apply: a place, a picture of a kind (`withPicture`), a kind's list narrowed by the same `filter` words a list page's `?filter=` and a stop's `in.filter` carry, a record, or the problems — each with its address on the routed face and a sentence the seat says ("Went to The week."). Where the words a list carries cannot say the narrowing ("overdue" on a kind whose status has several unfinished values), the answer is the records themselves and an offer to show them as a view. Everything is read through the seat's own sight: a kind it may not see has no place, list or record here, a record it may not see is never found, and asking for either is answered exactly as asking for a name that is nothing ("Nothing by that name here."). `graphResponder` answers these asks with their moves, grounded, so no model is asked; `llmResponder` lists the places a seat may see and reads a model's `"go"` names through the same resolver, so a model can only move the app where the graph itself would.

Compatibility: new exports and a new optional field. New: `@graview/tools/go` (`resolveAsk`, `movesFromNames`, `NOTHING_BY_THAT_NAME`, and the types), and the types `SeatMove`, `SeatOffer`, `AskAnswer`, `AskAbout`, `AskContext` from `@graview/tools`; `ChatReply.moves` and `ChatReply.offer`; `ChatContext.places` and `ChatContext.place`; `sceneMove(view, move)` and `SceneMove` from `@graview/primitives`, the stop a move is on the scene (the picture by `withPicture`, a kind's district down and close with `in.filter`, a record focused and selected) or the problems' pane. Changed: `graphResponder` answers "go to …", "open …", "show me …", a kind with a date or a status, and "tell me about" a name its seat cannot see with a move or with "Nothing by that name here.", where it answered with the graph's shape or the records the words found; its answers to "what's wrong" and to a named record begin with the move's sentence. Ops, stored formats, the wire, the declaration, check's finding codes and tool names and input schemas are unchanged. `graview create` aliases `@graview/tools/go` in a linked project's vite config, as it does every other subpath. What Graview Cloud changes: nothing it must; a chat that passes `places: placesOf(app)` in a turn's context is answered with moves it can apply.
