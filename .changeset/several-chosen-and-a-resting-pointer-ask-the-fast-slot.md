The inline AI's last part: records chosen together get the AI's suggestions in their selection bar, a record the pointer rests on can get one quiet suggestion beside it, and the menu's AI section is held to filling in under 400 ms. With two or more records chosen, on a page's list or in the scene, the bar offers beside the acts they share the Fast slot's pick for the set — one of those acts, run over every chosen record as the bar's own button runs it, or a panel or a place that shows them (`suggestForSeveral` on `@graview/tools/inline-ai`) — asked once per choice and kept as the menu keeps its suggestions until a chosen record changes, never of the Thinking slot, and not at all where the host said `suggestOnOpen: false`. A host that passes `ai.hoverChips: true` with a Fast slot gets the hover chip: the pointer resting on a record for the menu's own dwell shows the Fast slot's first suggestion there, only when it is at least 60% sure (`HOVER_CHIP_CONFIDENCE`), at most one on the screen, asked through the menu's keeping so the chip and the menu ask once between them; a pointer passing over asks nothing, the keyboard and a finger raise none, and a reader turns chips off under "AI suggestions on hover" in the person menu. Chips are off unless the host asks, because a hover is not a question the reader asked and a host that meters every call should not pay for one. AI off hides the bar's suggestions and every chip. `verify-inline-ai` now measures the menu's AI section filling in over twenty opens with a Fast slot that answers in 100 to 250 ms (262 and 277 ms at the 95th percentile on two runs), and holds the chips to one, none on a pass-over, and none with AI off.
