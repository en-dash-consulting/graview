An archive may begin at an epoch, so a host can delete the history behind a plan's window and keep playback inside it (FR-214). `archivedSeenBy` (FR-198) judged a seat's view of the archive over the whole history from seq 0, and threw "The archive is missing the change at seq N" once any early change was gone; a full export and the mending of a checkpoint written before `removed` and `writers` read the whole archive too. Now `archiveFrom(store, archive, seq)` on `@graview/core/seat` makes the epoch the app stood at at any seq of the whole history — moved back to the start of the gesture it would split, as a checkpoint is — keeping who made each record behind it, what each record removed behind it was and who wrote each field naming a record (`creators`, `removed`, `writers`, FR-199), and answers the archive from it: that epoch, then the ops and epochs after it. A host keeps that and deletes the rest. `archivedSeenBy` accepts an archive that begins at seq 0 or at the earliest epoch keeping `removed` from which its ops reach the horizon, judges the ops after it as a compacted store judges its tail, answers a range behind it with no op there and `keptFrom` (its seq, `ArchivedSeen.keptFrom`, so the timeline steps rather than refuses, FR-213), and serves an earlier epoch that keeps `removed` judged on its own. `archiveFrom(store, archive, store.log.horizon)` is the store's checkpoint made again from either kind of archive, which is how a host mends one now. `exportBundle(app, store, { full: true })` carries everything kept: the whole history from seq 0 as before, or, from an archive that begins at an epoch, the log from it with `horizon` its seq and the epoch as `checkpoint`; and every bundle that begins past seq 0, a tail export's too, carries its `checkpoint`, so it restores as a compacted store opened on it (`new Store({ log: bundle.log, epochs: [bundle.checkpoint], snapshot })`). An archive with a gap and no such epoch after it is still refused, by both. Core's test makes a window at a random seq of 300 random compacted worlds with sights and serves a seat every range after it as the whole archive did, with no unseen id, and the epoch made later from the window is the one the whole archive makes; ship's exports a window and restores it. `capabilities().shipped` gains FR-214.

Compatibility: ops and the wire are unchanged; `capabilities().shipped` gains FR-214. Stored formats: additive — `AppBundle.checkpoint` within bundle version 1, which an older `assertBundle` reads past; an archive kept from an epoch is an ordinary `LogArchive`. Additive on `@graview/core/seat` (`archiveFrom`, `ArchivedSeen.keptFrom`). What Graview Cloud does: when a Free app's history passes 30 days, calls `archiveFrom(store, archive, seq)` at the seq of the first archived op made at or after the window start, writes what it answers as the app's archive and deletes the objects behind its epoch; answers `archived` with `archivedSeenBy` over that archive; and mends an older checkpoint with `archiveFrom(store, archive, store.log.horizon)` instead of opening the whole history.
