A field typed together is edited live: the fourth phase of collaborative text, the editor. Under a host that carries typing — an embed handed a remote store, whose `remote.typing` the frame hands the provider as `typing` — prose the declaration types together (a Markdown field, a long text, a field marked so) opens with no draft and no Save: each keystroke is the field's at once, sent through `remote.typing(node, field)` as a splice read from the text area before and after it, the end it keeps never left of the caret, so a letter typed into a run of the same letter lands where it was typed. A run ends on a blur, Enter, an arrow or a click in the text, and by itself after 400 ms or a word. "Done" closes it, and so does Escape, which puts nothing back; Ctrl or ⌘ Z takes back this person's own last run of typing into the field and nobody else's, though they typed after it, and Ctrl or ⌘ Shift Z puts it back. Somebody else's typing lands in the text area as it arrives, the person's selection carried over it, a caret staying before text inserted where it stands; while an input method composes a word nothing is sent and what others type waits, and the two meet when the word is chosen. Markdown keeps its Preview, read from the live text, and the scene's record and the routed page are one binding. Where the host carries no typing, or the field is written whole, the draft and its Save are as they were. A presence may say where somebody's caret stands in such a field (`caret: { node, field, anchor, head, rev }`, offsets against their log at `rev`); the editor says the person's while it holds the keyboard, at most every 120 ms, and draws everybody else's as a thin line in their hue with their name on it, laid on a copy of the text that wraps as the text area does, carried over the typing the page's log took since they said it. A server keeps a caret only when it is a record, a field and three counts, and tells it to no seat whose sight keeps that record or that field from it (`presenceSeenBy(who, sees, seesField)`). `@graview/ship`'s handler loads `@graview/core/typing` itself, so `graview serve` and `graview preview` answer `type-text` as the main entry does. The editor is fetched when such a field is opened — with `@graview/core/typing`, which says whether the field is typed together — and keeps its own diff and carry rather than take `@graview/core`'s, so a page carries 0.4 KB more up front (the provider's `typing`, `TypingContext`, the presence's caret), under raises of the hosted page's budgets, the pages face alone's and a visitor's form alone's (`scripts/budget-raises/a-field-typed-together-is-edited-live-*.json`), and the scene face's limit before it draws moves to 994 KB. Primitives' tests hold that a keystroke is sent as a splice and nothing is saved, that a composed word is held and lands with what others typed meanwhile, that a remote splice keeps the caret on its word, that Escape does not put anything back, that ⌘Z takes back only the person's run, that others' carets are drawn and carried, and that prose written whole keeps its Save; ship's, that a caret is told only to a seat that sees its field. `pnpm verify typed-together` types into one long brief from two seats at once with real keystrokes, in three engines: both pages and the store converge, each draws the other's caret where they stand, and a caret stays on its word.

Compatibility: ops and primitives — unchanged. The wire — additive within protocol 1: a presence may carry `caret`, which a server of this build keeps only when well formed and withholds from a seat that may not see its record or field, and which a server before it passes on as any other field a client said; `WIRE_PROTOCOL` does not move. Snapshot and log formats, the declaration and check finding codes, derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged. The JavaScript API — additive: `PresenceCaret` and `Presence.caret` on `@graview/core`; the provider's `typing`, `TypingContext`, `FieldTyping` and `LiveTyping` on `@graview/react`, and `PresenceState.sayCaret`; `EmbedRemote.typing`, called without its remote; `presenceSeenBy`'s optional `seesField`. What Graview Cloud does: takes this build; a host that mounts the embed over its own remote passes that remote's `typing` (an `openRemote` store's own is passed already) and a field typed together is edited live; a host that tells who is here itself and judges sight passes `seesField` to `presenceSeenBy`.
