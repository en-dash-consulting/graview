The store's own bookkeeping tells a seat nothing of a record it may not see, the last three known limits of FR-55. An act ran under its caller's sight, but three of its reads still read the whole store and told a seat a hidden record was there; each is now held by a world-with and world-without comparison. An id minted from a label for a kind the seat may not see every record of is the seat's own — `room:green.person-gus`, which no other seat's label mints — stepping only past what it sees, where `room:green-2` used to step past a room it could not see; one that would land on a hidden record anyway takes a suffix no guess collides with. A seat that may not see every record may not ask for the id of one it makes: it is refused alike, reason `invalid` ("… makes its own id here"), whether a hidden record holds the id or nothing does, where it was refused "already taken" for a hidden one and made for an absent one. An act that sets a single-source link (`setSingleSource`) writes the new link before taking the old ones, so one that takes a hidden record's link is served to the seat whole, with what it may see, as it would be with no such link. A seat that sees every record of a kind mints and asks for ids as before.

Compatibility: ops unchanged in shape; `setSingleSource` emits its `add-edge` before the `remove-edge`s it replaces, so a new op lists its primitives in that order. A create by a seat that may not see every record of the kind mints `<kind>:<slug>.<seat slug>` instead of `<kind>:<slug>`, and its `id` argument is refused as `invalid` whatever holds it; `docs/stability.md` no longer lists the three as known limits.
