A record opened in the one face's city is lit where it stands, the page is drawn once, a board's post runs under any board it crosses, and a district's "⚠ n" opens that kind's problems (Graview HQ feature:f-one-face-record-stays-in-place, f-one-face-draws-the-page-once). Nick pressed a record's building at Nearby: it was chosen, but it moved to its district's top corner, the rest of its district laid out again round it, the whole city faded to a fifth, and "the periods it grew in: Cretaceous" floated over the city away from the page — where loading that record's page fresh lit it in place with nothing faded. The page opened by the press said its record to the provider as the page's choice, and the Scene took it for its own. Now, while the page is out, the Scene keeps nothing chosen after a press, a link or a pill exactly as on a fresh load: the record opened is lit where it stands, its district keeps its order, nothing fades that a fresh load does not fade, and no line's words stand over the city; the page's choice is kept for the page to have back when it comes in again. The lit state changes no geometry: the record the page is about is lit in the accent and outlined, no longer lifted seven pixels off its plot, and a record opened beside the city whose building is in the part of the city the page leaves moves no camera — the building pressed and every other building keep their boxes to the pixel, and only the boards and names go over a moment to where they stand for the new page. A record out of sight is still flown to.

With the one face on, its part is fetched as the page first draws, and the routed page stood as the fallback meanwhile: when the part arrived, some 300 ms later, the page and its bar were drawn again as new elements, and a press, the keyboard and a word typed in that time were lost. The page is now drawn once, into an element of its own, and that element moves — into the fallback's place, then into the one face's sheet — and the bar, drawn once too, moves into the face's slot when it is there; the keyboard is put back where it was, with its caret, wherever the browser cannot move a node without taking it out of the page. `data-graview-one-face-arriving` still says the face is on its way.

At the City and Nearby a board's post never crosses another board where a place by its district stands clear: each board placed is an obstacle to the posts placed after it, and its post to the boards placed after it, weighed after what a board must never stand on and before how far it stands from where it belongs (Nick's decision). Where no place is clear, the post is drawn under the board it crosses, never over its words. A district's "⚠ n" beside its name opens `/problems?kind=<kind>`: the problems that name one of that kind's records — as many as the chip counts — and the rules about the kind, titled "What is broken in specimens", with "See every problem (n)" beside them.

Tests: `the-page-is-drawn-once-as-the-one-face-arrives` (pages) holds that the page's main, its bar and its link, a field the keyboard is in, its word and its caret are the same once the one face has come, the page in its sheet and the bar in its slot, and nothing left behind when the face goes; `the-problems-narrow-to-one-kind` that `/problems?kind=duty` lists only the problems naming a duty and the rules about duties, and that no kind or an unknown one is every problem; `the-city-says-every-name-and-stands-every-view-clear` that no post crosses another board where one district holds three views, four side by side or three one behind another hold two each. `verify-one-face` holds, on Deep Time at 1280 by 800, that a species' building pressed at Nearby and a record pressed in the page beside the city are lit where they stand, their districts' buildings where they stood — for the press, every building of the district, the pressed one too, within a pixel of its box on the screen — as faded as a fresh load and with no line's words over the city; that the page is drawn once while the one face's part is held back, its elements, the keyboard in Find and the word typed there kept; and, at every size, that no post crosses a district's name, a post that crosses a board is under it, and the specimens' "⚠ 4" opens the specimens' problems alone. The pages face alone carries 2,629 bytes more (907 gzipped), every face 3,096 (1,132), and the embed with the studio handed in 957 gzipped, under raises named `one-face-record-in-place-*` in `scripts/budget-raises/`.

The JavaScript API — unchanged (`/problems` takes `?kind=`). Ops and primitives — unchanged. Stored formats — unchanged. The wire — unchanged. The declaration and check finding codes — unchanged. Derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged.
