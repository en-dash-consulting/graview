A workflow sets aside what is done with, asks about a run as one suggestion, and follows rules its people keep as records, Roadmap f-retention-rules, f-job-approvals and f-job-rule-inputs. A done grocery list stayed in every list until somebody archived it by hand, a workflow whose changes were to be approved first left one suggestion per change in the Suggested tray — twenty-eight rows for one Sunday's events — and the rules an owner wanted a workflow to keep lived in its prompt, where only the author could change them.

A workflow's step may now be `{ "archive": "grocery-list", "where": "status == 'done'", "after": "3 days" }` (or `"after": { "field": "doneOn", "days": 7 }`): each record of the kind the rule holds of, once it has stood that long since it last changed or after the date it holds, is archived through its kind's own `archive-<kind>` — never removed — one change per record under the run's lineage, so Activity says the run as one row. It passes by a record a person said to leave alone, every record of a kind the app says is never tidied, a record a person brought back after this workflow archived it (an undo of its archive, or "Bring it back"), and a record a rule it follows keeps, and says each. No model is asked. The checker says `workflow-kind-unknown`, `workflow-where-invalid`, `workflow-archive-never` (a kind that says `archives: false`) and `workflow-archive-after-invalid` (a wait that does not read as hours, days or weeks, or a field the kind does not have). `archiveStep`, `afterMs` and `afterWords` are on `@graview/core/workflows`, `runArchiveStep` on `@graview/tools/workflows`.

A workflow whose `approve` is "ask" now gathers its whole run into ONE suggestion (`askedOf`, `@graview/tools/workflows`): each change rehearsed as it is asked — refused there if the store would refuse it, left out if it changes nothing or a person declined it — and kept as one op whose `batchIntent` says the run as one ("Fill out next week's events: 28 events to add, 3 to change, 1 to archive", `proposalWords` on `@graview/core/tidying`), whose `suggestsSaid` keeps each call's own sentence, and whose lineage names the run. A later run's group takes the place of the one still waiting (withdrawn, which is no decline); a run that asks exactly what still waits leaves it standing. The tray says the run once after who suggests it, opens to "Show the 28 changes", each in its act's sentence with its own Accept (`Accept: <sentence>`); the row's "Accept all" takes what still waits and Decline the rest. `acceptSuggestion(store, id, { items })` takes some of a suggestion's calls by their places, its lineage naming them (`lineage.items`), and the rest wait; `suggestions()` lists what waits as `items: { at, call, intent }[]` with the maker's `headline`; a decline after a partial acceptance declines only what was not taken. A reader votes on the whole, and is offered no change of their own. An edit of a change before accepting it is not in this build.

A workflow may say `"follows": "<kind>"`: the app's own records of that kind are rules it follows, read by one convention — `action` (or `rule`, `instruction`, `text`; the record's label when none) the rule verbatim, `order` (or `rank`, `priority`) lower first, `appliesTo` a kind's name or plural or a rule-language condition read on each record, and `status` retired, off, inactive, paused, draft or archived (or `active: false`) to set one aside. An agent step is handed the rules that stand, numbered, after its instruction; an archive step passes by every record a rule whose action begins "keep" or "never archive" applies to. `policiesOf`, `policyApplies`, `policyInstruction`, `keeps` and `followsFindings` (`workflow-follows-unknown`) are on `@graview/core/workflows`.

A lineage that names which of a suggestion's changes a person took, a decline that leaves those out, and the tray's list of a run's changes cost a hosted page 0.3 KB more up front (338 bytes minified, 92 gzipped), and a page handed a compiled app 0.3 KB (338 / 111), under raises naming this changeset; the pages face alone 338 / 95 more, a visitor's form alone 338 / 104, a visitor's form handed a compiled app 338 / 116, every face 5_909 / 1_889 the embed with the studio handed in 4_095 / 1_378 and Cloud's worker 6_694 bytes, each under a raise of its own; the scene face before it draws measured 1_079_378 B (1055 KB).

Compatibility: ops and primitives — two optional additions and no primitive: a suggestion's op may carry `suggestsSaid`, and a lineage may name `items`, which `lineageOf` holds to at most 500 whole numbers; an op without either reads and folds as before, and a build before this one keeps both, lists the suggestion's calls whole and reads a partial acceptance as the whole suggestion taken. Stored formats — the same two fields, and nothing moves. The wire — unchanged in shape: a partial acceptance travels as a `call` with its lineage. The declaration and check finding codes — a workflow step may be `archive`, a workflow may say `follows`, and `workflow-archive-never`, `workflow-archive-after-invalid` and `workflow-follows-unknown` are new; nothing that compiled before is refused. Derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged. What Graview Cloud does: takes this build; its tray draws a run's suggestion as one, and a host that lists suggestions over MCP may offer `items` to accept some of a run's changes; a workflow it authors may archive on a schedule and keep its rules as records an owner edits.
