A list reads well by default. On the Program's Sessions grouped by day, the title stood at forty pixels, every row's name was set bold and wrapped to a third and fourth line on a phone so every row shouted and nothing was secondary, each row said "Sep 25, 2017" under a heading that said "Sep 25, 2017", its date stood at whatever x its length left it, and the related links were underlined heavily in the line's own ink. The routed face now sets its words on one scale, a minor third from the reader's own rem, written by the theme as four custom properties: `--graview-text-small` (0.833rem: a row's date, a count, a relation, a group's heading), `--graview-text-body` (1rem: reading text and a row's name), `--graview-text-heading` (1.2rem: a section) and `--graview-text-title` (1.728rem: a page's title, where it was 30 to 40 pixels by the window's width); the sizes the pages had picked between them, from 0.75rem to 1.4375rem and a title by the window's width, are gone, and the static export reads the same four. A row of a kind's list, and a hit on the search page, is one anatomy: its name at the reading size and a medium weight, two lines at most with the rest on its page (and in its title when it is long), the whole row its link with a hover, a press and a keyboard ring of its own; its glance small and muted in a column of its own on a desk, one column for the whole list so the dates down it start at one x, and under the name where the list is narrower than 36rem. A list's rows are one line or two, so it keeps two heights. A row leaves off its glance the field its group is by (a date grouped by day, unless the row's value has a time the heading does not say) and a choice the list is narrowed to one value of. A group's heading is small and semibold in the body face, its count beside it, and over the framework's own rows stays at the top of the scroll on a desk. A link on a page is in the accent `graview check` holds to 4.5:1 on the ground, the same visited or not, with a hairline underline at half strength. A page's end clears the ask field by its measured height and a phone's home bar. A declared row's title on a list, and the kind stop's heading, header row, small text and names (at a medium weight), read the same scale. The graview-brand skill says how an app moves a step.

Compatibility: ops, stored formats, the wire, the declaration and derived tools are unchanged. The look moves (docs/stability.md, "Not a surface: the look"): four new custom properties, `--graview-text-small`, `-body`, `-heading` and `-title`, which an app's own stylesheet may set to move a step everywhere it is used. A brand that names its own faces and weights keeps them: headings on the routed face no longer set their own family and weight but take the theme's heading rule (`--graview-font-display`, `--graview-weight-display`), so a brand's display weight now wins where 600 was written over it; a group's heading is set in `--graview-font-body` at `--graview-weight-label`. A row of the derived list is `li.graview-row` holding `a.graview-row-title` and `span.graview-row-glance` (a search hit is the same, still `[data-testid="search-hit"]`); a group's or a section's heading is `h2.graview-group-head`; `record-row`, `records`, `list-group`, `list-section` and `list-index` are as they were. Links on the routed face are drawn in `--graview-accent`, where they were the text's own ink.
