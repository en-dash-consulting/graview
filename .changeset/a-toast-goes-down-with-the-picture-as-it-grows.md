A toast goes down with its picture as the picture grows. A toast raised while the picture it stands over was still short — a hosted page as tall as what it drew, with only the bar and a view's first lines drawn yet — stood at that first foot and stayed there as the rest of the page arrived under it, over the view's own content and taking its presses, until a scroll, a resize or a change to the toast itself happened to place it again. On Graview Cloud's hosted page a view's link under it could not be clicked. The stack now watches its picture's size as well as its own, and is placed again at the new foot the moment the picture grows or shrinks; a picture that becomes a new element is watched in its stead. The hold on a toast under the pointer or the keyboard is unchanged. Held by a unit test of the primitives (`notices-float-and-never-move-the-page`: "stands at the picture's new foot once the picture has grown under it").

Compatibility: Host APIs — unchanged. Behavior a host may notice — a toast over an embed whose height follows its content moves down as the content arrives, where it stayed at the foot it was first placed at. The wire, stored formats, ops and primitives, derived tool names and input schemas, and the declaration and check finding codes — unchanged. What Graview Cloud does: nothing.
