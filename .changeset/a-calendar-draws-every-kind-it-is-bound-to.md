A calendar draws every kind it is bound to. Registered over one kind and bound to two — a dealership's diary over its test drives, bound to test drives and service appointments — it drew only the group's members and never read the other kind. The other bound kinds now come from the graph, on the same horizon the group keeps (their past only when the stop asks for it), after the group's own arranging.
