A view's place fits a phone. On Graview Cloud at 390 pixels, a view with something wider than the screen in it — an SVG 640 wide in a box that scrolls sideways, a row of tabs — widened the page: the region a view draws in (`[data-worker-view-place]`) was a grid item at its content's min-content width, so the home grew to 674 pixels and a race page to 1,040 in a 350 column, and the page clipped on the right. The region is now `min-width: 0`, so what is wide scrolls inside the view and the page never widens (Roadmap f-view-place-min-width). `bleed: true` took the gutter back on the home and a record's page but did nothing on a view titled as a place; a place now draws a view that bleeds edge to edge on a phone as they do (f-bleed-on-places). And where a view that bleeds did not draw — refused, past its limits, or not fetched yet — what is drawn in its stead ran edge to edge too; it is now marked (`data-worker-view-fallback`, laid out as if it were not there) and the bleed passes it by, so the app's own page keeps its gutters (f-fallback-lens-loses-gutter). The graview-view-drawing skill says a place bleeds too. `pnpm verify address` asks four more things, at 390 and 1440: a view's wide content never widens the page on a phone, on a place and a record's page; a place declared bleed draws edge to edge; a fallback page keeps its gutters; and, as before, the home and a record's view that bleed span the phone. The pages face alone carries 0.6 KB more, minified (0.1 KB gzipped), and the guest host 0.4 KB more when a view is registered, under raises (`scripts/budget-raises/views-on-phones-and-place-chrome-*.json`) shared with the next two changes.

Compatibility: ops and primitives — unchanged. Snapshot and log formats — unchanged. The wire — unchanged. The declaration and check finding codes, derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged. The JavaScript API — unchanged; a view drawn in a worker view's stead stands in a `div[data-worker-view-fallback]` with `display: contents`. What Graview Cloud does: takes this build; a view's `contain: inline-size` workaround is no longer needed, and `describe_place` may say a place's view bleeds where its manifest says `bleed: true`, which now holds on a place as on the home and a record.
