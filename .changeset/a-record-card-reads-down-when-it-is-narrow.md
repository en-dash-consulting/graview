A record's card reads down when it is narrower than its two columns. Seedbed's plot card on a phone held its facts and its relationships side by side, 200 and 220 pixels in a card 293 wide, and its relationships ran past the card and the screen, cut off. The two stand side by side, in the same proportion, where both fit, and one under the other where they do not.

Compatibility: no surface changes.
