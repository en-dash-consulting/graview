A view the host keeps is a place in the nav, and a kind kept off the places leaves the Pages drop-down. Roadmap q-custom-views-in-nav, filed from the hosted En Dash Social Media Playbook: its "To do" is a script view a chat drew and the host keeps by its manifest, and it showed only as an "Also as" link on the tasks list — `arrange-pages` refused it in `order` ('"to-do" is not a kind this app has') and in `first` ('"To do" is not a place, a kind or "home"'), since the declaration cannot know a host's views. The declaration now names one as `view:<its manifest name>` in `pages.order`, `pages.first` and `pages.primary`. Where the host hands its views' manifests to the edit (`EditOptions.views`, `HostView`: `name`, `title`, `attach`), `arrange-pages` takes the view's title or name and writes `view:<name>` (`order: ["To do"]` is `["view:to-do"]`), and refuses a name the host does not keep, naming the ones it does; unsaid, it takes `view:<name>` on the host's word and says "(once the host registers it)". `graview check` and the checked `compileDocument` (`CheckOptions.views`, `CompileOptions.views`) say a `view:` name no view registered here has is a note, `pages-view-not-drawn-here` — not drawn here yet, it stands where it is named once the host registers it — and, told the host's views, a name none of them is a warning, `pages-view-unknown`. On the page, the bar's places list a registered view `pages.order` names among the Lists at its place in the order (`arrangedEntries`), standing on a wide bar unless `pages.primary` says others; `pages.first` opens on it (`openingOf` reads `view:<name>` by the registration's name); the home's gallery puts it first. `placesOf(app, { views })` lists the host's titled pictures of a kind, each with `view: <name>`, where the order names them and `first` where `pages.first` does — what a host lists when an app is opened, and the places a drawn view's `data-place` is judged against.

And a kind `pages.hide` names really leaves the Pages drop-down: it was dropped from the home only and folded into More, so the playbook's eleven reference lists made the drop-down "insanely long". It is listed only while it is the place you are on, so the bar still says where you are, and it still opens at its address, in Find and from wherever it is linked. `set-lens-hidden` on a kind's name or plural (`title: "source"`) writes `pages.hide` where no lens has that title, refused where `pages.primary` stands the kind; the place menu's Hide on a kind's list, for whoever may change the declaration, hands the host's `keptViews.lens` that edit, and the drop-down's Hidden lists each kept-off list with Show. `arrange-pages` and the preview of a change say "the places and the front page leave off …". `core`'s tests name a view by title and by `view:<name>`, with and without the host's views, refuse the rest in words, check the note and the warning, list and open the view, and hide and show a kind's list; `pages`' render the drop-down with the view first among the lists and without the hidden kinds, and the hidden list at its address; `embed`'s hide a list from the place menu and show one from Hidden. `pnpm verify address` mounts the tasks as Cloud's shell does with a script view registered as `to-do` and `pages: { order: ["view:to-do", "task"], first: "view:to-do", hide: ["person"] }`: the bare address opens on it, it stands first after Home, and the people are not listed and still open at `/people`. A hosted page carries 0.9 KB more up front (903 bytes, about 340 gzipped; the compiled page the same), the bar's arranged list and the hidden kinds' Show, under a raise of each budget by that (`scripts/budget-raises/a-kept-view-is-a-place-in-the-nav-*.json`); the pages face alone 1.4 KB more (1_479, 572 gzipped), and the scene face's before-draw line is 964 KB.

Compatibility: additive for the declaration — `pages.order`, `pages.first` and `pages.primary` may name `view:<name>`, which a build before 0.1.44 reads as a kind or place it does not have and passes over (`graview check` warned of it), and `arrange-pages` takes a title or `view:<name>` in `order` where it took only kinds. A behavior change for `pages.hide`: a kind it names is kept off the bar's places as well as the home. `set-lens-hidden` takes a kind where no lens has the title, where it refused. Additive in the API: `HostView`, `arrangedEntries`, `viewReference`, `ArrangedEntry`, `EditOptions.views`, `CompileOptions.views`, `CheckOptions` and `checkApp`'s second argument, `placesOf`'s `views`, `AppPlace.view`, `BarPlace.hidden`; `arrangementFindings` takes `{ views }` where it took a list of places. The wire, the stored formats and the derived tools are unchanged.
