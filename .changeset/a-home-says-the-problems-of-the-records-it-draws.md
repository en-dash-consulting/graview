A home says the problems of the records it draws, and no others (Roadmap f-problems-in-context). The derived home said "Rules are broken — see what, and what would fix it" in the warning color for a rule broken on any record anywhere, so a home whose own records were all in order opened on a warning about a kind it does not show. The home now points to a problem only when it names a record its gallery or its jobs draw (`drawnInTheGallery`); a rule broken on a kind the home leaves off (`pages.hide`) is said by the bar's count alone, which still opens the whole list, and the home says neither the warning nor "All rules hold." A home of the app's own (a home view, a worker view or blocks) is handed the broken records as `flagged`, as a place's lens is, to mark where it draws them, and the page says nothing over it. A rule the app wrote that a record does not meet is said in the page's quiet ink (`data-graview-problems-tone="rule"`); a rule that could not be judged at all, in the warning color (`"error"`). What a store enforces is unchanged, and a list or place that draws a broken record still marks it on that record. A hosted page carries nothing more up front; the pages face alone carries 0.5 KB more minified and 0.2 KB gzipped, and every face 0.5 KB more gzipped, with the Suggested tray's wrapping and Activity's merges beside it (`scripts/budget-raises/a-home-says-the-problems-of-the-records-it-draws-*.json`).

Compatibility: ops and primitives, stored formats, the wire, the declaration and check finding codes, derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged. The look: the home's problem line is `[data-testid="problems-here"]`, and a home view's props carry `flagged`.
