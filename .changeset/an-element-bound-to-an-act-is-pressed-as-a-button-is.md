Any element a worker view binds to an act is pressed as a button is, and a headless run presses one and says what it would send. The guide said "a data-act button", and Graview Cloud's Threat Watch drew `<span role="button" data-act>` inside a fieldset: whether the host honored it, by a tap or by the keyboard, was found out only on the page, and `propose_view` could not press anything. Now it is decided and said. Any element with `data-act` is a press — a `<span role="button">`, a `<div>`, a badge, an SVG `<g>` — pressed by a click on it or on anything inside it, and a phone's tap is a click. One that does not press of itself is given the keyboard (`tabindex="0"`), is a button to assistive technology unless it says another role, is drawn with a pointer and `touch-action: manipulation` (`data-graview-press`, looked at again after every batch the view draws), and Enter or Space on it presses it, Space without scrolling the page; a button's own Enter, which clicks it already, is not a second press. Three things are never pressed: an `<option>`, a `<label>` (a press on it presses its field too, and would ask twice), and a field to type into or pick from that does not save (a click into it to type was taken for a press); a headless run says each among its problems, in a sentence naming what to do instead.

`runWorkerViewHeadless(…, { press: "<selector>" })`, or `{ press: { selector, typed: { note: "…" } } }`, presses the first element the selector matches once the view has drawn, as `graview.on` matches one, and answers `pressed` beside the description, judged in the host's context by the live host's own rules over what the view drew before the press: the link it lands in and where that goes, or why it goes nowhere; the act a bound press asks for with the arguments it would carry — the bound record, the manifest's constants, the fields in its fieldset, typed only where `typed` says and otherwise as the view left them or as the host would prefill them — and whether this seat's store would apply it, rehearsed with `previewAll` and never applied, with its sentence, or the refusal in the words the view would hear (`refused`, `untyped`, `undeclared` …); and what the view's own code did on hearing the click: the acts it asked for, where it asked to go, and whether it drew again; with all of it said in `pressed.says`. A selector that matches nothing, or a link that goes nowhere, is a problem of the description. `graview view check view.js … --press "<selector>" [--type name=value]…` prints the same. `describe_place` and `propose_view` can check a press before a view ships.

`guest`'s tests drive the host's press wiring over Threat Watch: a `<span role="button">` in a fieldset, a `<div>` and an SVG `<g>` bound to acts are given the keyboard and a button's role, and pressed by a click inside them carrying the words typed beside them, by Enter and by Space; a button's Enter is not pressed twice; a select and a label are never pressed. A headless run presses a card's "Acknowledge" span and says the act and the record it would carry, applied for the analyst and refused in the policy's words for a stranger, nothing applied; a press on a card's heading opens the card's record; the note typed is carried and an untyped one is not; a toggle's press is the view's own and it drew again, the description being of what it drew before; a selector that matches nothing is a problem; and the describer says a label, an option and a field that does not save are never pressed. `node scripts/guest-sandbox.mjs --transport=taps` holds, in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox, that the span is a press the host made, that Space on a card's "Acknowledge" acknowledges the threat, the view hearing it and the card not opening, and that Enter on a fieldset's "Save the note" saves the note typed beside it; and on a phone in Chromium and WebKit, that a tap on "Acknowledge" acknowledges it and opens nothing and a tap on "Save the note" saves it. `graview-view-forms` says what can be pressed and how to press it headless. The guest host drawing a worker view on the open kit carries 1.7 KB more (1_693 bytes, 784 gzipped), under a raise of that budget (`scripts/budget-raises/an-element-bound-to-an-act-is-pressed-as-a-button-is-open-kit.json`); a worker view's runtime 82 bytes, within its budget; nothing a hosted page loads up front grew.

Compatibility: the wire between a view and its host is unchanged — a press is still the person's click, or now their Enter or Space, judged by the host. A view's elements change: an element other than a button bound to an act is given `tabindex="0"` and, where it has no meaning of its own, `role="button"`; a `select`, a text field or a `textarea` with `data-act` and no `data-save`, a `label` and an `option` with `data-act` are no longer pressed by a click (give a field `data-save`, or bind a button). Additive in the API: `press` on `runWorkerViewHeadless`'s options, `pressed` (`HeadlessPress`) on its answer, `judgeHeadlessPress`, `press` on `HeadlessInput` and `pressed` on `HeadlessTranscript` (a host that runs its own isolate passes the input through as ever), `find` on the view runtime, and `graview view check --press` and `--type`. The stored formats, the declaration and the derived tools are unchanged.
