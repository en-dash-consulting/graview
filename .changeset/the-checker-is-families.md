The checker is a sequence of families. `checkApp` builds one context — the app, its kinds, its acts and rules, and the way a finding is said — and asks each family in `cli/check/` in the order it always asked them, so a report reads exactly as it did: routes, lenses, the way in, providers, the city, intelligence, settings, migrations, brand, modules, relations, fields, policy. Two comments that had drifted from their checks went back to them, and `bounded`'s comment now says what it does.
