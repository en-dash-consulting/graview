A host admits a call by what it would do, and a change refused before it is kept leaves nothing. A host's caps lived in its own wrapper around its acts — Graview Cloud's "an app holds at most so many records" and "an agent may remove at most 25 records in one call" — and `live.post` and the socket go round it, so they had to move into `limit`; but `limit` is asked before a call is compiled and cannot count what the call would do, and the spike rehearsed every agent call on a copy of the store to count its removals. Now `liveProtocol({ admit })` — and `createStoreHandler` and `serveStore`, by the same name — is asked after the calls (or the undo) are compiled and applied, before anything is kept, with `limit`'s `asked` and the store's own plan of the change, `PlannedChange`: its ops and primitives, the records and links it adds, removes and changes (a removed record's links among the links removed), and `nodesAfter` and `edgesAfter`. Its answers mean what `limit`'s do — `{ refuse }` refused `limit` (413 over HTTP), `{ retryAfter }` busy (429), `{ unavailable }` refused `unavailable` (503) — and a change it answers is put back: not kept, flushed or sent to anybody. No second rehearsal: it is `applyAll` and `undo` asking, through `ApplyOptions.admit`, which a host can hand the store directly; whatever it throws goes on up and the store is as if the change was never asked. And a batch refused part way — its second call naming a record that is not there, say — put the graph back but kept the first call's op in the log, a log that no longer folded to its graph and an op a host would flush and push for a change nobody made: the log is now cut back with the graph.

Compatibility: additive. `ApplyOptions` gains `admit`; `PlannedChange` is a new type export of `@graview/core`; `LiveProtocolOptions` and the handler's options gain `admit`. A batch refused part way now leaves the log as it was; one that relied on the earlier calls' ops staying was relying on a log the graph did not hold. The wire, `WIRE_PROTOCOL`, ops, stored formats and check codes are unchanged.
