A road and a connector say which two things they join: each `.graview-road` carries `data-graview-from` and `data-graview-to` with the ids its two plots wear in `data-graview-plot`, and each `path[data-graview-connector]` carries the ids its two ends land on, in the edge's direction. A design that lights the roads touching a district is a selector's work, not a path parsed back to the plot nearest each end.
