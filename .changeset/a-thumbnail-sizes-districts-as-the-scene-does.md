`sceneThumbnail` sizes each district by its count the way the live Scene does (FR-103). It already took `counts`, sized each plot by them, and stood a village on a populated district. But past 160 buildings it stood one block on each district, so a twelve-kind app with full districts drew twelve towers where the Scene draws twelve villages. And the village it drew was its own call to `villageOf`, not one held to the Scene's. `sceneDistricts` now returns each district's `village`, the buildings `villageOf` stands on its plot, one per member up to what the plot holds, and its `rest`. The thumbnail draws exactly those. A picture now holds up to 300 buildings, twelve full districts, and keeps the rule that a building must still be 4 px wide. Past either limit it stands a block on each district, as before. To pay for the extra buildings, each face is now a `<path>` in whole units with relative corners, about 170 bytes a building where it was 230. A tile of twelve full districts is 51 KB, and LifeLogics with its seed's counts is 4.7 KB where it was 6.4 KB. A layout test holds every district's plot, building feet, footprints and `rest` against the layout the Scene runs, with that layout's own members, at none, one, many, the plot's cap and past it. It also checks that the picture stands that many buildings. Only a building's height differs from the Scene's: the Scene's comes from the member's id, and counts carry no ids.

Compatibility: `SceneDistrict` gains `village` and `rest`. A thumbnail's buildings are drawn as `<path>` rather than `<polygon>`, and the same document and counts still give the same bytes every time. `capabilities().shipped` gains `FR-103`. Ops, stored formats, wire messages, check codes and tool schemas are unchanged.
