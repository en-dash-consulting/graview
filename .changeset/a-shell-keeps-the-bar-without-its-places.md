A shell with a rail of its own keeps the bar and goes without its places: `surface("shell", Shell, { without: ["places"] })`. n-dx's face lists its places in a rail, and the only way to stop the bar listing them again was `without: ["bar"]`, which also took away the switch between the scene and the pages, Find, the standing, who is here and the person. With `"places"`, the bar keeps every one of those and names no place on the pages, on the app's own address and inside an embed handed the same registry (`pages`). The scene's pictures are the scene's, and stay.

Compatibility: unchanged.
