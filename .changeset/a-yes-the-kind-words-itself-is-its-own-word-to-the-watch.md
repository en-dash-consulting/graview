A yes or a no a kind words itself is its own word to a watching harness (the ninth walk, W-193). A hive's `queenSeen`, labeled "Queen" and said "Queen seen" when true, was told to the watch as a key spoken where the declaration says otherwise, so every page that drew an inspection card was reported for showing "Queen seen". The worded values of a boolean field now count among the declaration's own words, as an enum's already did.

Compatibility: only what a page tells a watching harness (`globalThis.__graviewWatch`) changes; nothing a person sees, stores or sends.
