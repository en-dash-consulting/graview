A Notion connection lands each change through an act that fits it, found by an app that declared more than the connection needed (North Pole). A page made in Notion becomes a record through an act that creates the kind, takes no subject and requires nothing but fields the connection maps — `add-<kind>` when it fits, else the first declared act that does (`creatingAct(app, kind, mapped)`, the same choice the checker makes). It took `add-<kind>` or else the first act that created the kind, and North Pole's first was `log-health-check`, which logs a check on a reindeer: every page from Notion was refused ("names a record that is not there") and `graview check` said nothing. Now, when every act that makes the kind takes a subject or asks for a field the connection leaves out, `graview check` says so at `connections.<name>.kind` (`connection-creates`: "a page made in the database becomes a health check through an act that makes one from the page's fields alone, and log-health-check acts on a reindeer"), naming what to declare. A Google calendar's connection chooses the same way.

A change in Notion to a field another act writes lands too. It lands through the derived `edit-<kind>`, which covered only the fields no declared act writes, so a toy whose status `set-toy-status` moves and whose count `add-made` adds to could not be kept in step, and the checker refused the connection (`connection-edit`) until the app declared its own `edit-toy` writing every field. A kind's fields a declared connection maps are now kept in step (`keptInStep` on its definition, which the compile writes from `connections`), and its derived edit writes them whoever else does. Inbound changes keep one write path — a named act, judged by the policy and the invariants, logged and undoable, credited to Notion — rather than a second, system-only one that would bypass both; and a field Notion can set to anything is not one only a named step changes, so the record's own edit says so. A kind without a connection derives the edit it did. `connection-edit` is now said of an app's own `edit-<kind>` that leaves a mapped field out, and of a mapped field declared `fixed`.

A page that cannot become a record is said once. A blank row (no name, no date) was refused on every pass, the serve log repeating it every 30 seconds; the engine now keeps it in the sync state as skipped (`SyncState.skipped`, by the page's id, at the version refused), says nothing more of it, and makes it again only once its version changes in Notion — once it is filled in, it arrives.

`graview create --template` writes `tests/domain.test.ts` with the `checkApp` and `instantiateTemplate` it calls imported from `@graview/core/check`; `src/domain/app.ts` imported them instead and used neither. A scaffold test now compiles every TypeScript file a project starts with and fails on a name used and not imported, or imported and not used, with a template and without.

`scripts/verify-notion.mjs` holds four claims more against a workspace — the checker passing a connection whose fields another act writes, a page landing through the act that fits it, a status changed in Notion landing through `edit-<kind>`, a blank row said once and arriving once filled — 32 claims, all held live. The graview-sync skill says what a change lands through. The compile and the checker a page that compiles its document carries cost a hosted page 1.3 KB more up front (1,281 bytes minified, 406 gzipped), and a page handed a compiled app 0.2 KB (182, 38), under raises naming this changeset; every face 715 bytes more gzipped, the pages face alone 118 / 14 and a visitor's form handed a compiled app 182 / 66, each under a raise of its own. Cloud's worker grows 5,092 bytes, past the 562 its budget had left: its bytes are now raised by a file like the bundles' (`"budget": "Cloud's worker"`, `bytes`), never by an edit of the base, and this is the first.

Compatibility: ops and primitives and the wire — unchanged. Stored formats — additive: a `SyncState` is format 1 as before and may carry `skipped`, which a build that predates it drops, saying a blank page once more. The declaration and check finding codes — no new code: `connection-creates` is also said when every act that makes the kind takes a subject or requires a field the connection does not map, and `connection-edit` no longer of a mapped field another act writes. A compiled kind's plan may carry `keptInStep`. Derived tool names and input schemas — a kind kept by a connection has an `edit-<kind>` that takes every mapped field, where it took only those no other act writes; a kind without one is unchanged. `creatingAct` on `@graview/core/sync` takes the fields the connection maps. What Graview Cloud does: nothing for the acts; a host that keeps a connection's `SyncState` keeps `skipped` with it.
