A record page with a page slot and a view of its own says where each is drawn. Threat Watch, on Graview Cloud, gave a kind page blocks ("Read the sources", set with `set-view slot: "page"`) and a record view, and `describe_place` said neither the slot nor any sign of it, while on a kind with no view the slot headed the page. On the routed face the order is one and is now written down: the view, then the slot's blocks, then the facts they did not say — and the facts did say again what the slot said, since the view carried none of the slot's `says`; `registerWorkerView` now hands it on (FR-141), so the record page leaves those out under a view as it does without one. A view whose manifest says `replaces: "page"` is the whole page, as before, and the slot is never drawn. `describePlace` takes the host's views (`DescribePlaceOptions.views`, each a `DescribedHostView`: its manifest's `name`, `title`, `attach`, `cardinality` and `replaces`, and the `parts` a headless run drew), and a record's page whose kind has a view of one says a new part, `{ t: "view" }`, at its head, then the slot, then the facts; where the view replaces the page it says the view alone, is drawn by `view:<name>`, and says a problem at `views.<kind>.page`: the slot is never drawn. `shadowedPageSlots(app, views)` on `@graview/core/describe` says the same problems for a host's list beside `graview check`'s, which does not see the host's views. `graview-view-specs` says where a page slot draws beside a view and what `replaces` does to it, and `graview-worker-view` says the same in a line. `core`'s tests describe a record with a view that sits above the page, with none, and with one that replaces it; `guest`'s draw each on the pages face and find the slot under the view and over the facts, a fact the slot said not said again, and no slot under a view that replaces the page; `verify-address` draws both at 390 and 1440 (aRecordPagesSlotIsDrawnUnderItsViewAndAboveTheFacts, aViewThatReplacesThePageDrawsAloneAndItsSlotIsNot). The guest host registering a worker view carries 71 bytes more (23 gzipped), under a raise of its budget by that (`scripts/budget-raises/a-records-page-slot-is-drawn-under-its-view-guest-host.json`).

Compatibility: additive in the API — `DescribePlaceOptions.views`, `DescribedHostView` and `shadowedPageSlots`; a `DescribedPart` may now be `{ t: "view" }`, said only when the host passes its views, so a description asked for as before reads as before. A behavior change on the record page under a view that sits above the page: a fact the kind's page blocks say is not listed again among the facts. The manifest, the declaration, the wire, the stored formats and the derived tools are unchanged.
