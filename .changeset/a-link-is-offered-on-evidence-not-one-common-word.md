The app's own actor offers a link on evidence, not on one common word (FR-224), Roadmap f-tidying-link-evidence-not-one-word. A far record was put forward whenever one four-letter word of its name turned up in the near record's text — `its text says “just”`, `“blueprint”` — and a model then judged the pair at 0.55 to 0.76 and was paid for it, filling the tray with links nobody would make; a host's bands hid them without stopping the asking. Blocking now counts a word of a far record's name only when it is telling — not a stop word or a common one (just, things, week, plan, doing…), and held by few of the kind's names, three at most — and a far record named in three or more words is never put forward on one word alone: two of its telling words, its whole name or one of its key values (an email, a code) are what name it. A goal named "Why am I doing this, and for whom?" beside a note that says "You can just do things." is no candidate at all, and neither is "Just ship it, today"; "Harbor Catering" beside "Call harbor about the menu" still is. The same telling rule holds for the word two records share on an empty one-link relation. Tools' tests hold it (`a-link-is-offered-on-evidence-not-one-common-word.test.ts`). The common-word set and the telling rule put Cloud's worker 21 bytes over a limit it had no room under, so its budget is raised by 600 B (`scripts/budget-raises/a-link-is-offered-on-evidence-not-one-common-word-worker.json`); nothing a page loads up front changed. FR-224 is said as shipped.

Compatibility: ops and primitives, stored formats, the declaration and check finding codes, and derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged. The wire: `capabilities().shipped` gains FR-224. What Graview Cloud does: nothing; its bands may stay, and fewer candidates reach them.
