A search hit says its address (FR-129). Graview Cloud searches each of a person's apps with `search` under the seat's own sight and opens each record at its address, and it worked that address out itself from `placesOf`: the kind's place, then the id. A record hit now carries `address`, its path on the routed face as the face's own record link spells it (`/<plural>/<id>`, the id encoded), under `basePath` when the search is given one, as `addressOf` spells it. A kind hit carries its list's address and a place hit its place's (`/places/<as>`, with `?of=` when two kinds share the name, as `placesOf` gives it). Search also judged sight only kind by kind, so a seat that may see only its own records of a kind (`own`) was handed every record of it, and the address of each, when it searched the store itself rather than `seenBy`; it now finds only the records the seat may open. A test in `@graview/embed` draws the routed face under a base path and holds every record link on every kind's list to the address a search for that record gives: a renamed plural, a kind with no plural, and ids that need encoding.
Compatibility: additive. `Hit` gains `address` on its `node`, `kind` and `place` forms and `SearchOptions` gains `basePath`; ranking, counts and every other field are unchanged, and a search through `seenBy`, as the agent's `search_graph` makes, finds what it found. A search of the store itself by a seat with an `own` sight no longer finds records that are not its own. Ops, stored formats, the document format, wire messages, check codes and tool schemas are unchanged; `search_graph`'s answer carries the new field. `capabilities().shipped` gains FR-129.
