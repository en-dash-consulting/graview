Three skills that had reached the 11,000 characters a skill is held under are split along their seams, and each half has one job. `graview-embed` had 4 characters free and could not teach 0.1.39's drawn signal or draft views; it keeps the embed on somebody else's page — the stop, the face, the name, the look, seats, many on one page, the views, a framed view of somebody's HTML — and `graview-host-page` is new: a host whose page is the app, with the address bar handed over (`routing: "address"`, `basePath`), a chat's widget sized from the embed, a hosted reader sitting only as who signed in, the host's own menu, news and first look inside the app, `setApp` keeping the reader's place and `preload`; when a place has drawn and settled (`data-graview-drawn`, `mount`'s `onDrawn(place)` told the `EmbedWhere`, FR-188); a view proposed and not kept (`draftView`, FR-187); and pictures of places with `graview preview` and `graview shots`, `--view <view.js> --manifest <manifest.json>` included. `graview-worker-view` had 2 characters free and had shaved sentences to fit FR-191; it keeps the manifest, the global, links, limits, registering and the headless run, and has them back — the `Intl.DateTimeFormat` with `hour` and `minute`, the section looked for on the scene, the "stag" filter's sentence, drawing summaries, the heartbeat's 5 s, the home full width on a desk, and the pictures' line — while `graview-view-forms` is new: a bound press, a record's own text filled to edit (`data-prefill`), a field that saves as it is typed (`data-save`, `data-debounce` held to 250–10 000) and the view hearing what became of each act (`acted`), with `examples/a-call-sheet.js` moved beside it. `graview-lens` had 2 characters free; it keeps building a lens that binds roles and proving its reuse, and `graview-shipped-lens` is new: declaring a calendar, a status board, a coverage, a timeline, a board, a plan or a blocks place as a titled place, a table of what each binds and every option it takes (held to `SHIPPED_LENSES` by a test), and what `graview check` and `add-lens` refuse — `lens-option-unknown`, `lens-cannot-draw`, `lens-title-taken` and the rest. Each half points at the other in one line, and every one is under 10,000 characters. `graview skills install` installs twenty-two skills.

Compatibility: ops, stored formats, the wire, the declaration and derived tools are unchanged. A product's `.claude/skills` and `.agents/skills` gain `graview-host-page`, `graview-view-forms` and `graview-shipped-lens` on its next `graview skills install`, and the AGENTS.md list names them.
