Named edits for the people kind, grants and sights (FR-170). `add-kind` and `add-act` passed `people: true` and `$me` through, but nothing set `people` on a kind already there and no edit touched the policy, so an own-end grant or a sight via a relation could only be made by a raw JSON patch, which a builder's form or a chat's `propose_change` cannot offer as a named change previewed in words. `editDocument` takes five more ops. `set-people` (`{ "kind": "participant", "people": true }`) says which kind is the people who sign in, and refuses a second one with a sentence as `people-ambiguous`, so a move is `false` on the one, then `true` on the other, in one change. `add-grant` and `add-sight` take a grant or a sight as the policy holds one (`roles`, `mutations` and `kinds`, `self`, `ownEnd`, `describe`; `roles`, `kinds` and `own`, `via`): a role the document's `roles` does not list joins it, a first grant says that from now on only what a grant gives is allowed, and a first sight on an app with no policy comes with a grant to everyone of every act, since a sight alone would take every act away. `remove-grant` and `remove-sight` name the one to remove by what it says, never by its place in the list: a grant by its roles and acts, a sight by its roles and kinds, each in any order, any other key given matching too; two that match are refused, asking for the key that tells them apart, and one that matches nothing is refused with the ones there are, in words. Each says what it did in the declaration's words — "A participant may "Change the participant" on participants, only on their own record", "A participant sees matches, only those "givenBy" joins to their own participant" — and `diffDocuments` previews the change in the same sentences, where a policy changed was "Who may do what changes.". What the change leaves the people and the policy saying together is judged once it is whole, by the checker's own codes at its last such edit: `people-unsaid`, `sight-via-unknown-edge`, `sight-via-misses-people` and `grant-own-end-without-link`. The Secret Santa's and the idea board's people, grants and sights are each made from nothing by these edits alone, equal to their fixtures. The studio says a grant, a sight and the people kind it changed as these ops, where a grant or a sight was a `studio-unsaid` finding and a kind made the people was not said at all. And a creating act's line leaves out a sentence held in a string, as it leaves out a long text: "Add a feature: Named edits for the people kind, grants and sights (framework, building, 0.1.28)" where the feature's `layerWhy` stood in the parentheses; a value said in over 40 characters, or words ending in a stop, is not said again. A hosted page's first load is 103 bytes smaller (587 155 bytes over the vendors document, 587 258 on main), and 104 smaller handed a compiled app: the new line is paid for by saying a list in words in one place (`listed`). The studio and every face carry about 9 kB more minified, the edits and their sentences. `capabilities().shipped` gains FR-170.
