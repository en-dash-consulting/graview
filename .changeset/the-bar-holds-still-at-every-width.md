The bar holds still at every width. Nick, of "En Dash Social Media Playbook" on Graview Cloud's scene: "the nav does this weird glitching on certain page widths". The bar weighed whether its places stand by the app's name as it was drawn: places standing squeezed the name onto two lines, a name on two lines folded the places into the one control, the name went back to one line and the places stood again, frame after frame, at widths near the edge (964, 1072 and 1100 px among them). The places and the switch's words are now weighed with the name as it would stand on one line, read unwrapped off a ruler of its own, so the answer at a width no longer depends on the answer before it; and the room before Find counts the row's whole gap (Find stands at `margin-left:auto`, not the tools' pull of 8 px), so places that stand leave the name on one line. The name is on two lines beside standing places only when it is wider on one line than its room, and then the places fold as before. A hosted page carries 0.5 KB more up front for it (538 bytes, 143 gzipped; the compiled page 538 / 147), under a raise of each budget by that; the pages face, the embed without the studio, every face and the embed with the studio are raised by what they measured, and the scene face before it draws by 538 bytes. `verify-chrome-quiet` sweeps the bar from 900 to 2100 px and back down, on the scene and on Pages, and holds that nothing in it changes across 600 ms of frames at any width.

Compatibility: ops, stored formats, the wire, the declaration and derived tools are unchanged. Additive on the bar's markup: an `aria-hidden` ruler after the places' one holds the app's name on one line (`.graview-bar-ruler > .graview-bar-name`), and the places' ruler is found by `[data-graview-bar-ruler]`. Which places stand at a width may differ by a place near the edge, since the row is no longer weighed 8 px roomier than it is.
