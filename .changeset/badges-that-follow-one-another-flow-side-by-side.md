Badges that follow one another flow side by side. A card with three badges drew each on a line of its own, and so did a record's page, because a view is a column. A run of badges is one line now, wrapping when it is full, as chips do; a badge alone stands where it is.

Compatibility: no surface changes. A run of two or more badges is drawn inside `.graview-spec-badges`.
