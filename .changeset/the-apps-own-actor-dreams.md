The app's own actor can now find what to suggest, and a host can run it on a schedule. `@graview/tools/tidy` holds three judges that read the store as the actor sees it — `duplicates` (two records of a kind with one name, or one email or code, merged into the one with more links), `links` (a record whose text names another record, linked through a relation the app already declares and only where its cardinality allows; a name two records share links neither), and `archive` (a record its kind says is finished — its lifecycle, its main choice field read as the map reads it, or a done flag — unchanged for 30 days) — each giving its calls with a basis a person reads and a confidence. `dream(store, { now, limit })` runs them once as "Graview, tidying" and suggests each, surest first: never what a person declined, never twice what already waits, never about a record left alone, changed in the last day, or of a kind or relation the app says is never tidied. It applies nothing on its own unless the host passes `applyAtLeast`, since a declaration says only where the actor may never act; a model in the Fast slot's shape may weigh each candidate, and the run stands on the judges' own word when it does not answer. It returns what it did by judge. `whileAway(store, { seq })` is the tray's line: "2 suggestions waiting · tidied 12 things while you were away". A host that judges elsewhere suggests through the new `suggest_changes` tool, listed only for a seat that says `automated: true` and refused to any other in a sentence, and `graview dream <entry> --data ./data` runs it against a folder or a SQLite file. The tidying skill says how.

Compatibility: additive — a new entry, `@graview/tools/tidy`, a new `graview dream` command, and a new tool, `suggest_changes`, listed only for an automated seat; every other seat's tool names and input schemas are unchanged. Ops, stored formats and the wire are unchanged: a suggestion is the op `suggest` already writes.
