A move or a merge of a person says which links it left behind. `rebindPerson` moves the links a person's acts made through `$me` with them, read from the log; a person bound before the log begins (a store loaded from a snapshot, or compacted) may have made links the log cannot show, and those stayed on the record they left without a word. Both `rebindPerson` and `mergePerson` (`@graview/core/binding`) now answer with `leftBehind` (`LinksLeftBehind`): `count`, the links older than the log on the record the person left, `relations`, how many of each relation, and `sentence`, which a host can show the owner — "3 links on Ann are older than this app's history, so the move could not tell whether Bud made them and left them on Ann: given by (2), minds (1)." With nothing left behind, `count` is 0, `relations` is empty and there is no sentence; a merge moves every link of the person's own record, so it always says none. A link between the two records stays as before and is not counted. Moving those older links too is not part of this. `hostSeat`'s doc comment and the `graview-sight` skill now say that its mark does not survive JSON or RPC, so a host calls `hostSeat()` on its own side of the wire, where the store is, and never sends one. Held by a unit test of core (`a-move-or-merge-says-which-links-it-left-behind`).

Compatibility: the host's binding entry — additive: `rebindPerson` and `mergePerson` answer what they did (`ApplyResult`) and `leftBehind` beside it, and the types `LinksLeftBehind` and `PersonMoved` are new; a host that reads only the result's ops is unchanged. The wire, stored formats, ops and primitives, derived tool names and input schemas, and the declaration and check finding codes — unchanged. What Graview Cloud does: show `leftBehind.sentence` on the People page after a move or a merge where it is present, and mint `hostSeat()` in the process that holds the store rather than passing one over RPC.
