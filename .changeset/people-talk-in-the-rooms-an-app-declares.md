People talk to each other inside an app. A declaration says its `rooms` beside its kinds — one room for the whole app, or `about: "<kind>"` for a thread on each record of that kind — with the roles that `read`, `post` and `moderate` each, and `defineApp` and a compiled document fold in what they need: the framework's `message` kind and three acts, `post-message`, `edit-message` (its author's own) and `remove-message` (its author's, or anybody's for a role the room lets moderate). A message is a record, so who may read it, history and undo hold of it as of any record; a room that reads only some roles is kept from everybody else, and a thread follows the sight of its record, through two new keys of a sight, `where` (a field holding one of some values) and `follows` (a field naming a record the seat sees). An agent's seat has the acts as tools and posts as itself. `@Name` in a message tells that person through the subscriptions' hook (`when.on: "mentioned"`), and what a person has not read is counted on the bell from a read-up-to marker kept like any per-viewer value. On the routed face and the one face each app-wide room is a page at `/rooms/<word>`, a record with a thread shows it under its page in the page's column, and a room's log and composer are a live log region and a labeled field, Enter to send and Shift+Enter for a new line, at a desk and on a phone. Messages stand on no list, district, home or Activity row and in no static export; `withoutTalk` takes them out of a snapshot for a template or a remix. `graview check` judges rooms (`room-about-unknown`, `room-role-unknown`, `room-title-taken`, `room-title-missing`, `room-id`, `room-post-not-read`, `room-kind-taken`, `room-act-taken`), `graview describe` says them, the studio carries them, and the new `graview-chat` skill teaches them. `declareInstallation`'s `withPolicy` keeps the sights of the policy it is handed, which it dropped. Held by a new unit test of core (`people-talk-in-the-rooms-an-app-declares`) and a new harness, `pnpm verify rooms`.

Compatibility: the declaration gains `rooms` and a sight gains `where` and `follows`, both additive; an app without rooms is unchanged. Stored formats, ops and the wire are unchanged: a message is an ordinary record of the kind `message`, made and changed by ordinary acts. New check finding codes `room-*`; new derived tools `post-message`, `edit-message` and `remove-message` for an app with rooms. A subscription's `when` may say `{ "on": "mentioned" }`. The JavaScript API is additive (`@graview/core/rooms`; `roomsOfSchema` and `MutationContext.by` and `now` on `@graview/core`). What Graview Cloud does: deliver a `mentioned` match from `onSubscriptionMatch` by email or push as it delivers any other; pass a host `viewerState` so read-up-to markers follow a person across devices; call `withoutTalk` on a snapshot before it becomes a template or a remix; and set its plan limits on rooms and their history where it keeps them.
