A record can be archived and brought back, and two records that are one can be merged (FR-173). Every kind is given three more derived acts beside `edit-<kind>` and `remove-<kind>`: `archive-<kind>` and `unarchive-<kind>` ("Bring it back") take `{ id }`, and `merge-<kind>` takes `{ id, into }`. Archive keeps the record — its fields, its links, its history — and takes it out of lists, the Scene, counts, computed fields and rules: every graph read that lists records (`allNodes`, `nodesOfKind`, `count`, `allEdges`, `edgesOfKind`, `out`, `in`, `neighbors`) reaches the live ones unless it is given `{ archived: true }` or `{ archived: "all" }`, and a read by id (`getNode`, `has`, `outEdges`, `inEdges`) finds a record archived or not. A merge moves the duplicate's links to the kept record and archives the duplicate, its op saying `mergedInto`, both histories kept and undone as one. A host archives with `store.apply({ name: "archive-<kind>", args: { id } })`, and a mutation with `ctx.archiveNode`, `ctx.unarchiveNode` and `ctx.mergeNode`; each goes through the policy, the seat's sight and the log like any act, permitted through the acts that make the kind as a remove is, and a grant may name each on its own. The host decides what to archive; the framework keeps the state.

A rule or a computed field asks for archived records explicitly: `all('task', true)` for the archived ones, `all('task', 'all')` for both, and `archived()` says whether a record is. Search still finds an archived record, marked `archived` and after the live ones, and `is:archived` asks for those alone; the agent's `search_graph` and `get_graph` take `archived` and reach the live records unless asked, and `get_node` says `archived: true`. A lens and a list page take `archived` beside `sort`, `filter`, `group` and `q` (`in.archived=all`, `?archived=true`), a list says "+N archived" beneath it, and an archived record's page says so at its head with "Bring it back" where the seat may. A name finds a live record before an archived one, and an archived one when no live one has it. `editDocument` renames and drops the new acts with their kind, `graview check` knows them, and a kind's JSON Schema says `$archived`. The hosted page carries 1.3 KB more up front, under its budget, which is not raised; the embed's budgets for the pages face alone (gzipped), every face and the studio handed in are raised by what they grew (`scripts/budget-raises/a-record-can-be-archived-and-brought-back-*.json`).

Compatibility: additive to ops and primitives — an archived record is `$archived: true` on the record, written by an ordinary `patch-node`, and a merge's op carries the optional `mergedInto`; a log folds to the same graph on an earlier version, where an archived record reads as live. Derived tools: every kind gains `archive-<kind>`, `unarchive-<kind>` and `merge-<kind>`, and `search_graph` and `get_graph` take an optional `archived`; `get_graph` no longer lists archived records unless asked. The rule language gains `archived()` and a second argument to `all`. `capabilities().shipped` gains FR-173.
