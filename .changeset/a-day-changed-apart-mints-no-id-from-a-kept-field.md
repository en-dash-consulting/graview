A day of a series changed apart mints no id from a field a sight keeps. "Change just this one" (`detach-<kind>`) and "Change from here" (`split-<kind>`) make a record of their own from the series, and minted its id from the series' label with no kind before it (`board-call-re-layoffs`), past the rule every other minted id goes by since an id came never to quote a field a sight keeps: where a sight's `hide` or `fields` kept a meeting's title from a role, that role read the title in the id it was served. Now they mint as the rest do — saying their kind, and from the kind's name where the label quotes a field some sight may keep from some role (`meeting:meeting`), from the label where none does (`meeting:board-call-re-layoffs`). There is one rule for it (`quotesKeptField`, beside `mayKeepField`): `freshId` asks it of its arguments and of a record it is handed third (`freshId(label, kind, record)`), which an act of the app's own may pass where its name was worked out from a record rather than an argument; a document's act asks it of the record it makes, by the document's own policy, so an act compiled outside a store holds to it too; and an import asks it of the row's fields. A name that holds a kept field's words among others, not only one that is the field, is now minted from the kind's name too: a tip titled "Acme renewal at risk" whose client "Acme" the public may not see is `tip:tip`. Core's tests hold it (`a-day-changed-apart-mints-no-id-from-a-kept-field.test.ts`, and `an-id-never-quotes-a-field-a-sight-keeps.test.ts` for a document's act, in a store and outside one). The `graview-sight` skill now says what a seat reads beside a record keeps to its sight: an id never minted from a kept field, a record withheld whole refused as one not there, the app's own actor's suggestion basis withheld where it read a kept field, and a sight with `hide` or `fields` not counting as a worker view's total sight. The store mints on every page, so a hosted page carries 0.1 KB more up front (126 bytes minified, 73 gzipped; 134 / 77 handed a compiled app), under raises naming this changeset; the pages face alone 102 / 53 more, a visitor's form 102 / 56, one handed a compiled app 134 / 64, and Cloud's worker 239 bytes, each under a raise of its own.
