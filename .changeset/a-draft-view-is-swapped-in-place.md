A draft view is swapped in place, so a host picturing several proposals does not mount again for each (FR-187). `draftView` was read once, at `mount`; to picture the next proposal a host unmounted the embed and mounted it again, losing the store and the place and waiting for the whole page to draw from nothing. Now `handle.setDraftView(draft)` draws another draft where it attaches, and `handle.setDraftView(null)` the app's own views again, on the whole embed and on the pages alone: the store, the place and the seat stay, `data-graview-drawn` comes off in the call and comes back — `onDrawn` told again — once the new draft has drawn and settled. A draft handed after the embed first drew, before the worker's host is here, is drawn in once the host arrives, and the place is not said drawn until it is; the embed is never drawn again from nothing for it. `<Embed>`'s `draftView` follows its prop the same way. A draft under a base path — the page the app, served at `/a/<app>` — is drawn at `/a/<app>/places/<title>`, the attribute's value the place's path within the app; `graview preview` serves at its root and takes no base. A hosted page carries 0.5 KB more up front for it (the compiled page too), and their budgets are raised by that; the embed, the studio handed in and every face by what they measured.

Compatibility: ops, stored formats, the wire, the declaration and derived tools are unchanged. New: `setDraftView(draft | null)` on `EmbedHandle` (and `PagesEmbedHandle`); `draftView` on `mount` and `<Embed>` takes `null`. What Graview Cloud does: picture each proposal in one page — `handle.setDraftView(proposal)`, then wait for `[data-graview-drawn]` — rather than a page per proposal.
