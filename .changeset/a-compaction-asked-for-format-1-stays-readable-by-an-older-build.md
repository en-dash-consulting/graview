A compaction asked for format 1 stays readable by an older build. 0.1.60 said `pins: [store.publishedSeq]` keeps a store at snapshot format 1, and it does only when the horizon lands at that publish: an app that published twice, whose days or ops put the horizon between the two, keeps the earlier publish as `published` and is stamped 2, pin or no pin (Graview Cloud found it taking 0.1.60, and stepped the pin back itself until the stamp said 1). Now a host that must roll back asks for format 1 by name and the store does the arithmetic: `format: 1` on `CompactOptions` (`store.checkpoint`, ship's `opened.compact`) holds the horizon at the earliest seq readers are served behind it — the publish standing where the days, the ops and the pins would have put it, or a seq a later publish took readers back to, and again where that lands past another — until `formatStamp` says 1; the checkpoint's `seq`, and the compaction's `horizon`, say where it stopped, none is made when that is the horizon already, and a format this build does not write is refused (`RangeError`). The default is the format this build writes (`FORMATS.snapshot`), so a compaction not asked is what it was. `formatStamp` now stamps 2 for every checkpoint carrying `published`, not only one keeping the publish standing at its seq: a publish in the tail that took readers back to a seq behind the checkpoint is kept there too, and a build before format 2 would serve those readers the horizon the same way. Ship's `a-compaction-asked-for-format-1-stays-readable-by-an-older-build.test.ts` holds the claim both ways — a store that published twice, compacted with the option, is stamped 1 and 0.1.59's formats read it; the same store compacted without it, its pin at the published seq notwithstanding, is stamped 2 — and core's `a-checkpoint-asked-for-format-1-holds-the-horizon-at-what-stood-published.test.ts` where it stops: at the standing publish, whatever the pins say; nowhere when that is the horizon; back through more than one publish and past a take-back. `docs/stability.md` says it in place of the pin.

Reading the stamp and the formats this build writes where the store checkpoints costs a hosted page 0.5 KB more up front (537 bytes minified, 208 gzipped), and a page handed a compiled app the same 0.5 KB (555, 203), under raises naming this changeset; the pages face alone 549 / 174 more, a visitor's form alone 549 / 206, a visitor's form handed a compiled app 555 / 158, every face 321 gzipped and Cloud's worker 1_416 bytes, each under a raise of its own.

Compatibility: ops and primitives — unchanged. Stored formats — `CompactOptions.format` is new, and absent nothing changes; `formatStamp(checkpoint)` stamps `snapshot: 2` for a checkpoint carrying any `published`, where 0.1.60 stamped 1 for one carrying only a seq a later publish took readers back to — such a store, rare and misread by 0.1.59 either way, is stamped 2 at its next open; `formatStamp` reads only `published` of what it is handed. The wire — unchanged. The declaration and check finding codes — unchanged. Derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged. What Graview Cloud does: passes `format: 1` to `compact` while it may roll back, in place of stepping the pin back itself, and reads where it stopped from the compaction's `horizon`.
