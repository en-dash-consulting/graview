A seat is served its view at about the cost of the whole store again, and a protocol made on a wake reads nothing until it is asked. The FR-55 seat view judges every string of every op it serves, and Graview Cloud's spike measured what that cost: `GET /graview/state` as a planner went from 7.6 and 20 ms to 10 and 36 ms over 3,000 and 10,000 ops, and making `liveProtocol` on a wake from 1.3 and 3.4 ms to 2.1 and 5.3. Now `logSeenBy` keeps each seat's served log with what serving it read, and serves a log that only grew from it, judging only the new ops — unless one of them touched a record whose sight or service the old ops were judged by, or a record whose id they were judged to name nothing by, when the whole log is served again. A property test holds the kept log equal to the log redacted afresh over 1,000 random worlds read as they grow. A redaction judges each id and each record once (`SeatLens.pin`), follows version by version only the records a field of the seat's view clears or withholds, and reads who wrote what only when a field names what the seat may not see; `seenBy` of the system is the store itself; `sha256Hex` reuses its encoder and schedule; and `liveProtocol` reads the log's batch owners and field revisions the first time a call names a batch or carries a base, not when it is made.

Measured over a planner whose own tasks are half of a 10,000-op log (median of nine, this machine, the system's own read of the same store 13 ms): the planner's state read again 12.5 ms where it took 53 (17.7 before FR-55), and after the log grew 12.5 ms where it took 55 (18.9); making the protocol on a wake 0.02 ms where it took 4.4 (3.0). Over 3,000 ops: 3.4 and 3.8 ms where they took 14.7 and 15.0 (5.4 and 5.3), and 0.02 ms where it took 1.2 (0.65). The first read a seat makes after a wake still judges the whole log: 51 ms over 10,000 ops where it took 84 (19 before FR-55), 14 ms over 3,000 where it took 24 (5.9). A benchmark test holds a read again and after the log grew within 1.75 of the system's own read, the first after a wake within 8, and making the protocol within a tenth of it.

Compatibility: additive. `SeatLens` gains optional `pin()`, the lens as of now with its judgements kept, which `redact` uses when a lens has one. `logSeenBy` serves the same ops as before, and the same array while the log has not changed — read it, do not change it; what it keeps per seat lives as long as the log does. The wire, ops, stored formats and check codes are unchanged.
