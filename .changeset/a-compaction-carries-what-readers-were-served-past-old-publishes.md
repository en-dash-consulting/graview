A compaction of an app that publishes moves past old publishes, and undoing the latest still takes readers back to the one before it. Readers are served the app as it stood at the last publish, and an undo of that publish takes them back to the one before; both were folded from the log, so `store.checkpoint` held the horizon at the publish standing now and at the one before it, and an app that published rarely kept a log that only grew until somebody published again. Now a checkpoint keeps the app as it stood at each published seq behind it that readers are or may again be served (`Epoch.published`: a checkpoint at the seq standing there, and at each one a publish in the tail goes through, each with its makers, removed records and writers), carried from the checkpoint before where the log no longer holds it. A reader's view and `pastOf` read such a seq behind the horizon from it, an undo of a publish whose predecessor it kept is accepted, and the horizon is no longer held by any publish — only, before the first, at where the log begins. A seat's view of the log serves no epoch's `published`, and serves the horizon epoch (`log.horizonEpoch()`) as it serves every other epoch, judged for that seat, where it handed the store's own. `a-compaction-carries-what-readers-were-served-past-old-publishes.test.ts` holds it: a compaction past the publish before the latest, the undo serving readers that publish on the store and on a reopen, a store that publishes once compacting twice with readers served the same, and no seat served `published`.

Keeping those points and reading them costs a hosted page 0.3 KB more up front (286 bytes minified, 87 gzipped), and a page handed a compiled app the same 0.3 KB (286, 99), under raises naming this changeset; the pages face alone 286 / 66 more, a visitor's form alone 286 / 81, a visitor's form handed a compiled app 286 / 70 and Cloud's worker 1_380 bytes, each under a raise of its own.

Compatibility: ops and primitives — unchanged. Stored formats — additive within format 1: a checkpoint may carry `published`, a list of epochs; one written before it carries none, and a store opened on it serves readers what the log folds, as it did, refusing an undo that would send them behind the horizon. A build that predates it ignores the field, and on a store this build compacted past the publish standing now it serves readers the horizon — changes not yet published — so a host that may roll back passes `pins: [store.publishedSeq]` to `store.checkpoint` or `opened.compact` until it no longer may, which holds the horizon where an older build held it. Undo — an undo of a publish whose predecessor is behind the horizon is accepted where the checkpoint kept the app at it, and refused as before where it did not. The wire — unchanged; a seat's `log.horizonEpoch()` is judged for it. The declaration and check finding codes — unchanged. Derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged. What Graview Cloud does: nothing; a compaction of an app that publishes is no longer held at its last publish, and a checkpoint holds up to a few graphs more.
