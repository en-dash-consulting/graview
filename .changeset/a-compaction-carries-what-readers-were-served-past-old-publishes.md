A compaction of an app that publishes moves past old publishes, and undoing the latest still takes readers back to the one before it. Readers are served the app as it stood at the last publish, and an undo of that publish takes them back to the one before; both were folded from the log, so `store.checkpoint` held the horizon at the publish standing now and at the one before it, and an app that published rarely kept a log that only grew until somebody published again. Now a checkpoint keeps the app as it stood at each published seq behind it that readers are or may again be served (`Epoch.published`: a checkpoint at the seq standing there, and at each one a publish in the tail goes through, each with its makers, removed records and writers), carried from the checkpoint before where the log no longer holds it. A reader's view and `pastOf` read such a seq behind the horizon from it, an undo of a publish whose predecessor it kept is accepted, and the horizon is no longer held by any publish — only, before the first, at where the log begins. A seat's view of the log serves no epoch's `published`, and serves the horizon epoch (`log.horizonEpoch()`) as it serves every other epoch, judged for that seat, where it handed the store's own. A build before it would ignore `published` and serve those readers the horizon, so a store whose checkpoint lies past what stood published is snapshot format 2 (`FORMATS.snapshot` is 2): `formatStamp(checkpoint)` stamps 2 only for what begins at such a checkpoint, ship's `openStore` stamps the meta so at open and when it compacts (before the checkpoint lands when it moves to 2), and `exportBundle` stamps the bundle; every other store is stamped 1, as before. `assertReadable(stamp, reads?)` checks a stamp against another build's formats. `a-compaction-carries-what-readers-were-served-past-old-publishes.test.ts` holds it: a compaction past the publish before the latest, the undo serving readers that publish on the store and on a reopen, a store that publishes once compacting twice with readers served the same, and no seat served `published`; `an-older-build-refuses-a-store-compacted-past-its-publish.test.ts` that the meta and bundle of such a store are refused by 0.1.59's formats, and that one never compacted, a live app's and one pinned at its publish are stamped 1; and `stored-formats-carry-their-version.test.ts` over a fixture of what 0.1.59 wrote.

Keeping those points and reading them costs a hosted page 0.3 KB more up front (286 bytes minified, 91 gzipped), and a page handed a compiled app the same 0.3 KB (286, 99), under raises naming this changeset; the pages face alone 286 / 82 more, a visitor's form alone 286 / 82, a visitor's form handed a compiled app 286 / 70 and Cloud's worker 1_380 bytes, each under a raise of its own.

Compatibility: ops and primitives — unchanged. Stored formats — snapshot format 2, written only for a store whose checkpoint lies past what stood published: such a checkpoint carries `published`, a list of epochs, and its meta and bundles are stamped `snapshot: 2`, so an older build refuses such a store with `NewerFormatError` rather than serve its readers changes never published. A host that must roll back passes `pins: [store.publishedSeq]` to `store.checkpoint` or `opened.compact` before compacting, which keeps the checkpoint at the publish and the store at format 1. Every other store is stamped 1 as before, and a format-1 store — one 0.1.59 wrote among them — reads as written, serving readers what the log folds and refusing an undo that would send them behind the horizon. `formatStamp` takes the checkpoint a stamp is for, and `assertReadable` the formats to check against; a host that keeps its own meta stamps it with `formatStamp(store.log.horizonEpoch())`. Undo — an undo of a publish whose predecessor is behind the horizon is accepted where the checkpoint kept the app at it, and refused as before where it did not. The wire — unchanged; a seat's `log.horizonEpoch()` is judged for it. The declaration and check finding codes — unchanged. Derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged. What Graview Cloud does: stamps its stored meta with `formatStamp(store.log.horizonEpoch())` if it keeps its own, or pins compaction at the publish while it may roll back; a compaction of an app that publishes is no longer held at its last publish, and a checkpoint holds up to a few graphs more.
