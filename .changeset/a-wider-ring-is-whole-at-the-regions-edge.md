A view's region lets a wider ring than the default paint whole past its edge. Since 0.1.47 the region let 4px past its edge while something in it held the keyboard — the default ring's 2px and its 2px offset — so a view that set `--graview-focus-width: 5px` and `--graview-focus-offset: 4px` had the outer half of its ring cut off at the region's edge in Chromium and Firefox. The ring is now measured as a control takes the keyboard — its outline's width and its offset outward, at least 4px and never past 16px — and the region lets that much past its edge (`overflow-clip-margin: var(--graview-ring-reach, 4px)`; Chromium takes no `calc()` there, so the reach is handed to the rule as a plain length). In WebKit, which has no `overflow-clip-margin`, a control within that reach of the edge draws its ring inside its own edge, as a control within 4px of it did. Guest's tests find a ring's reach measured and held to 4–16px and the region told it as a control takes the keyboard; `guest-sandbox --transport=taps` finds both a default ring and one the view rings wider whole at the region's left edge in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit ("a focused link flush with the region's left edge shows its whole ring, and so does one the view rings wider").

Compatibility: a behavior change in a worker view's region only: while the keyboard is in it, the region paints past its edge by as far as the focused control's ring reaches (4–16px) rather than by 4px. The API, the declaration, the wire, the stored formats and the derived tools are unchanged. What Graview Cloud does: nothing — its views' wider rings are whole once it takes this version.
