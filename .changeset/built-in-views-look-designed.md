Built-in views look designed: one type scale, one left edge and one heading per page on the place header, blocks lenses, grouped row and card lists, kind lists and record pages (Roadmap f-auto-view-type-polish). Threat Watch's By type page stacked two headings — the place's title and count, then the lens's own larger headline saying the count again — inset its rows 16 pixels past the title's edge, ruled each group label with a one-sided accent, drew 69 row titles in the display face, said "high · resolved · supply-chain · disclosed May 18, 2026" as one string, and pressed its arrow against "All threats as a list→". Now a blocks lens that opens with a headline hands it to its page, which says it as the line under the title in place of the count and draws it once; on its page the lens stands in the page's own column, with no inset of its own; a group's heading is its label and how many it holds, in the text face, over the rows' own hairline; a record's name in a row or on a card is the text face at medium or semibold weight and normal tracking, never the display face; a card's or a row's line of facts with a choice field named bare in it draws each choice as a small chip and the rest as quiet words after them, in the order written, and a kind's list draws its glance's choices the same way; an arrow after a link (→, ↗) stands a space from its words and is not underlined; a place's header, its first block, its blocks and its groups are spaced on a 4px scale, given as tokens (`--graview-space-1` … `--graview-space-7`: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48); a page's title steps one step down the scale on a phone; and on a desk wide enough for a margin, a kind's list hangs its choose boxes in that margin so its names start on the title's edge. A hosted page carries 0.3 KB more up front for it (the compiled page too), and their budgets are raised by that; the pages face alone by what it measured.

Compatibility: ops, stored formats, the wire and derived tools are unchanged; the declaration is unchanged and no check finding is added. New: a resolved text block on a card or a row carries `runs` (each fact, a choice marked by its `field`) where one of its facts is a choice field named bare; describe_place says such a line as it is drawn, a chip a space from what is beside it ("High Contained Supply chain disclosed May 18, 2026") and a grouped list's group carries its `count`, said in the text as "Supply-chain compromises (6):"; the theme writes `--graview-space-1` … `--graview-space-7`; `PlacePageHead` on `@graview/primitives/pages`. What Graview Cloud does: nothing to keep Threat Watch's v19 workaround — a lens may open with a headline again, and its page says it once, under the title.
