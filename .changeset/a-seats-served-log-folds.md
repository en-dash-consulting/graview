The log a seat is served folds to the snapshot it is served again — a fix to FR-55's served log. A record withheld because a required field names a hidden one comes and goes as that field moves, and the served log judged each primitive alone: removing such a record served a `remove-node` the seat never had (`Cannot remove missing node`), pointing its field at a record the seat sees served a patch on a record it never had (and the record never arrived), and pointing a seen record's required field at a hidden one served nothing, so the client kept a record the seat's view no longer had. Graview Cloud's audit found it in 33 of 1,000 worlds. Each primitive is now judged by whether its record was served just before it and just after: served to served is the patch as the seat is served the record; not served to served is an `add-node` of the record as now served, with its links to records the seat is served; served to not served is a `remove-node`, which takes its links; neither is nothing; and a link is served when both its ends are. To know a record just before an op, `redact` walks the store's log back from the graph as it stands (`serveAlong`), so an op is judged where it stood however it is asked for — the whole log, a poll from a cursor, a socket's catch-up, an ack, a batch answered again. The seat view's `log.epochs()`, `log.lastEpoch()` and `checkpoint()` serve each base as the seat is served it, so a compacted log folds from its served checkpoint. Property tests over 1,000 random worlds each, with one kind's reference required and with every kind's, hold the invariant: the served log folds, from nothing and from every cursor, to the served snapshot, across acts, undo, a rebase and a compaction; and a client landing a welcome and every `ops` it is sent — or saying hello again from its cursor — holds exactly what the seat is served.

Compatibility: the wire and live protocols — narrowing, no field added or removed, `WIRE_PROTOCOL` unchanged. A seat's log now holds, in a withheld op, an `add-node` (with `add-edge`s) where a record it is served first becomes served by a change to it, and a `remove-node` where one stops being served; it no longer holds a removal of, or a patch to, a record it was never served. Ops a seat is served whole are unchanged, and a seat with nothing kept from it is sent exactly what it was. The seat view's epochs and checkpoints hold only what the seat is served. Ops, stored formats, derived tools and check codes are unchanged.
