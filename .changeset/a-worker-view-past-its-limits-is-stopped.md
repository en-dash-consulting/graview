A worker view past its limits is stopped, and the plain face of what it was shown is drawn in its place (FR-94). The heartbeat stopped a view that blocked its own event loop, and `maxNodes` stopped one that drew too much. A view that flooded the host was read on, one message dropped after another. One that spent seconds in each push was kept, and nothing was drawn where a stopped view had been. `mountWorkerView` now caps four things besides the heartbeat's `silentMs`. Its code may be at most `maxSourceBytes` (256 000 bytes by default) and is never started past it. It may draw at most `maxNodes` nodes (5 000). It may send at most `messages` messages in `messageWindowMs` (120 a second), and past that it is stopped rather than read on. Each push of what it is shown may take it at most `pushMs` (1 000 ms). Every props push is numbered, and the view's runtime says when it has drawn one and how long the view's own listeners took. The host stops the view when either passes `pushMs`, without counting time the host's own page was held up. A view that throws before it draws anything is stopped too. Past any limit the worker is terminated, and the host draws the plain face of what the view was shown in the region: its title, each record by its label, and a note saying why it was stopped. `onFailure` hears the reason (`source`, `nodes`, `flood`, `slow`, `silent`, `error`, `refused` or `manifest`) and that sentence. A host draws its own with `fallback`, or `fallback: false` leaves the region for it. `registerWorkerView` and `workerHome` draw whatever the registry drew for the kind before. The kit's `budget` reason is the open kit's `nodes`.

A unit test speaks for a stand-in worker. Code past its size is never started. 100 000 nodes are stopped at 50, and 500 messages at an allowance of 20. A push never acked is stopped as slow within `pushMs` and an interval of it, and so is one acked as taking too long. A view that acks each push in time is kept, a throw before drawing is stopped with the engine's words, and each failure draws the plain face, a host's own, or nothing. `guest-sandbox` gains `--transport=limits`, with real views in real workers. One spins in its listener and is stopped as slow at about 1 000 ms. One spins later and is stopped as silent at about 1 500 ms. One floods the host and is stopped within 50 ms. One draws 100 000 nodes, given time so that the node cap is what stops it, and is stopped at 5 000 within two seconds. One is longer than a view may be and is never started. Each draws the plain face with the reason said. A well-behaved view is kept, and the page's own 50 ms timer never went 250 ms between ticks. All eight claims hold in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox.

Compatibility: the wire — unchanged beyond FR-90's `pushed` and numbered `props`, which it now relies on. A worker view's runtime must answer each numbered push, and the only such runtime is this release's. Ops, stored formats, check codes and tool schemas are unchanged.
