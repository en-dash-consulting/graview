A drive-in's thumbnail is a picture of its lens, not the lens. It hands the lens its 12 most relevant members (the flagged, then the most connected) with `budget` and `total` — new on `ViewProps` — and is mounted the first time it is on screen with the scene still, one thumbnail a frame; `useSceneStill` and the scene's motion store say when. A lens with a budget draws no arrangement row, the coverage holds its rows and columns to it, and the board, timeline and calendar say "+N more" in the kind's words (`withMore`, exported). The row's "only…" menu offers at most the 40 most connected far ends — a release's menu listed every song. Over Tech N9ne's catalogue the city at altitude went from 21,335 elements to about 1,200, and rising from 21,495 to 2,493 with every thumbnail drawn. The graview-lens skill says what a lens does with a budget.
