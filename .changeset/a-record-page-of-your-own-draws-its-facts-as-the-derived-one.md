A record page of a project's own draws its facts as the derived one does. The page `graview create` writes drew every fact as text: a festival's trailer was its file name, and a date was changed only through "Change the film", where the derived record page plays a video, shows a picture where it is kept, links a phone and edits each value where it stands. `RecordField` from `@graview/pages` is one fact as the derived page draws it, the derived page draws with it, and the scaffold's record page does too.

Compatibility: no surface changes; `@graview/pages` exports `RecordField` and `RecordFieldProps`.
