A kind declares what a glance at one says — `display: { glance: ["price", "mileage", "fuel"] }` — and every card, list row and Find hit says those first, in that order. Unsaid, it is still the first fields the heading does not say; `graview check` notes `glance-unchosen` on a kind with more than five fields and no choice, and refuses `glance-unknown-field`.
