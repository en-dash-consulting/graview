A date can repeat: a weekly practice, a monthly bill or a yearly renewal is one record, not one per occurrence made by hand (the Roadmap's f-recurring-dates). A document says `"repeats": true` on a `date` or `datetime` field; a declaration puts `startsAtRepeat: repeatField()` beside `startsAt`, and `toDocument` writes it as `repeats`. A record keeps its first occurrence in the date and, beside it under the date's name and `Repeat`, how it repeats: `{ "every": "week", "on": ["tue"], "until": "2026-12-15" }` — `every` day, week, month or year, an `interval`, `on` weekdays for a week or days of the month for a month (`-1` the last), `until`, `count` and `except` days — or `null` once it no longer does. The store refuses anything else (`validRepeat`). Occurrences are worked out and never stored (`occurrences`, `nextOccurrence`, `dateOn`): a rule, a template, `today()`'s comparisons, a list's sort, filter and date groups, and a kind's stop read the next occurrence on the day they are judged on, so the tick that turns the day (FR-176) moves it with nothing written. A day a month lacks falls on the month's last (Jan 31 monthly is Feb 28, Feb 29 yearly is Feb 28 off a leap year), a moment's time of day is kept as written so six in the evening stays six across a clock change, and `count` counts skipped days. The calendar draws every occurrence in the days it shows, marked ↻ and "· repeats" and not dragged, and counts a series once, at its next, where it is a glance; a kind's stop opens on a calendar by a series' next occurrence and counts it in "in the next 7 days"; a record's facts, a row and a card say "Every Tuesday, next Oct 27" in the app's locale (`repeatAsRead`, `repeatingDateAsRead` on `@graview/core/words`). Three derived acts on a kind with a date that repeats, permitted as its edit is, each one undo with a sentence: `skip-<kind>` ("Skip this one", `{ id, on }`) adds the day to `except`; `detach-<kind>` ("Change just this one") makes that occurrence a record of its own, with the series' fields, the links it makes and what was changed, and an exception on the series; `split-<kind>` ("Change from here") ends the series the day before and starts a new one that day, `continues` naming the old. A record's page has a "Repeat…" control beside the date: a picker (does not repeat, daily, weekly, monthly, yearly; every how many; which weekdays; the month's day or its last; until) that says the rule back in words as it is chosen, every control labeled, kept by the edit act; a form asks for a repeat with the same picker (`FormField` gains `control: "repeat"`). `editDocument` gains `set-repeats`, the studio offers "Say whether it repeats" and writes `repeatField()` back, a migration moves a repeat with its renamed date, `graview types` writes `<date>Repeat?: Repeat | null`, and an act's tool schema describes it with `"x-graview-repeat": true`. Time zones beyond the one a moment says, and the rest of RFC 5545 (by-week-number, by-set-position, hourly), are left out. A hosted page's first load is 5 797 bytes larger minified, 2 381 gzipped, than the batch it joined (549 531 / 192 258 against 543 734 / 189 877, under its budgets of 550 912 / 192 512), and 5 425 / 2 274 handed a compiled app (507 320 / 177 411 against 509 440 / 177 664): the occurrence math, the repeat's check and the three acts, which a rule and the store need as the page loads; the words, the picker and the calendar's drawing are fetched with what draws them. The embed's every face, the studio handed in and the pages face alone are raised by files of their own. A document's `datetime` field now reads back as a date and time (`fieldSpecOf`), so a kind's stop finds it for its calendar and its dates, and the stop says its date by the field's label ("in the next 7 days by starts"). verify-repeats holds it at desk width in three engines.

Compatibility: additive. The declaration and check finding codes: the document format gains `repeats` on a date or datetime field, within `graview-document@1` and `graview-compiled@1`, the edit `set-repeats` and the finding `repeat-clash`; a build that predates them refuses the key as `unknown-key`. Derived tool names and input schemas: a kind with a date that repeats gains `skip-<kind>`, `detach-<kind>` and `split-<kind>`, and its `edit-<kind>` takes `<date>Repeat`; a kind with none is unchanged. `ReadableField` gains `repeats`, `PlacedEntry` gains `repeats`, `DerivedAct` gains `skip`, `detach` and `split`. Ops and primitives, the stored formats and the wire are unchanged. What a host does: serve 0.1.29 or later before writing `repeats`; a record's repeat travels as any other field value.
