A host adds its own rows to the right-click menu. Graview Cloud wants "Make changes in your own AI" there, handed what was under the pointer. `menuItems` on `mount`, on `GraviewProvider`, on the `Shell` and on a `PagesApp`'s context takes `{ id, label, run, hint?, offered? }` rows (`HostMenuItem`). They are drawn after every row of the framework's on either face, after the host's own `menuSections` too, and are shown as the menu opens rather than waiting behind More…. Each is a button like the framework's rows: labeled, reached by the keyboard, and pressed after a phone's long press wherever the menu opens on one. A row handed twice, by the provider and by a face, is drawn once. Pressed, it is run with `HostMenuAt`: the record id, the relation or the place slug under the pointer, the kind of what was there (a record, a kind's card, a list), the whole address of what was there where it has one on the host's page, and the app's link. The link is the app's home on the host's page: the pages' own home, the host's `hrefOf("/")` where it gives one, and under `mount` the same link on the scene as on the pages; under memory routing it is the host's page and no address is handed. `offered(at)` leaves a row out where it does not apply. A menu section may now say `at: "last"`, which the host's rows use. `HostMenuItems` on `@graview/react/drawing` gives rows to every menu drawn inside it, and `menuLink` on the provider says the link where no face does. Held by unit tests of pages (`the-context-menu-opens-on-the-pages`) and primitives (`a-host-adds-its-own-rows-to-the-scene-menu`), and by the menu harness's `aHostsOwnRowComesLastAndIsRunWithWhatIsUnderThePointer`: on the pages at a desk's width with the keyboard, at a phone's with a resting finger, and on the scene. A hosted page carries a few dozen bytes more up front.

Compatibility: the JavaScript API is additive (`menuItems` on `mount`, `GraviewProvider`, `Shell` and `PageContext`; `menuLink` on `GraviewProvider`; `"last"` for a `MenuSection`'s `at`; `HostMenuItem`, `HostMenuAt` and `HostMenuItems` on `@graview/react`). `useMenuSections` answers a `last` list beside `start` and `end`. Ops and primitives, stored formats, the wire, the declaration and check finding codes, and derived tool names and input schemas are unchanged. What Graview Cloud does: pass its "Make changes in your own AI" as one of `menuItems` on `mount`, in place of a `menuSections` source that worked out the record itself.
