Two ways to add one beside a list's heading wrap apart, never one over the other. Each press is a 28-pixel target that takes 4 pixels back from the line above and below it, so on a phone, where the presses wrap, the second row stood 8 pixels over the first: Cloud's vendor list at 390 wide had "+Add a vendor to this category" over "+Add a vendor", and a press on one landed on the other. The rows now stand 8 pixels apart.

Compatibility: no surface changes.
