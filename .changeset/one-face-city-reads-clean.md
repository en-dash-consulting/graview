The one face's City and Nearby read clean on a rich app (Graview HQ feature:f-one-face-city-reads-clean, f-one-face-nearby-shows-records). On a copy of Deep Time — eleven kinds, about three hundred records, ten worker views — the City had boards standing on district names and on districts, most districts said no name, the legend stood open over a quarter of the window, and ten views' boards each listed the first records of their kind; at Nearby each district was a near-opaque card over its own buildings, and no view stood there.

Now the one face places the city's words itself (`placeSigns`), on the screen as the camera will have them, so where a move heads and what is placed once it arrives are one answer. Each kind's views stand on their own district: a card over its back, its post coming down onto the district's own ground, never on another district or in a column off to the side; the page being read stands straight over its own building, on a post as tall as it needs to clear what is round it, and a page chosen at the City leaves from there; the views of no one kind stand on the plaza. Then each district's name, beside it or under it and never over its buildings. Nothing placed stands on anything placed before it, nor under the bar, the page or the level control — after a hand's pan too, when anything brought under one of them is put away until it comes out again. Every board and name travels between levels with the camera, comes up or goes down where it has no place to come from or go to, and goes over a moment to where it is placed again once the camera is written, never jumping; a move slows through a stalled frame rather than leaping, and a page chosen at the City is drawn before it moves, so the drawing of the chosen page never lands inside the move. Placing is kept for the same city and window and carried along a pan, and stays cheap with sixty views of one kind.

Each district's name is a control: it opens the district's list beside the city, and its problems, where it has any, are a chip beside it that opens them; the Scene's own district cards stand down on this face. The views stand at Nearby as at the City, where they have room, at most a fixed size on the screen. A view's board says whose view it is over its own title ("View · species", "Tree of life"), on two lines before it is cut; a list's says how many it holds and its first record. Beside the home the city is framed in the part the page leaves it. On a small window (a phone, or one turned on its side) a board's words are never under 9 pixels: the City pans rather than shrink them, and beside a page a board stands only where it can be read. A board the keyboard can reach is one that is seen. The level control shows one chosen level after the keys: a page with no title of its own takes the keyboard on its main part, not on the control. The legend is a button: closed until the Key opens it, then kept open in this browser until it is put away. In the Scene, a chosen record is brought to the front of its row only where it would otherwise be one of the "+n", so a building pressed in the city stays where it is as its page opens; and a building's name keeps five pixels clear of the next (two read as one run of words) and is drawn a weight heavier, its letters reckoned a little wider for it.

Tests: `the-city-says-every-name-and-stands-every-view-clear` (pages) holds that every district is named on no other district's ground, no other name and none of its buildings, that no board stands on another, a name or a district's ground and the page's own stands on its building, that each view's post comes down onto its own district, that a name inside what the camera shows slides along its district, and that sixty views of one kind among forty districts and a thousand buildings are placed in under 8 ms. `verify-one-face` holds the same on Deep Time at its size, dark, at 1520, 1280, 768, 390 and 360 wide and 844 by 390: nothing said stands on anything else, each kind's board stands on its own district even where the page covers one, no name or title is cut off or wraps, no board's words under 9 pixels on a small window, no board or name leaps between frames, everything the keyboard reaches is seen, the level control shows one level, a district's problems open from the City and Nearby by pointer and keyboard, the home frames the city beside the page, the legend stays open once opened, and every line at Nearby ends on a building.
