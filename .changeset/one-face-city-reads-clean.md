The one face's City and Nearby read clean on a rich app (Graview HQ feature:f-one-face-city-reads-clean, f-one-face-nearby-shows-records). On a copy of Deep Time — eleven kinds, about three hundred records, ten worker views — the City had boards standing on district names and the page's own shrunk board standing on a district, most districts said no name at all, the legend stood open over a quarter of the window, and ten views' boards each listed the first records of their kind, so none looked like itself; at Nearby each district was a near-opaque card over its own buildings, so the records could not be seen, and no view stood there at all. Now the one face places the city's words itself, in one pass (`placeSigns`): each district's name first, as a label under its front corner or beside it, on its own ground only where nothing else is clear and never over its buildings and their names; then the page being read on its building, on a post as tall as stands it clear of every name and every other district; then each kind's views beside that kind's district, never on any district and never nearer another; then the views of no one kind on the plaza. Nothing placed stands on anything placed before it, nor under the bar, the page or what stands at the foot, and where the camera cannot go out far enough to take it all in (a phone), everything is placed inside what it shows. The names and the views' boards stand at Nearby as at the City — a board a size on the screen at most — inside the part of the window beside the page, a district out of sight saying nothing there, and move with the camera between levels, a page chosen at the City leaving from the board pressed. The Scene's own district cards stand down on this face, and with them their "open" and problem controls. A view's board says whose view it is over its own title ("View of species", "Tree of life"), on two lines before it is cut; a list's says how many it holds and its first record, the home's and the connections' what they are. The legend is a button: closed on every visit until the Key opens it, then kept open in this browser until it is put away. A phone's boards are a seventh of its width rather than a fifth. In the Scene, a building's name keeps five pixels clear of the next (two read as one run of words) and is drawn a weight heavier.

Tests: `the-city-says-every-name-and-stands-every-view-clear` (pages) holds that every district is named on no other district's ground, no other name and none of its buildings, that no board stands on another, a name or a district's ground and the page's own stands on its building, that a kind's views stand nearer its district than any other, and that a name inside what the camera shows slides along its district and a district out of sight says none. `verify-one-face` holds the same on Deep Time at its size, dark, at 1520, 1280 and 390 wide: nothing said stands on anything else at the City or at Nearby and no board on a district, every district at the City names its kind, no name or title is cut off or wraps, the legend is closed on every visit and opens from its button, each view's board is drawn as that view with no two alike, at Nearby every building in sight is seen and every line ends on one, every view of a district in sight has a board there, and no district control stands under the bar, the page or the level control.
