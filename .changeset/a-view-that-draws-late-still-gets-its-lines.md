A view that draws its members late still gets its lines. The scene measures its lines when a frame lands and again on a scroll, a resize, a new selection or a caption that moved; a view whose picture is fetched as it is first drawn puts its members down in a commit of its own, after the frame landed and at the same size, so none of those came. On a CPU four times slower the week's lens arrived a few milliseconds after the last measurement, and with the lists raised its nine entries stood with no line to the lists holding them until something else moved the page. Nothing was shed for load: the measurement was older than the drawing. Now `connectorStrands` takes an optional map it fills with the members it looked for inside a view and found no drawing of, and the lines watch those views and are measured again once one of them is drawn.

Compatibility: ops, stored formats, the wire, the declaration and check finding codes, and tool names and input schemas are unchanged; `connectorStrands` gains an optional sixth argument. Nothing more is loaded up front; the scene face is 1 KB larger.
