A weekly record is one recurring event (FR-230), Roadmap f-connection-weekly-records-recur. A rota's duty says its weekdays and its times of day; a team's practice has a date that repeats; neither could be connected to a Google calendar, whose bindings took a datetime start and end and carried no rule. Now a calendar connection's `fields` may name `days` — a field that is an enum of weekdays or a list holding `mon` to `sun` — and then `start` and `end` name string fields holding times of day (`"09:00"`): each record is one event with `RRULE:FREQ=WEEKLY;BYDAY=MO,WE`, its first occurrence the first of its weekdays from the week it is first sent, at its time on the app's wall (`brand.timeZone`), and Google told the zone to expand the rule in. A `start` that repeats (`"repeats": true`, 0.1.57's repeating dates) needs no new key: its repeat is the event's RRULE — every day, week, month or year, an interval, weekdays or days of the month, an until, a count — and each day set apart (`skip-<kind>`, `detach-<kind>`) an EXDATE at the series' time; a day changed apart is an event of its own. Ten weekly duties become ten recurring events, a change to one moves only it, and a driver changed sends nothing, the driver being the app's own; a recurring event made in the calendar is one record with its repeat, made and then given the repeat through `edit-<kind>` in one batch (the act that makes a record does not take a repeat, as the form does not), and a one-off in a weekly kind or one week's exception in Google is left there, having no record to be here (`a-weekly-record-is-one-recurring-event.test.ts`). `graview check` judges the shapes at their paths: `days` that is not a choice of weekday or a list of them, and a start or an end beside `days` that is not words, each `connection-field-type` in plain words. The arithmetic is in one module (`sync/recurrence.ts`): the rule written and read, a time of day and a wall time turned into the instant Google wants and back. The `graview-sync` skill says both shapes. `capabilities().shipped` gains FR-230. A hosted page's first load is unchanged: the mapping is the connection's, run by a host. Cloud's worker, which runs connections and checks documents, carries 11_735 bytes more, and every face 1_060 / 331 (the checker's judgment of `days`), each under a raise of its own.

Compatibility: ops and primitives, the wire, and derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged. The declaration and check finding codes: additive — a Google calendar connection's `fields` takes `days`, which a build that predates it refuses by path; a repeating start is read as a series where it was read as one event; `connection-field-type` judges the new shapes and refuses no declaration that passed. Stored formats: `SyncState` is unchanged; what a connection's engine is handed to make a record (`createInbound`) may say `then`, the acts that follow the making act in its batch. What Graview Cloud does: offer `days` where it draws a calendar connection's fields, and let a repeating date's kind be connected.
