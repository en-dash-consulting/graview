An open page is told the app takes no changes for now, and why (FR-66). A host holding an app read-only — Graview Cloud's quarantine while a repair is checked — answered every write `unavailable` (FR-46), and `openRemote` kept the change and sent it again, backing off, without a word to the person; Cloud said why down the socket as `{ t: "error", sentence }`, which `openRemote` ignores, and watched its own socket for it. A host now says it holds the app: `liveProtocol({ held: () => sentence })`, read as it is asked so a hibernating host keeps the hold where a wake does not empty it, with `live.heldChanged(peers)` when the hold starts or ends; or `hold(sentence)` and `release()` on `createStoreHandler` and `serveStore`. While it stands, every call and undo that has not already landed is refused `unavailable` in the hold's sentence before `limit` is asked (503 over HTTP), every socket that said hello is told `{ t: "held", sentence }` — and `sentence: null` on release — and every welcome and every answer a poll reads (state, since, here, a post's) says `held`, so a page opened during a hold knows at once. `openRemote` says it: `status()` is `held` while the server is reached and holds the app, `held()` is the sentence, and `onHeld(listener)` is handed it and `null` on release, told at once when added during a hold and carried to the store a new declaration opens. A change made meanwhile stays shown and pending and waits on the hold rather than the network: it is not backed off, and goes on release in the order it was made. Ship's own shell has no connection status to show it in; a host's banner reads `onHeld`. Tests hold the push and the welcome, the refusal before `limit` and the ack for a call that already landed, `held` on every poll answer and its absence after release, a change kept through a hold and landed on release with no backoff over the socket and over HTTP, a page opened during a hold, and the hold carried across a declaration change. `capabilities().shipped` names FR-66.

Compatibility: the wire — additive to protocol 1, as `declaration` was: a `held` server message, `held` on a welcome and on the routes' answers, and an `unavailable` refusal in the hold's sentence; `WIRE_PROTOCOL`, the codec `graview.ship` and the subprotocol `graview.ship.1` stay, and a client before this ignores the message and the field and backs off on `unavailable` as it did. `capabilities().shipped` gains `FR-66`. `RemoteStatus` gains `held`, so a host that branches on every status has one more; `RemoteStore` gains `held()` and `onHeld`, `StoreHandler` and `ServedStore` gain `hold` and `release`, `LiveProtocolOptions` gains `held`, and `LiveProtocol` gains `heldChanged`. Ops, stored formats, check codes and tool schemas are unchanged.
