The card that stands for the districts the row could not hold is one control, and the districts are one press away. It listed every name inside its own box — a district card's height, which holds a count and nothing else — so "+6" over one clipped word read as a broken placeholder, dashed and faded, and nobody could see what else there was. The card is a button now that says how many more; pressing it opens a panel above the row, portalled onto the scene's ground so no plane paints over it, naming every district with what it holds, each a press to that district. The district you are already in, which lands here when the row has room for nothing else, is marked as here rather than offered as somewhere to go. `@graview/react` takes `react-dom` as a peer for the portal.
