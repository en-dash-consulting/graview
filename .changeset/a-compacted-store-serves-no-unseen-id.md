A compacted store serves no unseen id (FR-199). Found building FR-198: a store opened compacted, from a checkpoint and the tail after it, as Graview Cloud opens its rooms, never read the ops behind its undo horizon, so a record made and removed there was no record to it. A field that still named one was served as it stood to a seat that may not see its kind, and the hidden record's id, minted from its label, went with it: a `pub` whose `ref` named a seed `secret` removed behind the horizon was served `ref: "secret:s0"` in the seat's graph, its log, its epochs, the live welcome, `/graview/state`, `/graview/since` and `/graview/export`. Over 1,000 random worlds compacted twice and reopened, 43 leaked, and in 348 the welcome a seat opened with was not the one the never-compacted store sent, because a field the seat wrote itself behind the horizon was no longer known to be its own words. Now `store.checkpoint` keeps two more things beside `creators`: `removed`, the kind of every record made or removed behind it that its graph does not hold, and `writers`, who wrote each field of its graph that names a record. A store opened on the checkpoint judges a field naming a removed record as the whole log would, clearing it or withholding the record, and serves a seat its own words as before. Neither is ever served to a seat: a seat's epochs carry their graph and their makers only. A checkpoint written before this has neither, and a store opened on one fails closed: an id it cannot place that begins with a kind the app declares, `secret:s0`, is judged as a removed record of that kind, kept from a seat that may not see every record of it. A store `openRemote` holds, which its host already judged, shows what it holds. A host that keeps the archive mends an old checkpoint by opening the whole history once, taking the checkpoint again at the same seq and storing it in place of the old one. `core`'s test runs the 1,000 worlds through two compactions and a reopening: no unseen id in any seat's graph, log or epochs, the same view the never-compacted store serves, the same for a checkpoint mended over the archive, and no unseen id from an old checkpoint either. `ship`'s runs them over the welcome and the routes, with the same welcome as the never-compacted store. Now 0 leak and 0 differ. A hosted page carries 1.0 KB more up front (1,025 bytes, about 360 gzipped; the compiled page the same), what a checkpoint keeps of the records behind it and the seat view judging from it, under a raise of each budget by that (`scripts/budget-raises/a-compacted-store-serves-no-unseen-id-*.json`), and the scene face's before-draw line is 963 KB. `capabilities().shipped` gains FR-199.

Compatibility: ops, the wire, the declaration and derived tools are unchanged; `capabilities().shipped` gains FR-199. Stored formats: additive. A checkpoint epoch gains the optional `removed` and `writers`, written by `store.checkpoint` and carried by every adapter as part of the epoch. A build before this reads such a checkpoint and ignores them, and this build reads one without them, failing closed as above, so no format number moves. A seat whose sight is limited and whose store was opened on an old checkpoint may now be served without a field naming a declared kind's id that no record ever held.
