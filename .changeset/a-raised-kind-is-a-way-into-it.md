A kind raised as a relation is a way into it. From todo's "The lists", pressing the Tasks district raises the tasks as a relation of the lists, which is what a press there means, and then nothing on the screen went into them, where their list is sorted and filtered: only a double-click nobody is told about. The raised chip on the picture's trail is now two controls: the kind's name, "Go into the tasks", and its ×, "Stop showing tasks".

Compatibility: no surface changes; `data-testid="raised"` is the × alone, and the way in is `data-testid="raised-into"`.
