A file the app holds, lent to its views as a face, has a size it may not pass, and two faces lent under one name say which draws (Graview HQ f-lent-faces-bounded). A face the app lends its views (`brand.views.fonts`) was handed to `FontFace` by its address and loaded with the first view dressed in its brand, whatever its size, since `FontFace` has nothing to stop at a size with. For a file the app holds (where `asset` serves it) the host now fetches the file itself and hands `FontFace` the bytes: a file that says it is larger than `VIEW_FONT_MOST_BYTES` (2 MB, from `@graview/core/css`) is not read, and one that says no size is read only until it passes it. Either way the face is not loaded, and the view's notice and the host's `onProblem` say so: "…is more than 2 MB, the most a file the app holds may be as a face for its views." Two million bytes is more than any one Latin face needs and less than one weight of a whole CJK face, which wants subsetting before a page loads it eagerly. A face on an origin the host lists in `fonts.origins` is loaded by its address, as before; that origin bounds what it serves.

Two brands on one page that lend one name — a family, its weight (no weight is 400) and its style — from two files no longer leave the engine to choose: the page holds one of them at a time, the one lent last (lending a file again makes it the last), once it has loaded, in every view; the others are set aside, and the next drawn again when it goes. Once the one that will draw has loaded, each lender is told which. A file that fails is said to every view holding it, and is tried again when a view lends it after. A brand that changes one file for another keeps the old drawn until the new has loaded or failed, then gives it back, and nothing is said. The graview-view-drawing skill states the limit and the rule.

Tests: `a-lent-face-past-its-size-is-not-loaded` (guest) loads a file the app holds of two million bytes, refuses one that says it is one byte more without reading it, stops reading one that says no size once it passes the limit, says each, and loads a face on a listed origin by its address; `two-faces-lent-under-one-name-the-later-draws` (guest) draws the later of two files lent under one name though the earlier loaded last, says nothing until the one that will draw has loaded and draws the earlier where the later fails, tells every holder of a failed file once and tries it again, draws a file lent again as the last, keeps the earlier drawn until the later has loaded, keeps a brand's old file until its new one has loaded and says nothing, and keys no weight and 400 as one name. Each failed before the change, but for two guards that held before it as well: a face on a listed origin loads by its address, and two weights of one family both draw. guest-sandbox's brand claims serve a 3 MB file the app holds in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox and hold that it is not loaded and is said. The tests that mount a view with lent faces hand the page a `fetch`.

Every face grows by 2.4 KB (1.1 KB gzipped), as does the guest host drawing a worker view on the open kit, under a raise of their budgets.

Compatibility: ops and primitives — unchanged. Stored formats — unchanged. The wire — unchanged. The declaration and check finding codes — unchanged. Derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged.
