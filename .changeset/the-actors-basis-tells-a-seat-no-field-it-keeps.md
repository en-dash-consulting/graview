The app's own actor no longer tells a seat, in the words of a suggestion's basis, a field that seat's sight keeps. The actor judges as a system seat that sees every field, and says why in words that quote them — "Both have the code dummy-code-7731", "Its notes names “Harbor Catering”" — and the op kept that basis on its lineage, where field sight never looked: an op is withheld from a seat whose sight keeps a field only when the act's own sentence read that field (`described`). So a viewer whose sight hides a client's code read it, case-folded, in their Suggested tray and in the log they were served, while the client itself was served to them without it, and two apps that differed only in the hidden code gave that viewer different trays. Now a lineage says which fields its basis read (`lineage.read`, `<id>#<field>`, at most 200), and a seat whose sight keeps one of them is served the op withheld — the suggestion, the change the actor applied on its own, the change a person made by accepting it, and the undo that declined it, which carries its lineage. The judges say what each basis read: a shared key, both records' key; a shared name, the fields each name reads; a record whose text names another, that field and both names; a finished record, the field that says so and the date it was read by. A basis that says nothing of it — a model's, which was shown the records whole — is taken to have read every field they hold. A seat that sees those fields reads the suggestion as before. `the-apps-own-actors-basis-tells-a-seat-no-field-it-keeps` holds it: an admin's tray has the code and the notes, a viewer's tray and served log have neither, the viewer's tray is the same whatever the code holds, and nor does the code reach the viewer through a change applied on its own, accepted or declined. Checking and reading `read` costs a hosted page 0.2 KB more up front (190 bytes minified, 99 gzipped; 190 / 59 for a page handed a compiled app), under raises naming this changeset, and the pages face, a visitor's form handed a compiled app and every face carry it under raises of their own (`scripts/budget-raises/sight-side-channels-*.json`).

Compatibility: ops and primitives — additive: a lineage may carry `read`, which a build that predates it keeps as a later build's key and does not judge, serving such an op whole as before. Stored formats, the wire, the declaration and check finding codes, derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged. What Graview Cloud does: nothing; a host that writes its own lineage may say `read` for a basis that quotes a field.
