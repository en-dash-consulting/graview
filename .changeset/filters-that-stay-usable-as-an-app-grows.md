Filters that stay usable as an app grows (the Roadmap's f-filters-that-scale). On the Graview Roadmap's Requests, "Filter" opened one long menu listing every record at the far end of every relation — "The feature it became" alone was ninety feature names, wrapping, in a popover that ran off the screen — and a row's "1 vote" was cut off at a desk's right edge while "0 votes" was drawn as a badge. A list's choice fields now stand on its arranging line as chips with counts ("Considering 12"), always in view and on a phone a rail of their own beside "Arrange": one press keeps that value, another value of the same field takes its place, pressed again it lets go, and each counts among the rest of what is asked. What is on is a removable chip beside the count. Filter opens short: every heading starts closed and says how many of its own are on; a box at the top, which stays as the list scrolls, finds any filter or any record at a relation's far end by a few letters; a relation with more than eight records there lists only "anything" and "nothing" and is found by typing, eight matches at most; the list is opaque, at most 520 pixels tall with its own scroll, and a sheet along the foot of a phone, where Filter is now a word of its own beside "Arrange" (which holds the sort and the grouping). A list reaches through a relation to what is at its far end — `became.stage:building`, said "The feature it became · Stage: Building" — and `pages.lists.<kind>.filters` names what a kind's list offers, in order (a field, a relation, `is`, or `<relation>.<field>`); unsaid, a list offers every way but a relation to a kind with more than forty records, which it offers by the far kind's choices instead, and its first two choice fields as chips. `graview check` says a filter the kind cannot be filtered by (`pages-filter-unknown`) and a kind `pages.lists` names that is not there (`pages-kind-unknown`). A count of nothing ("0 votes", "0") is no badge, and a list's glance says a record's prose by its start.

Compatibility: ops, stored formats and wire messages are unchanged. The document format takes `pages.lists` (`{ "<kind>": { "filters": [...] } }`) and `arrange-pages` takes `filters: { "<kind>": [...] | null }`, which it refuses for a key the kind cannot be filtered by; `DocumentDiff`'s sentences may say "The request list filters by status, became.stage.". A filter's grammar takes `<edge>.<field>:<value>`, judged by `conditionHolds` (and so by search's `key:value`). New check finding code `pages-filter-unknown`. `@graview/core/arrange` exports `throughEdges`; `ArrangeOffer.about` may be `"via"`. `@graview/primitives` exports `offeredFilters`, `chipOffers`, `farCount`, `farCounts`, `MANY_AT_THE_FAR_END`, `TYPED_FROM` and `CHIP_FIELDS`; `ArrangeBar` takes `filters`, `chips` and `offers`. On a narrow line "Arrange" (`arrange-all`) holds the sort and the grouping and Filter (`arrange-add`) stands beside it; the Filter list's headings are `[data-testid$="-section"][data-key="<offer key>"]`, closed until pressed, and its box is `-filter-find`. What a host does: a harness that picked a Filter entry by `[data-value]` opens its heading first.
