Types flow from a document to a face. n-dx's face read every record as `{ id, kind } & Record<string, unknown>`, its fields with `typeof n.status === "string"` and its relations by bare strings, so a renamed relation was a blank section and not a compile error. `graview types <document> [--out <file.d.ts>]` now writes a document's kinds, fields and relations as TypeScript: one interface per kind, with each field's own type (an enum's options as a union, a number as a number, a list as a list; a required field required and the rest optional), `Kinds`, `Edges` (the kinds each relation of each kind reaches), `Types`, `AnyRecord` and `Reader`. It refuses a document that does not compile, with the findings. `typedReader<Types>(store)` from the new `@graview/core/typed` reads a store, or its graph, through those types — `nodes(kind)`, `node(id, kind?)`, `is(node, kind)`, `fieldOf(node, field)`, `edgesOf(node, relation)` and `inwardOf(node, relation)` — so a field or relation renamed in the document, or an option taken out of an enum, fails `tsc` in the product that reads it. A test compiles a face over the generated types as a product's `tsc` would and renames each. `TypesOf<typeof document>` gives the same types from a `defineDocument`, with nothing generated. The reader is its own entry: a page that does not import it pays nothing, and one that does pays for six one-line functions.

A product can delete its interim: n-dx's `Node = { id, kind } & Record<string, unknown>` and the `typeof` reads in `packages/graview-face/src/model/graph.ts`, and the hand-written `DocumentField`, `DocumentKind` and `GraviewDocumentData` in `packages/graview/src/document.ts` (`DocumentDraft` and `defineDocument` say the format).

Compatibility: ops, stored formats, wire messages, the document format, the compiled format, check finding codes and tool names and schemas are unchanged. New: `graview types`; `typedReader`, `TypedReader`, `DocumentTypes`, `TypesOf`, `RecordOfKind`, `ValueOf` and the name helpers on `@graview/core/typed`.
