An epoch kept behind where a windowed archive begins is served to a seat under the modules on now. `archivedSeenBy` judged each such checkpoint in a store of its own made with the starting modules and an empty log, so a module the workspace had turned off since was on there: the seat was served that module's records, and — with no sight declared — the epoch as the store keeps it, `removed` and all. Now each is judged under the modules the live store has on, as the seat's own view and the whole archive are, and what only the store judges by (`removed`, `writers`) is never served from it. `an-epoch-behind-an-archive-s-beginning-is-judged-under-the-modules-on-now.test.ts` holds it with no sight and with a sight that sees every kind, the module turned off before the last compaction and since.

Compatibility: ops and primitives — unchanged. Stored formats — unchanged. The wire — a `HistoryHost.archived` answer for a range behind a windowed archive's beginning no longer carries a turned-off module's records, or `removed` and `writers`, in its epochs. The declaration and check finding codes — unchanged. Derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged. What Graview Cloud does: nothing.
