A spreadsheet comes in as one undoable batch (FR-177). `importRows(store, kind, rows, mapping, { by })` on the new entry `@graview/core/import` takes rows as arrays under a header row or as records keyed by header, and a mapping from columns to the kind's fields and relations — a relation the kind declares, or one declared toward it (`{ relation, direction: "in" }`). Each cell is read by its field's type: a date written as ISO, as `M/D/YYYY` (`dayFirst` for `D/M/YYYY`), as "Sep 10, 2023" or as a sheet's serial day; a number with its currency, unit, thousands separators, a parenthesized loss or a percent; a choice by its option or its words, case aside; a yes or a no as yes/no, true/false, x or ✓. A cell that names another kind's record links it by its label as acts take names — an exact name, or the one label it starts — under the importer's sight. Every row that fits lands as one batch, each record its own op ("Add a vendor: Bloom & Co") under the batch intent "Import 52 vendors", attributed to `by` and recorded `via: "import"`, and `store.undo(result.batch)` takes it all back. A row that does not fit is left out with every reason in words — "“soon” under Booked on is not a date", "“Smith” under Event could be Smith reunion or Smith wedding", "it has no Name, which every vendor needs" — and the rest still land. The importer is held to the acts that create the kind: a policy or an agent's `may` that refuses them refuses every row in its own words, and a host's `admit` is asked with the import as planned before anything is kept. `previewImport` (or `preview: true`) says per row what would be made, linked and refused, and the totals, and keeps nothing. `proposeMapping(app, kind, header)` proposes a mapping from the header row — fields by name, label or name spoken, relations by name or by the noun of the kind at the far end — with the columns nothing answers to and the required fields no column fills. `parseCsv(text)` reads a CSV or a paste: RFC 4180 quoting, CRLF, the byte-order mark, and a tab or semicolon delimiter read off the first line. The import is a library function; no agent tool is derived for it. The graview-seed skill says when to reach for it.

Compatibility: additive. `@graview/core/import` is a new entry, nothing on a hosted page imports it, and `capabilities().shipped` gains FR-177.
