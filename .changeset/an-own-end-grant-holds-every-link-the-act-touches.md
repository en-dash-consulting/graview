A grant held to your own end of a link holds every link the act touches, not only the one it names. `ownEnd` read the records a call names at the far end of its link, and a connect on a relation of one replaces the link that was there, so "Lead it" with yourself named took the idea from whoever led it — breaking their end of a link nothing granted you. The store now holds what the act would do as well: every link it makes or breaks has the seat's own record at one end, or the call is refused with the grant's sentence, "Not permitted: “Lead it” on an idea — only on your own end of the link.", and the strip no longer offers it. A seat with no id of its own is no longer offered an act only an own-end grant allows. `graview check`'s `sight-via-misses-people` says its sentence in fewer words, and `grant-own-end-without-link`'s fix for an act with no record at its far end says to take one as an argument or say `$me` there.

Compatibility: a call an own-end grant allowed that replaced or broke somebody else's link is now refused; every call that touched only the seat's own is allowed as before. Ops, stored formats, the wire, the document and compiled formats, check finding codes and tool names and schemas are unchanged.
