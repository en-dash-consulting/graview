At altitude the city stands beside the rails, not under them. A five-kind app laid its leftmost district under the inspector at 1280 and 1560: the map was centred on its lattice's bounding diamond rather than on the districts, and "off the edge" was measured against the canvas rather than the room the rails leave. A city whose districts fit between the rails now slides the least distance that puts them all there.
