The scaffolder is four families of templates beside the function that asks for them: what a project is called (`names`), the project around the app (`project`), the declaration it starts with (`domain`) and the faces it has (`ui`). Split by the parser's own statements, not by text, since templates are full of code; what `graview create` writes is byte-for-byte what it wrote before.
