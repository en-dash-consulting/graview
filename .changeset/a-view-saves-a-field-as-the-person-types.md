A view saves a field as the person types, and hears that it saved (FR-191). A view's code is never handed what a person types where sight is not total, so the Farm Bureau workshop's call sheet could take notes during a call only through a Save button the person had to remember. A field now carries its act and says when it saves: `<textarea name="notes" data-prefill="notes" data-act="save-notes" data-save="input" data-debounce="1500">` inside a `fieldset data-record` is saved by the host once the person pauses for `data-debounce` milliseconds (1 000 when it is left out, held to 250–10 000), at once when they leave the field, and as the view goes, whatever was still waiting; `data-save="change"` saves on the field's `change` alone. A save is a press in every way but the click: the act is the field's own `data-act`, its record and its fieldset's fields are read as a press reads them and held to the same rules (only what the person typed, a prefilled value only back where it came from, under their grants), and a click into the field is not a press. The view hears what became of every act a press or a save asked for as an `acted` event on the bound element — `graview.on("acted", "textarea", (event) => …)`, `event.pressed` holding `as`, `ok`, and `reason` and `message` when it was refused — so it can say "Saving…" on `input` and "Saved" on `acted`. A headless run (`graview view check`, `runWorkerViewHeadless`) says when a field's `data-save` would save nothing: no `data-act` or `name`, no fieldset bound to a record, a `data-save` other than `input` or `change`, or a `data-debounce` it does not read. The graview-worker-view skill teaches it, with `examples/a-call-sheet.js`. The guest host's open-kit drawing carries 1.4 KB more (0.5 KB gzipped), fetched with the press reader when a view first draws; a hosted page's first load is unchanged. `capabilities().shipped` gains FR-191.

Compatibility: ops, stored formats, the declaration and derived tools are unchanged. The wire: a worker view's runtime is sent a `dom-event` whose `event` is `acted`, carrying `pressed`, after every act a press or a field's save asked for; a view that does not listen for it hears nothing new, and `capabilities().shipped` lists FR-191. What Graview Cloud does: teach `data-save`, `data-debounce` and `acted` in its views guide in place of the Save button a call sheet needed; nothing changes in how it mounts a view.
