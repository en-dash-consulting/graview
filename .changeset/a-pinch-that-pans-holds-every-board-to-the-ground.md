During a pinch that pans, every board and name on the one face stays fastened to its district. At Nearby and the Map on a phone, two fingers with one of them on a board or a district's name were two gestures: the finger on the sign panned the city's view, a layout a move, the finger on the ground dragged its picture, nothing zoomed, and the boards and names, drawn through the picture, stood still while the ground went under them and caught up on letting go. A browser moves a touch that lands beside a control onto it, so a pinch begun near a board did this too, which is what made the "two fingers apart, their middle moving" gesture come and go when it was first written. A touch on a board, a name or a line's ring is now handed to the Scene's own hand (`SceneCamera` gains `finger` and `pinching`) and read with those on the ground: two fingers anywhere over the city are one pinch that zooms and pans the picture together. A zoom the hand wrote into the view while still on the city — a pinch held still a beat, two fingers let go of and a finger's pan straight after, a wheel's zoom and then its pan — placed the boards and names again for the new zoom there and then, and they went over a settle while the hand moved the ground on under them: they are now carried by the zoom and placed for it only once the hand has been off the city a moment, each going on from where it is drawn, and a settle a hand comes on in the middle of stops where it is drawn and is placed again after. Held by a new claim in `one-face` on a phone at Nearby and the Map, `duringAPinchThatPansEveryBoardAndNameStaysFastenedToItsDistrictEveryFrame` — two fingers apart with their middle moving (and together, at Nearby), one finger on a board and one beside it, and a pinch let go of with a finger's pan straight after: every frame, each board's foot and each name within a pixel of where its district carried it — and by a unit test in `two-fingers-zoom-the-city-under-them`. A hosted page carries nothing more up front; every face grows by 1_037 / 393 bytes and the embed with the studio handed in by 241 / 109, under the raises `a-pinch-that-pans-holds-every-board-to-the-ground-every-face` and `a-pinch-that-pans-holds-every-board-to-the-ground-studio` in `scripts/budget-raises/`. A host that hands the Scene its own camera (`SceneCamera`) implements the two new members; nothing a host calls changes.

Compatibility: ops and primitives, stored formats, the wire, the declaration and check finding codes, and derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged. The JavaScript API is additive: `SceneCamera` gains `finger` and `pinching`, which a host that hands the Scene its own camera implements. What Graview Cloud does: nothing.
