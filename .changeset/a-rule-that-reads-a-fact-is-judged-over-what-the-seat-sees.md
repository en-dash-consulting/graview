A rule that reads a fact is judged, and worded, over what the seat sees. A document's fact (`fact('proposed')`) counts the whole graph, and a rule that read one was judged over the whole graph and its sentence said there, so a guest who sees one proposal was told "3 proposed, and at most two may be". A seat's problems are now judged over its sighted view, its lines and its facts with it, wherever it reads them: `seenBy(principal).violations()`, and so a seat's tools (`get_violations`, `get_node`), the store's reading for a served seat and a page's seat, which now agree with a page served only what its seat sees. A record the seat may not see never makes, mends or words one of its problems. An act's answer to a seat (`answerSeenBy`) gives what the act broke, and what stands after it, as the seat judges them once it is made; what it mended, and what a preview would do, were judged over the whole graph, and a rule there that reads beyond its subject (a fact, a sweep, a relation) is said by its title. `store.violations(context, over)` judges over another reading of the store's graph. `graview check` warns of a rule whose message says a fact over a kind some role told of the rule may not see in full (`rule-says-hidden-fact`), and the `graview-permissions` skill says what it means. The hosted page loads 30 bytes less up front (589,727), the compiled entry 36 less (547,420); the every-face budget is raised for chunks fetched later only.

Compatibility: ops, stored formats, wire messages, the document format, the compiled format and tool names and schemas are unchanged. Check finding codes: additive, `rule-says-hidden-fact` is a new warning, so a declaration that passed still passes. What a seat is told changes: where a policy keeps records from a seat, its problems are those its own view breaks, so a rule broken only by records it may not see is no longer reported to it, and one its view breaks that the records it may not see keep is; a preview's problems from a rule that reads beyond its subject are said by the rule's title. A store with no sights, and the system's seat, read exactly what they read before.
