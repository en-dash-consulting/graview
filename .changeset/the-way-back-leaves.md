The way back leaves, and the scene has one (FR-152, FR-153). Graview Cloud, on staging in a deliverables app on 0.1.18: after an act on the Pages face the notice with "Take back …" stood at the foot of the picture for the rest of the session — it did not go, and nothing on it closed it — so every page carried a stale offer; and Down in a district on the scene there was no history, no activity and no undo in reach. On the Pages face the way back now comes with an act made there, and goes: after ten seconds (`WAY_BACK_MS`), with the next act's own offer in its place, with a move to another page (not the move an act makes itself, a record made and opened), and with the × that is now on it, a button named "Dismiss: Take back “…”" that the keyboard reaches from the way back, which keeps the change and lands the keyboard on the page. A change made before the face was drawn is not offered. ⌘Z or Ctrl+Z still takes the last change back after the offer has gone, and a `PageUndo` a design places in its own row still stands while there is a change to take back. On the scene, the whole-page Shell and an embed alike, an act is said on the app's board of notices in its own words with Take back, for as long, and ⌘Z or Ctrl+Z takes the last change back while the keyboard is in the app (anywhere, under the Shell; never from inside a text field); taken back by either, or from Activity, the offer goes and "Took back “…”" is said. Activity is in the scene's bar at altitude and Down in a district, with the change and its undo. A toast, the way back's or any other, now stands while a pointer is on it or the keyboard is in it, and stands its whole time again when its time comes while it is held; a kept lens's "Take back" goes after ten seconds rather than waiting for an answer. An embed made without a board of notices hands the one it draws to the faces under it, so what a face says there is drawn. Nothing moves: nothing animates as a notice comes or goes, so there is nothing for reduced motion to stop. What judges and draws the way back is fetched when it is first needed — on the scene once it has drawn, on the Pages face with the first change — so a hosted page loads 93 bytes more up front, the scene 483 bytes more before it draws, and the Pages face 2,648 bytes fewer. `capabilities().shipped` gains FR-152 and FR-153.

Compatibility: ops, stored formats, wire messages, the document format, the compiled format, check finding codes and tool schemas are unchanged; `capabilities().shipped` names two more ids. Changed: the Pages face's docked way back (`face-undo-dock`) shows only after an act made since the face was drawn and goes after ten seconds, so a harness that made a change and looked for `page-undo` later than that, or after a page move, finds none — press it within ten seconds, or use ⌘Z; its panel is `page-undo-notice`, with `page-undo` and `page-undo-dismiss` in it. The scene now says an act on the board as a toast with an act "Take back", which stacks with a host's own toasts. `NoticeBoard` gains an optional `hold(held: () => boolean)`; a board made by `createNoticeBoard` has it, and a host's own board without it keeps its toasts' times as they were. What Graview Cloud changes: nothing it must; its staging app's offer now leaves, and its scene has the way back and ⌘Z without anything of its own.
