`resolveFor(store, principal, node, options?)` resolves a record's declared blocks — or the home's, for no record — over what that seat may see, exactly as the scene, the pages face and `describe_place` resolve them, and a record's page is read under a page's budget. n-dx's face built the context blocks are read in by hand — the seat's graph, the schema, every kind's shape, the kind's fields, a budget and a heading — with ten casts, and the promise that its face, the scene and `describe_place` agreed was a comment; and a record page was judged under a card's allowance of 500 steps, so a change with 98 tasks said "—" for every list on its page. `resolveFor` is on `@graview/core/describe`, beside the describer that goes through it: `resolveFor(store, principal, node, { app })` reads the kind's declared page (`slot: "card"` or `"row"` for the others, `blocks` for a caller's own), and the context it reads them in is `blockContextFor` on `@graview/core/blocks`, which the faces read their blocks in. The budget is an option, `budget`; by default a page, the home and a blocks lens — drawn once — take a page's (`PAGE_BUDGET`, 20 000 steps), and a card and a row — drawn many times a frame — keep a card's (`CARD_BUDGET`, 500). The scene's record and the pages face's record page now list a change's 98 tasks where they said "—", and a home or a blocks lens may sweep four times more than it could (5 000 steps before).

The hosted page measures 593_305 bytes up front, 243 fewer than before, and handed a compiled app 547_761, 184 fewer: the compiler, the evaluator and the declaration ask whether an object has a name with `Object.hasOwn`. The scene face measures 904_673 bytes before it draws, 364 fewer, and the pages face alone 535_469 / 191_047, 287 fewer minified and 75 more gzipped, under its budget.

Compatibility: ops, stored formats, wire messages, the document format, the compiled format, check finding codes and tool names and schemas are unchanged. Changed: a declared page, the home and a blocks lens are judged under 20 000 steps where they were judged under 500 and 5 000, so an expression that said "—" for want of steps now says its value. New: `resolveFor`, `ResolveForOptions` and `DeclaredBlocks` on `@graview/core/describe`; `blockContextFor`, `BlockContextOptions`, `BlockSource`, `PAGE_BUDGET` and `CARD_BUDGET` on `@graview/core/blocks`; `useSpecContext` takes the slot it reads for. What Graview Cloud changes: nothing.
