A document can declare facts of the whole graph. `computed` was a kind's only, so n-dx decided "the whole product map is proposed" in its own face with `every(n => n.proposed)`, and the scene, the pages and an agent could not say the same thing. A document's top-level `computed` is a name and a rule-language expression with no record as its subject — `"mapProposed": { "expr": "every(all('area') | all('capability'), proposed == true)", "label": "The map is proposed" }` — read anywhere the language is as `fact('mapProposed')`: a block on the home or a lens, a template, a rule over the graph, a kind's computed field, another fact. It is worked out over the reader's own graph, so what a seat may not see adds nothing to it, from the budget of the expression that reads it, and a fact that cannot be worked out is said where it is read, never thrown. `factValues(schema, graph)` and `factNames(schema)` on `@graview/core/blocks` work them out for a face of a product's own; `describePlace` (and so `describe_place` on the home, over MCP) lists each with its value for the seat; `graview describe` lists each with its expression; a compiled app carries them; a TypeScript app says them as `createSchema(kinds, computed)`. `graview check` judges a fact as a computed field — a bare name (no record is there to read it from), an expression that does not parse, a kind or relation nobody declares, work that grows with the square of the graph (a warning) or its cube (refused) — and refuses `fact('<name>')` naming no fact wherever a document reads it (`fact-unknown`). A diff says a fact added, changed or taken away; renaming a kind, a field or a relation renames it in a fact, and removing one removes the facts that read it.

To pay for it on a page, a page that compiles a document its host has already judged (`compileDocumentWithoutCheck`) no longer judges its kinds' computed fields: `compileDocument` and `graview check` do, as they judge an act's ranges and a brand. The hosted page loads 590 122 bytes up front (593 548 before, budget 593 613), 547 723 handed a compiled app (547 945 before, budget 548 045), and the scene 901 668 before it draws (905 037 before), with no hosted-page budget raised. The every-face and studio-handed embed budgets are raised by the checker's growth, as `scripts/lib/bundle-budget.mjs` notes.

Compatibility: ops, stored formats, wire messages, tool names and schemas are unchanged. New: the document's top-level `computed`, the `fact()` function of the rule language, `Schema.computed` and `createSchema`'s second argument, `factValues` and `factNames` on `@graview/core/blocks`, `GRAPH_FACTS` on `@graview/core/document`, `PlaceDescription.facts`, the finding code `fact-unknown`. Changed: `computedValues` takes `null` for the whole graph; `compileDocumentWithoutCheck` builds an app whose kind's computed field `compileDocument` would refuse (a page refuses that value where it reads it), so a host that compiles strangers' documents in the page without the checker judges them with `compileDocument` first, as it already must for everything else the checker says.
