The past and the app's own actor's suggestions leave the store for entries of their own, so a hosted page carries neither before somebody looks back or something waits. Each of 0.1.30's changes fit the hosted page's budget alone, and together they were 10.0 KB over it. `store.at(when, options)` and `store.seqAt(when)` are now `pastOf(store, when, options)` and `seqAt(store, when)` on `@graview/core/past`, beside `pastAt(store, when, host)` and `declarationAt`, which leave `@graview/core/history` for it; the provider fetches the entry the first time somebody asks to see the past, and the timeline's helpers stay on `/history`. `store.suggest`, `store.suggestions`, `store.acceptSuggestion`, `store.declineSuggestion` and `store.declined` are now `suggest(store, calls, options)`, `suggestions(store, principal?)`, `acceptSuggestion(store, id, options?)`, `declineSuggestion(store, id, options?)` and `declined(store, calls)` on `@graview/core/tidying`; the bar fetches the Suggested tray, and with it what reads which suggestions still wait, once the log holds a suggestion. Asked of a seat's view (`seenBy`), each reads the whole store and answers as that seat, as the methods did. What they do is unchanged.

That took 3.7 KB (1.2 KB gzipped) off what the hosted page loads first. What stays up front is what the page draws or judges with before anything is asked — the store's holding of the app's own actor to what people said, its refusal of a change to the past and the seam every face reads the moment from, a relation to another app compiled as its field and walked by the rule language, an emoji drawn as the app's mark, and the first look's door — and the hosted page's budget is raised by 7.4 KB (2.5 KB gzipped) up front to 544.9 KB, and the page handed a compiled app's by 5.3 KB (1.4 KB gzipped) to 501.3 KB (`scripts/budget-raises/the-0-1-30-wave-*.json`); every face's budget is raised by the 1.8 KB the changes measure together over their own raises.

Compatibility: `Store.at` and `Store.seqAt` are removed for `pastOf(store, when: number | string | Date, options?: { declaration?: PastDeclaration }): Store` and `seqAt(store, when: string | Date): number` on the new `@graview/core/past`, which also takes `pastAt(store, when, host?: HistoryHost): Store` and `declarationAt(marks, seq)` from `@graview/core/history`; `Store.pastEvery` reads `StoreOptions.pastEvery`. `Store.suggest`, `Store.suggestions`, `Store.acceptSuggestion`, `Store.declineSuggestion` and `Store.declined` are removed for `suggest(store, calls, options: SuggestOptions): Operation | undefined`, `suggestions(store, principal?): readonly Suggestion[]`, `acceptSuggestion(store, id, options?: ApplyOptions): ApplyResult`, `declineSuggestion(store, id, options?: ApplyOptions): ApplyResult` and `declined(store, calls): Operation | undefined` on `@graview/core/tidying`, which now exports `SuggestOptions` and `Suggestion` too (`Suggestion` stays a type of `@graview/core`; `SuggestOptions` leaves it). Ops, the wire and stored formats are unchanged.
