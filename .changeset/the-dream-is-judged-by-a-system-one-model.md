The app's own actor now leaves the judging to a System One model when the host lends one, and keeps code to finding, routing and making. `dream(store, { ai })` (or `{ model }`) puts forward far more than an exact match: two records of a kind that share a telling word, a key value or a near hit from the host's `near` ranker; a record whose text mentions another's name or one of its words; an empty choice field; an empty one-link relation. Each candidate is one request to the model, its records as named JSON as the actor may see them and every question about them together — a Score for whether two are the same thing with a Noul for the name and each key field, a Choice among the likeliest far records with "none of these" for each declared relation, a Noul for "finished or no longer relevant" with how long it has been unchanged and how its kind says it is finished, and, for the new `categorize` judge, a Choice over a field's options with "none fits". Code maps each answer to a confidence (a Score's expected level, a Choice's probability and its margin, a Noul's probability) and routes it by how much the change would undo (`BANDS`: merge 0.9 to act and 0.5 to ask, archive 0.85 and 0.55, a link or a filled choice 0.8 and 0.45; a split choice never acts): made unasked only where the host's `applyAtLeast` allows, suggested with a basis a person reads ("Jev: 0.93 that “Acme Corp” and “ACME Corporation” are the same vendor") and the model named in the lineage, or dropped. It asks six at a time within a run's budget of 60 requests and 200,000 input tokens, keeps each answer by a hash of what was asked (`memoryDreamCache`, or the host's own `DreamCache`) so an unchanged record is not asked again, and reports what it spent. Without a model, or where it failed or the budget ran out, the deterministic judges stand as the floor. `graview dream` takes `--model jev-latest` (with TYPESAFE_API_KEY), `--max-requests` and `--cache <file>`.

Compatibility: additive — `@graview/tools/tidy` gains `categorize`, `missingLinks`, `BANDS`, `routeOf`, `memoryDreamCache`, `systemOneOf` and their types, a judge may say its `severity`, a run's report its `usage` and `budget`, and an outcome may be `dropped`; `graview dream` gains three flags. Ops, stored formats, the wire and every seat's tools are unchanged: a judgment is the suggestion or the lineage-carrying change `suggest` and `applyAll` already keep.
