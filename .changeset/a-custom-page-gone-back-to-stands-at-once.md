A page drawn by a view of the app's own stands at once when a person goes back to it. The view was taken down as its page was left and drawn again from nothing on the return: a new worker, what it keeps for its viewer (`graview.mine`) asked of the host again, a frame loaded again, with the page blank meanwhile. What it drew is now kept for the visit and put back as its page comes, before the page is painted, with what the person typed and where it was scrolled; what changed meanwhile is drawn in place, and a press made before that is drawn waits for it, and is applied only where the control still asks for the same thing. The page's provider keeps at most six, for five minutes after each page was left, in memory alone, for the one store and seat they were drawn for, and lets them go as the store, the seat, the registry or being signed in changes, where a view fails, and where a record one shows is no longer its seat's to see. While its page is away a view asks for no act, keeps no value and goes nowhere. A frame guest is kept where its host says it may be (`guestView({ keeps: true })`, since its document runs on out of sight) and the browser can move a frame without loading it (`Element.moveBefore`: Chrome 133, Firefox 144) and drawn anew elsewhere, as before. Drafts, previews on the Scene, rows and cards are never kept: a face says which view is its page's own (`PageOfItsOwn`). A view drawn again is also first drawn with what it was last known to keep for that viewer, and is pushed what the host says instead when it answers. `WorkerView` and `GuestFrame` gain `rest` and `wake`, for a host that mounts views by hand.

Compatibility: ops and primitives, stored formats, the wire, derived tool names and input schemas, and the declaration and check finding codes — unchanged. What Graview Cloud does: nothing to call for a worker view's return. For a frame's, pass `keeps: true` to `guestView` for the frames it is content to have run on out of sight for up to five minutes. A view is kept for as long as its definition, its store and its seat are the same objects, so hand the views registry and the principal as they were across navigations. To shorten a first visit, ask for a view's code ahead of it (the `worker` function may answer from a fetch begun when the page was idle) and answer `viewerState.read` from values read with the page: a view's first draw waits for both.
