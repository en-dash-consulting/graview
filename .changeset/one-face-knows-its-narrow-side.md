The one face places and routes knowing that the city's side is narrow and which gaps hold words. Four things 0.2.1 left, and two that Graview Cloud's retest found:

- At Nearby on Deep Time's Solnhofen at 1280×800 the Tree of life had no board: the Dig map, standing over the species district behind its own, had taken the only room there. Beside a page the app's views now stand before the kinds' own lists, the district with least room over its back first, so a view whose district is in sight keeps its board.
- On the home at Nearby on a 360×640 phone the city sat about 26 pixels off the middle of its strip: the districts the home's views show fitted there on their own, and the framing fitted them and never came back to the whole city. Now it frames them at that zoom and brings the city as near the middle of its side as they let it, keeping them a little inside its edge.
- Beside a page a district's name stands beside its own district or nowhere. Placed after every board, a name whose district's room was taken went hundreds of pixels off: on Cloud's Deep Time at 1280 the names stacked in the city's top left corner, "Plants" under the Tree of life's district. The names now stand right after the views and before the kinds' lists, within a name's height of their district and never over its records; where nothing that near is clear, the district says no name there, and its list's board and the bar still reach it.
- On a phone a page's lines left the sheet straight up, across the rows above and the words over them. Now every line drops into the gap above its row, keeping to the row's own spacing so it never runs through a group's label or a heading, runs along it, and goes up the sheet's margin to its top edge. The lines into one district go up as one: each district has one riser, the one whose lines start lowest outermost, where Cloud's Cretaceous had ten verticals side by side.
- In a view (a worker view, a frame) the page cannot read the words between its records, and on Graview Cloud's Family tree the lines from a pill row rose into the paragraph above it and drew a box through the text. A line in a view now takes the gap below its row first and keeps to the row's own spacing, never a gap that may hold words; on a phone it never goes straight up through them.

Tests: `verify-frame-lines` holds that inside the panel no line crosses another pill, a row or the paragraph between a link and the pills, on a desk and on a phone. `verify-one-face` holds that every view of a district in sight has a board at Nearby; that the home's city is as near the middle as its views' districts allow, and draws no smaller than 0.2.1's, at every size; that each district's name stands beside its own district on the home and a record's page; and that on a phone the lines into one district rise as one and no line crosses a heading or a label. Unit tests place what the one face asked at 1280×800 and find every view's board and every name beside its district. The every-face bundle budget is raised for it.

Compatibility: the look — changed on the one face only (`sceneUnderPages`): beside a page a kind's list board may give its place to a view's or a district's name, a name with no clear place beside its district is not drawn there, the home's Nearby may stand the city nearer the middle on a small phone, and lines inside the panel take other gaps on a phone and in a view, rising as one a district on a phone. The JavaScript API — unchanged. Ops and primitives — unchanged. Stored formats — unchanged. The wire — unchanged. The declaration and check finding codes — unchanged. Derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged.
