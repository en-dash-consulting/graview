A provisional link of a relation that only its derived connect and disconnect make or break can be kept. "Keep this link" was permitted through declared acts that connect or sever its relation, so once every relation came with a derived pair, a link of a relation with no declared act was kept by nobody a policy names; it is now permitted to whoever its derived connect is, the seats that may make or write the kind the relation leaves.

Compatibility: no surface changes; a policy that named no act for such a relation now lets the kind's writers keep its links.
