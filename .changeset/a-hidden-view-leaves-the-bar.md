A view hidden from the place menu leaves the bar, even while it is the place open (Roadmap f-hidden-view-leaves-the-bar). Hiding the place you were on — a kind's list on Pages, a picture on the scene — listed it under the drop-down's Hidden and still offered it on the bar's row and in the drop-down beside it, because a place kept off the lists was listed "while it is where you are". Now a hidden place is offered nowhere on the bar, wherever you are, at once and without a reload; you stay on it, at its address, and Show puts it back. Saying where you are is not offering it: on a hidden place the bar is its one control at every width, naming the place you are on (`app-place-current`), and the place is no entry of the row or the drop-down. Every bar reads one predicate, `offeredPlaces` on `@graview/core` (the places a list offers: none `hidden`), as do the scene's tabs (`Places`). Embed's tests hold that a kind's list, a declared lens and a view the host keeps, on Pages and on the scene, leave the bar when hidden while open, the bar still naming where you are, and come back on Show; `pnpm verify drawn` holds it in a real browser at a phone and a desk, mounted as Graview Cloud mounts an app (`aViewHiddenFromThePlaceMenuLeavesTheBarAndComesBackOnShow`).

Compatibility: ops and primitives — unchanged. Snapshot and log formats — unchanged. The wire — unchanged. The declaration and check finding codes, derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged. The JavaScript API — additive: `offeredPlaces` on `@graview/core`; `barPlaces` marks a hidden kind's list `hidden` as before and now lists a hidden picture too, marked `hidden` (it left it out), and `scenePlacesOf` marks the hidden picture in view `hidden`, so a host that draws its own nav from either filters with `offeredPlaces`. What Graview Cloud does: takes this build; it draws no nav of its own, and `open_app`'s places already say `hidden`.
