A view's `navigate` goes to a kind's list or a lens narrowed, and to a section of a page (Roadmap f-navigate-filters). It took a place's slug and nothing else, so a custom home could not open "features where stage is building" or jump to a heading, and every filtered link needed a lens of its own: the Graview Roadmap's home needed six. Now `graview.navigate({ place: "features", where: { stage: "building" }, section: "Shipping soon" })` goes to the features' list narrowed, at the heading that says "Shipping soon"; `place` is a lens's slug first, then a kind's list by its plural or its name, and a link says the same with `<a data-place="features" data-filter="stage:building" data-section="shipping-soon">`. The filter is held to what the kind can be filtered by — the offers the list's own arranging line makes — and written in the words that line already writes, so the routed face's address is `/features?filter=stage%3Abuilding#shipping-soon` and can be shared: opened from a link it lands on the list narrowed, the section scrolled to and given the keyboard. A lens is gone to narrowed the same way (`/places/<as>?filter=`, the records it is handed narrowed and said so over it, "Only those where stage is building. Show all"); a lens drawn from blocks lists what its own blocks say. On the scene a list is the kind's stop narrowed (`in.filter`, which its arranging line reads) and a lens its picture narrowed; a stop is a picture, not a page of headings, so the scene leaves a section unsought. A field the kind does not have, a choice it does not offer, a filter on one record or a section that names nothing goes nowhere and says why to the view's console and the host's problems ("The filter on "features" goes nowhere: Features have no field "stag" to filter by; they filter by name, stage, area, summary, became, became.status and is."), as a place that is not there already did; a headless run says it too. The graview-worker-view skill teaches it, and `verify-growing` holds it in a browser.

A hosted page's first load carries 0.2 KB more up front for it and for keeping a lens off the lists, raised in `scripts/budget-raises/navigate-filters-and-hidden-lenses-a-hosted-page.json` and `…-a-hosted-page-handed-a-compiled-app.json`; the embed's pages face, the embed with the studio handed in, a worker view's runtime and the guest host's four budgets (the links that judge a navigate, fetched with the first one where a frame is drawn) are raised in their own files beside them.

Compatibility: ops, stored records and the declaration are unchanged. The guest wire takes more on its existing `navigate` message: `{ type: "navigate", nonce, place, where?, section? }` and `{ type: "navigate", nonce, to, section? }`, where `where` is `Record<string, string | number | boolean>` (`GuestWhere`); a host that predates them ignores them and goes unnarrowed. `graview.navigate` and a frame guest's `navigate` take `GuestNavigateTo`: a string, `{ record, section? }`, or `{ place, where?, section? }` (`navigateRequest` from `@graview/guest` spells the message; `GuestNavigateTo` and `GuestWhere` are its types). `GoTo` (`useGoTo`) is now `record(id, also?)`, `place(as, also?)` and a new `list(kind, also?)`, `also` a `GoToAlso`: `{ filter?: string, section?: string }`, the filter in the address's words; a face that provides `GoToContext` writes `list` too. `mountWorkerView`'s `onNavigate` hears an `Arrival` — `{ record, section? }`, `{ place, filter?, section? }` or `{ list, filter?, section? }` — and it and `mountGuestView` take `goTo` (the face's `GoTo`) in its place; `createGuestHost` takes `onArrive` and `onLost`. New subpath `@graview/core/navigate`: `narrowedPath(path, { filter?, section? })` and `sectionSlug(words)`. `PagesApp`'s `onNavigate` path ends with the section it went to (`#shipping-soon`), never a scene's fragment.
