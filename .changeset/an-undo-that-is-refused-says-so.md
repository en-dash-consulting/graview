An undo that is refused says so. `store.canUndo` answers what the log can answer — whether a later op read what this one wrote — and not what the declaration answers, so taking back a migration that added a required field threw out of the click handler: an unhandled error and a button that appeared to do nothing. The reason is shown on the control, the way the agent seat already shows its refusals.
