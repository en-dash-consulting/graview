A view of many may be its kind's list page (FR-203). In Graview Cloud an agent designed a view of a team's players and could not make it the players' list: a view of many was drawn at its own `/places/<slug>` when titled and nowhere when not, and `/players` always kept the app's own list. A worker view whose manifest says `replaces: "page"` on `cardinality: "many"` is now drawn on its kind's list page under the list's heading in place of the list — no arranging line, no rows, no forms: what is done there is what the view offers — as a view of one that says so is a record's page (FR-149); a titled one is its own place as well, which then offers no "All players as a list" back to itself. Should it fail, the kind's own face is drawn in its place. The list page reads whichever view the kind draws at many × full as the host's tidying leaves the registry, so a view the host deleted or hid stands in for nothing and the list is the app's own again; the host decides who may set the key, as for a view of one. `checkManifest` accepts the key on a view of many and refuses it only on the home's view, and `describePlace` says a list page so replaced as its heading and the view (`drawnBy: "view:<name>"`), with no form. The graview-worker-view skill and the guest guide say so. Pages' test opens `/players` with a view of many that replaces the page and finds the view and no rows, and without the key finds the derived list; guest's registers a real worker view each way and opens `/deliverables`; core's describes both. The pages face alone carries 840 bytes more minified (170 gzipped) and every face 843 (191), each under a raise of its budget (`scripts/budget-raises/a-view-of-many-may-be-its-kinds-list-page-*.json`); a hosted page's first load is unchanged. `capabilities().shipped` gains FR-203.

Compatibility: ops and primitives and stored formats — unchanged. The declaration — unchanged; `describePlace` says a kind's list page a host's view of many replaces as that view. Derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged. The wire — additive: a worker view's manifest may say `replaces: "page"` on a view of many, which an earlier build refuses; `capabilities().shipped` gains FR-203. What Graview Cloud does: lets an agent's list view say `replaces: "page"` once `capabilities().shipped` has FR-203, gated as it gates a view of one's.
