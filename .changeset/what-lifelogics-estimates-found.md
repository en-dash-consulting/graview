A timeline is accepted only where it draws. `add-lens` with lens `timeline` over an initiative's start and end dates applied cleanly, `get_problems` said nothing, and the lens was never a place: a timeline lays out spans by number — minutes of a day, 0 to 1440 — across columns, and dates with no column gave it neither. `add-lens` now refuses a timeline whose `start` or `end` is not a number field, or that has no columns, as `lens-cannot-draw` at `edits.<i>.bindings.<kind>.<role>` or `edits.<i>.options.columns`, and its fix names the bindings a timeline takes and the calendar that draws spans of dates (`"lens": "calendar", "bindings": { "initiative": { "start": "start", "end": "end" } }`); the checker says the same of a declaration written by hand, a warning at the binding's path. A timeline over numbers with columns is accepted and draws as before.

Money never reads ".00". A list read "$11,424.00" beside "$7,840" and "$22,758.40": a sum worked out as 11423.999… was not whole, so it took two places that rounded to ".00". An option is said by its label, and keeps its author's capitals.
