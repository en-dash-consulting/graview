A withheld act says why. The actions strip named a refused act and put the reason in a `title` on a disabled button, which cannot be focused — so the explanation was out of reach of a keyboard and required hovering a dead control. It is struck through with the sentence beside it now. `Grant.describe`, documented since it was added as the thing shown on a refusal, is finally read: a refusal repeats the policy's own words. And a refusal about a derived edit counts the grants that name it as well as the acts it rides, so a `mutations: "*"` grant no longer produces "no role can".
