A worker view is as tall as it draws on every face, and on the Scene a tall one is a card with Open rather than a column three screens tall. The host measures the drawn root (`[data-graview-view-root]`, now a block of its own so its height holds its children's margins) with a `ResizeObserver` and keeps that as the view's content height — on the region as `data-graview-content-height`, on the handle as `contentHeight`, and told to the face through `mountWorkerView`'s `onContentHeight`; a view never says its own height. On Pages a titled view's place, the home and a record's page already flowed with the page at that height (0.1.46), and still do. On the Scene a view of a kind's members was drawn as the whole of itself in its place's box — sixty rows spilling up under the bar and down past every card around it, with nothing behind them. `registerWorkerView` now marks such a view previewed (`markPreviews`, read by `previewOf`), and the Scene draws it as a card like its other pictures: its title, as much of the view as the box holds — at most its measured content height — the foot fading into the card's ground where there is more, and Open, which goes to its place on Pages (`PageDoorContext`, which the embed provides with the address of the page). Nothing in the card scrolls. Two manifest hints say otherwise: `flows: false`, for a fixed canvas such as a map or a chart, keeps its place's frame on Pages; `scene: { maxHeight }` holds its Scene card lower, 80 to 4000 pixels. `checkManifest` judges both in sentences and refuses either where it means nothing (a frame for a view that is no place, a Scene card for a view of one record). `graview-worker-view` says how a view is sized on each surface and the two hints. `guest`'s tests find the hints taken and refused, a view of the members marked previewed with its `maxHeight`, and a view that says `flows: false` drawn in its place's frame; `react`'s find a card held to its box at the height its host measured, faded and with Open where there is more, Open going to the place without choosing the card, no Open where there are no pages, and no fade where the card holds it all. `verify-address` opens a view eighty entries long on the Scene at 1440 and 390 and finds it a card no taller than its box and inside the window, at its measured height, faded, with nothing in it scrolling, and Open landing on its place, flowing whole (aTallViewOnTheSceneIsDrawnBoundedWithOpenAndOpenLandsOnItsPlace), and a view that says `flows: false` and `scene.maxHeight` held to both (aViewThatSaysFlowsFalseKeepsItsFrameAndItsScenePreviewKeepsToItsMaxHeight). A hosted page carries 0.5 KB more up front (469 bytes, 75 gzipped; 127 on the page handed a compiled app), the scene face 3_020 bytes more before it draws, the guest host registering a worker view 1_411 (352) and drawing one on the open kit 1_570 (420), under raises of those budgets by that (`scripts/budget-raises/a-view-is-as-tall-as-it-draws-*.json`).

Compatibility: additive in the API — the manifest's `flows` and `scene`, `WorkerView.contentHeight`, `MountWorkerViewOptions.onContentHeight`, and `@graview/react`'s `markPreviews`, `previewOf`, `PREVIEWS`, `ViewContentHeight` and `PageDoorContext`; a manifest without the hints is judged and drawn as before on Pages. On the Scene a worker view of a kind's members is now drawn as a card held to its box with Open. The wire, the stored formats and the derived tools are unchanged.
