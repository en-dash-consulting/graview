An app declares what it does on its own, and when: workflows (Roadmap f-jobs-declared, f-job-step-types, f-job-idempotency, f-job-pause-dry-run). The app's own actor ran only as dreaming, on a host's schedule, and anything else an app did by itself — fill out next week's events every Sunday, keep events in step with plan items — was a script outside the declaration that nobody could check, pause or read. A document now says it at the top, as `workflows`, by name (`defineApp({ workflows })` the same): a `title` a person reads; `when`, one or several of a clock in words (`{ "every": "sunday 18:00" }`, "day 03:00", "weekday 09:00", "month 1 09:00", "hour") or five-field `cron`, both on the brand's time zone, a record `made` or `changed` (a rule-language `where`, the fields that matter), a connection `synced`, or `byHand`; `status` active or paused; `approve`, what its changes become — "apply", "suggest" (made by how sure it is, as dreaming's bands route them) or "ask" — "ask" unsaid when a step writes to another system through a connection, else "apply"; and its `steps`, each one of a closed set told apart by its one key (`STEP_KINDS`): run an act once or over the records a rule picks (`{ "act", "over", "args", "key" }`), or let a model propose acts from a list over the kinds it reads, under a budget (`{ "agent", "reads", "acts", "model", "budget" }`). The word is `workflows`; the purpose's jobs are the questions an app answers and keep their name.

`@graview/core/workflows`, an entry on no page, reads them: `workflowsOf(app)` resolves each with its defaults, its words, the kinds it reads and writes, and lists dreaming as the built-in workflow every app has (`dreaming`, `builtIn: true`, every night at three) unless every kind says the actor never tidies it; `workflowWords` says one ("Every Sunday at 6 PM, and when a grocery list is made: fill out next week's events"); `nextRunAt(workflow, after, zone)` says when it next runs on the wall clock, DST and all — 6 PM on the Sunday the clocks spring forward, a time the clock skips run as it jumps past; and `triggeredBy(workflow, commit, store)` says which `made`, `changed` and `synced` triggers a commit or a pass fires, read by the subscriptions' own matcher as the workflow's author sees the app, so a workflow never starts itself. `graview check` holds each to the declaration in the person's words, each finding with the edit that fixes it where one can be written: `workflow-steps-empty`, `workflow-schedule-invalid` (naming what it reads), `workflow-connection-unknown`, `workflow-kind-unknown`, `workflow-field-unknown`, `workflow-where-invalid`, `workflow-act-unknown` (naming the near act), `workflow-key-field-unknown`, `workflow-agent-acts-empty`, `workflow-follows-unknown` and `workflow-shape`, errors; `workflow-sends-out-unasked`, a warning — a step that writes to another system with `approve: "apply"` said is allowed, and said. The edit vocabulary gains `add-workflow`, `set-workflow`, `remove-workflow`, `pause-workflow` and `resume-workflow`, the diff says each, `describeApp` lists every workflow with its steps one to a line, the studio carries them, and Activity says a workflow's run by its title ("Fill out next week's events: 6 things today").

`@graview/tools/workflows` runs one: `runWorkflow({ app, store, workflow, now?, trigger?, dryRun?, log, ai?, principal? })` answers a `WorkflowRun` — its outcome (`ok`, `nothing-new`, `partial`, `held`, `failed`), what it made, changed, set aside and suggested, what it held back and why, how far it read the log (`through`) and the hash of the workflow as it ran. It runs as an automated system author, `workflow:<name>` named by its title, every op under one `lineage.run`, so Activity folds a run into one row; the runs are the host's to keep (`WorkflowLog`, `memoryWorkflowLog()`), never written into the graph. The first run after a workflow is added or changed is a dry run — "First run after a change: nothing was kept" — and a run by hand of a paused one too; a paused workflow's clock or trigger starts nothing and says so. A keyed act step makes each record under an id worked out from the workflow and the key (`keyedId`, `<prefix>:wf.<workflow>.<key>`), so a rerun over the same input is `nothing-new`, a changed source changes what it made through the kind's edit, a record a person removed is not made again, and a retry after a step failed half way makes no second record. A model's step is shown the records it reads within its token budget and proposes only the acts it is given; with no model lent it is skipped, and the run is `partial`, never failed.

A hosted page carries none of it: a compiled app's workflows are read off the document it remembers (`declaredWorkflows`), never copied onto what a page draws, and only Activity's words for a workflow's run are up front, within the page's budget. The studio's checked compile judging them and its five edits add 21_592 / 7_038 bytes to every face and 21_592 / 7_052 to the embed with the studio handed in, and `graview check` and `describeApp` judging and saying them 37_912 bytes to Cloud's worker (from 2_382_984 to 2_420_897), each under a raise of its own; when a workflow next runs and running one are on entries the worker does not import.

Compatibility: ops and primitives — unchanged; a run's ops are ordinary ops by an automated `system` author, their lineage carrying `run` and `key` (a key a lineage already kept as JSON). Stored formats — unchanged; the declaration document gains the top-level `workflows`, optional within `graview-document@1`, which a build before refuses by path. The wire — unchanged. The declaration and check finding codes — the document gains `workflows` and the edit vocabulary its five ops; the `workflow-*` codes above are new, and no code that existed changes meaning. Derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged. What Graview Cloud does: keeps a `WorkflowLog` per app, runs each active workflow at `nextRunAt` in the app's time zone and on what `triggeredBy` says a commit or a connection's pass fires, with `runWorkflow` and the app's model lent as `ai` where the plan allows it, and offers Run now (a dry run while paused, or the first time after a change) and Pause, Resume through `pause-workflow` and `resume-workflow`.
