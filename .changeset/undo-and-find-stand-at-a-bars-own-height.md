Undo and Find stand at a design bar's own height. Nick, building a product face with its own bar (the TrueNorth recon app): placed in it, `<PageUndo>` said "Take back “…the whole act's words…”" at whatever width that took and pushed Find to a sliver, and both carried inline styles the design could beat only with `!important` — the app replaced Undo's words with a CSS `::after`. `<PageUndo compact />` is the arrow and "Undo", with what it takes back as its accessible name and in its title; uncompact, its sentence is cut at 18rem (`--graview-undo-widest`) and still whole in its title. Both take `className` (on Undo's button, on Find's form; its box is `.graview-find-box`) and `size` — `"sm"` (30 px, the bar's tools), `"md"` (36 px), or a height as a number of pixels or a CSS length, said as `--graview-control-height` — and what they look like is now rules behind `:where` a design's one class outweighs, not a style attribute. Placed nowhere, each stands as before: Find at 30 px, the way back at 36. `graview-pages-design` says so: graview-pages had no room left under its limit, so its last rung — a product design over every surface — is a skill of its own, split along its seam, with Find and the way back in the design's bar beside it; 27 skills now. The pages face alone carries 0.5 KB more up front (482 bytes, 192 gzipped) and the embed with the studio the same (482 / 187), every face 1.4 KB in all (1_390 / 576), each under a raise of its budget by that; a hosted page is unchanged.

Compatibility: ops, stored formats, the wire, the declaration and derived tools are unchanged. New on `@graview/pages`: `compact`, `className` and `size` on `PageUndo`, `className` and `size` on `PageFind`, and the types `ControlSize` and `UndoLook`. The placed way back's markup is additive: `.graview-undo` on its button, `.graview-undo-place` on its box, `data-compact` and `data-size` where said; Find's form is `.graview-find` and its box `.graview-find-box`, with no style attribute on either. A shell that placed `PageUndo` sees a long sentence cut at 18rem where it stood uncut.
