`sceneThumbnail` can be fitted to what stands (FR-107). At a card's size, 264 × 132, the faithful picture keeps each plot the size its count gives it on a map whose blocks are five cells apart, so a seeded template drew as a few specks on bare ground: on Graview Cloud's twelve templates its narrowest district was 26–73 px wide and its narrowest building 8–15 px, and Cloud kept its own tile art. `sceneThumbnail(source, { fit: "content" })` stands each district on its whole block, the most its plot grows to, from the same corner, and crops the picture to the plots and what stands on them with a narrow margin. On each it stands what the size can show, the first of three that keeps every roof at least `minBuilding` pixels wide (16 by default when fitted): the Scene's own village, one building per member, when the picture is big enough; else one block per member up to five, spread across the block, and five taller blocks past that; else one block for the district. The corners, the iso lattice, the order and the hues stay the Scene's. On the twelve templates with their seeds' counts, the fitted picture's narrowest district is 86–130 px wide, its narrowest building 22–34 px and the drawing fills 61–72% of the tile, against Cloud's own tiles at 64–93 px, 19–28 px and 40–55%; each is 1.5–2.0 KB. Fitted to the map, `minBuilding` (default 4) is the width under which a district stands one block rather than its village, as before. A test measures every template's districts, buildings and fill from the SVG string with no DOM, holds the fitted corners to the layout the Scene runs, and holds the default to its old bytes.

Compatibility: additive. `SceneThumbnailOptions` gains optional `fit` (`"map"`, the default, or `"content"`) and `minBuilding`; a call without them gives the same bytes as before. `capabilities().shipped` gains `FR-107`. Ops, stored formats, wire messages, check codes and tool schemas are unchanged.
