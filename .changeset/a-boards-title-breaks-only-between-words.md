A board's title on the one face breaks only between words, on every system's type. At Nearby and the Map a title took two lines and broke a word it could not fit, hyphenated where the browser could and split bare where it could not: on a Linux runner, where system-ui is DejaVu and wider than the Mac's type, Deep Time's boards read "Research / ers" and "Extinctio / ns". A title's lines now break only at its spaces. Where a word is too wide for the board's line, the title's type is drawn smaller until it fits, measured in the type it is drawn in (`titleThatFits`), and measured again once the boards are shown and once a typeface has loaded, so the word is read whole; it goes no smaller than what the face says it is, which a phone's board draws at 9 pixels, and a word wider still is cut on its own line with an ellipsis. Words are what the browser breaks between: a no-break space keeps "New York" one word, a word is measured apart after its own hyphen or a zero-width space, a title in a script written without spaces (Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Thai and their neighbors) is left to the browser to wrap between its characters, a cut word's ellipsis follows its own direction, a title renamed is drawn anew, and the title is measured again as each typeface loads. The board's own name — its tooltip, what a screen reader is told and the title's words in the page — is still the whole title. Held by a new claim in `one-face`, `noBoardTitleSplitsAWord` (every board's title at the Map and Nearby on Deep Time, at six window sizes: each letter's box read off the page and gathered into the lines it was drawn on, and no line starting with a letter straight after the last one's, but after a hyphen or between characters of a script written without spaces), and by a unit test (`a-board-title-breaks-only-between-words`). With a building going sinking where it stood (`@graview/react`, in this release), every face grows by 2_725 / 1_018 bytes, under the raises `one-face-titles-and-slow-removal-every-face` and `one-face-titles-break-as-the-browser-breaks-every-face` in `scripts/budget-raises/`. Nothing a host calls changes.
Compatibility: the look — a board's title with a word too wide for its line is drawn smaller rather than hyphenated or broken. The JavaScript API, ops and primitives, stored formats, the wire, the declaration and check finding codes, and derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged.
