One guest client, served not copied (FR-88). `connectGuest` is a module, so every frame guest bundled or inlined its own copy, and under a policy that allows inline script alone it had to be pasted in by hand. `@graview/guest/client.js` is now the client as one prebuilt classic script (2,412 B), which leaves one global, `GraviewGuest`; `GraviewGuest.connect()` is `connectGuest()`. A host serves it at a path of its own, or inlines its text: `GUEST_CLIENT` from the new entry `@graview/guest/client`, with `GUEST_CLIENT_SHA256` for a policy that names the hash. It is built from `src/client-script.ts` by `pnpm guest:runtime`, and a test holds both current. The `graview-embed` skill gains the authoring guide for a frame guest: how to register one, its props with what it reads, the theme's tokens, acting, navigating and sizing. Its worked example is `examples/price-sheet.html`, each package with its offers, painted from the theme, a press that notes something, links to the offers, and its own height. `guest-sandbox --transport=client` serves that example, with the client inlined, from another origin under Graview Cloud's view policy (`default-src 'none'; script-src 'unsafe-inline'`), in the embed over the offers fixture as Lin. In Chromium, WebKit and Firefox it connects, draws each package's offers read across kinds and nothing of the offer Lin may not see, is painted from the app's light panel and repaints dark when the app's toggle goes dark though the system says light, takes the height it asks for, applies Lin's note through `view:prices`, follows a link to the record's page, and is a place by its title; a guest attached to the home is the home's body. A linked project made by `graview create` aliases the two new entries.

Compatibility: unchanged. The client speaks the protocol `connectGuest` does. `capabilities().shipped` gains `FR-88`. Ops, stored formats, check codes and tool schemas are unchanged.
