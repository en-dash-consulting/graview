A time of day is asked for. An argument whose pattern is a date with a time in it (`YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM`, the gauntlet's workshop start and a talk's slot) described itself as a plain date, so every face offered a date picker, the picker gave `2026-09-20`, and the act's own pattern refused it: no workshop could be made and no talk given a slot, on either face, at either width, with either hand. The date shape and its form field now carry `time: true` for such a pattern; the routed form, the scene's ask, the edit in place and the studio's agent panel ask with a date-and-time control, and the conversation and the starter fill one with a time of day.
