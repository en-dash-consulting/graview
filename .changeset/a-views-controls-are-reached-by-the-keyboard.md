A worker view's controls are reached by the keyboard by default. On Graview Cloud's Threat Watch, Tab reached 6 of the home's 92 things a person could click, and none on most views: the host gave the keyboard to a card bound to a record and to an element bound to an act, and to nothing a view drew and heard itself. Now any control a view draws that does not press of itself — a `role` of button, checkbox, switch or tab, an `aria-pressed`, an `aria-expanded`, on a `<div>` or a `<span>` the view hears with `graview.on("click")` — is given the keyboard where it has none (`tabindex="0"`; a tab the view keeps at `-1` keeps it) and drawn as something to press, and Enter or Space on it is told to the view as the key and then a `click`, as a button's is, with the page kept from scrolling under Space; an element bound to an act is told the same `click` after its key. A table's row bound to a record keeps its role and gives the keyboard to its first cell, where Enter opens the row. Whatever has the keyboard shows a ring: a whole outline at least 2px wide, set 2px off, in the app's accent, `!important` so a view's `outline: none` does not take it away — a view restyles it with `--graview-focus-color`, `--graview-focus-width` (never under 2px) and `--graview-focus-offset`. An `<svg>` with no `role`, `aria-label`, `aria-labelledby` or `<title>`, and nothing inside it that takes the keyboard, is hidden from assistive technology (`aria-hidden="true"`). `.graview-sr` is a class the region draws as nothing on the page and says to a screen reader. And the keyboard stays where it was across a render: an element with a `data-key` the view moves among its siblings was drawn anew by the host, which took the keyboard and what the host knew of the element with it; the host now puts the same element back where the view moved it and gives it the keyboard again if it had it.

`guest`'s tests draw a row, a `<div role="button">`, an `aria-pressed` and an `aria-expanded` span, a checkbox, a switch, two tabs, a button that says `aria-pressed` and four pictures, and find each control given the keyboard, pressed by Enter and Space as the key and a click with the default kept, the button left its own, the row opened from its first cell, the picture that says nothing hidden and the others left to say what they say; and a view's own runtime drawing a keyed list again in place, re-sorted twice and with the focused row gone, the keyboard on the same element each time and handed to nothing when it went. `node scripts/guest-sandbox.mjs --transport=taps` holds it in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox: Tab from the top of the page reaches all fifteen links and presses Threat Watch's home draws (Option-Tab in Safari, which passes over any link with an address without it), Space on a `<div role="button" aria-pressed>` the view hears itself presses it without scrolling the page and the keyboard stays on it as the view draws again, and the ring is solid, 2px, set off and at 3:1 or more against the ground behind it, light and dark. The worker views' guides say it: `graview-worker-view` (links and buttons by default) and `graview-view-forms`.

Compatibility: additive for a view — a control it drew with a role, `aria-pressed` or `aria-expanded` and no `tabindex` is now given `tabindex="0"`, and its listener for `click` now also hears Enter and Space on it; a decorative `<svg>` now carries `aria-hidden="true"`; a view's `outline` on `:focus-visible` gives way to the host's ring unless it sets the `--graview-focus-*` properties. The wire, the stored formats, the declaration and the derived tools are unchanged.
