Formulas join text and build a date-time (Roadmap f-formula-text-datetime). A computed event title or an act's start time could not be built: the rule language counted days and added numbers, and had no way to say "Tue 27 Oct · 9:30 AM" or to make the instant a plan item's day reaches at 9:30. Seven functions now: `concat(name, ' · ', place)` joins words, nothing joining as no words (where `+`, which already joined words, makes the whole nothing); `format(start, 'EEE d MMM · h:mm a')` says a moment by a closed pattern — `EEE`, `EEEE`, `d`, `MMM`, `MMMM`, `yyyy`, `h:mm a`, `HH:mm`, any other words in single quotes — in the app's `brand.locale` and on its `brand.timeZone`, never the reader's, since what the language builds may be kept on a record; `datetime(due, '09:30')` and its alias `at(due, startTime)` make the instant that day reaches at that wall time in the app's zone, kept as a datetime field keeps one (`2026-11-01T14:30:00Z` for 9:30 in New York on the day its clock falls back), on UTC when the app says no zone so a server and a page agree; `addMinutes(start, 90)` moves a moment, an instant staying an instant across a change of the clock; `weekday(due)` is `mon` to `sun`; `startOfWeek(due)` (or `startOfWeek(due, 'sun')`) is the day its week starts. Each is total — nothing in is nothing out — and every type mistake is a sentence. The arithmetic is one module (`expr/when.ts`), the clock the app's (`EvalContext.locale`, `timeZone`, which a compiled app passes from its brand). The checker types them from the declaration alone (`expr/typing.ts`, judged with `compileDocument` and kept off the page): `format` of a number field, `weekday` of words, a time of day not written `HH:MM`, minutes that are words and a first day it does not know are `expr-type`; a pattern piece outside the list, a pattern not in quotes and a time piece of a date that has no time are `format-pattern` — each at the expression's path, with the fix. `formulas-join-text-and-build-a-date-time.test.ts` holds them, on the days New York's clock changes.

An act updates the records it is linked to (Roadmap f-acts-update-linked). `setsOther` set fields on the far record only while `connects` made the link, so a person editing a plan item could not carry the change to its event. An act `on` a kind now says `"setsLinked": { "event": { "title": { "expr": "name" } } }`: the fields are set, in the same change, on every record its subject already reaches through the relation — from whichever end the declaration puts the subject on — each expression read standing on the subject with `to` the far record (`concat(name, ' for ', to.title)`) and `$to` that record; one undo takes all of it back. The store judges each far record's own edit before the act runs (`writesLinked` on the mutation; `edit-<kind>` as the policy and W-201 read it), and refuses the act naming the record where one is not the person's to change: "Not permitted: “Rename the item” would change “Old title”, an event that is not yours to change." Under a seat's sight the act reaches only the linked records it sees. The far kind's own edit still offers a carried field, since the act carries a value to it rather than naming a step. `graview check` says `act-sets-linked-relation` (no such relation joins the subject's kind, naming the ones that do; an act on no kind) and `act-sets-linked-field` (no such field at the far end; a value it could not hold), and `computed-written` for a worked-out field, in the person's voice; the hidden-reads check counts the far kind as read. `editDocument` keeps a carried field in step with a renamed field or relation, drops it with a removed one, and drops an act left doing nothing; `add-act` takes the key. `graview describe` gains "Carried across links". `an-act-sets-the-records-it-is-linked-to.test.ts` holds it. The graview-invariant, graview-view-specs and graview-sight skills say both.

A hosted page carries 7.1 KB more up front, minified (6.5 KB handed a compiled app), and its scene face 7.1 KB more before it draws: the evaluator and the date arithmetic, the compiled act's `through` effect, and the store's judgment of a far record; the typing stays with the checker. Cloud's worker carries 31_494 bytes more, unminified; each face, each visitor's form and each guest host measure under a raise of its own in `scripts/budget-raises/formulas-and-linked-sets-*.json`.

Compatibility: ops and primitives, and derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged (a `setsLinked` adds no argument; its far records are reached, never named). The wire: unchanged. Stored formats: `graview-compiled@1` may carry a `LinkedSetEffect` (`{ set, through }`) in an act's plan, which a page that predates it would not run — a host serves a compiled app from the build that compiled it, as it does; snapshots and the log are unchanged. The declaration and check finding codes: additive — an act takes `setsLinked`, which a build that predates it refuses by path; the rule language takes seven more functions, which such a build refuses as `unknown-function`; `expr-type`, `format-pattern`, `act-sets-linked-relation` and `act-sets-linked-field` are new, said by the checker alone, and no declaration that passed is refused. `effectsOf` may return a `LinkedSetEffect` beside the effects a document lists, a mutation definition may say `writesLinked`, and `EvalContext` takes `locale` and `timeZone`. What Graview Cloud does: offer `setsLinked` where it edits an act, and let a computed title or an act's value be built with the new functions; its worker's import budget is raised by name.
