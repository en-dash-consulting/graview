The seat draws a view on the fly, and it can be kept as a lens. Nick: "i want it to be able to generate the uis for me on the fly, quickly, with the ability to save that as a new lens". Asked for a way of seeing, `draftView` (`@graview/tools/draft`, a new entry fetched when a view is first asked for) drafts one from what the app already declares: "a board of decisions by status" is the `columns` lens on the `status` choice, "a timeline of dates" a calendar on the date a kind declares (a timeline where a kind's roles are minutes of a day in columns, as todo's week is), "who covers what" a coverage grid over the relation that joins two kinds, and "a list of decisions grouped by status" a lens drawn from blocks. With no model and no key, a template reads the ask: its words pick the lens and the kind, and the declaration's roles and field types fill the bindings. Given the app's `complete`, an ask no template reads goes to the model for the same JSON, and what comes back is judged as a declared lens is (`judgeLens`: the shipped lenses' own table, the bindings against the fields, each block's expressions parsed and every kind, field and choice they name read) before anything is drawn. A draft is a `SeatDraft`: its title, one line saying what it draws ("A board of decisions by status."), the lens, the `DrawnLens` the primitives draw it with, and the `add-lens` edit keeping it would make. `refineDraft` asks again — "group by owner", "only this month", "as a calendar", "call it The pipeline" — and each turn replaces the draft; a turn that cannot be drawn says why in one sentence ("Couldn't draw that: no date field on dates.") and hands back the last good one. Every draft is judged against the kinds the seat may see (`draftSight`, read from the policy's sights), so a kind it may not see reads as one that is not there: "Nothing by that name here". Keeping one changes the declaration, which is the host's, so it is another entry, `@graview/tools/keep`: `keepLens` runs the edit through `editDocument`, `compileDocument` and `diffDocuments` for a document app ("Adds “Decisions by status” to the places."), or `checkApp` over the declaration with the lens beside its own for an app declared in code, and hands back the place it made and the `remove-lens` edit that takes it back (`takeBackLens`). `withReaderLenses` puts a reader's own kept lenses beside a declaration's. An agent reaches both as tools when its host offers them: `createToolRuntime(store, { app, drafts: { complete, keep } })` lists `draft_view`, a read, and with `keep` `keep_lens`, which hands the host the checked edit. In the studio, taking a titled lens away is the `remove-lens` edit, where the studio said no edit could. The drafting engine judges with what a hosted page already runs, so it adds nothing to what the page loads before it is asked: the hosted page up front carries 43 bytes more (583.5 KB, its budget unchanged), the engine is a door of 28.4 KB, and the seat's two tool definitions ride with it (the scene before it draws 1.8 KB more, the pages face alone 2.0 KB; those budgets rise by that).

Compatibility: ops, stored formats, the wire, the document format and the edit vocabulary (`add-lens` and `remove-lens` were already there), and check finding codes are unchanged. New: the entries `@graview/tools/draft` (`draftView`, `templateDraft`, `refineDraft`, `judgeLens`, `draftSight`, `lensEditOf`, `takeBackEditOf`, `lensFromSpec`, `recipeOfLens`, `draftFromLens`, `draftAnswer`, `withReaderLenses`, `runDraftTool`, `isDraftFailure`, and the types `SeatDraft`, `DraftLens`, `DraftRecipe`, `DraftResult`, `DraftFailure`, `DraftSight`, `DraftOptions`, `AddLensEdit`, `RemoveLensEdit`) and `@graview/tools/keep` (`keepLens`, `takeBackLens`, `LensHolder`, `LensKept`, `LensRefused`); `ToolRuntimeOptions.drafts` and `ToolDefinitionsOptions.drafts`, which list the tools `draft_view` and `keep_lens` (a surface that does not ask for them keeps its tools and its hash); the types `DraftTools` and `KeptLensEdit`; and the studio's act `remove-lens`. What Graview Cloud changes: to let a connector's chat draw and keep views, build its tool runtime with `drafts: { keep }`, where `keep(edit)` applies the `add-lens` edit to the stored document as its other declaration edits are applied (or with `keepLens` from `@graview/tools/keep`), and list its tools with `toolDefinitions(app, principal, { drafts: "keep" })` so the hash it compares agrees.
