A worker view is handed its records' computed values (Roadmap f-computed-in-views). A kind's computed values showed on the built-in pages and were missing from the records a custom view was handed, so a view counted again from the edges what its page already said. Now every record a view is handed — its own record, the members it is drawn for and the records it reads — carries each computed value beside its fields, by name (`props.node.squad`), always, with nothing to ask for in the manifest: a number, text or a yes as itself, a record as `{ id, kind, label }`, a set as a list of them; one that cannot be worked out is left off, as a field with no value is. They are worked out over the viewer's graph, as every page works them out, so a field the viewer's sight hides and a record it may not see add nothing to one: a parent who may not see a player's phone is handed "no number", and a team's count leaves out the prospects they may not see. The evaluator is a chunk of its own, fetched before a view whose kinds have computed values starts (`readsComputed` on `@graview/guest/host/views`), and a view run headless is handed them too. The guest host carries about 3.1 KB more before a worker is drawn (1.4 KB gzipped), and as much registering and drawing one, since the modules that chunk shares with what loads first keep what it reads, under raises of those budgets (`scripts/budget-raises/a-view-is-handed-computed-values-*.json`). Guest's test hands a view a team and its players as a coach and as a parent and holds the parent's computed values to what the parent may see, with no hidden phone or record anywhere in what the view is handed, headless or not. The graview-worker-view skill says a record carries its computed values.

Compatibility: ops and primitives, stored formats, the wire, the declaration and check finding codes, derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged. A view's records carry more keys: a computed value's name, which no field of the kind may share. Additive on `@graview/guest/host/views` (`readsComputed`, `ComputedOn`; `WorkerViewPropsInput.computed`). What Graview Cloud does: nothing in its code; a view can read `props.node.<computed>` once it builds on this.
