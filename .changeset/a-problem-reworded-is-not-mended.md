A problem reworded is not mended (the ninth walk, W-191). A rule that is a function says its count in its message — "Meadow 07 has not been opened for 22 days" — and `problemsMoved` knew such a problem by its message, so on a co-op of 299 hives the day turning (`store.tick`) answered 34 problems cleared and 280 appeared, when none had cleared and 246 had appeared; an act that left such a rule broken and changed its count was said to break it in the activity too. Now a problem the same rule says about the same record under the same name, in other words, is paired off with the one it was before anything is called made or mended.

Compatibility: `problemsMoved` (and so `Store.tick`'s `appeared` and `cleared`, `previewLines`' and the activity's `introduces` and `resolves`) no longer lists a problem as both made and mended when only its message changed; its shape is unchanged.
