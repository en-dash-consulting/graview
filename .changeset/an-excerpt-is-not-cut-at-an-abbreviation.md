An excerpt is not cut short at an abbreviation's stop. `{summary|excerpt}` said the first sentence of "Dr. Okafor reviews every request…" as "Dr.", and of "E.g. a garden…" as "E.g.": any stop followed by a space ended the first sentence. A stop that ends an abbreviation ("e.g.", "U.S.", "Dr.", "Mr.") is passed over, and so is one before three words, unless it ends the text; a short first sentence that is a sentence ("Ship it on Friday.") still ends the excerpt.

Compatibility: unchanged. What a card says of a text that opens with an abbreviation changes.
