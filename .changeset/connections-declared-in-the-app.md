An app says which calendar it keeps in step with in its own declaration, rather than in code. A document's `connections.<name>` names the system (`google-calendar`), the calendar (or the environment variable holding its id), the kind its events are and which field holds each part of one, a filter (the whole calendar, one of Google's colors by name, or a title tag such as `#graview`, kept in the title or not), the direction (`read`, where the calendar owns the kind and an act here that would change one is refused with a sentence saying so, or `both`) and the conflict policy. `graview check` says each wrong shape at its place: a kind or field the app lacks, a field of the wrong type, a color Google has no name for, a tag that is not one word, and an app with no act to land an inbound change through. A `date` field is an all-day event's day, its end inclusive here; a `datetime` field a timed event's instant. Filters apply as events arrive, since Google refuses a sync token beside a search: an event that leaves the filter is gone here, and one made here carries the color or tag that keeps it in, and a private property naming the app. `graview serve` and `graview preview` run the connections beside the store, with credentials from the environment (`GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID`, `GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET`, `GOOGLE_REFRESH_TOKEN`, or the prefix and file `auth` names), each pass credited to `google-calendar` in Activity and the cursor kept in `connections.json` beside the data; `--no-sync` or a declared `paused` leaves them off. A host runs them with `runConnections` from `@graview/ship` or `@graview/ship/runtime`, handing its own `token({ connection, refresh })` and a place to keep their state. The studio carries an app's connections through an edit, the calendar following a renamed kind.

A page that draws an app reads `connections` loosely and carries none of this, so a hosted page is unchanged; the studio's checker judges them, which adds 5.6 KB (2.1 KB gzipped) to every face and to the embed with the studio handed in, and those raises are filed in `scripts/budget-raises/`.
