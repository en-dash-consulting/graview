The app's name on a phone's bar keeps room for its mark, however many tools stand beside it. On a bar under 300 pixels wide with Activity, the standing and the person in it, the name shrank to a 5-pixel press: graview.dev's docs app at 320 wide, once its run had written three docs. The name now keeps 28 pixels, its mark, and a bar that narrow puts 4 pixels between its parts instead of 10.

Compatibility: no surface changes.
