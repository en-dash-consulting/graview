Letting go of a pan on the one face, nothing moves. At Nearby and the City every board, district name and line moved with the ground while a person panned, and when the pan stopped they all snapped back to where the pan began, then slid over to where they had been. The boards and names are placed in the picture's pixels as the city was last read; a pan written into the view lays the city out again where the hand left it, and the old numbers, drawn against the new picture, stood where the pan began, and the settle after the city was read again started from there. The city read again now says how its districts moved (`carriedBetween`: the pan, and a zoom's growth), what was drawn is carried by the same before anything settles, and a pan that changed nothing but where the city stands against the window places nothing again: what is seen stays where the last frame drew it, and only a board or name with no place yet, or one the pan took out of the window or under the bar, the page or the control, is placed, round them, coming up where it lands (`placeSigns` takes `held`). Where the districts disagree (one grew under a record added as the hand panned), the camera the last frame drew through says how far to carry. A board placed again a little smaller, or back to its full size, now goes there over the settle, never from one size to the other in a frame. A drag begun on a board and a scroll over a district, which pan through the view rather than the Scene's hand, no longer trail the ground and slide after it. A zoom let go of places the boards and names again for the new zoom, as before, and each now goes there from where the last frame of the zoom drew it rather than jumping back to where the zoom began. Held by two new claims in `one-face` — `letGoOfAPanEveryBoardAndNameStaysWhereTheLastFrameDrewIt` (a drag on the ground, a drag begun on a board, a drag toward the page, a scroll and a scroll over a district at a desk, a finger's drag on a phone, at Nearby and the City: each moves the ground at least 20 pixels, and no box on the window moves more than a pixel for 600 ms after) and `letGoOfAZoomEveryBoardAndNameGoesOnFromWhereTheLastFrameDrewIt` (ctrl and the wheel closer and further, over the ground and over a district, two fingers on a phone: the first frame after letting go draws every box where the last frame of the zoom did, and no frame takes more than a third of its way) — and by a unit test (`a-pan-let-go-of-carries-what-was-drawn-with-the-ground`). A hosted page carries nothing more up front; every face grows by 2_635 / 975 bytes, in the one face's city chunk fetched later only, under the raise `letting-go-of-a-pan-nothing-moves-every-face` in `scripts/budget-raises/`. Nothing a host calls changes.
Compatibility: the look — a board or a name no longer moves when a pan is let go of. The JavaScript API — unchanged; `placeSigns` takes an optional `held`. Ops and primitives, stored formats, the wire, the declaration and check finding codes, and derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged.
