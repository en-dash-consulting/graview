A routed page can be read over the Scene's own city, and stepping back from it is the Scene's camera rising (Graview HQ feature:f-page-zooms-out-to-the-city, a prototype behind a flag). A `PagesApp` handed `views` and `sceneUnderPages: true` mounts the Scene of `@graview/react` under its routes, in the provider the pages already draw with, at its overview, so the city is the one `layout` and `placeCity` give the Scene face, never a second city laid out beside it. While the page is read the Scene is hidden and takes no pointer and no keyboard. One control, "Up", in the window's lower left corner, is both the step back and the Scene's Up. On a record's page at a desk it goes page, stepped back, district, city: stepped back, the camera comes down close over the record's building, lit as the Scene lights a chosen record, and the page stands on its roof at about a third of its size, turned a little on its upright axis; at the district the camera takes in the record's district and the page is small on its building; at the city the camera is level, nothing stands over the Scene and the Scene's own hand, zoom and keyboard work. On a phone (600 pixels or narrower) the stops are page, district and city, and the page is drawn flat from above. A kind's list stands on its district, a place that shows several kinds stands where their districts meet, or on a plaza above the city where that falls inside one kind's district, with a line to each district, and home stands over the whole city with the camera level; stepping back from home once more is the overview at `/places/overview`. The app still opens on its home page. The camera follows where the Scene draws the place now, every frame, so a city that reflows carries the page with it and the camera eases after it; nothing freezes the layout. Pressing a building at any stop above the page flies the camera to it and opens that record's page; the Scene's own way down (a record focused, a district gone into) opens that record's page or that kind's list. Following a link on a page steps back over where it was, flies to where it goes and comes in there. The address is the page's route throughout, so Back and Forward work, and back in, the page is scrolled where it was. Stepped back, a line runs from each part of the page that shows a record (a link to its page, a row that names it) to that record's building, or to its district where the city draws no building for it, and from each link to a kind's list to its district, up to forty, each ending in a ring the keyboard reaches that opens what it points at. A pinch out, or ctrl and the wheel on a trackpad, steps back from a page; stepped back, the wheel goes up and down the stops and Escape comes down one. With reduced motion asked for, every move cuts straight to where it ends.

The Scene, the camera and the lines are a chunk fetched as the page first draws. The pages face alone grows by 14.0 KB (5.4 KB gzipped), every face by 18.3 KB (7.1 KB gzipped) and an embed with the studio handed in by 3.6 KB gzipped, under raises of their budgets.

Tests: `a-page-stands-on-its-place-in-the-scenes-city` (pages) holds the stops a record, home and the overview have at a desk and on a phone, that a record page's foot is on its building's roof wherever the camera stands, that home and the city stop keep the camera level, that the page is turned at a desk and flat on a phone, and that a place of several kinds never stands inside one kind's district.

Compatibility: the look — unchanged unless `sceneUnderPages` is given; the examples Things and the discography turn it on. The JavaScript API — additive: `PageContext.sceneUnderPages`. Ops and primitives — unchanged. Stored formats — unchanged. The wire — unchanged. The declaration and check finding codes — unchanged. Derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged.
