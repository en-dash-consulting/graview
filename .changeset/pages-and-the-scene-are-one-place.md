A routed page can be read over the Scene's own city, and going up from it is the Scene's own camera rising (Graview HQ feature:f-page-zooms-out-to-the-city, a prototype behind a flag). A `PagesApp` handed `views` and `sceneUnderPages` mounts the Scene of `@graview/react` under its routes, in the provider the pages already draw with, at its overview, so the city is the one `layout` and `placeCity` give the Scene face, never a second city laid out beside it. While the page is read the Scene is not drawn at all (`display: none`), so it takes no room, no scroll, no pointer and no keyboard. There are four levels. The page (L0) is the routed page, the whole window, with an Up control on its bar. The panel (L1) is the Scene's own map panel holding the page's place, the city beside it on a desk and above it on a phone (where the panel is a sheet from the foot), the camera centered on the record's building or the kind's district in the room the panel leaves: a record's panel shows its fields and, under "Connected", what it is linked to grouped by relation, with a line from each connected row to that record's building (or its district where the plot draws no building for it); a kind's list shows its district's panel; a place shows its lens. "Open ↗" in the panel, which says it in place of "Go to it" and "Go in", is the page again. Stood back (L2), the page stands on its building's roof (a place of one kind on its district, of several where they meet, home over the whole city), turned a little at a desk and flat on a phone, with a line from each part of it that shows a record to that record's building. The overview is the whole city with nothing over it; for home, it is at `/places/overview`. The Scene's lens drawn as a card over the grid and its shelf of districts are not drawn on this face: the Scene stays at its overview, and a place is a page like any other.

There is one camera. A level asks the Scene's own zoom from altitude and its pan for the place (a new `camera` on `Scene` hands out `SceneCamera`: `zoom()`, `zoomTo(scale, clientX, clientY)`, `panBy(dx, dy)` and its `range`), and the page and the lines are placed every frame from where the Scene draws the place in that frame, in the window's own coordinates, so a drag, a zoom or a city that reflows carries them with it and nothing is drawn against a second transform. Above the page the Scene's own hand has the wheel, the drag and the pinch: a zoom past a level's range is the next level, one level a gesture, and the camera is then aimed as that level asks; the Scene's own reach keeps the city from being dragged off the window. Up and Down stand together in a corner above the page; on the page a pinch out, or ctrl and the wheel on a trackpad, is Up; Escape comes down a level; a press on the page standing in the city brings it in. Pressing a building or a connected row puts that record in the panel, the camera flying to it; the Scene's own way down opens that place's page. Following a link on a page stands it back over where it was, flies to where it goes and comes in there. The address is the page's route at every level, so Back and Forward work, and back in, the page is scrolled where it was. With reduced motion asked for, every move cuts straight to where it ends.

The map panel is handed in by the host (`sceneUnderPages: { panel: MapPanelOpeningPages }`, from `@graview/primitives/scene`) rather than imported by the pages, and the page's way into the city is fetched only where a face asks for it, so a hosted page loads what it did, in the same thirteen chunks. The pages face alone grows by 5.5 KB (3.2 KB gzipped), every face by 25.0 KB (11.2 KB gzipped) and an embed with the studio handed in by 1.6 KB gzipped, under raises of their budgets.

Tests: `a-page-stands-on-its-place-in-the-scenes-city` (pages) holds the levels a record, home and the overview have, at a desk and on a phone; that the camera comes closest at the panel, less stood back, and level over the city; that a zoom past a level's range moves one level and a hand resting near its own moves none; that a record page's foot is on its building's roof, turned a little at a desk and flat on a phone; and that a place of one kind stands on its district and of several where they meet, never inside one kind's district.

Compatibility: the look — unchanged unless `sceneUnderPages` is given; the examples Things and the discography turn it on, and a routed face's bar holds Up when it is. The JavaScript API — additive: `PageContext.sceneUnderPages`, `SceneProps.camera` and the `SceneCamera` type, and `MapPanelOpeningPages` and `MapPanelOpensPage` on `@graview/primitives/scene`. Ops and primitives — unchanged. Stored formats — unchanged. The wire — unchanged. The declaration and check finding codes — unchanged. Derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged.
