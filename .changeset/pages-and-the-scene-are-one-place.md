A routed page can be read over the Scene's own city, and the page itself is what moves as the camera rises (Graview HQ feature:f-page-zooms-out-to-the-city, a prototype behind a flag). A `PagesApp` handed `views` and `sceneUnderPages: true` mounts the Scene of `@graview/react` under its routes, in the provider the pages already draw with, at its overview, so the city is the one `layout` and `placeCity` give the Scene face, never a second city laid out beside it. Nothing is chosen on the Scene, so it draws no lines of its own and no outline of a choice, and its lens over the grid and its shelf of districts are not drawn on this face. While the page is read the Scene is laid out out of sight, with no pointer, no keyboard and no room in the page, so the first frame out has it to move.

One number says where a person is, the altitude: 0 is the page, the whole window; 1 is the same page as a panel, a column at the window's right on a desk and a sheet from the foot on a phone, the city beside it centered on its building (or its district, or where its districts meet), with a Connected list grown into its foot of what the record is linked to, by relation; 2 is the same page standing on its building's roof, turned a little at a desk and flat on a phone, the city around it; 3 is the whole city, the page shrunk into its building. Every step between is drawn: the page's box narrows from the window to the panel frame by frame, its words laid out again as it narrows, while the city fades in and the camera pulls back behind it; from the panel the same element tilts and shrinks onto its roof as the camera pulls back; Down reverses each step. Up on the page's own bar, and Up and Down together in a corner above the page, move a level; ctrl and the wheel (a trackpad's pinch) and two fingers on the page scrub the altitude as far as the hand goes, and two fingers over the city zoom the Scene's camera, which the altitude is read from; let go, it settles to the nearest level. A drag pans the camera at every level. With reduced motion asked for, every move cuts straight to where it ends.

There is one camera, the Scene's own. `SceneCamera` (from `Scene`'s new `camera` prop) hands out its zoom and its move between stops: `live()` (what is painted now), `liveTo()` (paint a move now, in this frame, outside React), `commit()` (write it into the view, the city laid out again where the move left it, nothing tweening) and `moving()`. Each animation frame reads that camera, sets the page's box and standing from the altitude, and draws the lines into one overlay without React state: a line's building end is the building's place in the picture carried through the camera painted in that frame, and its page end is read from the page after its transform for that frame is written. A line whose building is under the page ends at the page's edge with an arrow toward it; each line's end that is in sight is a ring the keyboard reaches. Pressing a building or a connected row puts that record in the panel, the camera flying there; the address is the page's route at every level, so Back and Forward work.

The page's way into the city is fetched only where a face asks for it, so a hosted page loads what it did, in the same thirteen chunks. The pages face alone grows by 5.8 KB (3.7 KB gzipped), every face by 28.8 KB (12.5 KB gzipped) and an embed with the studio handed in by 1.9 KB gzipped, under raises of their budgets.

Tests: `a-page-stands-on-its-place-in-the-scenes-city` (pages) holds that the altitude is read off the camera's zoom, steadily between levels and back, a little of a pinch a little of the way; that the page's box narrows from the window to the panel through every box between, a column at a desk and a sheet from the foot on a phone; that the page's foot stands on its roof at 2, turned at a desk and flat on a phone, and is gone into its building at 3; that the camera moves the place in a straight line on the window; that a line to a building under the page ends at the page's edge; and that a place of one kind stands on its district and of several where they meet, never inside one kind's district.

Compatibility: the look — unchanged unless `sceneUnderPages` is given; the examples Things and the discography turn it on, and a routed face's bar holds Up when it is. The JavaScript API — additive: `PageContext.sceneUnderPages`, `SceneProps.camera` and the `SceneCamera` type. Ops and primitives — unchanged. Stored formats — unchanged. The wire — unchanged. The declaration and check finding codes — unchanged. Derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged.
