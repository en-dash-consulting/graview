The app bar is calmer: the app and its pages on the left; Find, one inbox, a host's tools, the people and a host's Share, and you on the right (Roadmap f-app-bar-calm, which takes in f-bar-status-item and f-scheme-choice-mount). Homeflow's bar held ten things in seven shapes: the name, a Scene | Pages pill, the pages, a Find box, "25 Suggested", "4 Activity", Cloud's "Workspace" and "Send feedback", the people and the person. Every face that wears the one app bar — the pages, the Scene, the whole-page Shell and the embed — now draws:

- **An app menu on the app's name.** A chevron beside the name (which stays the page's heading and the way home) opens a menu that holds Scene and Pages, the one drawn checked, then a host's rows (`appMenu`), then the scheme where a host turns it on (`schemeChoice: { value: "system" | "light" | "dark", onChoose, offers? }`). The Shell's light and dark choice moved there from the person's menu. The menu's rows are fetched with the bar's panes when it is first reached for.
- **One inbox in place of Suggested and Activity.** A bell, the bar's one size, with one count — what waits for you and what has happened, said apart in its name ("Inbox — 2 waiting for you, 4 changes") — that opens three tabs: For you (what people proposed, what the app's own actor suggests, what was done while you were away, and a host's `notifications`), Activity (the same feed, each change with its way back, and the agent's seat), and Your asks (the quick ask's and the inline AI's kept interactions, `ai.interactions`, not drawn where AI is off). Each open lands on For you where anything waits. The tabs are fetched as the bell is first pressed.
- **A host's Share beside the people.** `share: { label?, mark?: "lock" | "globe" | "people" | <glyph or svg>, description, link? }` is a button with a small mark and a word whose sentence says who can open the app — on hover, to a screen reader as its description, and in the popover a press opens, with the link to where that changes.
- **Every tool one shape.** The inbox, the standing, a host's button and Find's magnifier are the bar's 30 px icon tools; a count is a badge, the standing's included. A host's `BarAction` may be `iconOnly: true`, its label the button's name and tooltip, and up to two such stand on a phone's bar too. Find stays a box where the bar has room and is the magnifier below 900 px of the bar's own width (it was 640), opening its box over the row.

The embed's `mount` and the pages' `mount` take `appMenu`, `schemeChoice`, `share`, `notifications` and `onNotificationsSeen`, and their handles `setAppMenu`, `setShare`, `setSchemeChoice` and `setNotifications`, each a change without a reload. The Shell takes `appMenu` and `share`; the provider takes `notifications` and `onNotificationsSeen`, inherited from the provider around it. The graview-embed, graview-host-page, graview-new-app and graview-tidying skills say the new bar.

verify-chrome holds `theAppMenuHoldsTheScenePagesTheHostsRowsAndTheScheme`, `oneInboxIsOneBellWithOneCountAndThreeTabs`, `aHostsShareSaysInASentenceWhoCanOpenTheApp`, `aHostsButtonDrawnAsItsMarkIsNamedByItsLabel`, `findIsABoxWhereThereIsRoomAndTheMagnifierSooner` and `theBarHoldsStillAndNothingOnItOverlapsAtEveryWidth` on both faces of a hosted todo filled as Graview Cloud fills it, the Shell and the routed face, at 1440, 1024, 768 and 390 px in both schemes; every popover of the family is driven there with the rest. The unit test `the-app-bar-is-calm` holds the same in jsdom. A hosted page spends 4.8 KB more up front (0.9 KB gzipped) for the menu's control, Share and the inbox's bell; the tabs and the menu's rows are fetched when first opened.

Compatibility: the look — the app bar changed shape. Removed: the switch on the row (`.graview-bar-faces`, `.graview-bar-face`, `data-switch`, `data-switch-drawn`), the `suggested-button` and the app bar's `activity-button` (Activity's `activity-button` remains only on the studio's own bar), the "N Suggested" and "N Activity" words and `.graview-activity-word` / `--graview-activity-mark`, and the profile pane's `profile-scheme-light` / `profile-scheme-dark`. Moved: `app-faces`, `app-face-scene` and `app-face-pages` are now inside the app menu (`app-menu-open` opens `app-menu`); the Suggested list's `suggested` and Activity's `activity` are now inside the inbox (`inbox-button` opens `inbox`, tabs `inbox-tab-for-you`, `inbox-tab-activity`, `inbox-tab-asks`). New: `app-menu-open`, `app-menu`, `app-menu-row`, `app-menu-scheme-<choice>`, `inbox-button`, `inbox-count`, `inbox`, `inbox-panel-<tab>`, `inbox-notification`, `inbox-empty`, `app-share-open`, `app-share`, `app-share-said`, `app-share-link`, `standing-count`, and `.graview-bar-tool`, `.graview-bar-badge`, `.graview-bar-host-words`, `[data-icon-only]`. Find is the magnifier below 900 px of the bar's width, no longer only below 640. The popover registry (`POPOVERS`) gains `app-menu`, `inbox` and `share` and loses `suggested`; `activity` is the studio's. The JavaScript API — removed outright (pre-1.0): the embed's `switch` option and the `BarSwitch` type, `BarActivity` and `BarSuggested` (now `BarInbox` and `useInboxWanted`), `SuggestedTray` (now `SuggestedList` and `SuggestedCount` inside the inbox), and `Profile`'s and `ProfilePane`'s `scheme` / `onScheme` (the Shell's `scheme` / `onScheme` props stay, drawn in the app menu). New: `appMenu`, `schemeChoice`, `share`, `notifications` and `onNotificationsSeen` on both mounts with `setAppMenu`, `setShare`, `setSchemeChoice` and `setNotifications` on their handles; `appMenu` and `share` on the Shell; `notifications` and `onNotificationsSeen` on `GraviewProvider`; `BarAction.iconOnly`; the types `AppMenuRow`, `BarScheme`, `SchemeChoice`, `BarShare` and `HostNotification`; `Inbox`, `inboxWords`, `badgeCount`, `BAR_FIND_FOLDS`. Ops and primitives — unchanged. Stored formats — unchanged. The wire — unchanged. The declaration and check finding codes — unchanged. Derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged.

What Graview Cloud does: fill `share` with `reachOf`'s sentence (lock for a workspace, globe for anyone with the link or public) and a link to People & sharing, calling `setShare` when sharing changes; hand the inbox Cloud's notifications (`notifications`, marking them read from `onNotificationsSeen`, `setNotifications` as they arrive); add Cloud's App settings, Connections and Appearance to the app menu (`appMenu`, and `schemeChoice` from the person's account Appearance in place of the row that stepped through the three); make Send feedback `iconOnly: true`; and drop the CSS that hid `.graview-bar-host .graview-bar-host-action>span`.
