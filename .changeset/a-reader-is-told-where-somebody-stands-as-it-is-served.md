A reader of an app that publishes is told where somebody stands only as it is served the app (FR-211). Who is here was judged against what a seat's sight reaches at the head, and an app that publishes with no `sees` judged nothing at all, so a reader was told a collaborator's `stop`, `over` and `caret` on a record not yet published — an id the reader had never been served. Now a reader is told a presence as the published view says it: a record the log knows and the view does not hold is one it may not be told of, so the person is shown here, standing nowhere it could not go itself, and a caret is told only in a field the reader is served as it now reads, since where somebody types in words not yet published says what they are. Once the record is published, where they stand is told again. A collaborator is told everything, as before. `readsPublished` (whether a seat is served what was published) is on `@graview/core`'s main entry. Ship's test holds a reader told nothing of a record not yet published, then where somebody stands once it is, and no caret in text not yet published.

Compatibility: ops and primitives, stored formats, the declaration and check finding codes, derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged. The wire: `/graview/who`, `/graview/here` and a socket's `presence` tell a reader of an app that publishes less — no `stop`, `over` or `caret` that names a record or text it is not served.
