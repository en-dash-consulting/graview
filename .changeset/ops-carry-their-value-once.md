An op carries its value once less: op format 2 keeps no inverse. Roadmap f-ops-without-inverse-on-the-wire: an op wrote what it changed in the call's arguments, in its primitives' `before` and `after`, and again in an `inverse` that was nothing but those primitives turned round — so a 19 KB picture changed in a text field was a 95 KB op, and most of why a heavy hosted app's log ran to 4.9 MB. The inverse is now worked out from the primitives where it is read, `inverseOf(op)`, remembered per op, and read by undo, a selective undo, a rebase's rollback, playback, verifying a fold and the seat's view of a withheld op alike; every primitive's inverse is another primitive, so nothing it needs was missing. `FORMATS.op` is 2. On the Beatles app with a 19 KB cover on each of its 18 releases, each cover changed once and 200 plain edits (`node scripts/op-bytes.mjs`), a cover's change is 57.4 KB where it was 95.5 KB, an added cover 38.4 KB where it was 57.5 KB, an undo of a change 38.4 KB where it was 76.5 KB, and the whole log 1.96 MB where it was 3.17 MB (38% less); with 479 covers, 72.6 MB comes to 45.5 MB. What is left twice is the value in the call and in the patch, which a rebase plays again and a sync sends on, and the value it replaced, which undo puts back.

A format-1 log opens on this build as it did, and nothing on disk is rewritten to open it: a store drops an inverse that is its primitives' own from every op it opens on, receives or rebases onto (`bareOp`, the `upgradeOp` step from 1), keeps one that is not (a host could once hand `append` its own), and its file adapter's compaction writes what it keeps in format 2. An export is stamped `op: 2` and its ops carry no inverse; a bundle from before imports and undoes the same. The live wire carries format 2 as an addition to protocol 1: `openRemote` says it reads it — `opFormat` in its hello, `?opFormat=2` on `/graview/state` and `/graview/since`, `opFormat` in the body it posts — and is sent ops bare; a client that says nothing, as every one before 0.1.43, is sent each op with its inverse written out (`opsFor`), so an open tab on the last build goes on undoing and playing back against this server. A host that routes its own requests passes `WireAsked.opFormat` (`opFormatOf(url)` reads the query). `core`'s tests fold, undo one batch at a time, take back an early batch with what depends on it and redo it, play back at every seq, verify a fold and find where it parts, rebase and receive with the same log in both formats and get the same graph each time, and open 0.1.0's format-1 fixture and go on; `ship`'s open a 0.1.42 store on disk, undo, append and reopen it, and serve both readers on every route and the socket. A hosted page carries 0.5 KB more up front (515 bytes, about 450 gzipped; the compiled page the same), the derived inverse, the format-1 inverse dropped and the format said on the wire, under a raise of each budget by that (`scripts/budget-raises/ops-carry-their-value-once-*.json`), and the scene face's before-draw line is 962 KB.

Compatibility: breaking for ops and stored formats, before 1.0 — `Operation.inverse` is optional and this build writes none (read it with `inverseOf`), and `AppendOp.inverse` is gone; op format 2, so a build before 0.1.43 opening a store this one has opened, or importing a bundle it exported, refuses it with `NewerFormatError` ("Upgrade the framework to read it") rather than undo an op it finds no inverse on, and a host rolling a store back writes its log out with `withInverse` under `op: 1`. Additive on the wire: `hello.opFormat`, `?opFormat=` and a post's `opFormat` are fields a server before 0.1.43 ignores, sending format 1, which this build reads; a client that says none is sent format 1 as before. `WIRE_PROTOCOL` and the subprotocol do not move, and the declaration and derived tools are unchanged.
