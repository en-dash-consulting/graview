A store `openRemote` opened is trusted to show a field its host judged, and no other store on the page is (Roadmap f-field-sight). Opening one set a switch every store read, so a page that also held a whole store of its own — `openStore`, or a `Store` — showed that store's seats every record a sight with `hide` or `fields` reaches, phone numbers and all, without field sight loaded to keep them. The trust is now the mirror's own (`FIELD_SIGHT.served`, a set `openRemote` adds its store to), and a store without field sight loaded fails closed everywhere it judges: `kindsKeptFrom` keeps a kind only such a sight shows from a seat that may begin none, and whether a seat sees every record of a kind — what an act may mint for it — says no. An agent's tool for an act no longer names a field the seat may not see: an argument that writes a field no sight granting the seat that kind shows is left out of the act's input schema (`argumentsNeverShown` on `@graview/core/json-schema`), as a form leaves it out, while one the seat sees on some records — its own child's phone — stays.

Compatibility: derived tool input schemas narrow for a seat whose sights keep a field on every record of a kind it may write: the argument is no longer listed, so that seat's tool-surface hash moves; every other seat's surface is unchanged, and the store refuses such a write as it did. `FIELD_SIGHT.on` now means only that field sight is loaded; `FIELD_SIGHT.served` is new. `sees` takes an optional sixth argument, whether a sight that shows only some fields may show the record. Ops, stored formats, the wire and the document format are unchanged. docs/stability.md says it under the seat's sight.
