Type in the right-click menu, Roadmap f-menu-type-anything (from request:when-i-right-click-on-anything-i-want-to-be-able-to-type-in-something-about-it). "When I right-click on anything, I want to be able to type in something about it": the menu offered its rows and nothing to type, so a long menu was read row by row and a question about the thing under the cursor meant closing it and opening the quick ask. The menu now has a field at its top, on both faces — it is the same pane on the scene and on the pages. It opens with the keyboard on its first row as before, so the arrows still walk the rows and a finger on a phone raises no keyboard until the field is tapped; a letter typed anywhere in the menu goes into the field, the keyboard with it, where the acts' type-ahead used to jump to the next act that began with it. What is typed narrows every row — the acts, the withheld ones, Open, Open in a new tab, Copy link, Show in the scene, Find, Notify me, Inspect — to those whose words begin with it, by a plain word-start match (each word typed begins one of the row's words, in any order, without case or accents: `menuMatches` in `packages/primitives/src/menu-words.ts`), not Find's matcher: the menu's rows are a dozen sentences already drawn, not records to rank. Where the host gave a Fast slot (Jev, or its `decide`), the local match still answers on the first key, and once the typing rests for 250 ms the Fast slot is asked one Choice — which of the menu's rows the words mean, "none of these" among them — whose answer refines the list: a row it finds likely is shown though none of its words begins with what was typed ("complete" finds "Finish it"), and its best is lit; never in the way of a key, nothing asked while the keys come, and an answer for words since changed is dropped (`useFastRank`, `packages/primitives/src/menu-rank.ts`). The first that fits is lit, never one that cannot be taken back; Enter runs it, the arrow down walks into the rows and the arrow up from the first comes back, and Escape takes the words back before it puts the menu away. Where the host lent the app a model and the quick ask is drawn, the last row is always "Ask about Pay the deposit: <words>", lit when nothing else fits: Enter puts the menu away and opens the quick ask's card beside what the menu was opened on, answering the words at once — the same path "Ask about this…" takes — and while it answers a small mark, "Thinking about Pay the deposit…", sits on the thing itself (the scene's card, the page's row or card, the record's heading), a polite status for a screen reader, never a modal; the change it proposes waits on the card as a ghost, accepted as one batch with its one undo. Free words take two steps: where a Fast slot is given, `answerHere` first asks it, in Jev's own typed questions, which of the catalog's candidates (`catalogOf`) could answer or act on the words — a Choice, "none of these" among them, kept best first while likely enough, at most eight — and, a Noul each, whether the words are about each record around the target (`aiContextOf`'s selection and neighbors), kept where more likely than not, at most six — and only those reach the Thinking slot (or the host's Answer slot), which may pick only among them; with no Fast slot the default the target is scoped to is sent. Either way the interaction's entry says what was sent and who chose it (`AiInteraction.context`, `AiContextSent`: `by` "fast" or "default", the Fast slot's `model`, the candidate ids and record ids), and Activity says it under the entry: "Told the model of 1 thing it could do and 1 record, as Jev picked them". `a-quick-ask-sends-the-thinking-slot-what-the-fast-slot-picked.test.ts` holds it.

To try it with real models, the dev server lends the page both slots. Where it holds a key for Jev (`TYPESAFE_API_KEY`, or `JEV_API_KEY`), the Fast slot is Jev itself: `aiThroughDevServer` finds `decisionBridge`'s door open and makes `jevDecide` through it the Fast slot (`jev-latest`, the key held by the server), and the todo example's dev server now draws that door. Without one, `aiDevProxy` names a small quick model in its probe (`fast`, `claude-haiku-5-5` unless `GRAVIEW_AI_FAST_MODEL` names another) and asks it, at low effort and a short answer, each request a page posts with `tier: "fast"` (`AiBridgeAsk.tier`); `aiThroughDevServer` makes that the page's Fast slot, answered as a decision provider would (`completionDecide`) — a stand-in for Jev, in a dev server only, since a build carries no door. And the dev server's model door (`aiDevProxy`) takes xAI and OpenAI beside Anthropic: the provider is the one whose key is set — `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` (the Messages API, as before), `XAI_API_KEY` (xAI's chat completions at https://api.x.ai/v1, `grok-4.7`, its Fast stand-in `grok-4.20-0309-non-reasoning`) or `OPENAI_API_KEY` (OpenAI's, `gpt-5.4` and `gpt-5.4-mini`), one chat-completions adapter for both — and `GRAVIEW_AI_PROVIDER` (anthropic, xai, openai) decides when several are; `GRAVIEW_AI_MODEL` and `GRAVIEW_AI_FAST_MODEL` still choose the models. The page's contract is unchanged — a prompt in, its text out — and the server translates; a refused key is said by its variable ("The model refused the key in XAI_API_KEY."), and the key is in no answer and no error. The probe says `provider` and `turns`: a seat's tool-reading turn goes to Anthropic only, and on xAI or OpenAI the seat answers by completion (`aiThroughDevServer` lends no `turn` there). `AI_HOW_TO` names all three keys; `aiDevProvider(env)` says which is chosen. `TYPESAFE_API_KEY=… XAI_API_KEY=… pnpm dev` and right-click a task. A harness's dev servers are started with neither key, as with the model's. `the-menu-and-a-quick-ask-are-judged-by-jev-live.test.ts` asks Jev itself where a key is set. The card now stands beside a row of the pages' lists and a listed card as well as a scene card, and in WebKit it is as tall as what it holds — WebKit's own popover height stretched it to the window's, over the row it was beside. With AI off or no model given the field only filters, and words that fit nothing say "Nothing here matches “…”." The AI section's own rows stand down while words are typed, the Ask row standing for them. `typing-in-the-menu-filters-its-rows-and-asks-about-what-it-is-on.test.tsx` holds it, `a-menu-of-acts-is-one-tab-stop.test.tsx` now holds that a letter narrows the acts, and `verify-menu` drives both faces with a fake host AI — a Fast slot that answers each choice at once, a Thinking slot that answers after a moment and proposes finishing the task (typingInTheMenuFiltersItsItems, enterRunsTheItemTyped, freeWordsAskAboutWhatIsUnderTheCursor, theAnswerShowsBesideItsTargetAndCanBeTakenBack, theFastSlotsPickIsWhatReachesTheThinkingSlot, withoutAiTheFieldOnlyFilters). The journeys walk a menu by typing, as a person would: the fewest letters that light the row, Enter in the field running it. The graview-desk skill says it.

And no row moves under the pointer as suggestions arrive (HQ, from Homeflow: "right-click options are jumpy, i end up often clicking a different option when i try to hit Send Feedback because it jumps down as AI items are populated"). Asked as the menu opens, the AI's suggestions hold their room from the first frame — the most that can come, each a row's own height, "Thinking…" in the first — and the room never shrinks while the menu is open: what arrives fills it, a shorter answer leaves it empty, and a suggestion is one line however long its words. A Fast-slot ranking that arrives while the pointer rests on a row is held until it leaves the rows. `verify-menu` mounts an app as Cloud does, its host adding "Send feedback" below the AI's rows, and moves the pointer straight to it while the Fast slot is still suggesting: its box is the same before and after the suggestions land, and the press sends feedback (aRowUnderThePointerNeverMovesAsSuggestionsArrive, Chromium and WebKit; on the code before this change the row moved 41 pixels and the press missed it).

"Inspect", which 0.1.63 put at the foot of the menu on the pages, is removed — the row, the sheet it opened and its harness claim (inspectSaysWhatIsUnderTheCursor): the menu is for what can be done; Copy link stays. And a right-click with Option (Alt) held shows the browser's own menu on every face — the scene's cards, lines and ground, the pages, an embed — where Shift and a text field already did; a Ctrl+click, which a Mac's one-button trackpad sends as a right-click with Ctrl held, opens ours as any right-click does, and a finger resting on a phone still opens ours. `verify-menu` holds both on the scene and the pages (aRightClickWithOptionShowsTheBrowsersMenu, aCtrlClickStillOpensOurMenu) and that the menu has no Inspect row (theMenuHasNoInspectRow).

A hosted page's scene face carries 813 bytes more before it draws (1_104_937, under 1080 KB): a popover's first stop passing over the menu's field and Option heard on the scene's cards, lines and ground. The rest is fetched later: the field and the Ask row are the menu's, fetched with it, and the thinking mark and its pulse are the quick ask's card's, and the Fast slot's pick of a quick ask's context is the inline AI's, fetched when first asked. A popover's first stop passing over the field costs a visitor's form alone 43 / 25 bytes, and every face carries 5_241 / 1_186 more, each under a raise of its own (`scripts/budget-raises/type-in-the-right-click-menu-*.json`); the pages face alone is 936 / 542 smaller, Inspect and its sheet gone.

Compatibility: ops and primitives — unchanged. Stored formats — an `AiInteraction` a host keeps (`ai.interactions`) may carry `context`, an optional key a build before this one ignores. The wire — the dev server's model door takes `tier: "fast"`, its probe says `fast`, `provider` and `turns`, and it answers prompts from xAI and OpenAI as from Anthropic; a door before this one asks the Thinking model of a fast request and names no Fast slot, so a page lends none. A quick ask with a Fast slot now makes one Fast call before its Thinking one, and the Thinking slot is told fewer candidates and records. The declaration and check finding codes — unchanged. Derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged. The look: the pages' menu no longer draws `[data-menu-item="inspect"]` or `[data-testid="inspect-sheet"]`, and a right-click with Option held reaches the page's own `contextmenu` listeners undefaulted; the context menu draws `input[data-testid="menu-type"]` above its rows, the row Enter runs carries `data-menu-lit`, the Ask row is `[data-menu-item="ask-typed"]`, the quick ask's thinking mark is `[data-testid="ai-thinking-mark"]` (its target carries `data-graview-ai-thinking` while it shows), and a popover's first stop passes over an element marked `data-graview-not-first`. What Graview Cloud does: where it gives a seat Jev in the Fast slot, a quick ask's thinking model is told only what Jev picked, and its meter sees one Fast call more per ask and one per pause in typing in a menu; it may show `context` from a stored entry; a host whose own `menuSections` rows have labels gets them filtered with the rest, and a section drawn with `render` alone is hidden while words are typed.
