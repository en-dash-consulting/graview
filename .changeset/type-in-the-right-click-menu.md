Type in the right-click menu, Roadmap f-menu-type-anything (from request:when-i-right-click-on-anything-i-want-to-be-able-to-type-in-something-about-it). "When I right-click on anything, I want to be able to type in something about it": the menu offered its rows and nothing to type, so a long menu was read row by row and a question about the thing under the cursor meant closing it and opening the quick ask. The menu now has a field at its top, on both faces — it is the same pane on the scene and on the pages. It opens with the keyboard on its first row as before, so the arrows still walk the rows and a finger on a phone raises no keyboard until the field is tapped; a letter typed anywhere in the menu goes into the field, the keyboard with it, where the acts' type-ahead used to jump to the next act that began with it. What is typed narrows every row — the acts, the withheld ones, Open, Open in a new tab, Copy link, Show in the scene, Find, Notify me, Inspect — to those whose words begin with it, by a plain word-start match (each word typed begins one of the row's words, in any order, without case or accents: `menuMatches` in `packages/primitives/src/menu-words.ts`), not Find's matcher: the menu's rows are a dozen sentences already drawn, not records to rank. Where the host gave a Fast slot (Jev, or its `decide`), the local match still answers on the first key, and once the typing rests for 250 ms the Fast slot is asked one Choice — which of the menu's rows the words mean, "none of these" among them — whose answer refines the list: a row it finds likely is shown though none of its words begins with what was typed ("complete" finds "Finish it"), and its best is lit; never in the way of a key, nothing asked while the keys come, and an answer for words since changed is dropped (`useFastRank`, `packages/primitives/src/menu-rank.ts`). The first that fits is lit, never one that cannot be taken back; Enter runs it, the arrow down walks into the rows and the arrow up from the first comes back, and Escape takes the words back before it puts the menu away. Where the host lent the app a model and the quick ask is drawn, the last row is always the person's own words, "Tell Graview “assign all these to nick”", lit when nothing else fits: Enter puts the menu away and opens the quick ask's card beside what the menu was opened on, answering the words at once — the same path "Tell Graview…" takes — and while it answers a small mark, "Thinking about Pay the deposit…", sits on the thing itself (the scene's card, the page's row or card, the record's heading), a polite status for a screen reader, never a modal; the change it proposes waits on the card as a ghost, accepted as one batch with its one undo. Free words take two steps: where a Fast slot is given, `answerHere` first asks it, in Jev's own typed questions, which of the catalog's candidates (`catalogOf`) could answer or act on the words — a Choice, "none of these" among them, kept best first while likely enough, at most eight — and, a Noul each, whether the words are about each record around the target (`aiContextOf`'s selection and neighbors), kept where more likely than not, at most six — and only those reach the Thinking slot (or the host's Answer slot), which may pick only among them; with no Fast slot the default the target is scoped to is sent.
