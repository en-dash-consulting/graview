Type in the right-click menu, Roadmap f-menu-type-anything (from request:when-i-right-click-on-anything-i-want-to-be-able-to-type-in-something-about-it). "When I right-click on anything, I want to be able to type in something about it": the menu offered its rows and nothing to type, so a long menu was read row by row and a question about the thing under the cursor meant closing it and opening the quick ask. The menu now has a field at its top, on both faces — it is the same pane on the scene and on the pages. It opens with the keyboard on its first row as before, so the arrows still walk the rows and a finger on a phone raises no keyboard until the field is tapped; a letter typed anywhere in the menu goes into the field, the keyboard with it, where the acts' type-ahead used to jump to the next act that began with it. What is typed narrows every row — the acts, the withheld ones, Open, Open in a new tab, Copy link, Show in the scene, Find, Notify me, Inspect — to those whose words begin with it, by a plain word-start match (each word typed begins one of the row's words, in any order, without case or accents: `menuMatches` in `packages/primitives/src/menu-words.ts`), not Find's matcher: the menu's rows are a dozen sentences already drawn, not records to rank. Where the host gave a Fast slot (Jev, or its `decide`), the local match still answers on the first key, and once the typing rests for 250 ms the Fast slot is asked one Choice — which of the menu's rows the words mean, "none of these" among them — whose answer refines the list: a row it finds likely is shown though none of its words begins with what was typed ("complete" finds "Finish it"), and its best is lit; never in the way of a key, nothing asked while the keys come, and an answer for words since changed is dropped (`useFastRank`, `packages/primitives/src/menu-rank.ts`). The first that fits is lit, never one that cannot be taken back; Enter runs it, the arrow down walks into the rows and the arrow up from the first comes back, and Escape takes the words back before it puts the menu away. Where the host lent the app a model and the quick ask is drawn, the last row is always the person's own words, "Tell Graview “assign all these to nick”", lit when nothing else fits: Enter puts the menu away and opens the quick ask's card beside what the menu was opened on, answering the words at once — the same path "Tell Graview…" takes — and while it answers a small mark, "Thinking about Pay the deposit…", sits on the thing itself (the scene's card, the page's row or card, the record's heading), a polite status for a screen reader, never a modal; the change it proposes waits on the card as a ghost, accepted as one batch with its one undo. Free words take two steps: where a Fast slot is given, `answerHere` first asks it, in Jev's own typed questions, which of the catalog's candidates (`catalogOf`) could answer or act on the words — a Choice, "none of these" among them, kept best first while likely enough, at most eight — and, a Noul each, whether the words are about each record around the target (`aiContextOf`'s selection and neighbors), kept where more likely than not, at most six — and only those reach the Thinking slot (or the host's Answer slot), which may pick only among them; with no Fast slot the default the target is scoped to is sent. Either way the interaction's entry says what was sent and who chose it (`AiInteraction.context`, `AiContextSent`: `by` "fast" or "default", the Fast slot's `model`, the candidate ids and record ids), and Activity says it under the entry: "Told the model of 1 thing it could do and 1 record, as Jev picked them". `a-quick-ask-sends-the-thinking-slot-what-the-fast-slot-picked.test.ts` holds it.

To try it with real models, the dev server lends the page both slots. Where it holds a key for Jev (`TYPESAFE_API_KEY`, or `JEV_API_KEY`), the Fast slot is Jev itself: `aiThroughDevServer` finds `decisionBridge`'s door open and makes `jevDecide` through it the Fast slot (`jev-latest`, the key held by the server), and the todo example's dev server now draws that door. Without one, `aiDevProxy` names a small quick model in its probe (`fast`, `claude-haiku-5-5` unless `GRAVIEW_AI_FAST_MODEL` names another) and asks it, at low effort and a short answer, each request a page posts with `tier: "fast"` (`AiBridgeAsk.tier`); `aiThroughDevServer` makes that the page's Fast slot, answered as a decision provider would (`completionDecide`) — a stand-in for Jev, in a dev server only, since a build carries no door. And the dev server's model door (`aiDevProxy`) takes xAI and OpenAI beside Anthropic: the provider is the one whose key is set — `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` (the Messages API, as before), `XAI_API_KEY` (xAI's chat completions at https://api.x.ai/v1, `grok-4.7`, its Fast stand-in `grok-4.20-0309-non-reasoning`) or `OPENAI_API_KEY` (OpenAI's, `gpt-5.4` and `gpt-5.4-mini`), one chat-completions adapter for both — and `GRAVIEW_AI_PROVIDER` (anthropic, xai, openai) decides when several are; `GRAVIEW_AI_MODEL` and `GRAVIEW_AI_FAST_MODEL` still choose the models. The page's contract is unchanged — a prompt in, its text out — and the server translates; a refused key is said by its variable ("The model refused the key in XAI_API_KEY."), and the key is in no answer and no error. The probe says `provider` and `turns`: a seat's tool-reading turn goes to Anthropic only, and on xAI or OpenAI the seat answers by completion (`aiThroughDevServer` lends no `turn` there). `AI_HOW_TO` names all three keys; `aiDevProvider(env)` says which is chosen. `TYPESAFE_API_KEY=… XAI_API_KEY=… pnpm dev` and right-click a task. A harness's dev servers are started with neither key, as with the model's. `the-menu-and-a-quick-ask-are-judged-by-jev-live.test.ts` asks Jev itself where a key is set. The card now stands beside a row of the pages' lists and a listed card as well as a scene card, and in WebKit it is as tall as what it holds — WebKit's own popover height stretched it to the window's, over the row it was beside. With AI off or no model given the field only filters, and words that fit nothing say "Nothing here matches “…”." The AI section's own rows stand down while words are typed, the row of their own words standing for them. `typing-in-the-menu-filters-its-rows-and-asks-about-what-it-is-on.test.tsx` holds it, `a-menu-of-acts-is-one-tab-stop.test.tsx` now holds that a letter narrows the acts, and `verify-menu` drives both faces with a fake host AI — a Fast slot that answers each choice at once, a Thinking slot that answers after a moment and proposes finishing the task (typingInTheMenuFiltersItsItems, enterRunsTheItemTyped, freeWordsAskAboutWhatIsUnderTheCursor, theAnswerShowsBesideItsTargetAndCanBeTakenBack, theFastSlotsPickIsWhatReachesTheThinkingSlot, withoutAiTheFieldOnlyFilters). The journeys walk a menu by typing, as a person would: the fewest letters that light the row, Enter in the field running it. The graview-desk skill says it.

And no row moves under the pointer as suggestions arrive (HQ, from Homeflow: "right-click options are jumpy, i end up often clicking a different option when i try to hit Send Feedback because it jumps down as AI items are populated"). Asked as the menu opens, the AI's suggestions hold their room from the first frame — the most that can come, each a row's own height, "Thinking…" in the first — and the room never shrinks while the menu is open: what arrives fills it, a shorter answer leaves it empty, and a suggestion is one line however long its words. A Fast-slot ranking that arrives while the pointer rests on a row is held until it leaves the rows. `verify-menu` mounts an app as Cloud does, its host adding "Send feedback" below the AI's rows, and moves the pointer straight to it while the Fast slot is still suggesting: its box is the same before and after the suggestions land, and the press sends feedback (aRowUnderThePointerNeverMovesAsSuggestionsArrive, Chromium and WebKit; on the code before this change the row moved 41 pixels and the press missed it).

Graview is here while it thinks. From the words asked until the quick ask's card is put away — its close, Escape, a press away — the app's AI stands among who is here, as a bot editing does: "Graview, for Ada", at what it was asked about (its `robot.at` and `over`, so the scene marks the thing), on the bar of this page and, over the presence channel, of the others' (`PresenceChannel.alsoHere`, a word of its own beside the page's, which `createBroadcastPresence` carries; said once with its time up when it goes, and lapsing by the presence's own time if the page dies). The provider's `aiHere(presence | null)` says it (`PresenceState.aiHere`); a channel without `alsoHere` — the served store's — shows it on the asking page alone. `verify-menu` holds it in two tabs (graviewIsHereWhileItThinksAndGoesWhenTheCardCloses).

An ask about a list acts on every record in it as one change: the quick ask hands the model the records the list or place shows (`QuickAsk.members`) and the acts they take, and "mark these done" comes back as one act over each — previewed on the card, accepted as one batch, taken back with one undo (anAskAboutAListActsOnEveryRecordInItAsOneChange). And a missing capability is offered as one plan that finishes the ask. Nick: "I'd say 'assign these to nick' and it'd say 'tasks have no assignment, but I can add that property, and add a People kind and add Nick to it and assign him to them all… should I?'" Where the model finds no act can do it because the app lacks a field or a link, it says so plainly and names the need (`InlineResult.missing.need`: a link from each record, by what it is called and what it points to, or a field), and code writes the least change that gives it (`planEdits`, on `@graview/core/catalog`: the kind a link points to where the app has none, with its own "add" act, the link or the field, and the one act that sets it — no rule, no new place, nothing the ask did not need), then the records that finish the ask (the person the words name, found among the app's people or made; each record the list shows given to them). Every step is said from those edits and records, numbered, in sentence case, each a thing that will happen: "1. Add a People list. 2. Give each task an “Assigned to” field that points to a person. 3. Add Nick to People. 4. Assign Nick to the 2 tasks in The board." — and "Tasks have no one to assign them to. I can do it in 4 steps. Should I?", with Yes and No ("yes" or "no" in the reply too; other words revise it). Where the host lends its composer, the changed app is judged by the checker and a scratch store rehearses every record before the plan is offered; one that would fail is refused. Yes hands the host the proposal (`ReshapeProposal.edits`, `records` with a made record named by its `mintedHint`, `via` the AI's), which lands it as one change and answers its one way back (`{ applied: true, undo, note? }`). A seat that may not change the app, a host that lends no composer, and an app declared in code each get the whole plan and one line on who makes its first steps: "An owner would add steps 1–2; then I can do steps 3–4." The composer and the checker stay the host's, off the page: a host lends them as `composeChange` (on the provider, the embed and the pages' context), and `composeAppChange` on `@graview/core/compose` is one — a plan's edits (or a reshape's traits) applied, what it adds granted and shown as the app's widest grant is, judged by the checker. `verify-menu` asks it of tasks with nobody to give them to, mounted with a host that applies a change to the app: the question and its four steps, nothing changed before Yes, the kind, the link, Nick and both links as one change after it, `via: ai:fake`, and one undo taking it all back (aMissingCapabilityIsOfferedAsOnePlanThatFinishesTheAsk), the steps in plain words (aPlanSaysItsStepsInPlainWords) and only the edits the ask needed, no rule added (aPlanTakesOnlyWhatTheAskNeeds).

A host that lands a plan is checked as Graview checks its own: `checkPlanHost(host, { document, kind, members })` on `@graview/core/conformance` runs the assign-to-a-person plan against a host's `composeChange`, `onProposeChange` and the store it serves, and names what failed — composed, landed as one change said `via` the AI, every record linked, one undo taking it all back (`a-host-that-lands-a-plan-is-checked-as-graview-checks-its-own.test.ts`). `docs/stability.md` says the contract under the wire.
