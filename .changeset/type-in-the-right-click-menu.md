Type in the right-click menu, Roadmap f-menu-type-anything (from request:when-i-right-click-on-anything-i-want-to-be-able-to-type-in-something-about-it). "When I right-click on anything, I want to be able to type in something about it": the menu offered its rows and nothing to type, so a long menu was read row by row and a question about the thing under the cursor meant closing it and opening the quick ask. The menu now has a field at its top, on both faces — it is the same pane on the scene and on the pages. It opens with the keyboard on its first row as before, so the arrows still walk the rows and a finger on a phone raises no keyboard until the field is tapped; a letter typed anywhere in the menu goes into the field, the keyboard with it, where the acts' type-ahead used to jump to the next act that began with it. What is typed narrows every row — the acts, the withheld ones, Open, Open in a new tab, Copy link, Show in the scene, Find, Notify me, Inspect — to those whose words begin with it, by a plain word-start match (each word typed begins one of the row's words, in any order, without case or accents: `menuMatches` in `packages/primitives/src/menu-words.ts`), not Find's matcher: the menu's rows are a dozen sentences already drawn, not records to rank. Where the host gave a Fast slot (Jev, or its `decide`), the local match still answers on the first key, and once the typing rests for 250 ms the Fast slot is asked one Choice — which of the menu's rows the words mean — whose answer refines the list: a row it finds likely is shown though none of its words begins with what was typed ("complete" finds "Finish it"), and its best is lit; never in the way of a key, nothing asked while the keys come, and an answer for words since changed is dropped (`useFastRank`, `packages/primitives/src/menu-rank.ts`). The first that fits is lit, never one that cannot be taken back; Enter runs it, the arrow down walks into the rows and the arrow up from the first comes back, and Escape takes the words back before it puts the menu away. Where the host lent the app a model and the quick ask is drawn, the last row is always "Ask about Pay the deposit: <words>", lit when nothing else fits: Enter puts the menu away and opens the quick ask's card beside what the menu was opened on, answering the words at once — the same path "Ask about this…" takes — and while it answers a small mark, "Thinking about Pay the deposit…", sits on the thing itself (the scene's card, the page's row or card, the record's heading), a polite status for a screen reader, never a modal; the change it proposes waits on the card as a ghost, accepted as one batch with its one undo. Free words take two steps: where a Fast slot is given, `answerHere` first asks it which of the catalog's candidates (`catalogOf`) and which records around the target (`aiContextOf`'s selection and neighbors) matter to the words — each kept best first while it is likely enough, at most eight and six — and only those reach the Thinking slot (or the host's Answer slot), which may pick only among them; with no Fast slot the default the target is scoped to is sent. Either way the interaction's entry says what was sent and who chose it (`AiInteraction.context`, `AiContextSent`: `by` "fast" or "default", the Fast slot's `model`, the candidate ids and record ids), and Activity says it under the entry: "Told the model of 1 thing it could do and 1 record, as jev-latest picked them". `a-quick-ask-sends-the-thinking-slot-what-the-fast-slot-picked.test.ts` holds it.

To try it with a real model, the dev server lends the page a Fast slot too: `aiDevProxy` names a small quick model in its probe (`fast`, `claude-haiku-5-5` unless `GRAVIEW_AI_FAST_MODEL` names another) and asks it, at low effort and a short answer, each request a page posts with `tier: "fast"` (`AiBridgeAsk.tier`); `aiThroughDevServer` makes that the page's Fast slot, answered as a decision provider would (`completionDecide`) — a stand-in for Jev, in a dev server only, since a build carries no door. `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY=… pnpm dev` and right-click a task. The card now stands beside a row of the pages' lists and a listed card as well as a scene card, and in WebKit it is as tall as what it holds — WebKit's own popover height stretched it to the window's, over the row it was beside. With AI off or no model given the field only filters, and words that fit nothing say "Nothing here matches “…”." The AI section's own rows stand down while words are typed, the Ask row standing for them. `typing-in-the-menu-filters-its-rows-and-asks-about-what-it-is-on.test.tsx` holds it, `a-menu-of-acts-is-one-tab-stop.test.tsx` now holds that a letter narrows the acts, and `verify-menu` drives both faces with a fake host AI — a Fast slot that answers each choice at once, a Thinking slot that answers after a moment and proposes finishing the task (typingInTheMenuFiltersItsItems, enterRunsTheItemTyped, freeWordsAskAboutWhatIsUnderTheCursor, theAnswerShowsBesideItsTargetAndCanBeTakenBack, theFastSlotsPickIsWhatReachesTheThinkingSlot, withoutAiTheFieldOnlyFilters). The journeys walk a menu by typing, as a person would: the fewest letters that light the row, Enter in the field running it. The graview-desk skill says it.

Nothing a hosted page loads up front changed: the field and the Ask row are the menu's, fetched with it, and the thinking mark and its pulse are the quick ask's card's, and the Fast slot's pick of a quick ask's context is the inline AI's, fetched when first asked. A popover's first stop passing over the field costs the pages face alone 43 / 17 bytes and a visitor's form alone 43 / 24, and every face carries 9_680 / 3_132 more, each under a raise of its own (`scripts/budget-raises/type-in-the-right-click-menu-*.json`).

Compatibility: ops and primitives — unchanged. Stored formats — an `AiInteraction` a host keeps (`ai.interactions`) may carry `context`, an optional key a build before this one ignores. The wire — the dev server's model door takes `tier: "fast"` and its probe says `fast`; a door before this one asks the Thinking model of a fast request and names no Fast slot, so a page lends none. A quick ask with a Fast slot now makes one Fast call before its Thinking one, and the Thinking slot is told fewer candidates and records. The declaration and check finding codes — unchanged. Derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged. The look: the context menu draws `input[data-testid="menu-type"]` above its rows, the row Enter runs carries `data-menu-lit`, the Ask row is `[data-menu-item="ask-typed"]`, the quick ask's thinking mark is `[data-testid="ai-thinking-mark"]` (its target carries `data-graview-ai-thinking` while it shows), and a popover's first stop passes over an element marked `data-graview-not-first`. What Graview Cloud does: where it gives a seat Jev in the Fast slot, a quick ask's thinking model is told only what Jev picked, and its meter sees one Fast call more per ask and one per pause in typing in a menu; it may show `context` from a stored entry; a host whose own `menuSections` rows have labels gets them filtered with the rest, and a section drawn with `render` alone is hidden while words are typed.
