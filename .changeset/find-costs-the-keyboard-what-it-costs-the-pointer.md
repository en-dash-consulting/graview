Find costs the keyboard about what it costs the pointer. The gauntlet's longest title took 89 presses from the keyboard, every talk before it a row, and narrowing a list by a number took 62 against the pointer's 6, most of them Tabs. PageDown and PageUp walk the strip a page of records at a time; an answer of more than twelve records says "another word of the name narrows them"; Enter on a kind goes into the kind whole, where it used to narrow it by its own name ("talks" kept ten talks of 1,960); and where Enter went the keys go, onto the place's arranging line, rather than being let go on nothing. Home and End stay the words' own: the box is a field being written in.

Compatibility: no surface changes.
