A count of one relation is read whatever its size, and a computed field is the same value wherever it is read, Roadmap f-count-past-graph-budget (request:bug-a-computed-count-shows-dashes-in-a-row-past-the-graph-budget-while-the-list-shows-it). TECH N9NE's genres say `{songCount} {songCount|plural: 'song'}` on each row and card, where `songCount` is `count(in('inGenre'))`: every genre drew its count but Hip hop, about 690 songs, whose row drew "— —" and whose `describe_place` said "this rule looks at too much of the graph to judge in one go" at `views.genre.row.1`, while the same list's line said "Songs 692". The two paths differed by budget: a row or a card is drawn many times and is held to `CARD_BUDGET` (500 steps), the list's line works a record's computed values out under the evaluator's own (10,000), and the walk spent a step a song. Now `count(in('<relation>'))`, `count(out('<relation>'))` and `exists(...)` of the same are read from the edge index for one step whatever their number, and a computed field read from a template, a block or a rule is worked out under a record's budget (at least `DEFAULT_BUDGET`) and costs its reader one step, so a row shows what the list's line and the record's page show. A walk a template writes itself is still held to the card's budget: `{count(in('inGenre') where title != '')}` on a row over 700 songs still draws "—", and `count(all('song'))` past a budget still says it looks at too much. `a-count-of-one-relation-is-read-whatever-its-size.test.ts` holds it at 700 and 5,000 linked records.

The `check:` prefix on a finding code is systematic, and stays (request:lens-cannot-draw-code-prefix): `compileDocument` runs the checker over the app a document compiles to and says each of its findings as `check:<code>`, while what the document's own judgments find, `graview check`, `checkApp` and an edit's refusal say the bare code. So `check:lens-cannot-draw` from a compile is the checker's `lens-cannot-draw`, the code an `add-lens` refusal and the 0.1.63 notes name; a program that matches a code wherever it was said strips `check:` first. `docs/stability.md` says so where codes are promised, and `a-checkers-code-in-a-compile-carries-check-before-it.test.ts` holds it. The graview-view-specs skill says a computed field reads the same on a row as on the page.
