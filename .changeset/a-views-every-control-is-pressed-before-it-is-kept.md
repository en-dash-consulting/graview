A view's every control is pressed before it is kept, and a press that is refused says why beside it, Roadmap f-views-work-end-to-end. Nick, Oct 30: "too often I find that the views a chatbot creates actually don't have a clickable section or the Save button doesn't work… this would never be the case. Ever." What a view drew was judged — its markup, its links' names, its accessibility — and what pressing it did was found out by the person. Seen in Staffing Placement: a fieldset bound to a placement with `data-act="set-economics"` and four number fields the host filled from the record; emptying the fixed fee sent `fixedFee: ""`, the act's own schema refused it, and nothing on the page changed.

`runWorkerViewHeadless(…, { presses: checkPresses })`, the checker from `@graview/guest/headless/presses` — an entry of its own, so a host's bundle that never presses carries none of it — presses every control a view drew, as the live host would take the press, nothing applied: each `data-act` and field that saves, with its fields as drawn, then filled in as a person would fill them, then with each optional field the host filled emptied; each link, card, count or chip bound to a record or a place; each `href="#id"` jump and `data-file` link; and each button or control the view's own code hears, in a run of its own. One that does nothing, throws, asks for an act the host refuses from code, goes nowhere, or is refused fails with a plain finding, among the description's problems and in `presses`: "“Save economics” is refused: “fixedFee” expects a number." `graview view check` presses every control by default (`--no-presses` leaves them). A run can press an element by the view's own id for it (`press.target`). `@graview/guest/headless/presses` is a new entry.

A press the act's schema refuses now says what was refused — "Not applied: “fixedFee” expects a number." — where it said "The act could not be applied."; an act's own error still says only that. A refused press or save is said beside its control by the host, in a note of its own outside what the view drew, without the view listening for `acted`; a view that hears `acted` on the control takes it over (the runtime tells the host where it hears `acted`, with where it hears a click). A field the act cannot take blank — a number emptied, a date never picked — is left out of the call, never sent as `""`. A value the view wrote into a field is sent where it carries nothing the view read: `0`, `false`, the argument's declared default, or what the bound record holds there now; any other words of the view's are refused as before. And a titled view that says `heading: "own"` has nothing drawn above it on its place's page: no title to see, no count, and now no "All playlists as a list" link. The graview-view-forms, graview-worker-view and graview-pages skills say so. The press check runs off the page and out of what a headless run's entry carries; the note beside a refused press and the jumps, the section in sight and the file links of f-views-scroll-and-anchors are chunks of their own, fetched when a worker view first needs one, so a hosted page's first load is unchanged. Raised, each in a file of its own under `scripts/budget-raises/`: every face by 9_905 / 4_489 bytes and the open kit's worker view by 10_154 / 4_551 (both lazy), the guest host's three first loads by about 1_000 / 470 each (the session saying a refused argument in words, and where a view hears `acted`), a worker view's runtime by 89 / 67, and Cloud's worker by 5_604 bytes (what a headless run must judge as the page does; the press check itself stays out of it). Tests: `the-view-check-presses-every-control`, `a-press-leaves-out-a-blank-number-and-sends-a-view-written-zero`, `a-view-that-hears-acted-says-its-own-refusals`, `a-view-that-draws-its-own-heading-is-not-titled-twice`, and the guest sandbox's presses transport in Chromium and WebKit.

Compatibility: ops and primitives — unchanged. Stored formats — unchanged. The wire — a view's `clicks` message carries `acted`, the selectors it hears `acted` on, and a headless run's input may name its press by `target`; a press the act's schema refuses answers `failed` with the arguments it refused in words. The declaration and check finding codes — unchanged. Derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged.

What Graview Cloud does: its view gate (`propose_view`, `apply_view`, the Studio's keep) imports `checkPresses` from `@graview/guest/headless/presses` where it gates a view and runs `runWorkerViewHeadless` with `presses: checkPresses` and refuses to keep a view whose `presses.ok` is false, answering with each `presses.problems[].says` so the chat that wrote it can mend that control; a page it hosts says a refused press beside its control with no change of its own; and a view of many that says `heading: "own"` is drawn with nothing above it.
