A document's `connections` take a third system, `"todoist"`: a Todoist project, or one section or label of it, kept in step with a kind both ways through the account's personal API token (`TODOIST_TOKEN`, or the host's `token`; in no document and no op). A task's content, description, due date, deadline, priority, labels, section, assignee, parent and completion each land in a field, or a relation for the parent; a task completed in Todoist sets its field and never removes a record, and a record set aside or merged away never deletes its task. The first run sends nothing: records here before it go when they are changed, or all of them with `"sendExisting": true`. Pulls go by Todoist's sync token, a push sends only the parts that changed, our own writes are not taken back as news, conflicts are held, and Todoist's rate limit is waited out. A due date that repeats is kept as a repeat where Todoist's words for it can be said back ("every 2 weeks", "every mon, wed"); any other stays in Todoist, and a change to it here is held unsent and said. `graview check` refuses a part in a field that cannot hold it, `document.schema.json` carries the shape, and a record's page opens its task. `scriptedTodoist()` is a Todoist in memory; `scripts/verify-todoist.mjs` is the same claims for a real account. The `graview-todoist` skill says how. (hq:feature:f-todoist-connection)

Compatibility: the look — a record kept in step with Todoist says so on its page as one kept with Notion does. The JavaScript API — additive: `todoist`, `todoistMapping`, `scriptedTodoist`, `readRepeat`, `sayRepeat` and their types on `@graview/core/sync`, `projectId` on `ConnectionRun`, `project` on `RunConnectionsOptions`, an optional `changed` on `RemoteWrite`, an optional `fields` and `held` on `RemoteAck`, an optional `aside` on `ResourceMapping` and `holdExisting` on `SyncOptions` (without them Notion and Google Calendar are kept as they were); `KeptInStepWith.system` and `ConnectionSpec.system` may be `"todoist"`, which a host that switches on them meets. Ops and primitives — unchanged. Stored formats — unchanged; an engine's outbox entries may carry `changed`, and its state `existing`, both optional. The wire — unchanged. The declaration and check finding codes — a connection of `system: "todoist"`, and one new warning, `connection-completed`. Derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged.
