An app's picture comes at a tab's size (FR-130). Graview Cloud's tab showed every app as the same cube in its accent, so twelve open apps were twelve cubes. `sceneThumbnail(document, { size: "icon" })` from `@graview/core/scene` draws the Scene from altitude at 32 by 32: each district on its whole block from the corner the Scene gives it, in its hue, with one block standing on it, taller for more when counts are given; no streets, no edges, no strokes. The plot is drawn solid in its kerb's colour, because the Scene's translucent wash cannot be seen at 16 pixels. Coordinates are the icon's own pixels to a tenth, so Cloud's twelve templates draw in 785 to 1,329 bytes. The icon is a standalone SVG with its namespace, named by the app's name alone, with nothing outside itself, and `background: false` leaves it transparent. A test measures it from the SVG on those templates in both schemes: every district's plot is at least 2.6 pixels tall and its block 3 pixels wide at 16 pixels, every plot stands apart from the ground, every pair of templates differs in its hues or in where its districts stand, and every other size of picture is the same bytes as before.
Compatibility: additive. `SceneThumbnailOptions` gains `size`; without it every picture is byte for byte what it was. Ops, stored formats, the document format, wire messages, check codes and tool schemas are unchanged. `capabilities().shipped` gains FR-130.
