A view is hidden, shown, deleted and restored from the place menu. Views pile up as chats draw them, and the person annoyed by the clutter is standing at the places drop-down: now the place you are on in the bar, and each place in its drop-down, open the Pages menu — a right-click, Shift+F10 or the menu key, a finger resting on it — with Hide and Delete for a view. Hide takes it off the places, the scene's places and the home's gallery for everyone, still at its address; the drop-down's foot lists it under Hidden with Show. Delete takes it out of every face and lists it under Recently deleted with Restore, and the day it is gone for good, thirty days on. A script view a chat drew is the host's: `mount`'s new `keptViews` (`KeptViews` from `@graview/core`: `current()`, `onChange`, `hide`, `show`, `remove`, `restore`, and `lens`) is where the page asks, and `current().may` says what this viewer may — the views a chat drew for an owner or an editor, the declaration's lenses for the owner. A declared lens is hidden with `set-lens-hidden` and deleted with `remove-lens`, handed to `lens` as a change to the declaration the host writes with its rollback. `registerWorkerView` registers a view under its manifest's `name` and `registerDeclaredLenses` marks a lens `declared`, which the menu reads; a view the app's own code registers is offered neither. A served store answers `/graview/views` (`createStoreHandler`'s `views` and `mayTidyViews`, `remoteViews` from `@graview/ship/browser` for the page), `graview serve` keeps what was tidied in `views.json` beside the store, `graview preview` writes a declared lens's change into the document it holds, and `devViews()` from `@graview/ship/dev` is the dev server's door. `memoryViews` is on `@graview/core`, and `tidyViews` — the registry as the host's tidying leaves it — on `@graview/react`. A page carries only what hears the gesture and says the drop-down's foot has something to list: which place, whether a finger rested, the menu's rows, the toast, the foot and the tidying are fetched on first use. A hosted page carries 1.4 KB more up front for it (the compiled page too); their budgets are raised by that, and the pages face, the embed, every face and the guest host by what they measured.

Compatibility: ops, stored records, the declaration and derived tools are unchanged. The wire: a new route, `/graview/views` (GET what is hidden and recently deleted and what the asking seat may do; POST `{ hide | show | remove | restore: name }` or `{ lens: set-lens-hidden | remove-lens }`), and `createStoreHandler` takes `views` and `mayTidyViews`; every other route answers as before. New: `KeptViews`, `KeptViewsState`, `KeptViewsMay`, `RemovedView`, `LensTidyEdit`, `memoryViews`, `NO_KEPT_VIEWS`, `REMOVED_VIEW_DAYS` and `removedLensName` on `@graview/core`; `ViewMeta`, `ViewRegistration` and `Place` take `name` and `declared`; `keptViews` on the embed, the pages embed and `GraviewProvider`, `GraviewContextValue.keptViews`, `useTidiedViews` and `tidyViews` on `@graview/react`; `ViewTidyingSections`, `useViewTidyingSection`, `TidyGroups`, `useTidied`, `useKeptViews`, `useKeptViewsState`, `tidyingOf`, `placeGesture`, `openPlaceMenu`, `placeAim` and `PLACE_MENU_EVENT` on `@graview/primitives`; `answerViews`, `keepsTheApp`, `remoteViews`, `fileViews` and `VIEWS_PATH` on `@graview/ship`, `devViews` on `@graview/ship/dev`. What Graview Cloud does: keep each app's hidden view names and its deleted views (with the definition and its versions, thirty days, then gone), answer `KeptViews` for the viewer with `may` from their share (owner and editor: views; owner: lenses), write a lens's edit as a change with its usual undo, keep registering a deleted view's definition while it is kept so Restore brings it back in place, and pass the object as `keptViews` to `mount`.
