`graview check` judges the look, and says what to take away (Roadmap f-design-critic). Nothing said an app had too much: the Graview Roadmap app grew a "Stage board" and then four "Stage: X" places each showing one of its columns, fifteen lenses beside six lists, and the advice that proposed them only ever adds. The checker now says clutter, focus and repetition as warnings — never errors, so a check that passed still passes — each with the edits that fix it in `propose_change`'s words, removals included: `design-repeated-place` (a lens bound as another is, or blocks places that each show one value of a field a status board or a grouped list already shows split whole: remove them, the whole split stays), `design-too-many-places` (more than twelve places, the bar's row of six and a "More" as long again: keep the least specific off the bar by name with `set-lens-hidden` — a slice of a split first, then a kind's bare list a board already shows whole — never one `pages.primary` stands up), `design-home-unfocused` (a declared home with no headline, given one, or with several, the others said as words), `design-card-repeats-title` (a card or row that says only the name, or a describe that says it again: hand it back to what is derived), `design-raw-value` (an id shown where a person reads: the record said by its name) and `design-no-card-for-main-kind` (the kind the app is most about, with no card and no glance, whose derived card would show a long text, a list or a file: a glance to choose). What the framework already derives well is never flagged: a gallery home, a card from a glance, a derived card whose three facts read at a glance. `designFindings(document)` on `@graview/core/check` asks it alone, for a host to say beside its own advice; it takes the app's jobs too, and says a job no place answers and a place no job asks for once documents carry them (f-app-jobs). Said of the Roadmap app: remove the four "Stage: X" places, and keep five slices off the bar. Said of every app in this repository: nothing.

Compatibility: the declaration and check finding codes — a `Finding` (the document's and `checkApp`'s) gains an optional `edits`, the document edits that fix it, carried in `graview check --json`, in `sayFindings` and in `formatFindings` as one `edit:` line each; `graview check` and `compileDocument` gain warnings coded `design-*`, never errors; `CheckOptions` gains `design: false` to leave them out. Ops and primitives, stored formats, the wire, derived tool names and input schemas, the JavaScript API otherwise, the theme and the embed — unchanged.
