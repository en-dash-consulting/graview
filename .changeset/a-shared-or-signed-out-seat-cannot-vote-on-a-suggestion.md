A shared or signed-out seat cannot vote on a suggestion, Roadmap f-votes-shared-seat (from Cloud's asks after 0.1.62: the shared anonymous seat's votes, advisory votes, the tray's "Sign in to vote" and the away line's tally). A host that serves every signed-out visitor as one principal — Cloud's `anonymous` — got one shared vote on 0.1.62: a stranger's `POST /graview/ops` was kept as that seat's vote, the next stranger flipped it, and the tray showed one stranger another's. Now a principal says it is nobody in particular with `shared: true` (`Principal.shared`), and the store refuses a vote on a suggestion from such a seat, from a `visitor`, or from an agent acting for either, in the words "Sign in to vote on this suggestion." — through `permits`, so Accept and Decline, `voteSuggestion`, a raw call, the wire, a socket and an RPC all meet it, in an app with a policy or without one. A host that wants strangers' sentiment says `votes: { advisory: "visitors" }` on the store (`StoreOptions.votes`, the handler's and `serve`'s `votes`, `openRemote`'s `storeOptions`) and names each visitor behind the shared seat with `visitorKey`: such a vote is kept with `advisory: true` and `voter` (that key) on its op, one standing vote per visitor, counted apart in `tally.advisory: { for, against }`, never toward `autoAcceptAt`, a visitor's own in `tally.mine`, and only that visitor's to undo — a shared seat naming no visitor is still refused. `signedOutSeat`, `advisoryVoterOf` and `SIGN_IN_TO_VOTE` are on `@graview/core/tidying`, `VotesOptions` on the main entry; `votesIn(ops, { advisory: true })` reads the advisory votes alone. The Suggested tray asks the store before a press: a seat whose vote would be refused sees the host's `signIn` as one native button, "Sign in to vote", where Accept and Decline were, and no vote at all where the host gave no way in; whoever may decide reads visitors apart, "1 for, 0 against · 2 visitors agree". `AwaySummary` takes `agreed`, and `whileAway` counts it — the most people whose vote stands for one waiting suggestion — said "3 readers agree with one that waits". `a-shared-or-signed-out-seat-votes-only-where-the-host-keeps-its-vote-advisory.test.ts` (core), `a-strangers-vote-over-the-wire-is-refused-unless-the-host-keeps-it-advisory.test.ts` (ship), `a-visitor-is-offered-sign-in-rather-than-a-vote.test.tsx` (primitives) and `while-away-says-how-many-agree-with-one-that-waits.test.ts` (tools) hold it, and the chrome harness opens the tray on both faces as the host's shared signed-out seat and finds Sign in to vote in place of a vote, pressing it asking the host and keeping nothing (aVisitorIsOfferedSignInRatherThanAVote).
