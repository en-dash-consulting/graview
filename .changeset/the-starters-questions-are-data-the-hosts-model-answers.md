The starter's questions are data, and the host's model answers them (Roadmap f-starter-judgments, the third framework half of Cloud's starter). A person types what they want to keep track of; a box answers with the templates that fit, and on "kinda" asks which of a template's parts to keep and which traits to add. Code owns the catalog, the parts and the composing (`traitsOf` and the starting document composed from parts, on `@graview/core/compose`); what a System One model supplies is the common sense of matching words to structure, so the questions are data of the Fast slot's shape over one state, answered in one request by whatever `decide` the host passes — Jev, as `dream` takes one; a word-overlap stub in the tests — and read back by code with thresholds a host may move without asking again. `@graview/tools/starter`, a new entry on no page: `starterQuestions(said, catalog)` asks a Score per template (`fit:<id>`, over `FIT_LEVELS`: "a different job" to "exactly this"), a Noul per trait of `TRAITS` (`need:<trait>`), a Choice for who will use it (`audience`, over `AUDIENCES`: just me, a household, a team, the public) and one for whether it ends (`horizon`, over `HORIZONS`: a one-off, or kept going), its state what they said and the catalog in words — each template's id, title, pitch, what it is for, what it keeps and what it is made of, the last read off its declaration by `traitsOf`, never off a tag. `kindaQuestions(said, template, reply)` asks keep? per kind, rule and lens of the template (`keep:<part>:<name>`) and add? per trait it lacks (`add:<trait>`), its state adding the parts in words and the line they typed. `readStarter(answers, thresholds)` reads either turn by its keys: `{ turn: "starter", ranked, top, traits, needs, audience, horizon }` — every template ranked best first, so "No" shows the next without asking again; the ids to show (at most three, at level 2 "covers part of what they described" or above, as Cloud's floor is); the traits needed at 0.7 or above — or `{ turn: "kinda", keep, drop, add, parts, needs }` (a part kept at 0.5, a trait added at 0.7). Every raw answer is kept beside the reading, so a host that keeps the answers reads them again under other thresholds without asking the model. `askStarter` and `askKinda` run a turn through the host's `decide` and hand back the reading, the answers and the usage; `stillUnmet(reading, template)` says what a refused template leaves wanting. The test holds the acceptance over Cloud's 22 templates: for each of thirty sentences written for a template, the template it was written for ranks in the top three under the stub. `graview create` is not wired to it: the framework holds no catalog of templates with a pitch to rank (`--template` takes one file), so the box in a terminal waits on a catalog a host hands it.

Compatibility: ops and primitives, stored formats, the wire, the declaration and check finding codes, and derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged. What Graview Cloud does: its own question set in `workers/cloud/src/starter.ts` can be replaced by `starterQuestions`, `kindaQuestions` and `readStarter`, its `decideFast` passed as `decide`; the keys and levels are the framework's now.

The starter says a template's kinds by their nouns and plurals as the declaration spells them, never from a kind's id: `@graview/core/compose` carries `nounWords` and `pluralWords` for a host that writes about a document's kinds, on no page.
