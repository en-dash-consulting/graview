An act asked for in another spelling is that act, and a name that only nearly says one is refused with it. A relation `partOf` gives the acts `connect-threat-part-of` and `disconnect-threat-part-of`, while a relation `hits` keeps its spelling in `connect-threat-hits`; an agent building Threat Watch, on Graview Cloud, called `connect-threat-partOf` and was told it "is not something this app can do", with nothing to say what is. Now a name that says the same words as exactly one act — case, hyphens and underscores aside — is that act wherever acts are found by name: `store.apply`, `applyAll`, `preview`, `permits`, `missingFor` and `mutation`, and the agent's tools, `preview_mutation` and `act_on_several`. The log, the policy and every check read the act's own name. A name no act says is refused as before, and `UnknownMutationError` carries `nearest`, the act within a few letters of it ("Unknown mutation "connect-threat-partof-it" — did you mean "connect-threat-part-of"?"), which the agent's tools say too. `actSpelledAs`, `nearestAct` and `spellingOf` are on `@graview/core`. A cardinality-one relation a kind holds to its own kind was offered as "Set the threat", which says nothing of which threat: it is titled in the relation's own words, its description when that names the record ("The family it belongs to." → "Set the family it belongs to", "Clear the family it belongs to"), else the relation's words after the noun ("Set the threat (part of)"); a grant said in words says it the same way. `core`'s tests apply, preview and permit `connect-threat-partOf` as `connect-threat-part-of`, refuse a near name with the nearest act, and title a relation to the kind's own kind by its description and without one; `tools`' run the act by the relation's spelling and refuse a near name with "Did you mean". With the two changes landed beside it — a derived edit naming the act that writes a field it refuses, and a headline that opens on a count in words starting with a capital — a hosted page carries 2.4 KB more up front, minified (1.0 KB gzipped), and so does one handed a compiled app: the store, the act boundary and the blocks are on every page.

Compatibility: additive in the API — `actSpelledAs`, `nearestAct` and `spellingOf` on `@graview/core`, and `UnknownMutationError.nearest`. A behavior change in the store and the derived tools: a call naming an act in another spelling of its name runs it rather than being refused, and a cardinality-one relation to its own kind gives its connect and disconnect a different title. The declaration, the wire and the stored formats are unchanged; the act names are unchanged.
