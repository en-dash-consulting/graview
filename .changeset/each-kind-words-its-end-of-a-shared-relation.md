Each kind words its own end of a relation several kinds declare. An edge's name was one relation to every surface, so a record listed everything that reached it by `by` under the first declaring kind's words, and `graview check` refused two kinds declaring one name in two sets of words (`edge-name-shared`). n-dx's `under` sits on six kinds: a change wants "the tasks inside it", a zone "the zones inside it", and they could not both be said. Now a record's page, a record's connections in the scene, and the cards around a focus list what reaches a record by each reading: kinds that word a relation alike together, kinds that word it their own way apart, each under its own words (an artist's page says "Their songs" and "Their releases"), and a band of a kind is captioned in that kind's own words. So the refusal is gone: a kind may word its own end of a shared relation.

Compatibility: ops, stored formats, wire messages, the document format, the compiled format, tool names and schemas are unchanged. Changed: the finding code `edge-name-shared` is no longer reported; a document or app it refused now compiles. A record's relation groups (`recordFacts(...).links`, the scene's connections) are one per relation, direction and reading, where they were one per relation and direction.
