The home opens on the jobs, and one design is composed from the declaration rather than written into it (Roadmap f-design-composer). An app looked generic until somebody told a model, several times over, to make it usable and beautiful, and a host's answer was a table from a field's type to a lens that only ever added. An app that says what its people come to do (`purpose.jobs`) and draws no home of its own now opens on it: the first job with records waiting is the page's title, with how many answer it as the seat sees them, its first five records and the job's act on each (the record page's own act and form); the other jobs follow smaller, one with nothing waiting is a single quiet line, and the gallery of places comes after them, smaller. The rest of a job is the kind's list: an address narrows a list by a field's value, not by a job's rule, so the link says "All tasks". An app with no jobs keeps its home exactly. The embed hands its pages the app, so a hosted document's home answers its jobs too. `@graview/core/compose` proposes the rest as one change: `inferJobs(document | app, { records? })` reads at most three jobs off a declaration, conservatively — a choice that ends and the date a record is due by ("Which vendors are due within a week and not yet booked?"), a relation of one that a quarter or more of the records lack ("Which features have no area?"), a status whose first value a record waits at ("Which requests are still open?") — never drawn until a person declares them; `composeDesign(document | app, { records? })` answers `{ edits, said }`: those jobs where the app says none, the critic's own edits asked again until it has none left, and an accent from a fixed palette by the app's name where the brand has none. Applied, every document in this repository and its fixtures compiles and the critic warns of nothing. Said of the Graview Roadmap app: two jobs ("Which releases are still in draft?", "Which requests are still open?"), the four "Stage: X" places removed, five slices kept off the bar, and the open requests listed on its home. Said of todo: nothing, its jobs declared and its brand its own. `design-job-unanswered` is now said only of a declared home that lists none of a job's kind, and carries the `set-home` that answers it; the graview-pages skill says how the home opens. A hosted page carries 0.0 KB more up front — 40 bytes, and 32 handed a compiled app, that the batch's jobs and critic came to together; the jobs home is fetched with the pages — and the pages face alone 5.9 KB more, the jobs answered as the home first draws.

Compatibility: the declaration and check finding codes — `design-job-unanswered` is no longer said of an app with no declared home (the derived home answers its jobs), and now carries a `set-home` edit; `@graview/core/compose` is new (`composeDesign`, `inferJobs`, `accentFor`, `ACCENT_PALETTE`, `MOST_INFERRED_JOBS`), its edits held to `editDocument` and the critic (`docs/stability.md` §4). The look: the derived home of an app with jobs leads with them (`[data-testid="jobs"]`, `job`, `job-row`, `job-act`, `job-quiet`), its standing a line after them, and the gallery under the jobs is smaller (`[data-testid="gallery"][data-small]`). Ops and primitives, stored formats, the wire, derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged.
