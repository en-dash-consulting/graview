A declared row names its record. Threat Watch's threat row was set to `{severity} · {status} · …` alone, and its list drew lines of facts with no threat named in any of them — a list of links nobody could tell apart — while describe_place said each record's title above them: the words and the picture disagreed. A row is a link to its record and needs a name, for a reader and for a person: one whose blocks show no `title`, no `headline` and no words holding the record's whole name is now drawn with the record's name first, as a title is; one that names the record is drawn as declared (`blocksName`, on `@graview/core/blocks`). describe_place says the name that leads the row, then what its blocks say — a row led by a headline is said with the headline as its title, once, where it was said as the record's title and then as a heading. `graview-view-specs` says a row is always named, and how to choose how. Primitives' tests find a row of facts alone drawn with its record's name first and said with it, and a row led by a title, by a headline or by words that hold the name drawn as declared with the name once. A hosted page carries 0.9 KB more up front for this and the abbreviations of `a-creating-form-asks-its-name-first` (0.4 KB on the page handed a compiled app), the scene face 1.2 KB more before it draws, and the pages face alone 1.6 KB more (0.8 KB gzipped), under raises of those budgets by that (`scripts/budget-raises/a-declared-row-names-its-record-*.json`).

Compatibility: the declaration, stored formats, the wire, check findings and derived tools are unchanged. A row spec that names its record nowhere now draws the record's name before its blocks; describe_place's item for a row led by a headline has the headline as its `title` and leaves it out of `parts`. New on `@graview/core/blocks`: `blocksName`. What Graview Cloud does: nothing — its pages and describe_place agree once it takes this version.
