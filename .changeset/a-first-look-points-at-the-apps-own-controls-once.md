A new app can point a person at its own controls once. A host that wanted to introduce the switch between the scene and the pages, the problems count, Activity and the Ask box could only do it by their selectors, which are not a surface it may hold the framework to, so its tour broke the first time the bar was drawn differently. `firstLook` on `mount` and `<Embed>`, the whole-page `Shell` and `PagesApp` runs a short sequence of hints the framework anchors to its own controls: one at a time, beside the control each names and never over it, a non-modal dialog the keyboard moves into and is said as it opens, with Next, Done on the last, Skip and Escape; a press anywhere else ends it, and the keyboard goes back where it was. A hint whose control is not drawn — no Ask box, nothing yet in Activity, the switch gone from a phone's bar while Find is open — is skipped, and it works on either face of an embed, kept across a change of face. It is shown once per viewer: the host says whether this viewer has seen it and is told when it is over; with no host seam, the browser remembers it in `localStorage` for the app. A host adds hints of its own after the framework's by naming one of the framework's controls. The hints are fetched only when a first look is shown: a hosted page's first load is about 1.1 KB more (0.4 KB gzipped), inside its budget, and every face 7.6 KB more minified.

Compatibility: additive. New: `firstLook?: FirstLook` on `FrameOptions` (so `mount`, `<Embed>` and `@graview/embed/pages`' `mount`), `ShellProps` and `PagesAppProps`, where `FirstLook = boolean | { seen?: boolean | (() => boolean); onDone?: (how: "finished" | "dismissed") => void; steps?: readonly { anchor: FirstLookAnchor; title: string; words: string }[] }` and `FirstLookAnchor` is one of `FIRST_LOOK_ANCHORS`: `"faces"`, `"problems"`, `"activity"`, `"ask"`, `"find"`, `"profile"`, `"home"`. `FIRST_LOOK_ANCHORS`, `FirstLookDoor`, `firstLookKey` and the types `FirstLook`, `FirstLookAnchor`, `FirstLookEnd`, `FirstLookOptions`, `FirstLookStep` and `FirstLookDoorProps` from `@graview/primitives`, `/frame` and `/pages`; `FIRST_LOOK_ANCHORS` and those types from `@graview/embed`. Off unless given: an app that passes nothing draws and loads what it did. Ops, stored formats, the wire, the document format, the compiled format, check finding codes and derived tool names and schemas are unchanged. What Graview Cloud does: pass `firstLook: { seen: () => <the viewer has seen it>, onDone: () => <record that they have> }` to `mount` for a new app, and name an anchor rather than a selector in any hint of its own; `true` remembers it in the browser instead.
