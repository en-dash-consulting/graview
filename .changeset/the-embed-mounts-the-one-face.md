The embed's `mount` takes `sceneUnderPages` and puts its bar in the one face's bar slot (FR-239), HQ feature:f-embed-mount-takes-scene-under-pages. Graview Cloud draws every app's page through `mount`, and 0.2.0's one face — Pages and the Scene as one place, Page, Nearby and City — was reachable only through `PagesApp`'s context, so Cloud patched the embed to carry it. `FrameOptions` (so `mount`, `<Embed>` and `@graview/embed/pages`) now takes `sceneUnderPages`: the routed face stands over the Scene's own city, drawn with the `views` the embed already has. There is then no scene face: the bar offers no Scene or Pages, a `face` other than a picture is drawn as the pages (and `where()` and `drawn()` say the pages), and the overview's address is the City over the home, on memory routing as on the address bar (`handle.setPath("/places/overview")` rises to the City there). A desk fetches no other face while it is idle: the one face draws none. An `<Embed>` that turns the option off later is drawn the face it would have opened on. The embed's bar, which stood in the frame's flow and was covered by the city at Nearby and City, stands in the one face's bar slot, fixed at the window's top above every layer of the face: the app's name, its places, Find, the inbox, a host's Share and the person are seen and pressed at every level, and the page keeps its room under the bar. The bar is drawn once into an element the frame owns, and that element is what moves into the slot when the one face's part arrives and back when it suspends, so the keyboard, a word typed in Find and an open menu stay as they were. The slot carries a public attribute, `data-graview-one-face-bar`, beside its test id `one-place-bar`, and `PageContext` gains `onBarSlot`, which hands a host that draws the bar itself the slot once it is drawn. On the one face the page is in the window's flow: the embed's content box scrolls nothing of its own and its root holds no fixed height and clips nothing, so the window scrolls the page and the embed is as tall as it, at least its `height`; the root is still its own stacking context. The keys 1, 2, 3, `[` and `]` move the levels on an embed-mounted page as on the routed face. Without the option every face draws as before. `graview preview` draws the one face at an address given `?graview-one-face=1`, which it takes off the address once read. The embed's test (`the-one-face-is-mounted-whole-with-its-bar-in-the-slot`) holds it, and a new harness, `embed-one-face`, mounts it whole as Cloud does over `graview preview` and holds, at 1280 and 390 wide, `drawsTheOneFace`, `barStandsInTheSlot`, `pageKeepsItsRoomUnderTheBar`, `theWindowScrollsThePage`, `barVisibleAndPressableAtPage`, `…AtNearby` and `…AtCity` (every control of the bar inside the window, a press at its middle landing on it, the person's menu opening from it) `keysMoveTheLevels` and `keysTypedInFindLeaveTheLevel` ("1", "3" and "]" typed into Find in the slot at Nearby are words, and the level stays). The embed's README and `docs/stability.md` say the option and the slot. A hosted page carries 1.5 KB more up front (1_553 bytes minified, 526 gzipped; handed a compiled app, 1_553 and 518), its budget 1.4 KB more up front past the headroom it had, under raises named `the-embed-mounts-the-one-face-*` in `scripts/budget-raises/`, with the pages face alone 1_261 / 356, every face 1_756 / 613 and the studio handed in 515 gzipped; the scene face before it draws measured 1_157_339 B (1131 KB). Said as shipped: `capabilities().shipped` gains FR-239.

Compatibility: ops and primitives, stored formats, the declaration and check finding codes, and derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged. The wire: unchanged; `capabilities().shipped` lists FR-239. The JavaScript API is additive (`FrameOptions.sceneUnderPages`, `PageContext.onBarSlot`). The look (not a surface): on the one face the bar's slot is `[data-graview-one-face-bar]` (and `[data-testid="one-place-bar"]`), kept and said here when it moves. What Graview Cloud does: drop its interim patch of the embed (INTERIM(FR-239)) and mount with `face: "pages"` and `sceneUnderPages: true`; `bar: { faces: false }` is no longer needed for the one face.
