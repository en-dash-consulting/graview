The embed's `mount` takes `sceneUnderPages` and puts its bar in the one face's bar slot (FR-239), HQ feature:f-embed-mount-takes-scene-under-pages. Graview Cloud draws every app's page through `mount`, and 0.2.0's one face — Pages and the Scene as one place, Page, Nearby and City — was reachable only through `PagesApp`'s context, so Cloud patched the embed to carry it. `FrameOptions` (so `mount`, `<Embed>` and `@graview/embed/pages`) now takes `sceneUnderPages`: the routed face stands over the Scene's own city, drawn with the `views` the embed already has. There is then no scene face: the bar offers no Scene or Pages, a `face` other than a picture is drawn as the pages (and `where()` and `drawn()` say the pages), and the overview's address is the City over the home, on memory routing as on the address bar. The embed's bar, which stood in the frame's flow and was covered by the city at Nearby and City, stands in the one face's bar slot, fixed at the window's top above every layer of the face: the app's name, its places, Find, the inbox, a host's Share and the person are seen and pressed at every level, and the page keeps its room under the bar. The slot carries a public attribute, `data-graview-one-face-bar`, beside its test id `one-place-bar`, and `PageContext` gains `onBarSlot`, which hands a host that draws the bar itself the slot once it is drawn. On the one face the page is in the window's flow: the embed's content box scrolls nothing of its own and its root holds no fixed height and clips nothing, so the window scrolls the page and the embed is as tall as it, at least its `height`; the root is still its own stacking context. The keys 1, 2, 3, `[` and `]` move the levels on an embed-mounted page as on the routed face. Without the option every face draws as before. `graview preview` draws the one face at an address given `?city=1`. The embed's test (`the-one-face-is-mounted-whole-with-its-bar-in-the-slot`) holds it, and a new harness, `embed-one-face`, mounts it whole as Cloud does over `graview preview` and holds, at 1280 and 390 wide, `drawsTheOneFace`, `barStandsInTheSlot`, `pageKeepsItsRoomUnderTheBar`, `theWindowScrollsThePage`, `barVisibleAndPressableAtPage`, `…AtNearby` and `…AtCity` (every control of the bar inside the window, a press at its middle landing on it, the person's menu opening from it) and `keysMoveTheLevels`. The embed's README and `docs/stability.md` say the option and the slot. A hosted page carries 614 bytes more up front (218 gzipped; handed a compiled app, 614 and 214), its budget 0.4 KB more up front past the headroom it had, under raises named `the-embed-mounts-the-one-face-*` in `scripts/budget-raises/`, with the pages face alone 470 / 126, every face 929 / 305 and the studio handed in 187 gzipped; the scene face before it draws measured 1_156_400 B (1130 KB). Said as shipped: `capabilities().shipped` gains FR-239.

Compatibility: ops and primitives, stored formats, the declaration and check finding codes, and derived tool names and input schemas — unchanged. The wire: unchanged; `capabilities().shipped` lists FR-239. The JavaScript API is additive (`FrameOptions.sceneUnderPages`, `PageContext.onBarSlot`). The look (not a surface): on the one face the bar's slot is `[data-graview-one-face-bar]` (and `[data-testid="one-place-bar"]`), kept and said here when it moves. What Graview Cloud does: drop its interim patch of the embed (INTERIM(FR-239)) and mount with `face: "pages"` and `sceneUnderPages: true`; `bar: { faces: false }` is no longer needed for the one face.
