A list narrows by a number and by a word. The arrangement offers every number field as a filter — `price:at-most:25000`, `mileage:at-least:10000` — and every word field by the values it holds; the arrange bar's Only… lists four round steps through what the list holds ("at most £25,000") and a word field's values by name ("Make: Kia") when there are up to sixty. `roundSteps` is exported.
