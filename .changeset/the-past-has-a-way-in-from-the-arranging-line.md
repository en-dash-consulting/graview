The past has a way in from the line that arranges a list. A rescue team with one callout under way and 1,458 behind it drew a picture of one row with no arranging line, and on a phone the district that says "+1458 past" was folded away with the others, so the past could not be reached or narrowed. A lens's line (`useArranging`) and a kind's stop now draw wherever records stand behind the horizon, with "+1458 past" on the line itself (`ArrangeBar`'s new `past`); pressed, it widens the view to the past and the keys go on along the line.

Compatibility: no surface changes; `ArrangeBar` takes an optional `past`.
