apps/seedbed/src/domain/chapters.ts declares the garden thirteen chapters at a time (plot; gardeners and who tends what; the rule; plantings and the horizon; a seat; remembering; policy; brand; the other face; a coverage lens; a board lens; ship with a migration; the garden's own face). Each chapter is a real GraviewApp subset, a seed and a stop. scripts/progression.mjs opens every chapter in a browser, photographs it in both schemes and judges 13 claims; scripts/site-progression.mjs regenerates the chapter section of docs/site/index.html; the page's opener is chapter one's declaration beside the chapter running live, the Lenses section says lenses are the app's to make with three starters, and the copy says the framework is built to be worked on with an agent. The hand-made Try-it demo is gone. Every chapter on the page is live through @graview/embed. Chapter 13 replaces every pages surface with the garden's own design and puts a lens the garden drew of itself in the scene.
