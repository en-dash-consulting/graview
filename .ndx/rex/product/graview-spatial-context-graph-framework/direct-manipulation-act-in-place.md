Clicking a thing currently TRAVELS to it, which is the wrong default. Most of the time you want to act on it where it is — substitute a player without leaving the formation, move an event without leaving the week. Travel should be the deliberate second gesture, not the only one.

This feature reworks the interaction model so a single click selects in place and surfaces the context actions, a double click travels, and a right click opens the actions at the pointer. It also fixes the surfaces that made selection hard to read: a raised relation with no visible origin, a rule that appears to do nothing when selected, an activity rail that sits over the scene, and a mutation nobody can interpret from its name.
