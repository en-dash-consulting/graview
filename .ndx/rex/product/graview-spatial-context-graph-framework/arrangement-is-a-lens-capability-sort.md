Every surface that lists things arranges them, and today each does it its own way or not at all: the pages list page groups by an edge and narrows by a relation through URL keys of its own, the scene's districts sort by id, and the board, timeline, calendar and coverage lenses take no sort, filter or group at all. One headless module derives what a kind can be arranged by from its declaration — its fields by type, its edges, its lifecycle, its standing — and turns an arrangement into ordered groups; one grammar carries it in a stop (in.sort, in.filter, in.group in the fragment; the same keys in a page's search) so an arranged picture is a link; one control row draws it; and each default lens takes it through an option that can be turned off.
