The derived pages face is an admin site over the schema: kinds, lists, records, problems. The scene is the app: named pictures (places) on a board per kind, roads between kinds, a seat you can talk to. Out of the box the pages face should be the same app in the traditional web idiom: the app's pictures as pages with an index that lands you among them, the kinds and their relationships as visible structure rather than only per-record link groups, and the assistant embedded on every page with the page's subject as "this". Three tasks, each shippable: pictures first (it brings the scene provider and the view registry into the routed face, which the other two build on), then relationships, then the assistant.
