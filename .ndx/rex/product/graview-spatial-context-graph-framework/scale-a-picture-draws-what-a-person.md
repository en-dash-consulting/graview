Design at docs/scale.md, hardened three times. A real catalogue (Tech N9ne from MusicBrainz: 1,177 songs, 568 artists, 479 releases, ~5,000 edges) broke the scene: the altitude home was 21,340 elements (drive-in thumbnails drawing every member), focusing Tech N9ne drew 1,259 hosts and 4,433 line paths at ~1 frame a second, and every transition re-rendered and re-measured the whole scene per frame. The rule: a surface draws as many marks as a person can read in the room it has and says what the rest are in the graph's own words; below that, nothing changes. Over budget the relation band groups each relation's far ends by the best arrangeable grouping as aggregates opened by the existing expanded toggle, lines follow marks, thumbnails are budgeted, and nothing measures while moving.
