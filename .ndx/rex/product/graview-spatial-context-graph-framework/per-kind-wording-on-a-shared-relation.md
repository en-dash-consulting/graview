An edge name is one relation across every kind that declares it: under on six kinds must share one description and inverse, so the far end cannot say 'the tasks inside it' for a change and 'the zones inside it' for a zone. Either let a kind word its own end of a shared relation, or have the checker say the one-relation rule is by design where it refuses. Surfaced by n-dx-graview's declaration (n-dx.graview.json).
