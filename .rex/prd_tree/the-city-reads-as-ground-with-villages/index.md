description: "WHAT IS WRONG NOW (from altitude screenshots of todo, rota, seedbed, squad, groundskeeper on 2026-09-19, and Nick's own Squad screenshot). The map arithmetic is right — every district has a plot on the 2:1 lattice, one uniform scale — but nothing on screen SAYS ground, and the chrome around it reads as an engineer's console. THE PICTURE: (1) No ground: the lattice is a faint hatch and a plot is never drawn, so districts float on paper; an opened or showing plot gets a dashed ring that reads as a selection marquee. (2) A district is one glyph — a cube, a stick figure, a set-square — of the same visual weight whether it holds one member or fifty; the population lives in a height nobody reads. A village is a cluster of buildings, and the cluster's size is the population. (3) Relations are thin curves from member chips to nameplate corners, drawn over the buildings; a focused screen's every member is wired across the whole city; roads on the ground exist in code (latticePoints, roadsOf) but rarely draw. (4) The drive-in screen hovers at the top of the window with its marquee down on the plot; a screen should stand ON its plot. (5) Nameplates are floating pills with an OPEN chevron on every one; signs should stand on the land and say the chevron only when reached for. (6) The descent from altitude to the stack is a cut between two worlds, not a zoom into a village. THE CHROME: (7) The chat's gear opens a pane of prose over the map — four rungs with paragraphs, a key field, sentences about the dev server — clipped at the window's edge; that is a setting and belongs in the profile as one row. (8) The altitude control says \"Graview\" and \"Focus\", neither of which says up or down; \"moved ×\" appears for moves nobody made; the legend lists every edge kind in the schema whether or not it is on screen. The fix is drawing and wording, not layout: the layout already knows where everything is. Order: ground tiles → villages → roads → screen on its plot → the door as a setting → the bar and plates → signposts and descent."
