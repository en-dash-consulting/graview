description: "WHAT IS WRONG NOW (from altitude screenshots of todo, rota, seedbed, squad, groundskeeper on 2026-09-19). The map arithmetic is right — every district has a plot on the 2:1 lattice, one uniform scale — but nothing on screen SAYS ground. (1) No ground: the lattice is a faint hatch and a plot is never drawn, so districts float on paper. (2) A district is one glyph — a cube, a stick figure, a set-square — of the same visual weight whether it holds one member or fifty; the population lives in a height nobody reads. A village is a cluster of buildings, and the cluster's size is the population. (3) Relations are thin curves from member chips to nameplate corners; roads on the ground exist in code (latticePoints, roadsOf) but rarely draw. (4) The drive-in screen hovers at the top of the window with its marquee down on the plot; a screen should stand ON its plot. (5) Nameplates are floating pills; marquee pills stack; signs should stand on the land. (6) The descent from altitude to the stack is a cut between two worlds, not a zoom into a village. The fix is drawing, not layout: the layout already knows where everything is."
