The rule: a surface draws as many marks as a person can read in the room it has and says what the rest are in the graph's own words; below that, nothing changes. Over budget the relation band groups each relation's far ends by the best arrangeable grouping as aggregates opened by the existing expanded toggle, lines follow marks, thumbnails are budgeted, and nothing measures while moving."
