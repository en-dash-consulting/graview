description: "The routed face is derived for free and Seedbed's chapter 13 shows one custom design (the almanac), but Things — the demo people open first — runs on the derived pages, and nothing in the repository looks like a product somebody polished. At least two demos need a routed face that reads as shipped: a design of the app's own over every surface (shell, home, problems, list, record and the not-found page) with a real typographic system, considered spacing and rhythm, a palette derived from the brand and measured by the checker, and interaction that feels finished — in-place editing on records, act forms inline with live validation and refusals said where the press happened, list pages with grouping, sorting and a filter the URL remembers, keyboard-first navigation with visible focus, transitions that honour reduced motion, empty states that say what to do next, and the problems page as a real inbox with one-press repairs. Both faces stay one declaration: the design takes its acts from the derivation through store.permits, withholds with the policy's own sentence, and renders every route including ones it did not replace. Things gets this first (its face is a to-do product: lists as columns, tasks as cards with due, done and what they wait for, drag to reorder where the graph allows it); Rota gets it as part of its own feature, held to the same bar. The pattern goes into the graview-pages skill as the worked example of a finished face, and the scaffold's one custom page points at it.
