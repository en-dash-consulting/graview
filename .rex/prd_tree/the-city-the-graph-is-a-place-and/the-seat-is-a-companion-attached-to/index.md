description: "The agent seat is drawn today as a robot figure in the scene: docked on its own pad in the city, walking to what it wrote after a turn, following the pointer when pressed, with the chat panel anchored beside it as a bubble while it follows. A thing in the middle of the picture that moves on its own reads as a distraction rather than a helper, and it has no place at all inside a full-screen lens. The seat becomes a companion attached to the viewframe, on the LEFT, where the scene already keeps everything that is about the current subject: the inspector (the acts for the selection, opened at the pointer by right-click), the quick relations and the relation key. Those and the chat are three panels saying the same subject; they become one — the companion rail: a subject header (the selection, else the hovered pick, else where you are), the acts that can be taken here with the withheld ones struck through, the subject's relations, the seat's answers and its questions back, the seat's state. Right-click or the keyboard's menu key opens the same panel at the pointer, so the context menu and the assistant are one construct. It is fixed to the frame: identical at altitude, on the ground, flying closer and inside a full-screen lens. Where the seat worked is marked on the thing itself with a way to fly there, not walked to. The figure, its pad and follow mode retire; people's presence figures are unchanged; the seat's questions stay pinned at the nodes they are about. The same companion is what the pages face embeds, as a left drawer beside the reading column."
