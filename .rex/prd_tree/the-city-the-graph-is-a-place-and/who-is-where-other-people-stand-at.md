description: "WHAT IS THERE NOW. Multi-client state exists once and works: @graview/ship serveStore (packages/ship/src/serve.ts) with GET /graview/state, POST /graview/ops, GET /graview/since?seq=N, GET /graview/health; openRemote (remote.ts) polls /since every 800ms and lands ops through Store.receive (store.ts:619-633); identity on the wire is x-graview-seat / x-graview-roles headers (serve.ts:75-83, seatOf override). Polling is deliberate (curl-reproducible); there is no WebSocket, SSE or BroadcastChannel anywhere. Author {kind, id, session} is on every op; participantOf(op) = kind:id:session (activity.ts:39) is read by exactly one function and is the ready-made presence key; Manner \"co-edited\" already exists. Settings (react/src/settings.ts:3-13) are the recorded precedent for \"a fact about this person at this screen, not in the op log\". ViewState is the complete answer to where somebody is: toUrl(view) (view-state.ts:113) carries focus, overview, zoom, in.view, selection, pan. Principal.id is a user node id (apps/todo/src/ui/app.tsx:35-48) so a name is one lookup; ANONYMOUS is \"Nobody in particular\". Demo apps persist via createBrowserAdapter only (localStorage), no server. Known gap: openRemote wraps only store.apply — applyAll and undo never reach the wire (remote.ts:158-195).\n\nWHAT TO BUILD.\n\n1. Presence payload, no pixels. Presence { participant, name?, hue, stop: string (toUrl(view)), over?: pickId, robot?: { at, mode }, at: ISO }. Each viewer draws the others on THEIR OWN map by useWhereIs(fromUrl(stop).focusId | over), so different window sizes agree. A per-tab session id (crypto.randomUUID kept in sessionStorage) fills Author.session for that tab's ops AND the presence key, so the log and the figures agree about who a participant is (today session is \"ui\" for every seat).\n\n2. Channel. PresenceChannel { here(p), onWho(listener), leave() } in @graview/ship with two implementations behind one interface: (a) browser: BroadcastChannel(\"graview:who:<scope>\") beside createBrowserAdapter — same-origin tabs see each other, which is multiplayer for free and deterministic under Playwright (pages in one context share BroadcastChannel); (b) served: POST /graview/here and GET /graview/who kept in a TTL Map inside serveStore's closure (never touching openStore, the adapter, or the log), seatOf(request) naming the poster, folded into openRemote's existing poll so presence costs no extra round trip. Heartbeat every poll; gone after 3 missed (~2.5s); pagehide → leave(). curl can still reproduce it. Fix the openRemote gap first (applyAll and undo must travel) since a robot's plans and take-backs go through them.\n\n3. State. usePresenceState(channel, view, principal, robot) owned by GraviewProvider beside useActivityState (context.tsx:339), exposed as `who: ReadonlyMap<participant, Presence>` on GraviewContextValue; it publishes on view change, robot change, and heartbeat, throttled to the poll interval. Nothing ever writes presence to the store.\n\n4. Figures. The Occupants overlay from the robot task draws a Figure per other participant: at altitude on the plot their stop focuses, in the AUDIENCE ROW in front of that kind's screen when their stop names a showing (useWhereIs(\"screen:<kind>\")), many at one drive-in → a row of initials with +n overflow; their robots drawn beside them, captioned with the owner's name; their `over` node outlined in their hue for the hold window; on the ground level (not altitude) others at a different stop appear as edge indicators with their names. Presence and activity share the participant's hue so an edit lighting a district and the figure standing there read as one person. Anonymous viewers are a count on the plot, not a figure.\n\n5. Follow a person. Clicking a figure adopts their stop (go(fromUrl(stop))) and keeps adopting it as it changes until you navigate yourself or press Escape — the mirror of robot-follow; a \"following <name>\" line in the bar while it holds.\n\n6. Privacy is a reader setting. Sharing your stop is on for seated (named) principals and off for anonymous; sharing `over` (what you hover) is off until turned on in Profile (loadSetting/rememberSetting, localStorage, never the graph); an embed broadcasts nothing unless EmbedOptions.presence is set. Profile shows a \"seen by others as\" line.\n\n7. Attribution of agents. Another person's robot is their seat's participant; its ops already arrive as autonomous marks via receive → activity; the figure's caption is \"<name>'s agent\" so whose agent is doing what is legible from the city without opening Activity.\n\nHARNESS. scripts/verify-who.mjs (browser, todo, two pages in one context sharing BroadcastChannel, ?as= different seats): B sees A's figure at the tasks plot when A focuses the tasks drive-in; A picks a showing → A moves to the audience row on B; A runs a turn → A's robot appears at the written kind on B before B's district lights; A hovers a task with sharing on → B sees the outline; B clicks A → B's URL follows A's; A closes → gone within the TTL. Served: packages/ship/tests/integration/serve.test.ts gains two openRemote clients whose presence round-trips through /here and /who and expires; applyAll and undo now reach the wire (a test fails without the fix).\n\nTRAPS. remote.ts patches store.apply by monkeypatching — extend to applyAll/undo the same way or replace with a proper hook; BroadcastChannel is per origin so the launcher's mounted apps share nothing unless scoped by the store scope; a driven browser starts fresh (navigator.webdriver) so the harness must pass remember=1 only where persistence matters; never let a presence heartbeat count as activity (markActivity must not see it); the hold window must expire figures whose tab died without pagehide."
