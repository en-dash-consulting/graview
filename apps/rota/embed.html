How the hall gets covered

The committee keeps a rota. For years it was a sheet of A4 on the noticeboard, which worked until somebody took it down to photocopy it. What follows is the same rota, running here on this page — not a screenshot of one.

The week

Every slot, and who has it. A shift nobody has taken shows as a gap, because a rota whose gaps look the same as its fills is a rota nobody reads twice.

Who is covering what

The same roster, seen the other way up: volunteers down, shifts across. It is one graph and two pictures, which is the whole reason the thing is a graph.

Both of these are the live application, mounted twice at two stops. Everything you can do in them is an act the roster judges, records and can take back — including the ones this seat is not allowed, which say so rather than disappearing.
