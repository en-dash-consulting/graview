0.1.63

Published October 29, 2026 · the current version

An app declares workflows that fill next week, keep records in step, archive what is done and label what arrives; people propose changes for review; Notion makes the properties it needs; a connection says whom it invited; and the right-click menu says what is under the cursor.

  • Workflows. A document's workflows run on a schedule (words or cron, in the app's zone), when a record is made or changed, after a connection's pass, or by hand. Steps run an act over a query, make dated records from a pattern (generate), archive what is done after N days (archive), label each record with a model and a floor of plain rules (classify), or ask an agent within a budget. A run never makes a record twice, its first run after a change is a dry run, a paused workflow holds, and what it asks waits as one suggestion a person accepts whole or one change at a time. Workflows stand on the Scene's map; graview workflow lists and runs them; an owner's seat runs, pauses and resumes them.
  • Records in step. A kind may say it derives from another: a plan item's rename renames its event in the same change and the next sync sends it; a computed field may be sent out through a connection.
  • Proposals and votes. A grant may say propose: such a seat's form reads Propose, and a reviewer accepts or declines what it proposed. A shared or signed-out seat is refused a vote unless the host keeps visitors' votes advisory, apart from the tally that accepts.
  • Connections. A Notion connection keeps several data sources of one database, one kind each, and makes the properties it needs. A calendar pass says in history whom it invited or took off, caps an event's guests, and lets a host read them back.
  • Pages. The right-click menu offers Copy link and Inspect, leaves selected text to copy, and offers Add and Show only on a column; a blank field an act writes is an empty slot to fill, and a reader sees values plainly; an iPad's bar fits and its Scene glides once per tap; money never reads .00; a timeline that cannot draw is refused with its bindings named.

Before you upgrade: New: the document's workflows with its step kinds and workflow-* codes, @graview/core/workflows and @graview/tools/workflows, the edits add-workflow, set-workflow, remove-workflow, pause-workflow, resume-workflow, set-derives, set-app-tidying; a kind's derives with derives-* codes and the stored-data code derived-out-of-step; a grant's propose, the built-in act propose-changes and the tool propose_changes; run_workflow, pause_workflow and resume_workflow where a host wires them; a Notion connection's sources and create, and connection-sources; a calendar's guests.max and connection-guests-max; the optional op fields sentOut and suggestsSaid and the lineage keys items and proposedBy; Principal.shared and visitorKey, and votes: { advisory }; a money field's places; splashHtml's per-page words and the caustics shader. Changed: a vote from a shared or visitor seat is refused; a provider given no principal in an app with a policy offers no in-place editors; runWorkerViewHeadless reads a place's missing kind from the app or throws; add-lens refuses a timeline that cannot draw; a kind may work out 60 computed fields. The wire is otherwise unchanged.

0.1.62

Published October 28, 2026

Calendar events invite the people they are about, a view links to the Scene, formulas build words and times, an act carries a change across a link, a rule covers every record, and the seat proposes a change to the app.

A view links to the Scene and the home (data-place="overview", graview.navigate), read from one vocabulary the live host and the headless check share; a worker view's draw budget fits the device it runs on; brand.views gives every view one stylesheet and a fade between records; <graview-video autoplay cover> is a muted, looping hero background.
  • Rules and acts. concat, format, datetime, addMinutes, weekday and startOfWeek build words and moments in the app's locale and zone; setsLinked carries a change to the records an act's subject is linked to, in one undo; a rule's covers judges that each record reaches every one of another kind without a cost warning; a date that knows only its year or month is compared by its span and drawn across its month.

A notice carries its tone as a chip, not a side accent, and the framework's own error toasts offer a host's Report this (onReport); a home's Recently leaves out what the reader cannot see; brand.splash puts the app's opening screen in its declaration, drawn by @graview/pages/splash and graview preview.
  • The seat and tidying. For an owner or editor with a host to apply it, the seat proposes a change to the app from words, as a card of what it adds, keeps and drops; a suggestion pressed by someone who may not make it records their vote, and whoever may decide sees the tally.

Before you upgrade: New: a Google connection's fields.guests and the codes connection-guests-field and connection-guests-via; every Google write now says sendUpdates (none unless notify); placeNamesOf and placeNamesFrom on @graview/core/navigate; the rule functions concat, format, datetime, at, addMinutes, weekday, startOfWeek and the codes expr-type, format-pattern; an act's setsLinked with act-sets-linked-relation and act-sets-linked-field; a rule's covers (making require optional) with rule-covers-relation; brand.splash and brand.views with brand-splash-* and brand-views-css; @graview/core/css, @graview/core/traits, @graview/pages/splash, @graview/tools/reshape; onReport and onProposeChange beside barActions; the op vote-suggestion, tidying.autoAcceptAt and the warning tidying-votes-unreachable. Changed: PageContext.overview takes an optional stop; a partial date compares by the span it covers; a worker view's push and draw budgets scale with how slowly its worker started, up to four times. Stored formats and the wire are otherwise unchanged; capabilities().shipped names the three ids these changesets announce.

Published October 27, 2026

A loopback serve and preview believe a seat only with the run's key; typing and Edit ride an act only where it would run; compaction moves past old publishes, and an older build refuses what it would misread.

0.1.59

Published October 27, 2026

A security release: a served store and a dev server answer only their own page, the store judges every undo, merge and keystroke as the act it is, and nothing a seat may not see reaches it sideways.

  • A served store on loopback believes only its own page. graview serve keeps to its own origin and host, answers no wildcard CORS while it trusts seat headers (--allow-origin names an origin that may), reads x-graview-kind: system as a person unless --trust-seat-headers is given, holds a body to 16 MiB before routing, and caps a live socket's fragments.
  • A dev server's doors ask for a key. The studio, local, model, decision, files and views doors answer only a page opened from the address the dev server prints, which trades a key kept in ~/.graview/door-key for a cookie only its own pages send; a page opened at the plain address is told its doors are closed. The local claude bridge reads only its own folder.
  • The store judges what a change is. An undo of an undo is judged as the act it puts back, and a change no act made is taken back only by its maker, the system or a seat granted every act; what a page offers to take back is what the store would take back. A merge and a kept link ride the making of a kind only over what the seat made; a keystroke rides an act only while that act could run; a split takes only what the kind's edit takes.
  • Nothing hidden leaks sideways. A record withheld whole refuses like an absent one, ids are never minted from a kept field or reused from the log, the app's own actor's suggestions say nothing a seat's sight keeps and a planted duplicate steers no merge, and a seat acting for a person reads another app as that person.
  • Data never becomes code. A template's title and the studio's write-back are written as text, a kind's figure is judged where it is drawn, and a typeface or scoped theme stays in its box.

Before you upgrade: Changed: a dev server's doors refuse a page not opened from the printed address (GRAVIEW_DOOR_KEY sets the key, GRAVIEW_DOOR_HOSTS names a network host); graview serve on loopback no longer answers * CORS and reads no system seat from headers without --trust-seat-headers; serveStore takes maxBodyBytes, ownOrigin, allowOrigins and trustSystemSeat; an undo, a merge, a typed edit and a split may now be refused where they were let through; Store.mayUndo is new.
