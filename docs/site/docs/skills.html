The skills

Graview's 33 skills are instructions an AI coding assistant reads before it writes a Graview app. Each teaches a model one shape of the declaration, and each ends in graview check — reporting what it actually said, rather than claiming the work is done.

graview skills install .

All of them

  • graview-agent-seatWire an agent seat into a Graview app that shares the interface's own actions rather than shadowing them, and verify the diffs are identical rather than assuming they are.
  • graview-brandPut an installation's own name, mark, typeface and palette on a Graview app without forking a package, and let graview check measure the contrast rather than trusting it.
  • graview-chatLet the people in a Graview app talk to each other inside it — a room for the whole app, a thread on each record of a kind, mentions that tell the person named, unread counts on the bell — declared as rooms and checked by graview check; and let a host offer “Chat with …” its own staff through a support transport, each message carrying where the person was.
  • graview-deskPut a model INSIDE a Graview product — a surface that takes photographs or words, reaches the model through a declared door, and turns the answer into a plan somebody reviews before it touches the graph.
  • graview-embedPut a Graview app on somebody else's page — a picture in an article, a chapter in the docs, a live demo in a landing page — with its own theme scoped to one element, and nothing on the host touched.
  • graview-host-pageHost a Graview app as the page itself — a service that serves it, a chat's widget, the address bar handed over, the host's own menu and news inside it — and picture its places honestly: wait for data-graview-drawn or onDrawn rather than a timer, open a page on a view proposed and not kept (draftView), and photograph places with graview preview and graview shots (--view, --manifest).
  • graview-invariantWrite a Graview invariant that judges the graph and NAMES the mutations that would repair it, then verify it with graview check and by making it actually fire.
  • graview-lensBuild a Graview lens that binds ROLES rather than field names, so a second domain can reuse it unchanged — and prove the reuse rather than asserting it.
  • graview-linked-recordsLink a record in one Graview app to a record in another app — a relation whose target is another app's kind, read there as whoever is looking, walked by computed fields and rules, its far changes kept in this app's history — and verify it with graview check and by opening it as a viewer who may not see the other app.
  • graview-listsKeep a Graview app's lists usable as it grows — what a list offers to filter by, the order and groups it opens in, cards read at a glance, several records acted on at once, a list printed as a static page or exported as CSV, and a date that repeats rather than a record each time — and verify each with graview check and the page itself rather than assuming it.
  • graview-localeSay a Graview app's money, days, times and numbers the way its people say them — a currency, a locale and a time zone declared once on the brand, a field kept as money, {net | money} and {at | time} in a template — and let graview check refuse a code, a locale or a zone it cannot write rather than trusting the page.
  • graview-logoPut a client's own logo and page icon on a Graview app — inline SVG or a path, one for each scheme so a picture on white is not a white slab in the dark, an emoji as the icon — drawn in the default shell and handed to a design's own, and let graview check refuse a mark that could act rather than trusting it.
  • graview-new-appStart a product on Graview in its own repository — the shape of the declaration, the shell that comes for free, and the CI that keeps it honest afterwards.
  • graview-node-kindAdd a node kind to a Graview app — fields, edges, label, plural and field roles — and verify it with graview check rather than claiming it worked.
  • graview-pagesGive a Graview app the routed face it wants — from the derived pages, to one page in the app's own words, to the door of a product design that replaces every surface (graview-pages-design).
  • graview-pages-designGive a Graview app a product design over its routed face — its own shell under the one bar, every surface replaced, its tabs lit from the section a kind's pages are in, and a trail that says where a person is — and verify it by rendering each page and opening it, rather than assuming the derived pages are gone.
  • graview-permissionsDeclare who may do what in a Graview app once, so the store enforces it, the actions strip narrows and an agent seat narrows with it — verified by graview check and by trying it.
  • graview-port-appPort an existing application onto Graview — deciding what is a node, what is a field and what is an edge — and proving the port with a parity test rather than an assertion.
  • graview-seedGet a Graview app from a blank graph to a useful one — the order the declaration already states, a plan a model proposes, and one turn that can be taken back.
  • graview-shipDeploy a Graview app — persistence, op-log-native migrations, export and health — with one declaration and one adapter, self-hosted or behind a service.
  • graview-shipped-lensDeclare one of the lenses Graview ships — a calendar, a status board, a coverage, a timeline, a board, a plan, a map, a blocks place — as a titled place with its bindings and options, and read what graview check and add-lens refuse (an option the lens does not take, a lens that cannot draw, a title taken) rather than drawing a lens that quietly ignores a key.
  • graview-sightSay who sees which records of a Graview app, and which of their fields — so a seat is served only what it may see, on every surface at once — verified by graview check and by comparing what a seat is told with a hidden value and without one.
  • graview-studioOpen an app's declaration as a graph in Graview's own interface, change it with ordinary acts, let graview check judge the result before it is applied, migrate a stored graph, and write the declaration back as the files graview create writes.
  • graview-syncKeep a Graview app in step with another system — a graph projected from it as a snapshot, a SyncEngine that sends this app's acts back and holds a field both sides changed, a remote that speaks MCP behind a door on the dev server, and a Google calendar or a Notion database declared as a connection — and verify the first run sends nothing and a conflict is held rather than assuming the two agree.
  • graview-tidyingTidy a Graview app without losing anything — set records aside (archive, bring back) instead of deleting them, merge a duplicate into the one that stays, and let the app's own actor link and prune by suggestion a person accepts or declines, held to what people said, and suggest changes to the app itself learned from how several people use it — verified with graview check and by undoing it rather than assuming nothing was lost.
  • graview-todoistKeep a kind of a Graview app in step with a Todoist project, or one section or label of it, declared as a connection — a task's content, due date, deadline, priority, labels, section, assignee, parent and completion each in a field, both ways through a personal API token the host holds — and verify with graview check and a scripted Todoist that the first run sends nothing and a task completed there removes no record.
  • graview-view-drawingDraw in a Graview worker view — the HTML, SVG and CSS the host keeps of it, the app's tokens and brand, the images a view may show (the app's logo, the app's own images, the pictures on records), a record's video, the app's reel or a YouTube or Vimeo video the host plays and its full-screen viewer, files a person drops on it — and find out what the host refused rather than assuming it drew.
  • graview-view-formsLet a Graview worker view change records — a bound press (data-act), the fields a person types into, a record's own text filled to edit (data-prefill), a field that saves as it is typed (data-save) and the view hearing what became of each act (acted) — and prove a press applies and a field the view filled is refused, rather than assuming the form works.
  • graview-view-linksSend a person from a Graview worker view to a record, a place or a list — an <a data-record>, or a whole card or row bound to a record that opens on a click, a phone's tap and Enter — narrowed and to a section, and prove each lands by pressing it on a phone as well as a desk rather than assuming a card the describer lists opens.
  • graview-view-specsSay how a Graview kind's card, row and page read, as data — blocks from a closed set bound by {field} templates and rule-language conditions, headlines, figures and lists of related records — and the values worked out from others (computed) that they show, verified by graview check and the page rather than by assuming the spec drew.
  • graview-visitor-formsOpen one act of a Graview app to people who never join it — an RSVP, a sign-up, a request — as a form drawn alone, run as a visitor the host mints per answer, its answers landing as records and nothing else of the app shown — verified by graview check and by opening the form as that visitor.
  • graview-worker-viewWrite a Graview worker view — one plain script, no imports and no build, that draws HTML, SVG and CSS for a kind or the home through the graview global — with a manifest the host enforces, and prove it runs rather than assuming it does.
  • graview-workflowsHave a Graview app do something on its own — fill next week from a pattern, keep one record in step with another, set aside what is done, send what is public each morning — declared as a workflow (or a kind that follows another), checked by graview check and proven by a dry run before anything is kept.
