- 055b1eb: A suggestion takes a vote from someone who may not accept it, Roadmap f-suggestion-votes (the framework half; Cloud's signed-out visitors, its tidying notice count and the owner's switch are Cloud's). Someone who could see a suggestion and could not make its change — a reader, a visitor by link, a seat signed out on a public app — pressed Accept in the Suggested tray and met a refusal. Now their Accept and Decline are a vote, for or against: an op of the built-in `vote-suggestion` act (`VOTE_SUGGESTION`, `{ suggestion, vote: "for" | "against" | null }`), which every store answers to and no list offers, as `keep-link` is — `mutation` the call, no primitives, the vote on the op (`Operation.vote`), the records the suggestion names as what its sentence read, so a seat kept from one is kept from the vote as it is from the suggestion. One standing vote per person, the latest, an agent's counted as the person it acts for; voting again changes it and `null` withdraws it (`voteSuggestion`); a vote is its voter's to undo, the system's, or a seat's the policy grants every act. `acceptSuggestion` and `declineSuggestion` ask the store the question Accept would meet (`mayDecide`: every call of the suggestion permitted to the seat) and, where the answer is no, keep the vote instead and answer `voted`; where it is yes they make or decline it as ever, and a store with no policy lets everyone decide as it always did. Every entry of `suggestions()` carries `tally: { for, against, mine? }` — the reader's own standing vote as `mine` — so a list sorts on it, and `votesIn` and `tallyOf` read it off a log. The tray says "Your vote was recorded" to a voter and keeps the row, the pressed button saying which way they voted, Withdraw vote beside it; to whoever may decide it shows "4 for, 1 against" beside Accept, kept current as votes land. An app may say `"tidying": { "autoAcceptAt": 4 }` at the top of its document (`GraviewApp.tidying`, `StoreOptions.tidying`, handed to the store by every host that hands it the policy): at that many votes for, the app's own actor makes the suggestion in the deciding vote's own gesture — one batch, one undo — with its lineage naming the suggestion and the voters (`lineage.voters`, at most 200), having rehearsed that the actor would be let: a record a person said to leave alone leaves the vote standing and the suggestion waiting. `graview check` warns `tidying-votes-unreachable` when the number is set and the app has no policy, since then nobody votes. The agent tools' `accept_suggestion` and `decline_suggestion` answer `voted` from such a seat and `list_suggestions` carries each `tally`; no tool is added and no input schema moves. `a-suggestion-takes-a-vote-from-someone-who-may-not-accept-it.test.ts`, `an-app-says-a-suggestion-is-made-at-so-many-votes.test.ts` and `the-suggested-tray-takes-a-readers-vote-and-shows-the-tally-to-who-may-decide.test.tsx` hold it, and the chrome harness presses Accept as a reader on both faces and reads the tally as the keeper (aSuggestionTakesAVoteFromAReaderAndShowsTheTallyToWhoMayDecide). ## 0.1.2

### Patch Changes

- d2683c5: Guest views: a view somebody else wrote, in a sandboxed frame that can only ask. `@graview/guest` is a new package, the fourteenth.
  
  The host half is `@graview/guest/host`. `guestView({ url, name })` registers like any other view. `mountGuestView(element, { url, view, store, principal })` does the same without React, and `createGuestHost` is the host without the DOM. The frame gets `sandbox="allow-scripts"` and never `allow-same-origin`, so its origin is opaque and nothing of the host's is reachable from it. The host answers a guest's `guest-ready` only from that frame's own window and only from the opaque origin. Each answer is a fresh nonce and a `MessageChannel`, and the port before is closed. Every request over the port carries the nonce, or it is dropped unread.
  
  The host pushes `GuestProps`, the plain-data half of `ViewProps`: `node`, `nodes`, the `edges` among them, `implicated`, `flagged` and the rest, plus `acts`, the acts the viewer may run. All of it is read from `seenBy(principal)`, so a record the viewer may not see is in none of them. No principal, role or token is ever sent. The guest may ask for an act by name with its arguments, a navigation to a record, or a height. An act is applied through `store.apply` as the viewer, with `via: "view:<name>"`, so the policy refuses it in the same sentence it gives a click and the rail says "via <name>". A navigation goes only to a record the viewer may see, and a height is held to `maxHeight`. Acts are limited per frame (30 a minute by default) and messages too (120 a second), and saying ready again buys a new nonce, not a new allowance.
  
  The guest half imports nothing of the framework. `connectGuest()` from `@graview/guest` takes the first hello from its parent, and from `hostOrigin` when it is given. It offers `props`, `subscribe`, `act`, `navigate`, `size` and `autoSize`. `useGuest()` from `@graview/guest/react` is the same connection as a hook. `scripts/guest-sandbox.mjs` holds the sandbox in a real browser against a hostile guest in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit. It also found that Firefox and WebKit send a host cookie without `SameSite` on a guest's credentialed fetch when the guest has no CSP. The README says to set `SameSite` or serve guests with `connect-src 'none'`.
  
  `graview create` writes `@graview/guest` into a new project's dependencies and its linked dev aliases. `capabilities().shipped` names FR-04 (FR-04).
  
  Compatibility: additive — a new package and protocol, `GUEST_PROTOCOL` 1, held to the wire's rules (docs/stability.md). `capabilities().shipped` gains FR-04. Ops, stored formats, the wire, the declaration and derived tools are unchanged.
